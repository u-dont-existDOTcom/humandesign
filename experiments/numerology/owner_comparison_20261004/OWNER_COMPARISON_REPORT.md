Their richer relationship wording is a different result from improved impact classification. There is no improvement in the counted error total when the familiar indicators are combined. The always-flag control's lower error is an important warning: eight of the ten selected year labels are positive. This is not an unbiased sample of your whole life. Consequently, the figures are **not 70% versus80% predictive accuracy** and cannot establish sensitivity, specificity or a reliable ranking of complete systems. They are a useful failure check on the claim that adding systems necessarily improves predictions.
