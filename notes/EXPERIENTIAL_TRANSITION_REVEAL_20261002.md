## Methodological lesson
Owner-recalled dates are fallible labels. A post-reveal interpretation can become contaminated even when the underlying blind prediction is frozen correctly.

Therefore:
1. preserve original blind predictions;
2. distinguish memory-supplied labels from documentary dates;
3. mark chronology confidence/source for every life event;
4. prefer contemporaneous records (email, calendar, journals, photos, official records) when available;
5. if a remembered date later changes, withdraw any interpretation that depended on the old date rather than retrofitting the chart;
6. do not treat agreement with an unverified memory as strong evidence.

The corrected chronology is development data only.
