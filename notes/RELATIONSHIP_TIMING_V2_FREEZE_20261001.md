# Relationship timing V2 freeze

Date: 2026-10-01
Status: development model; future scan frozen before inspection.

## Purpose
Generate a forward ranking of relationship/romance-active periods from the owner natal chart after the first transit-only model failed to rank the revealed historical periods correctly.

## Established components reused
- annual profections as a year-context technique;
- transits to natal relationship significators;
- secondary progressions;
- solar-arc directions.

This is an adaptation/composition of established astrological timing techniques, not a claim of a novel astrological method. Scientific validity is not established.

## Frozen target set
Direct relationship targets:
- natal Venus;
- natal Moon because it is the natal 7th ruler;
- ASC/DSC axis.

Secondary contextual targets:
- natal Mars because it is tightly conjunct Venus in the owner chart;
- natal MC/IC only for major-life-context annotation, not primary relationship scoring.

## Frozen aspect/orb rules
Aspects: conjunction, sextile, square, trine, opposition.

Slow transits:
- Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto;
- qualifying orb <=1.0 degree to a direct relationship target.

Secondary progressions:
- progressed Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars;
- qualifying orb <=0.5 degree to a direct relationship target.

Solar arcs:
- directed Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto, ASC and MC;
- qualifying orb <=0.5 degree to a direct relationship target.

## Scoring
For each calendar month:
- +2.0 per qualifying slow-transit contact to Venus or Moon;
- +2.5 per qualifying slow-transit contact to ASC/DSC;
- +1.5 per qualifying secondary-progression contact to Venus or Moon;
- +2.0 per qualifying secondary-progression contact to ASC/DSC;
- +1.5 per qualifying solar-arc contact to Venus or Moon;
- +2.0 per qualifying solar-arc contact to ASC/DSC.

Annual-profection modifier, active only after at least one direct timed contact qualifies:
- x1.30 if the profected house is the 5th or 7th;
- otherwise x1.15 if the annual ruler is Venus or Moon;
- otherwise x1.00.

Cluster bonus:
- x1.20 if qualifying contacts come from at least two distinct timing families among transits, progressions, and solar arcs.

No points are awarded for generic whole-network density.

## Interpretation boundary
The score measures timing density under this development model, not probability of meeting a partner and not relationship quality. Saturn/Uranus/Neptune/Pluto contacts may coincide with formation, commitment, change, idealization, separation, or restructuring.

## Evaluation boundary
Historical periods already revealed by the owner are development cases only. Future ranking begins after 2026-10-01 and must not be reweighted after inspecting the resulting peaks.
