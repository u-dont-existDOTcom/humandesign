# Personal-reading specificity test — draft for new consented cases

Status: prepared, not launched. This tests the user's reported individual-reading phenomenon. It does not replace the distinct offender/outcome question and does not authorize contacting people or publishing private data.

## Frozen method

Recover the exact astrology/numerology reading method that appeared to work, its permitted inputs and all interpretation/combination rules. Record its version and freeze it before the new cases. Do not substitute the reduced date-only classifier tested in this follow-up. Birth-time-dependent claims require source quality and a declared uncertainty policy; unavailable inputs remain unavailable.

A method can be holistic and combine rules, but may not be revised after learning which profile was the true one. Record any discretionary interpretation made by the reader. Development work and confirmatory work remain separate.

## Participants and information boundaries

Use fresh consenting adults, ideally at least twenty for a first cohort; this is a planning target, not a power guarantee. Explain before participation that several readings may use comparison data and that the exercise is experimental, not advice. Store names and birth records privately outside the public repository. No contact or data collection has been authorized by this draft alone.

The chart/numerology calculator may receive the required birth/name data but must not receive personality, survey answers, life history or prior-chat context. The interpreting reader receives only the calculated symbolic inputs that the frozen method actually needs, not identities or biographical clues where avoidable. Never assume a fresh prompt alone prevents model recognition or repository/memory contamination.

For each person, create one own-data reading and four decoy readings, with matching criteria frozen in advance for birth cohort, sex and naming-language conventions. Use one common self-contained prompt/method and equalize detail, length and tone. Keep unchanged-name versus birth-name layers separate. Remove visible names, dates, sign labels, numerology numbers and explicit identity clues from the reading presented for ranking, while retaining the meaning of the predicted traits. Audit that masking itself does not change the predictions. A participant who recognizes their configuration is not automatically a valid blind case; record it under a predeclared recognition rule.

If using existing public figures' decoy inputs, do not access biographical content, and never publish their guessed psychological traits as established facts. Prefer consented cohort-derived decoys where practical.

## Freeze and primary outcome

Freeze/hash inputs, method, complete readings, presentation and randomization key before asking for ratings. Keep the key away from the reader and participant. Record all five initial fit ratings, then a first-choice selection and full ranking before reveal or clarification. No feedback-driven editing is allowed in the primary test.

Primary statistic: fraction of people selecting their own-data reading first. Uniform randomized guessing among five gives 20%. Predeclare tie handling: either a mandatory first choice, or fractional success 1/k when the own reading is among k first-place ties; then use randomization inference appropriate to that rule. Do not switch tie rules after seeing results.

One participant is one primary unit. Do not count separate claims, repeated rankings or many decoy sets for the same person as independent participants. Freeze cohort size, exclusions and stopping rule before confirmatory scoring. Previously discussed people can check the procedure but are not fresh confirmation.

## Controls and interpretation

A separately disclosed generic-profile comparison may measure how much broad applicability contributes to ratings. Do not deceptively label an identical generic profile as uniquely calculated without advance participant consent to that research design.

High ratings for many readings with chance-level own-reading identification would support applicability without demonstrated birth-data specificity in that experiment. Strong above-chance identification with preserved blinding would support person-specific information from the supplied data, but would still require separating astrology/numerology from ordinary demographic/name information and replication on new people. Low performance would apply to that frozen method, not every conceivable reading method.

A trait-level success would not establish a murder/happiness prediction rule. To continue that separate outcome track, obtain a more valid independently sourced cohort, usable birth-time/full-name inputs for the chosen theory, and an independently defined outcome. Do not revive weak Life Path 7/33 findings merely because they were noticed earlier.
