## Methods, results, failures, independence

1. **Dean 1985 Part 1:** 1,198 timed Eysenck Personality Inventory respondents furnished extreme-E/N samples; two 54-person sets per dimension for computer analysis and a separate 160-person chart-reader test in Part 2. The computer compared tropical/sidereal signs, decans, elements, aspects, harmonic/orb/house/zone/angular factors: 132 individual tests and a 75-factor discriminant family (Placidus, midpoints ±2°, aspects ±7.5°, angularity ±10° among settings). Only 58 directional confirmations versus 66 expected, and 12 nominal p<.1 versus 13 expected; discriminant predicted 19 E and 18 N versus 18 expected for each. No replicated E/N chart factor. The self-attribution Sun-sign signal is a different, unblinded mechanism. Shanks helped analysis but is not a coauthor of this original.
2. **Gauquelin 1985:** 15,334 previously collected eminent professionals; five planetary pairs among Venus/Mars/Jupiter/Saturn (excluding Jupiter–Saturn); conjunction, sextile, square, trine, opposition ±5°, within-profession comparisons. Mean aspect departures ~1.4%, far below claimed key-sector differences; individually interesting cells at chance frequency, no stable association between aspect and key-sector signal. Schneider-Gauquelin variant is an alternate analysis of overlapping data, not replication. This paper does **not** independently revalidate sectors.
3. **Dean 1985 Part 2:** 160 extreme E/N people sampled from the same 1,198-person frame; 45 astrologers plus 45 no-chart guessers, 5,400 decisions per dimension. E/N correct astrological judgments 50.3% against 51.0% no-chart, chance 50%; no reproducible advantage by confidence/experience/technique or birth-time exactness. An auxiliary cold-reading cue task reached 72% for a skilled observer; sequential cue observations cannot be treated as a clean randomized intervention. Same parent E/N cohort as Part 1; no independent replication.
4. **Ertel 1988:** 20,528 Gauquelin profession records from eleven groups; five VE/MA/JU/SA pairs × three aspect types (0/90/180 ±5°) = 15 windows, 1,697 observed aspect events, ~8,462 births in evaluated windows. Equal-duration neighboring windows and ±266-day birth-date shifts furnish baseline/control; 165 comparisons had two nominal p<.05 versus five/six in shifted controls, none p<.01. No coherent aspect-window or gestational signal. Cohort overlaps the Gauquelin program; negative reanalysis, not a new census.
5. **Klein 1988:** 122 partner participants asked to rank one actual relationship description among five; control descriptions comprised unrelated couples or constructed partners sharing the actual partner’s Sun sign. Interchart aspects only; unknown times mean no houses/angles. Author reports aggregate p<.01, but 54 responding pairs create within-couple dependence, there are sex/control variations, and test instructions were changed during collection. The sign-matched contrast and all ranked failures must be retained; a participant-level independent prospective test is absent.
6. **Ertel 1995:** Gauquelin profession-series G-sector time-precision analysis, 27,198 planet-specific observations (duplicates from four of eleven profession groups; an earlier chart-series figure N22,689); around 8,388 non-rounded versus 19,010 rounded in aggregate. Contrary to precision-as-stronger-signal hypothesis, non-rounded births had ~1.02 percentage points *lower* G-sector prevalence overall, ~1.52 points lower after 1880 (reported p≈.03). Moon/Jupiter tendencies differ by planet and Saturn goes the other direction; a separate small Müller–Ertel French physician sample is contextual, not a clean overall replication. Cohort/era, socioeconomic recording habits, and duplicated planet observations complicate independence. Ertel explicitly does not identify a causal explanation.
7. **Dean 1995/96:** Re-assesses Jung’s historical 180+220+83 married pairs, 50 interchart contacts, incomplete reporting for sets 2–3. The first set’s leading Moon–Sun conjunction 18/180 is p=.00232 only **after selecting that contact**; approximately p=.11 across 50 candidate contacts. Top-four contacts overlap once between the three sets (approximately chance), none in all three. The combined 107 observed versus ~68.4 expected for three selected contacts yields inflated kappa≈.093. This is a **reanalysis of Jung**, not a new synastry cohort or a Dean/O’Neill meta-analysis.
8. **Dean 1997/98:** Reanalysis of John Addey’s harmonic/character-trait tests on Gauquelin biographical data: 158 selected traits, four professional subsets, four planets, 20 harmonics, single/multiple counts, yielding very many candidate amplitudes. About 12,000 amplitudes can pass Addey’s loose selection cutoffs by chance. Repeated synonymous traits per subject, variable biography length/eminence, preselected harmonic peaks, and expected counts weighted by *trait mentions* rather than independent *persons* invalidate simple nominal p-values; illustrative 94 self-willed athletes have sector-8 expectancy 10.9 by traits versus 8.2 by persons. This is a critique of **reused** Gauquelin biographical records, not a new independent negative birth cohort.
9. **Brady 2002:** 1,250 parent–child relationship records recruited through Australian astrologers/students, excluding times uncertain >10 minutes and nonfamily/nonhospital sources (~20% collected excluded); siblings and two parents create non-independent rows. Eight declared hypothesis families cover parental angular planets (±9°), rulership old/new, Moon “nodding,” reversed angular inheritance (child ±5°), matching signs, Moon–planet conjunction (±9°, applying/separating), exaltation and double signs. 338 chi-square tests (including controls): 18 p<.05 versus 16.9 expected, four p<.01 versus 3.4 expected, two p<.001 and one p<.00001. None of 42 new-rulership comparisons significant; many firstborn subgroup positives. Parent Moon nodding to firstborn Moon was strong in the recruited set but **failed in a 630-parent Gauquelin subsample**; exaltation alone null and reversed inheritance one of many significant. Sign/Ascendant null used approximate frequencies and 500 simulated births. No family-cluster correction or prospectively frozen familywise endpoint.
10. **Ruis 2008, TWO IDs/ONE original:** 77 male serial-killer charts with rated time data and 216 date-only male cases (noon chart; Moon and aspects imprecise), manually selected by a predeclared crime checklist; controls include ~6,000 AstroDatabank regional/era comparators, shuffled components and shuffling coupled month/day. Five main/minor hypotheses: mutable-sign excess 235 versus ~203 in timed 77 (Glass Δ=.31, one-sided p=.002, control variants p=.002–.008), 12th house 95 versus ~70 (Δ=.31, p≈.008), Moon aspect distribution p≈.001 timed; combined p≈.0005. Pisces alone p≈.065 in pooled sample; timed Moon–Saturn nominal p=.03, untimed Moon-aspect omnibus p=.27 and untimed Moon-Saturn not individually confirmed. Neptune hard-aspect pooled 457 versus 413.9 p=.008; Mars–slow-planet stress aspects **null** (four pair p≈.14–.98; global ≈.83); five Chiron–personal aspect families **null** (pair p≈.50–.87). Multiple outcomes share planets/participants and control construction; author applies Bonferroni .01 to five named families, but within-family searches, source selection and timing uncertainty remain. No prediction of criminality from birth chart is warranted.
11. **Ruis 2012 follow-up:** Recomputes the **same 77+216 subjects**, not new serial killers. Reports 54/77 versus 41 expected timed charts with ≥3 mutable indicators; 122/216 versus 94 expected in untimed (no Ascendant); timed and untimed mutable distributions homogeneity p=.13, but this does not prove exact-feature replication. Untimed Moon aspects nonsignificant, pooled without angles p=.01, Neptune hard-aspect effect smaller in untimed. New post-original omnibus signs p=.0002 and 10° longitude scan p<1e-5, house/sector omnibus p<.005 from the same people; additional comparisons/feature search must be counted. A control made of other public figures may retain ascertainment imbalance; no independent held-out cohort was added.
12. **Tarvainen 2013:** 910 technical authors + 1,849 historical mathematicians, of whom 99 award winners are a **subset**, total unique N2,759; date-only/noon. 25 favorable indicators (17 published, five Saturn–Sun, three Pluto–Mercury), major orbs tuned to 8°, sextile 5° on observed outcomes. Mean 1.847 versus 1.783 indicators/person (3.6% surplus), reported combined p=.001, groups .003/.02 and nested 99 p=.04; 17-item subset p=.03, five-item subset p=.005, three-item p=.08; **none of 25 individual tests Bonferroni-significant**. Historical and occupation selection; date/year shuffles and ±90-day temporal comparisons, not fresh independent replication.
13. **Tarvainen 2014:** ~20,892 Gauquelin hereditary couples/families, with 6,236 aspect-bearing fathers and 6,249 mothers; Venus–Saturn five Ptolemaic aspects (major ±7°, sextile ±5°), shuffled controls. Marriage-incidence hypothesis **failed**: men +0.7% p=.5, women +0.5% p=.6. Paternal age at first recorded child +92 days p=.008; maternal +62 days p=.04; husband–wife age difference +74 days p=.002 for aspect-bearing men, women ~−1 day p=.9; ≥10-year gap 343 vs303.6 p=.004. “Marriage delay” substitutes first child for marriage date; family data repeated in later papers, multiple subgroups/aspects uncorrected.
14. **Tarvainen 2015:** 6,285 Finnish theologians from biographical source, dates-only/noon; 11 prior Jupiter aspects p=.035. Expanded to 25 Jupiter aspects chosen after inspecting planet pairs p<.5 and changing orbs to 9° major: p=.003; 12 selected minor aspects ±1°/±3° p=.01. Individual semisquare and 108°/decile lacked expected working range; neither selection step held out. Out-of-sign versus in-sign strength not convincingly different. Mathematician comparisons reuse 2013 persons, not independent support.
15. **Tarvainen 2016:** 20,895 Gauquelin heredity couples, same family source as 2014. Seven traditional planets to mean North/South Node, conjunction orb tuned near 8°–14°, reported ±10°: husband North 8,318 vs8,164 p=.05, husband South 7,973 vs8,080 p=.12; wife North 8,083 vs8,141 p=.70, wife South 8,006 vs8,096 p=.15. Four-case pooled p=.07 **failed**; men pooled p=.03, women p=.41. Natal own-Node stratification men p=.007; observed interdependent couples and post hoc search. Appendix fake-couple control-generator comparison: Ruis time-window mean excess 1.6% (range −.3–4.8) versus targeted 2%, Tarvainen generator 1.8% (.8–2.8); original O’Neill scheme failed to detect injected excess in 9/10 simulations. This is **methodological simulation**, not independent evidence that a natal effect exists.
16. **Tarvainen 2017:** 23,231 Gauquelin persons in 15 occupational/other groups; 26 literature-derived planet rulers scored by conjunction to Sun/Moon/ASC/MC (±8°, luminaries +2°), angular houses, domicile and corresponding house; 1,000 shuffles. Combined score +0.8%, normal p=.008 (five of 1,000 shuffles reached/exceeded, empirical .005). Many prespecified groups/rulers **fail or reverse**: actors VE/NE −.3/−.4%, military SO −2.6%, aviators UR −2.2%, murderers PL −4.7%, politicians SA −5.2%, athletes MA −2.8%. Koch vs Whole Sign virtually tied (+1.04% p=.013 vs+1.06% p=.015), Equal p=.13; orb and ruler substitutions discussed after observed data. All Gauquelin reuse; not independent profession cohort.
17. **Tarvainen 2018 chart rulers:** 20,394 Gauquelin records/ten groups, partly same 2017 people; Ascendant ruler in focal profession house plus house-cusp ruler in same house, Koch, modern rulers, 1,000 component shuffles. Combined p=.005 / .003 and about +10% focal Chart Ruler count, but almost every individually named group **nonsignificant** (military .41, artists .34, journalists .15, mental .12, murderers .44, physicians .18, scientists .10, athletes .20, writers .50); only politicians p=.01. Source itself proposes different houses for military and writers after looking at results. 2021 Ptolemy-rule paper reanalyses this cohort.
18. **Tarvainen 2018 time-twin critique:** Synthetic 2,100 births spread over one week, injected +5 IQ for births during a narrow Mercury-first-house pulse; 100,000 simulations. The regular two-group test detects an injected synthetic change (mean p=.003) while adjacent-birth serial correlation gives mean p≈.48; its illustrative normal IQ SD is internally inconsistent (text says 15 then generation says 5). This is a **power counterexample under one chosen simulation**, not reanalysis of Dean’s actual N2,101 London birth cohort or evidence that actual same-hospital near-time comparisons succeed; primary theory-neutral outcome remains separate.
19. **Currey 2021:** Reanalyses Press’s original 311 timed NYC suicide cases and 311 matched birth-record controls (originally drawn from 2,250 deaths, only ~14% timed); no new deaths/controls. Press’s original broad scan failed consistency across three death-year groups. Currey scans ten planets/signs/Koch houses and five aspects after seeing prior null; Fire 470 vs436 p=.03, Air 427 vs472 p=.008, Saturn without 0/90/120/180 ±8° 31 vs17 p=.001 RR1.82, Pluto 40 vs56 p=.015; **other eight of ten unaspected planets** not convincingly increased; all-unaspected 244 vs228 p=.151. Aspect squares 1238 vs1202 p=.141, trines 1064 vs1091 p=.201, opposition deficit 469 vs523 p=.008 and sextile 949 vs882 p=.011. Some element table totals/labels disagree internally: distinguish raw counts from narrative direction; across 311 subjects, 4,595 aspect events cannot be treated as 4,595 independent persons. Natal factors cannot identify suicide risk; no held-out replication.
20. **Tarvainen 2021:** Same 20,394 Gauquelin group/house-ruler cohort as 2018; sweep 0°–10° house-cusp shift on observed labels and compare Koch/Equal/Placidus. Koch original p=.005 improves to reported 4×10⁻⁷ after outcome-selected 5° shift; observed focal rulers 1,833 vs1,690 initially and 1,853 vs1,670 shifted. Equal optimum ~6°, Placidus weaker. This is an in-sample sensitivity/reanalysis, **not confirmation on an independent cohort**, and cannot set a prospective 5° rule post outcome.
21. **Tarvainen 2022:** Four reused analyses: mathematicians N2,759 (2013), theologians N6,285 (2015), men and women in same ~20,892 couples (2014); the sum ~50,828 is **not independent births**. Tight-versus-wide trend appears principally among husband Venus–Saturn, **not consistently** across the other three. Applying-max-orb curves ~1°–2° wider in reported fits, but same-data threshold inspection, noon-imputed times for two groups and female reversal: male applied 7° +89 days versus separated 6° +70 days; female applied 7° +44 days versus separated 5° +76 days. No four-way held-out replication of one exact modifier.
22. **Godbout/Coron 2023 replication:** Their preceding 35(2) *training* paper optimizes 7 weighted dominance indicators after screening 42, using 189 biographies, 73 reused from Godbout 2020. The separate 36(1) article obtains 61 *person-disjoint* AstroDatabank AA/A biographies (reject rounded xx:00 times), but reuses the same authors, Mastro-generated biographical target method, semantic keyword evaluator and pre-optimized weights. Across three max-rank × three tries settings, selected rank≤2/two tries achieves 39/61 versus 23.8 expected, reported p≈.0000317/r=.512; rank≤3/three tries 47 versus 43.8 expected, p=.182 **fails**. Training-based 30,000 shuffles do not necessarily rerun the full biography/keyword/model-selection pipeline; person-disjoint replication is not independent software/endpoint validation. Explicit scoring weights from parent: Moon major aspect 8.3, ≥5 major aspects 8.2, Asc ruler 7.6, Placidus XII 5.3, DC ruler 5.2, aspect to Asc 4.7, Placidus IX midpoint ±5° 1.9. Orbs 0/180 ±10°, 90/120 ±8°, 60 ±6°, midpoints 2° direct/1.5° indirect. Preserve all nine reported settings and the original table where prose misstates the rank≤3/three-try expected count.
23. **Godbout 2024:** Twelve planetary-dominance schemes compared on a total N250 formed by **189 training + the same 61 replication biographies** (Astrotheme comparator N246); this is not a third independent replication. Best alternative Lenoble 2 tries 76/250 versus51.6 expected, p<6.75×10⁻⁵/r≈.24; other comparators include nulls. Changed model coefficients and ranks are compared after viewing overlapping outcomes; familywise selection, ambiguous keyword target and full end-to-end null must be considered.
24. **Godbout/Brun 2026, direct CF-004:** N141 named public figures: **68 southern** with AstroDatabank AA/A/B timed births, sufficient ChatGPT-accessible biography, and **73 northern** Le Monde portraits **reused from Godbout 2020 and the 2023 training set**. Mastro Expert's 2,885 astrology keywords from ~25 authors score *combined* planet-in-sign **and** aspect factors and midpoints; house placements excluded, but chart angle aspects included. South and north each scored twice, original and 180°-ayanamsa-inverted all planetary/angle signs, aspects and midpoints left unchanged. ChatGPT (authors cite 2025 GPT-5) was prompted by **person's name** to output ~150 single-word biographical/personality nouns including speculative “inner life”; new Semantic Proximity Evaluator counts exact word matches, no synonyms; sum Mastro scores divided by Mastro word count, compare two scores per person. Inversion wins **48/68 south versus 26/73 north**, one-tailed two-proportions z=4.155/p≈1.627×10⁻⁵/h=.716; separate 50/50 binomials p=.000457 and .00931. Distance from equator **fails** two exploratory tests. No matched N/S sampling, concealed name-independent measured traits, semantic permutation/null rerunning ChatGPT+keyword dictionary, or prospective independent replication. **Crucial reproduction failures inside printed tables:** Table 1 Allende labels original .9989 versus inverted .9425 yet states positive inverted-minus-original difference .0456; Anitta .3616 versus .9617 gives difference .6001, not printed .0201. Table 4 Musk .3170 versus .3329 gives .0159, not printed .1108. Figure 6 calls the June 28 birth's *non-inverted* Sun Capricorn and *inverted* Sun Cancer, while the prose calls Capricorn the inverted position: impossible to reconcile with standard tropical labels without correction. Therefore the 48/68 count cannot be mechanically verified from the published examples alone. Inversion effect is a direct **reported** contrast but its mapping/result table requires author clarification or independent reimplementation; this is not a scientific-model decision.
25. **Daigno 2026:** Gauquelin French murderers N620 timed and Wikidata serial killers N751 date-only/noon, two persons deduplicated across sets. Mars with Moon/Sun/Mercury/Venus, 0° or 180°, orb ±2°: eight individual tests; one-sided Bonferroni .00625. Mercury–Mars conjunction in both (French 21/105 vs772/7,472 p=.00231; Wikidata 27/122 vs1,329/12,891 p=.00010). Venus–Mars conjunction only one cohort; most Moon/Sun/oppositions fail corrected threshold and several reverse. Pooled aspect-*event* measures 73/517 vs3,555/37,425 French p=.000194/h=.144 and 101/630 vs6,241/64,496 serial p≈4.3×10⁻⁸/h=.191: events within person correlated; calendar-day ephemeris controls not demographic/person-matched. French birth thirds p=.0035/.0056/**.210 fail** and Mercury conjunction thirds .00062/**.151**/**.449**; serial thirds mixed too. Date-only Wiki quality, ascertainment, shared historical Gauquelin cohort, sex/region/secular calendar confounding prevent person-level or forensic use.

**Adjacent 2024 zodiac-sales paper (not one of the 25 direct articles):** Godbout/Brun, *Correlation* 37(1), 73–84, uses 313,368 German 1991–94 Sun-sign-book sales and rankings by 70 astrologers, not 141 Northern/Southern public figures. ## Cohort graph and outstanding access

- Gauquelin eminent-profession records: Gauquelin 1985 → Ertel 1988/1995 → Dean 1997 trait critique → Tarvainen 2017/2018/2021. Reuse and partial overlap, **not six independent datasets**. Gauquelin heredity couples: Tarvainen 2014/2016/2022 (men and women are paired); Brady’s smaller Gauquelin-family subset is a separate *analysis*, not a fresh cohort.
- E/N test: Dean 1985 Parts 1 and 2 draw from the same 1,198 volunteers. Serial-killer Ruis 2012 repeats his 2008 77+216. Godbout 2023 parent 189 → replication 61 new *people* with inherited pipeline → 2024 250 combined *old people*; Godbout/Brun 2026 reuses the original 73 northern Le Monde biographies. Currey 2021 revisits Press’s 311 cases/311 controls.
- No additional missing **issue** is required for the 25 listed requests. Full scans and the Ruis author-copy issue are identifiable by the hashes. Exact 2024 37(1) terminal page and mathematical per-cell OCR of early scans can be checked in the local PDFs; avoid manufacturing unavailable count detail. Controlled subscription access is not a license to publish PDFs into this repository.
- No owner-known outcomes, prospective participant data, recruitment, model fitting, or product decisions were used here. The frozen Pro decision and Wave 4 version are historical, unchanged by this evidence-only audit.
