## Findings and constraints for the coordinator

- **Direct blind or partly blind human chart tests:** Ertel's 40-chart politician/painter classification (11 follow-up astrologers; group mean 10.7 per 20, p=0.75) and Smit's five-case Knegt replication (21 astrologers; 29/105 correct versus 21 chance expectation) warrant originals. Knegt's initial ten 1933 readings include unusually specific successes, but chain of custody and possible prior knowledge remain open; the follow-ups reuse five of those subjects. Simkhada and Simkhada report a null in 100 Nepali sex workers/trafficked girls, with factors and comparison details unavailable in UA. Nasser's 12 styles read just **one** woman's chart; six retrospective life outcomes are six rows on the same person.
- **Proposed feature families:** Verhulst tested five slow/fast planetary pair phase distributions in 102 anthroposophic pioneers with 15° bins and an original Steiner reference chart dated incorrectly. His normality p values (Sun–Saturn .012; Moon–Saturn .30; Mercury–Neptune .18; Venus–Jupiter .002; Mars–Pluto .10) are original reported statistics. UA's runs tests and critique are separately recorded. Gibson's five DSM-IV patient groups share one control N=24; the p.335 plot names major depression N=18, anxiety disorder N=10, schizophrenia N=14, ADHD children N=16, addictive disease N=13. Magi aspects/parallels, Tarnas outer-planet archetypes/transits, and Fuzeau-Braesch's 30 cloned cows have unresolved denominators, flexible meanings, orbs or controls. Do not convert these proposals directly into tuned algorithm rules.
- **Baseline and leakage controls:** Bourque's 50-person study reports sun-sign matching only among people interested in astrology; get the thesis and subgroup statistics. King and Koehler's graphology experiments are methodological analogs: semantic correspondences outweighed intentionally manipulated empirical correspondences. Rowe's strangers generated numerous personal coincidences. Sham readings, wrong charts, identity knowledge, and visual scoring each have examples here. They motivate blinded decoys and deterministic coding, not a conclusion on the natal claims by themselves.
- **Prevalence and selection:** UA derives the chance coverage for Tarnas's many broad outer-planet aspects and computes a ~0.26 baseline for Magi's enhancement challenge; these are **review-author calculations**, not original effects. UA's Bayes examples use reported frequencies but do not identify their primary tabulations here; trace the Mars-sector champion and Eysenck personality datasets before reuse. The 2004 Sedna survey is about how meaning is assigned and must not be read as a test of Sedna's predictive value.
- **Contextual analogs:** Biorhythm, graphology, phrenology, clone-behavior, Tarot/archetype and bibliometric entries are marked by their `study_family`/`source_type`. They test error mechanisms, comparators or outcome coding. They are not positive or negative natal astrology replications. Theoretical books, commentaries, historical essays, practitioner advice and the remaining §7.2.2011.3 text contain no new quantifiable natal study in this range; they were read but not padded into D rows.
