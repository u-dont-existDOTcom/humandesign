### Numerical audit detail

The read-only numerical audit used a synthetic 20-network by 20-pair design, not human data. Exact recipe: each network has its own hospital-date fixed effect; repeat `linspace(.1,2.9,20)` for `x` over the 20 networks. A fresh `Generator(PCG64(1234))` draws 400 standard-normal `z`, then 400 standard-normal `noise`. Set the first nuisance column to `x + epsilon*z`, the other thirteen to zero, and `D=.2+.03*x+.003*epsilon*noise`; both exposure bounds equal `x`. The residual rank ratio exceeds `1e-10`. Compare the retained within-transformed weighted design with QR coefficients and cluster-score CR1 covariance, using the identical weights and `k`.

The gate independently reproduced the defect in its test environment above with `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1`, `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1` and `MKL_NUM_THREADS=1`. At `epsilon=1e-6`, the implementation returned approximately beta `0.02994624`, SE `0.06880094` and weighted RSS `2.975e-11`; QR gave beta `0.02997909`, SE `0.000151843` and optimum RSS `1.700e-16`. At `epsilon=1e-7`, returned beta/SE/RSS were `0.03096769` / `34.99761` / `5.034e-8`, while QR gave approximately the same beta/SE as above and RSS `1.700e-18`. A separate reviewer also reproduced instability in a different NumPy/BLAS environment and using controls built from synthetic birth records.
