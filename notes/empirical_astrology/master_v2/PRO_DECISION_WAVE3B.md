## What the expanded evidence changes

### Matching, ablations and null calibration

Godbout's 2020 first-rank counts are 6/41 and 3/32; the latter reported first-rank p=.077 does not establish replication of that endpoint. The 41 and 32 people are distinct records within one programme. Whether the analysis and vocabulary were fixed before the second set's outcomes is a separate question. Shared software or dictionaries alone do **not** invalidate a replication; applying the same frozen method to new people is normally desirable. The unverified analytic chronology, corpus construction and null calibration are the limiting issues.

The 2021 midpoint comparison reuses all 73 records. Five variants, semantic modes and several top-k choices make it in-sample selection, not a held-out component gain. The 2022 random-keyword audit shows why the fixed one-hit expectation cannot simply be assumed for the actual many-to-many matching geometry. It is simulation evidence about calibration, with no new people. Its approximate .59 random-predictor scale is not a human effect size or a numerical correction to the later r=.51 claim. Without raw data and executable code, I cannot supply a corrected effect or p-value.

NC-016 is now binding: any reopened semantic model must freeze blinded biography coding, vocabulary, semantic model, list lengths, ties, ranking statistic and search universe, and must pass complete-pipeline label and random-keyword controls. A favorable semantic overlap score can reflect descriptor construction or corpus structure without birth specificity.

### Orb selection and failed holdouts

Tarvainen's minimum-p orb procedure estimates a threshold from the same outcomes used to evaluate it. Choosing ±90-day controls because they improve p adds another search dimension. In the theologian study, the planned 18-combination aggregate was −0.5%/null; three later nominal signals and an optimized 1.9° orb do not reverse that failure. NC-017 requires all such decisions to remain development choices until frozen and replicated.

Noblitt searched 313 derivation equations and selected five. Their separate N=91 holdout correlations were .1131, −.0024, −.0196, −.0109 and .1464, with zero of five reported holdout successes. The holdout has limited precision and noon charts, so it does not settle every minute-sensitive hypothesis. It does provide direct failure evidence for the equations actually selected.

Niehenke screened 579 astronomical predictors, selected 28, and failed the global held-out test. Its alternating internal split belongs to one volunteer recruitment frame; it is not an external cohort. Its knowledge/self-attribution findings reinforce concealment and prior-knowledge measurement. These results join Startup's one N=911 cohort and broad null, rather than adding four independent Startup studies. NC-019 makes global and holdout failures mandatory reporting alongside positive subsets.

### Positive relationship and sector evidence is retained with its scope

Westran's later 1,300 relationship records report conjunction/trine/opposition counts of 148/311/159 against expected 117/232/116: descriptive excesses of approximately 2.38/6.08/3.31 percentage points. This is a substantive positive pattern to preserve and audit. It does not establish a natal personality feature. The 2.6 million constructed false pairings do not supply 2.6 million independent people. Recurrent partners, connected relationship networks, timing cleanup, ascertainment and the complete development path need record-level verification before cluster-adjusted uncertainty or external replication credit can be assigned.

The Gauquelin minute-jitter study addresses random rounding under its assumed distribution, using approximate houses. It does not resolve nonrandom rounding, source selection, exact sectors or disputed admission rules. Carter's 27/38 versus theoretical 19/38 is a 21.05-percentage-point descriptive excess in distinct historical births. A prior finding can legitimately motivate a new-data replication; the date of the motivation is not itself disqualifying. Here the approximate mapping, uncertain original ascertainment/time quality, small sample and non-equivalent mortality outcome prevent exact replication claims or a transportable profession/personality coefficient.

Ertel's Carlson reanalysis adds no people. The 86/116 first-or-second choices versus 77.3 expected is about a 7.5-point excess, with reported one-sided p=.054. The reconstructed τ=.088/p=.037 result is marginal among several dependent endpoints, alongside p=.24 and .28 failures. It fails even a three-test Holm threshold; the extraction actually lists four endpoints. Reconstructed ratings clustered by chart and astrologer are not independent subjects.

NC-018 is binding with a qualification: participant independence, analytic independence, external transport and shared measurement methods are different properties. Reanalyses add no cohort; unknown overlap adds no verified corroboration. A common frozen pipeline does not by itself duplicate otherwise independent people.

## Frozen prospective protocol

The JSON is the complete implementation contract. Its scientific constants are frozen now; operational site identities, permissions, closing dates and file/software hashes must be completed before any authorized launch.

- **Design:** two independently evaluated response cohorts, A and B, with disjoint people, families, hospitals and healthcare/recruitment networks. Birth events can be historical; the questionnaire responses and analysis are prospective relative to this freeze. Both manifests are fixed before either reveal.
- **Sampling:** 20 independent networks per cohort, one hospital each, with 100 preselected disjoint pairs per hospital and at least 20 birth dates. Primary participants are English-speaking adults aged 18–65 without prior chart interpretation or own-sign trait knowledge. Recorded birth times require documented uncertainty no wider than ±1 minute. Multiple births and known shared family/household/relationship components are excluded by outcome-blind rules.
- **Pairing:** same hospital/local date, sex, delivery mode, recruitment source and precision category; at most 180 minutes apart over the full uncertainty intervals. Maternal-age and gestational-age calipers are fixed. A seeded SHA-256 ordering greedily chooses disjoint pairs before answers; no person contributes repeated pairs and no missing response is re-paired.
- **Outcome:** the official 50-item IPIP Big-Five factor-marker sample, with exact positive/reverse item keys in the JSON. Each of the five ten-item means contributes equally to normalized RMS personality distance on [0,1]. No trait selection, profile-correlation alternative or outcome-derived standardization is permitted. The strict confirmation gate requires every preselected pair to complete the measure; attrition is reported as incomplete-cohort evidence rather than repaired by re-pairing. The official sample and scoring instructions were checked on 2026-09-28: [questionnaire](https://ipip.ori.org/new_ipip-50-item-scale.htm), [scoring](https://ipip.ori.org/newScoringInstructions.htm).
- **Estimand:** the partial slope of that distance per elapsed birth hour, conditional on hospital-date fixed effects and a fixed set of clock-phase, maternal-age, gestation, birthweight covariates. Matching/fixed effects preserve site, cohort, season and recruitment structure. This estimates a conditional association, not a causal birth-time effect.
- **Inference:** equal network weighting, a network-cluster CR1 interval, and 99,999 restricted wild-cluster bootstrap-t draws. The null retains the actual timestamps, pairs and conventional covariates. It is an approximate clustered-model test; it is not exact random assignment of birth times.
- **Threshold:** a two-sided 95% lower confidence bound above **0.005 normalized distance units/hour**, plus one-sided bootstrap p≤.025. This minimum is an explicit prospective design choice, not an empirical or clinical threshold. A development-only power/null-calibration report is required before launch; the nominal sample counts do not guarantee power.
- **Robustness:** all leave-one-network-out and lower/upper birth-time-uncertainty analyses are reported. Prespecified random-feature and maternal-age negative controls share a Holm family. Model rank failure, leakage, overlap, nonfinite inference or failed diagnostic gates cannot be repaired by switching models on the held-out cohort.
- **Replication:** A and B must each meet the full gate. Pooling, a selected subgroup or a secondary result cannot rescue a failure in either cohort.

Estimating the prespecified association coefficient inside a validation cohort is necessary for this inferential test. It does not retrain a chart-to-personality predictor, select a model or authorize production weights. Any future predictive coefficients must be learned in development and frozen before prospective responses.

The protocol is deliberately conditional on measured covariates, documented timing and the selected response population. It may prove infeasible or underpowered; that is a reason to report the limitation and obtain a new Pro protocol before outcomes, not permission for Work to loosen it. Even replicated success would leave environmental, obstetric, cultural and unmeasured-confounding explanations open.

## Success, failure and what Work may do

A positive label requires both untouched cohorts to satisfy the same complete statistical, quality, diagnostic and robustness gates. If an upper interval lies below the minimum effect, report that the specified meaningful effect is excluded under the working model. A wide interval is inconclusive; nonsignificance alone is not evidence of exact absence. An opposite estimate stays opposite. Missing raw data, a nonestimable model and an uninstantiated feature are reported as such, never as null findings.

**Work may now implement this decision and run mechanical development evaluations** after both decision files and corrected evidence checkpoint `e7f82ab` are present. It may implement the conventional baselines, theory-neutral elapsed-time feature, empty astrology mappings, provenance, null calibration, ablations and failure reporting. Eligible non-owner development data and synthetic engineering controls are permitted. Summary p-values cannot substitute for unavailable participant data.
