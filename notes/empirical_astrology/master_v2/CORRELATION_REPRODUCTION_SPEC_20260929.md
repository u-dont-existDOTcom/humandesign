## A. Dominance: reproduce the measured target before interpreting its association

Requestable artifacts, or equivalent lawfully available reproducible materials:

1. Stable public-person identifiers linking the 189 training, 61 replication and 250 comparison records; exact source biographies, language, versions and extraction/cleaning chronology; records of exclusions and rounded-time handling. Keep licensed full text out of public Git.
2. Complete keyword corpus, thesaurus/cleaning procedure, Mastro version and settings, ten planet-tripled chart-conditioned portraits per person, their full word scores and denominator totals, and the resulting target scores/ranks including ties.
3. Exact seven predictor definitions, weights, all orbital/house/rulership conventions and tie handling; an immutable checkpoint establishing what was fixed before the 61 outcomes were examined. Do not substitute a modern reimplementation without identifying and testing its departures.
4. All nine rank/try success vectors, not only their sums; the original 30,000-shuffle procedure, seed/software, per-sample planet frequencies and candidate geometry. Determine which decisions, if any, used the 61 test outcomes.

**First acceptance boundary — source reproduction.** Recreate the original 189/61 counts and all nine endpoints with no fitted repairs. Differences remain differences; any corrected source export must be versioned with the author's or reproducer's explanation. Reproducing a published table is engineering evidence, not empirical validation of its construct.

**Second boundary — fixed-model biography-specific null.** Preserve each person's chart and fixed seven-factor predictor. Reassign whole biography profiles within predeclared defensible exchangeability sets, reuse the frozen chart portraits, recompute their biography-dependent target scores/ranks and all cleaning/coding stages affected by reassignment, and calculate the same nine endpoint statistics. Preserve person-level dependence, rank ties, profile lengths, lexical distributions and selection. The combined null must account for the endpoint family; finished-target shuffling alone does not preserve the chart's role in target construction.

Exact exchangeability strata, statistic/family procedure, seeds, draw count and handling of sparse strata must be frozen before this new null is run. Do not declare them frozen here: required person-level covariates and complete software are not yet available. A fresh valid frozen-model null is a reanalysis of existing data, not an untouched replication.

**Separate development question.** To estimate how often the original discovery process finds such a rule under no biography specificity, rerun the entire development search, including selection among 42/18 factors, coefficient fitting and affected target construction. This is not the same as the frozen-model test. Do not automatically redo training optimization inside an untouched validation test, or charge historical training choices again simply because they existed.

**Future transport question.** An independent, name- and birth-fact-blind trait measurement can test whether the construct predicts personality outside its own chart-conditioned target. It needs its own exact pre-outcome mapping and protocol; no such feature is activated here.

## B. Southern inversion: reproduce all 141 binary decisions

Required artifacts:

- All 141 stable person IDs, source/selection rules, northern/southern membership, birth-data precision and exclusions, with intersections against all prior Godbout cohorts.
- Both chart longitudes and displayed labels, the 180-degree operation and its exact settings, aspects/midpoints/angle handling and software version.
- Complete frozen French biographical word lists and their generation provenance, including model/date, prompts, retries, edits, duplicate handling and actual list lengths. Do not silently treat “about 150” as exactly 150 unique traits.
- Full word-score exports and denominators for both chart versions, individual paired scores/differences, exact-zero/tie policy and hemisphere aggregate counts.

**Source replay must resolve** the three printed subtractions, Figure 6 orientation and any other discrepancies before accepting the headline count as reproduced. Do not infer a correction to the full dataset from three examples. Keep both the original and corrected/reimplemented outputs with provenance.

**Conditional semantic null.** Once the fixed scoring system is reproducible, compare real matched biographies with appropriately matched wrong biographies, preserving north/south ascertainment, era/language/fame/profile-length structure and reused-person dependence. Compute the same paired transformation and hemisphere contrast. Two models do not imply an equal-probability binary null; demonstrate the actual baseline rather than assume 50/50.

**Identity/world-knowledge control.** Named-person GPT output can contain information correlated with birthplace, era or known chart interpretations. A profile permutation does not prove these channels absent. Use independent masked biography inputs or independently measured traits for a separate sensitivity/validation design. Do not regenerate unaffected frozen profiles solely to make a permutation look “end-to-end”; regenerate all stages that actually depend on the reassigned input. If adaptive prompting or profile selection was part of discovery, it belongs in the development search ledger.

Preserve the two failed latitude-distance tests and distinguish the combined sign/aspect/midpoint system from an isolated zodiac-sign mechanism.

## C. Other candidates: minimum information before a stronger claim

Tightness/applying requires an exact body/aspect/outcome rule, a frozen orb curve and speed/application definition, correct noon-time uncertainty treatment, couple clustering, and untouched same-domain data. Four reused applications with different fitted settings are not one fixed replication.

Sector/profession claims require actual participant intersections, verified birth-record quality, era/site/recording controls and exact sector geometry. Aspect-null papers do not independently validate sectors. Family studies require parent/child/sibling cluster units and preservation of the unsuccessful comparison as well as the recruited-sample positive.

Case-ascertained violence/suicide analyses require person-level event vectors, correctly matched person controls and timing strata for any stronger aggregate inference. Calendar-day counts and multiple events per person are not independent people. No methodological improvement in this lane authorizes clinical, forensic or individual-risk use.

## D. Complete prospective specification before any new outcomes

Any future request to add an astrology hypothesis must specify and hash, before outcome access: one exact claim; endpoint/instrument and immutable scoring; chart/trait mapping; body, aspect, orb, angle, house and timing definitions; eligible population and record quality; site/calendar matching; person/family/network unit; exclusions and missingness; complete selection and multiplicity family; source null and controls; sample size and power assumptions; software/dependency versions and seeds; stopping; collection and reveal windows; blinding/access separation; all success, failure, opposite and nonestimable outcomes; and an untouched replication cohort.

These requirements do not retroactively preregister existing results. A development pass only admits a candidate to fresh testing; a development failure cannot be relabeled a successful repair by adding more process. No tested outcome may be reused to validate a version selected from it. A and B must both satisfy the actual version's declared gate, rather than pooling to rescue a failure.
