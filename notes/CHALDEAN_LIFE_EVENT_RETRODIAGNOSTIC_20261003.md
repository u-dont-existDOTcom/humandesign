This is worth preserving prospectively but one past hit is not validation.

## Bottom line

Chaldean/Cheiro WOULD have supplied useful additional clues:
- 2005 first love;
- 2010 8-state;
- early 2013 life-year 1 / Ann life-year 2;
- especially 2014 and 2018;
- 2020 and 2026 at year level.

But the source-defined Cheiro number set is broad and generates many candidate periods.

Therefore:
- it looks **more promising than the Decoz current-name overlay alone** for event timing;
- it has **not yet shown that it predicts owner life events better than the full Decoz forecasting system**;
- its strongest incremental value may be as an independent numerology feature layer alongside astrology, not as a standalone event clock.
