# Impact-salience timing hypothesis

Date: 2026-10-02
Status: development hypothesis; not validated.

## Observation motivating the hypothesis

Two revealed parental-loss cases differ sharply in subjective and life-course impact:

- the father's 2002 death was sudden and highly disruptive to the owner's life;
- the mother's 2022 death was prolonged and had comparatively little effect on the owner's life trajectory.

The father-specific post-reveal V4 development repair ranked the 2002 event very strongly.
The blind maternal-loss models missed the 2022 event.

## Hypothesis

If there is any real longitudinal astrological timing signal, it may correlate more strongly with **major person-level transitions / subjective salience / life-course disruption** than with the mere objective occurrence of an external event such as a relative's death.

This is a hypothesis, not an explanation of the current cases.

## Required distinctions

Keep separate:
1. objective event occurrence;
2. event morphology (sudden/prolonged, violent/nonviolent, acute/chronic);
3. subjective impact;
4. durable life-course change.

Do not use low impact after a miss to retroactively excuse the miss.

## Test design

Before chart scoring, collect event records and independent impact labels using behaviorally anchored criteria such as:
- major change in residence, work, family structure, daily routine, identity, finances, relationships, or long-term goals;
- duration of downstream consequences;
- whether the person describes the event as a major turning point.

Then compare frozen timing models on:
- high-impact non-death events;
- low-impact bereavements;
- high-impact bereavements;
- ordinary control periods.

Primary question:
Does timing score discriminate high-impact transitions from low-impact events and background better than it discriminates event occurrence alone?

## Controls

- Impact labels must be collected before chart/timing results are shown.
- Person-level holdout required.
- Preserve false peaks.
- Test neutral, sudden/acute, chronic/prolonged, and impact-salience variants separately.
- Do not retune on the owner father/mother cases and count them as validation.
