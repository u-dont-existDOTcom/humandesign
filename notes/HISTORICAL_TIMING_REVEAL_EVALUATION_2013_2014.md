## Method changes suggested by the failures

1. Separate endpoints:
   - relationship formation/onset;
   - chronic relationship pressure;
   - acute danger/escalation;
   - pregnancy/family-state activation;
   - exact child-birth timing.

2. Do not expect one monthly density score to identify all endpoints.

3. Progressions/solar arcs are primarily state/window indicators unless a separate exact-event timing rule is frozen.

4. For acute-event timing, a future development model may test a two-stage design:
   - slow/progressed background state;
   - fast trigger such as Mars for exact timing.
   This is a post-reveal hypothesis and requires independent testing.

5. Preserve the original misses. Do not relabel the 2012 danger candidate or 2011 child-birth candidate as successes.
