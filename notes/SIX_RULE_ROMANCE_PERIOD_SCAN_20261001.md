## Main conclusion

Using the exact same <=1° slow-transit/network criterion that made January 2026 stand out:

- January 2026 is strong, but not uniquely strong for romance.
- Stronger or at least equally strong adult past candidates include **Sep–Oct 2004**, **Oct 2010–Jan 2011**, and **Oct–Nov 2024 / Apr–May 2025**.
- The **next clearly comparable future period is March–May 2030, peaking in April 2030**.
- Later higher-intensity clusters appear in **early 2039**, **late 2044**, **spring 2049**, and especially **spring 2052**.

Do not interpret intensity as positive outcome. Saturn/Uranus/Pluto combinations can coincide with commitment, separation, redefinition, disruption, or transformation as readily as with relationship formation.
