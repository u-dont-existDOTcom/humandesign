## Why Decoz was used

Earlier work used the labels:
- Expression
- Heart's Desire / Soul Urge
- Personality
- Minor Expression / Minor Heart's Desire / Minor Personality

Those labels and the A=1...I=9 cyclic letter table belong to the modern Western/Pythagorean lineage. Hans Decoz is a particularly explicit, internally consistent, well-documented implementation of that lineage, with clear rules for:
- full birth name;
- current/use name;
- master numbers;
- component-wise reduction;
- name changes.

Therefore Decoz was a reasonable *implementation choice inside that lineage*.

It was NOT justified to treat Decoz as "the numerology rule" without first comparing competing lineages.

## Main competing English-name systems

### 1. Modern Western / Pythagorean — Decoz-style

Primary rule:
- full name at birth -> core Expression, Heart's Desire, Personality;
- current/use name -> Minor versions layered on top.

Decoz explicitly says:
- the birth name remains the lifelong blueprint/core;
- the short/current name influence is minor in comparison;
- current name is the name actually used to introduce oneself, regardless of legal status.

Primary/author source:
- https://www.worldnumerology.com/numerology-expression/
- https://www.worldnumerology.com/numerology-for-name-changes/
- Hans Decoz & Tom Monte, *Numerology: Key to Your Inner Self* (1994).

Implication:
changing Joel Rosenblum -> Joel Shipowo changes only the current-name overlay, not the core birth-name numbers.

### 2. Chaldean / Cheiro

Cheiro's rule is materially different.

In *Cheiro's Book of Numbers*, Cheiro states that for the name number one should use:
- "the name one is most known by."

This makes the active/public/use name primary for the Name Number rather than a merely minor overlay.

Primary text copy:
- https://dokumen.pub/cheiros-book-of-numbers.html

Cheiro uses the Chaldean letter table:
- 1: A I J Q Y
- 2: B K R
- 3: C G L S
- 4: D M T
- 5: E H N X
- 6: U V W
- 7: O Z
- 8: F P
- 9 is not assigned to letters.

Compound totals are emphasized along with the reduced root.

Implication:
the current/public name can matter much more strongly than under Decoz.

## Other systems

Kabbalistic / Hebrew gematria traditions are not directly interchangeable with English Pythagorean or Chaldean numerology:
- they depend on Hebrew spelling/transliteration and different symbolic structures;
- they do not provide a clean apples-to-apples answer for the English-name question without first freezing a transliteration convention.

Chinese name-number systems are likewise structurally different and should not be mixed into the same calculation.

## Project methodology going forward

Do NOT silently mix systems.

For every numerology result, record:
1. system/lineage;
2. letter-value table;
3. which name role is being used (birth / current / public / legal / spiritual / nickname);
4. reduction rule;
5. master/compound-number policy.

For Joel name-change questions, compare at minimum:
- Decoz/Pythagorean;
- Cheiro/Chaldean.

Do not average their numbers.

If the systems disagree, preserve the disagreement as a testable model difference.

## Scientific boundary

Neither Decoz/Pythagorean nor Cheiro/Chaldean numerology has established empirical predictive validity.

The project should therefore treat the disagreement as an opportunity for prospective testing:
- freeze both systems before outcomes;
- compare which, if either, better predicts independently rated personality/behavior or changes after real name use;
- do not choose whichever system gives the more attractive reading after seeing the person.

## Calculation-procedure correction — 2026-10-03
