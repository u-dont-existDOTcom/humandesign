## Conclusion

The repository has the initial blind synthetic foundation, but it is not the full research harness. The structural Model B/source-recovery limit is tracked separately. The items below are the remaining engineering and scientific gates after exact known-month/known-date, the 75-case Model A baseline, structural Model B V1, noise comparison primitives, and person-level human protocol primitives.

## Must-have gaps

1. **Claim-grade blind provenance.** Close every item in `reports/blind_boundary_audit.md`, including current run-manifest verification, exact resume-config identity, full prediction→freeze→reveal→evaluation provenance, and physical keyless decoder isolation.
2. **CLI-first workflow completeness.** Human import/fit/protocol/score/freeze/reveal/evaluate commands are absent. Architecture-level engine validation, questionnaire optimization, and independently reusable reporting commands also remain.
3. **Normative human records.** The current flat `HumanCase` cannot preserve response cluster, behavioral confidence separately from measurement reliability, examples/counterexamples, complete birth time/timezone/location/resolved UTC, or birth-record provenance. It cannot independently compute and verify imported charts.
4. **Empirical development track.** The current empirical model is a minimal flat-feature categorical learner. Nested/internal validation, development-only hyperparameter selection, dependency-aware clusters, calibration diagnostics, learning curves, simple-versus-expanded comparisons, mismatched-HD controls, and durable model-version history remain.
5. **Untouched final-test isolation.** Logical person splits exist, but the final cohort needs physically separated/sealed truth and response artifacts, frozen selector/stopping rules, a single-use release ledger, and auditable freeze-before-reveal ordering.
6. **Adaptive questioning.** Expected-information-gain ordering exists, but its likelihood assumptions and policy are not a frozen artifact. Stopping rules, burden/reliability adjustment, per-step entropy/EIG, questions/clusters-to-top-k, and development-versus-untouched optimization are not persisted end to end.
7. **Noise/robustness acceptance curves.** Combined tiers exist, but isolated missingness, flip-rate, and reliability curves, contextual/domain degradation, near-neighbor robustness, and the existing robustness primitives are not wired into a reproducible CLI report.
8. **Failure diagnosis.** The evaluator preserves failures, but structural-equivalence, search-completeness, candidate-containment, and independent-scorer-disagreement evidence are not supplied at classification call sites, so unexplained residuals can collapse into `scoring_bug`.
9. **Canonical completion ledger/reporting.** Run manifests do not accumulate final output hashes in one verified receipt. Transparent reports omit several binding and rectification fields, no standalone report regeneration command exists, and structured run logs are absent.
10. **Scientific acceptance suites.** Unit coverage is strong, but independent golden charts/timezones, real-ephemeris CLI blind E2E, keyless-container enforcement, deterministic replay, physical final-test isolation, automatic human baselines/permutations, and independent exact-minute agreement suites remain.
