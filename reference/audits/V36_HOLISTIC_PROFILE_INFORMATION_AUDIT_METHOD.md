# V3.6 holistic profile information metric

The primary quantity in the V3.6 holistic-profile audit is the entropy of a deterministic participant-observable fingerprint over the verified century candidate universe.

For each candidate state, every frozen V3.6 target-aligned mapping is evaluated. Multiple structural mechanisms that were explicitly intended as alternatives for the same behavioral construct are collapsed to one binary observable. The resulting vector is the candidate's predicted observable fingerprint.

Two information views are retained:

- interval-uniform: every verified structural interval has equal prior weight;
- duration-weighted: candidate prior is proportional to interval duration, corresponding more closely to a uniform timestamp prior.

For an observable fingerprint F, the reported entropy is `H(F)`. This is a deterministic partition-capacity measure. It is not empirical mutual information between chart and human behavior because the latter requires blinded participant data and response-noise estimation.

For the previously studied 1985 reference state, specific information is `-log2(P(F = f_reference))`, reported under both priors. It describes the rarity of that state's predicted observable fingerprint; it is not a posterior probability of the birth state.

A second mapping-pathway fingerprint retains each matched gate/channel/type pathway separately. Its entropy is explicitly an upper bound on mechanical discrimination and must not be presented as survey-visible information unless the corresponding mechanisms are converted into independently justified, distinguishable behavioral predictions.

The two post-selection Moon/Mars carrier mappings are excluded from the clean primary result. Their inclusion is reported only as a contamination sensitivity analysis.
