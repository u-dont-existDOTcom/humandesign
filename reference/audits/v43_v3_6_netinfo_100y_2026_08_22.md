## Interpretation

The audit falsifies the stronger claim that the known 1985 interval remains independently rank 1 merely by switching to the V4.3 NetInformation-first rule.

The defensible conclusions are:

1. **Without the two candidate-exposed Moon/Mars discriminator refinements, 1985 is rank 2 and the 2013 interval is rank 1.**
2. **With the best-current post-selection V3.6 refinements, the exact 1985-01-29 00:22:21.562548Z → 08:10:03.560047Z state is rank 1.**
3. Therefore `1985 is rank 1` is presently a **best-current descriptive** result, not independent confirmation.
4. The new primary competitor under the less contaminated model is the 2013 Projector/Splenic/2-4 state with 13-33, 18-58, 23-43, 24-61, 26-44, and 28-38.
5. No new broad questionnaire should be used merely to re-ask already-established constructs. Stronger validation would require genuinely independent historical/autobiographical material or prospective evidence whose mapping is frozen before inspection.
