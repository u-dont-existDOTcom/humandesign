# Nodal environment/perspective v2 capacity audit

## Purpose

Measure whether the separate Human Design Nodal layer resolves structural ties left by the primary trait/behavior survey **without mixing environment or perspective evidence into the primary natal trait score**.

This is a deterministic predicted-claim capacity audit, not empirical validation.

## Source-grounded separation

The official Jovian Archive Nodes material distinguishes:

- **Design Nodes**: environment — the recurring qualities/background in which the body operates;
- **Personality Nodes**: perspective/View — how attention and interpretation are naturally oriented;
- **South Node**: familiar earlier-life territory;
- **North Node**: territory that becomes more relevant in later life, especially around/after the Uranus Opposition.

Accordingly, this layer is scored and reported separately from trait/behavior evidence.

## Frozen candidate bank

`mapping_v2_nodal_environment_roles.json` combines the four side/node roles with the same frozen 64-gate catalog, yielding **256 candidate-blind questions**.

North-Node questions include `Not yet observable for my life stage`; participants are not asked to speculate about a later-life pattern that has not occurred yet.

All questions retain `Other / context-dependent`, `Unsure`, repeated-pattern wording, contexts, examples, and counterexamples.

## Audit baseline

The baseline is the complete primary v2 structural claim fingerprint:

- clean V3.6 observable profile;
- Personality + Design gate positions for Sun, Earth, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, and Pluto;
- complete channels.

The primary layer remains separate even if Nodal information resolves its residual ties.

## Secondary capacity

The audit measures the incremental partition information from:

1. Design South + North Nodes;
2. Personality South + North Nodes;
3. both South Nodes;
4. both North Nodes;
5. all four Nodal gate positions together.

If all four Nodes close the remaining gap to the verified 288,938-state structural universe, the correct interpretation is only that the *combined primary + secondary frozen claim vocabulary* can uniquely encode the cached intervals in the noiseless model. It does not imply participants can report those claims perfectly or that the claims are empirically true.

## Scientific reporting rule

Blind participant results must report primary trait/behavior recovery first. Nodal environment/perspective evidence may then be reported as a prespecified secondary analysis, including whether it breaks primary ties. It must never be silently folded into the primary confirmatory score.
