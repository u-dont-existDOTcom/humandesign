# Mapping Library v2 evidence protocol

## Purpose

The century feature-capacity audit shows that gates, planetary activations, and channels can discriminate candidate birth states far more strongly than the current coarse model. Capacity alone is not behavioral evidence. This protocol defines how a rich structural feature may become a frozen AstroHD behavioral hypothesis without inventing interpretations to improve recovery.

## Separation of claims

Every proposed mapping must keep four things distinct:

1. **Structural predicate**: a deterministic chart fact such as Personality Moon in Gate 53 or Channel 16-48 defined.
2. **Doctrinal source claim**: what an identifiable Human Design source explicitly says that feature means.
3. **Operationalized observable**: a participant-reportable pattern that can be elicited without birth-chart disclosure.
4. **Empirical status**: whether the operationalized claim has independent evidence beyond Human Design doctrine.

A high-capacity predicate does not receive a larger score merely because it distinguishes more candidate states.

## Evidence classes

- `official_hd_primary`: official Human Design/Jovian Archive material stating the feature meaning. This is sufficient to preregister a Human Design hypothesis, but is not independent empirical validation.
- `independent_empirical`: non-Human-Design evidence directly validating the same chart-to-behavior association under a suitable design. This is stronger evidence and must include study identifiers and enough metadata for audit.
- `historical_system_source`: an identifiable earlier Human Design teaching/source that predates the present experiment.
- `participant_derived`: wording or interpretation learned from participants in this project. This is exploratory only and must never be retroactively promoted into the same confirmatory cohort.

## Confirmatory eligibility

A candidate may enter a future frozen confirmatory mapping library only when all of the following are true:

- the structural predicate is deterministic and test-covered;
- at least one pre-existing source explicitly supports the intended construct;
- the operationalized observable is narrower than or equal to what the source supports;
- source text, predicate, question wording, answer scoring, weight, and inclusion decision are frozen before the new participant cohort;
- the item has not been selected or reworded using outcomes from that same cohort;
- contradictions and null-source findings are retained rather than silently dropped;
- the mapping version and source-registry hash are included in the participant freeze.

`official_hd_primary` is enough to test whether official Human Design claims predict behavior. It is not enough to describe the claim as scientifically established.

## Question design

Operationalized questions should ask about repeated patterns rather than isolated memorable events. Prefer patterns that the participant can assess across childhood and adulthood, with explicit context/exception capture. Every fixed-choice item must allow `Other / context-dependent` free text, and free text must be retained even when it does not produce a scoring token.

Do not put Human Design terminology, gate/channel numbers, planets, or the predicted pole into participant-facing wording before reveal.

Where a claim concerns a trajectory rather than a stable trait, record childhood pattern, current pattern, transition age/range, contexts, exceptions, and counterexamples separately. Progression/transit hypotheses stay outside the natal confirmatory score unless separately preregistered.

## Weighting and selection

Structural information gain is an engineering diagnostic, not evidence strength. Use it to prioritize which already-supported claims are worth operationalizing, not to manufacture mappings.

For a new frozen mapping version:

1. collect all source-supported candidate claims under the declared source protocol;
2. operationalize them before looking at participant recovery;
3. deduplicate semantically overlapping observables;
4. assign weights by a preregistered rule based on evidence/source quality, not candidate-state rarity;
5. freeze the mapping and question bank;
6. only then run the century discrimination audit and participant test.

This ordering prevents information-gain cherry-picking from becoming an astrology interpretation engine.
