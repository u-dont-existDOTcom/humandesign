# Shared evidence capture — v1

Version: 2026-10-26.1. This is a source-preservation and transport guide for the existing Life Patterns pilot. It adds no behavioral question, required event inventory, construct, scoring rule or runtime policy. Follow the installed Instructions and existing recovery, completed-interview, handoff and module guides.

## One source record, reused faithfully

Keep one source-preserving interview. Reuse an adequate answer when the distinction, context and time period are the same. A new researcher, model, question ID or wording does not make that distinction unanswered. Several later analyses using one answer still have one observation. Retain corrections alongside their originals and distinguish a changed account from independent corroboration.

For a verified completed/saturated interview, the main interview remains complete. Recover its source and go to independent review; do not start a new questionnaire or event battery. Ask only admitted reviewer clarifications. An empty coverage cell is not a reason for a question. Retain explicit skips, uncertainty, no opportunity and not-applicable states across wording variants.

The current tendency-first menu/policy remains in force. Research drafts of optional topic supplements are not automatically active Knowledge or a replacement policy. Do not attach a new question_policy identity/hash, restart an old interview from another bank or apply a proposed supplement's per-sitting allowance as a lifetime cap on review. No chart or model prediction may select a question.

## Preserve what an event account actually establishes

When an event is already reported in permitted source, preserve:

- Its exact description, including whether it actually occurred, was planned/wanted, or was an internal change.
- The person's date, age, range or relative-time phrase and any uncertainty. Keep “around 2018” or “when I was about 20” as such; never invent a day, exact age, year or birthday to complete it.
- Any stated basis for the date, such as memory or a contemporaneous record. Do not upgrade memory into documentation, require documents by default or invent that a source was checked.
- Conditions and explicitly reported changes over time. A specific episode is not automatically a usual pattern.
- Whether the account contains selected examples or explicitly covers all relevant occurrences in a stated period. A few turning points are a selective account unless the source says otherwise.
- What remains unasked, uncertain, declined or not applicable. An omission is not an explicitly absent event. An explicit negative applies only to its stated category and observation period.

Do not infer an event type, cause, role, count or legal status from a looser description. Do not derive a total from a few examples or treat an aspiration as an outcome. Preserve both uncertainty and scope when later summaries refer to the account. No additional precision is required merely because a later researcher might find it useful.

These are preservation instructions, not new participant questions. An extra clarification still needs the existing source-based admission: an unresolved, material distinction that the authorized source has not settled. Ask one task, accept a short answer or skip, and do not require exact clock times for ordinary life events. Preserve exact reviewer question_text when the reviewer admits a question.

## Existing supported record fields

Preserve the exact received question_text and answer_text in their original source turns. Do not append inferred labels to answer_text or rewrite it into a new questionnaire answer. Use the existing conditions, corrections, process_feedback and correction_of fields only for source-supported material and their existing purposes. Retain source references and the complete source-preserving composite required by the handoff guide.

recorded_at describes collection/provenance time. It is not a field for when a life event occurred. Preserve a reported event's time in its actual source text and applicable source-supported conditions. Leave unsupported details unknown; never substitute the collection date.

Do not add event_history_json, birth_quality, system_familiarity_json or other invented outer Action arguments. Do not assume that a free-form nested candidate object gives arbitrary fields operational meaning. The current API does not define a new structured event-index submission. Later extraction or coding must preserve the source and its authority; this guide does not certify such an adapter or coding procedure.

## Birth information and familiarity

The blind interviewer does not request birth information, birth-time source/uncertainty, chart values or reading contents. Birth-quality data belongs in the separate trusted intake. Do not infer it from ages, life events or other biographical clues. Follow the existing exclusion and type/location-only exposure rules for accidentally visible target information.

After behavioral freeze, collect only the two familiarity metadata items already specified in the CF003 module and actual visible exposure by type/location. Use turn_role=collection_metadata, excluded from the classifier, and never revise a frozen behavioral answer because of metadata. Ask whether relevant knowledge existed, never its value or interpretation. Memory status is not collected.

A broader per-system familiarity extension needs the study's explicit selected-system list, post-freeze collection specification and supported intake/storage route. None is invented or silently activated here. Do not place model identities or prior-reading metadata in the pre-freeze reviewer candidate. The optional Experience & Meaning module remains separately consented, unscored and governed by its existing guide.

## Failed transfer is not evidence about the person

Create the real source-preserving backup and saved request envelope before review Actions, as the handoff guide requires. On rejection or interruption, retain those files and the exact safe diagnostic. Do not fabricate a success, duplicate the review, change the retry envelope or silently rewrite source to make it pass.

The last inspected backend has a conservative filter that can flag text containing both a numeric calendar date and a clock time even when the person described a nonbirth event. Such a rejection is a software diagnostic, not proof of actual chart exposure. Preserve the permitted source and report that the transfer could not be completed; do not erase the date, falsify exposure metadata or bypass the guard. This package does not claim to repair that backend filter.

## Presentation precedence

The installed Instructions and this package's corrected tendency-first guide use the participant_purpose explanation only when a scenario, example or follow-up is unclear. Do not add it before every direct, understandable question. Keep any explanation separate from the exact question and never expose scoring directions or claim diagnostic validity.

The existing Action handoff guide governs early Railway continuation: a saved review may move to the private Railway page before ready. The independent ready state still governs final review/confirmation and storage. Once handed off, do not run a second GPT interview or submission in parallel.
