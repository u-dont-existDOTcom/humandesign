# Life Patterns recovery guide v2

Use this whenever the participant has earlier interview answers, either visible in the current chat or supplied as a prior record. These are provenance/admission rules, not personality evidence.

## Existing interview visible in this chat

- Preserve every visible prior participant question/answer as historical source material; do not restart or re-ask resolved questions.
- Never reconstruct missing old turns from Memory, summaries, recollection, or what they probably said. If exact text is not visible, mark it unavailable rather than inventing it.
- Recover route IDs only when they were actually recorded or are present on a new tagged question; never guess them after the fact.
- Record prior birth/chart/score exposure as contamination type/location only and never use its value for question selection or interpretation.
- Use all preserved participant evidence when deciding what remains unresolved; do not rely only on already-coded facets.
- Do not show a personality/interpretive summary before any necessary new questions.

## Imported response record

- Preserve the imported record exactly as received as source material and identify its source type: raw transcript, prior JSON, edited response record, answer-only notes, or other accurate label.
- Participant question/answer text copied into the research turns must be word-for-word from the received record. Never silently normalize, summarize, or rewrite it.
- Do not claim edited or reconstructed question wording is the exact original prompt.
- A condition that appears only in edited/unverified question wording cannot by itself prove the participant saw that condition, establish the answer's scope, or satisfy a conditional antecedent.
- Conditions explicitly stated by the participant in their answer can support that answer at the scope they stated.
- If an important interpretation depends on an unverified question-side condition, ask a fresh neutral clarification only if it passes the normal admission rules.
- A missing hedge, exception, correction, or condition is not evidence that none existed.
- Editor-written headings, summaries, labels, or third-person paraphrases are not participant evidence.
- Participant confirmation can confirm the gist of their own recorded words; it cannot retroactively verify original question wording or create framing/negotiation evidence that was never elicited.
- Any substantive clarification is a new turn; never silently rewrite the imported record.
- Do not require reconfirmation of every old answer or repeat usable evidence merely because route IDs, JSON metadata, consent, corrections, review, or blinding metadata are missing.
- Missing metadata stays unknown unless actually recovered; unknown never means false.

Explain briefly that any new questions address distinctions the prior record did not establish, not that the old answers were wrong.
