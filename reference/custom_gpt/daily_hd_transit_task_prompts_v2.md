“Open [CUSTOM GPT LINK OR SAVED CHAT LINK] and type TODAY. ```

## Option 2 — Direct daily brief in regular ChatGPT, only with verified live data

Use this only when regular ChatGPT can access a deterministic current-transit source. Paste the complete verified natal fingerprint directly into the task because Scheduled Tasks cannot rely on Custom GPT Knowledge files.

```text
Create a recurring task every day at [TIME] in [IANA TIMEZONE].

Verified birth data: [DATE], [LOCAL TIME], [BIRTHPLACE], IANA zone [ZONE], resolved UTC [UTC].
Verified natal HD fingerprint: [TYPE; STRATEGY; AUTHORITY; PROFILE; DEFINED/UNDEFINED CENTERS; CHANNELS; PERSONALITY AND DESIGN ACTIVATIONS NEEDED BY THE TRANSIT ENGINE].
Verified deterministic live-transit source: [SOURCE OR API].

Each day:
1. State today's local date, local time, UTC timestamp, source, and the transit chart's exact validity interval.
2. If exact transit gates/lines and natal connections cannot be verified, do not improvise. Send only: “Exact transit data unavailable. Open [CUSTOM GPT LINK] and type TODAY.”
3. Otherwise distinguish natal features from temporary transit activations. Describe at most three relevant temporary interactions as possibilities, not predictions.
4. Give one body-awareness prompt, one low-stakes reversible experiment, and one journal question.
5. End with: “Transits are temporary symbolic weather. They do not override Strategy or Authority. Authority guides a decision process at its proper timescale; it does not guarantee long-term outcomes.”
6. Never recommend abrupt medical, legal, financial, housing, career, or relationship action.

Keep the brief to 200–350 words and avoid manufacturing a continuing story from previous days.
```
