AstroHD scientific intake

This page keeps your birth information out of the interview conversation. It sends the birth tuple directly to the AstroHD backend, freezes the chart predictions before you answer behavioral questions, and returns only an opaque session code to give the interviewer.

For the strongest blind test, do not tell the interview GPT your birth date, time, place, astrological placements, Human Design type, or the chart of someone whose identity would reveal those facts.

The v1 intake requires an IANA timezone identifier so historical daylight-saving rules are deterministic. A future geocoding layer can derive this from birthplace.
