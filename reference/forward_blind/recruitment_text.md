# Forward-Blind Human Design Study — Development Pilot

We are testing whether a Human Design chart calculated from independently supplied birth data predicts blinded behavioral responses better than alternative charts.

Participants provide date of birth, exact birth time, and birthplace. The study calculates the chart privately but **does not show the participant their Human Design Type, Authority, Profile, Gates, Channels, or expected answers before the questionnaire**.

Before any scored response is collected, the study freezes a cryptographic record of the chart-derived prediction model, question set, and expected answer directions. The participant then answers neutral behavior-first questions without seeing which answers the chart predicts. Only after the response record and score are frozen is the chart revealed.

For the strongest test we prefer people who:

- have a documented birth time, ideally from a certificate or hospital record;
- have little or no prior Human Design knowledge;
- are willing to answer concrete questions and provide examples/counterexamples;
- understand that the current DEVELOPMENT pilot is for improving the study procedure and is not itself proof of Human Design.

Birth/contact identity is not committed to the public code repository. Research records should use pseudonymous participant IDs and hashes.

For a later untouched validation cohort, participants must not have been used to improve question wording, mappings, scoring rules, or thresholds.
