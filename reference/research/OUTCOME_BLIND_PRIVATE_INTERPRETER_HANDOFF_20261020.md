### Allowed workflow

1. **Owner launches a new Unpersonalized Temporary Chat**, not a new chat inside this HumanDesign Project. This session's available tools have no verified ability to create a fresh isolated ChatGPT conversation on its own.
2. Owner uploads ONLY the private three-file packet. New interpreter receives date/time/place and source-only astronomical facts; no participant name, personality or outcome evidence, and no existing person-specific predictions. Do not search for participant identity/history or survey-linked files.
3. Interpreter states calculation/source variants and creates **new directional hypotheses** with exhaustive exclusive outcome options, counterexamples, context, source IDs and shared-signal and source-lineage keys, preserving nulls and contradictions.
4. If actual measured population baselines, model calibration or independent chart-decoy predictions are missing, leave probability fields **unscored** and freeze a DRAFT directional claim set rather than fabricating numbers. No claim of conformity to the full forward-scorer schema.
5. Interpreter exports exact results plus artifact hash BEFORE any outcome reveal. The new interpreter must never open respondent survey even after exporting.
6. A SEPARATE evaluation context can later compare the sealed predictions to outcomes for diagnostics. No survey data are written publicly; only appropriately de-identified aggregate results, method changes and cryptographic receipts may be stored in Git without broader publication authorization.
