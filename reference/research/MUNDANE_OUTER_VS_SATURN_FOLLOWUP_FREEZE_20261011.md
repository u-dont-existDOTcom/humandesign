Earlier 2026-10-11 ERT analysis exposed correlations of **+0.3313** (outer-planet hard aspects) and **−0.3850** (Saturn-to-outer hard aspects) with ERT regime-onset frequency, selected *after* ERT outcomes were visible. **Goal:** check whether the contrast `outer_hard - saturn_hard` transfers at all to independent measures of political instability, and quantify dependence on individual eras and window definitions.

## Fixed data, before observing new outcomes

- Astronomy is EXACTLY the frozen annual daily hard-pair index from the first backtest, generated using `FLG_MOSEPH`, tropical geocentric longitude, one UTC sample per day, **3.0°** orb and 0/90/180° only. No recomputation with favorite aspects or revised obliquity, and no retuning or sign switches. Both components involve exactly three disjoint planet pairs.
- `contrast = outer_hard - saturn_hard` in **unstandardized pair-days/day**. Positive contrast = more Uranus/Neptune/Pluto-to-each-other hard activity than Saturn-to-outer hard activity. This is derived after inspecting original ERT data, so no ERT-origin inference can be called independent.
- NON-INDEPENDENT DISCOVERY CONTROL: original ERT stable-to-active onsets and COW interstate-war unique `WarNum`, using the **existing pinned source CSVs**. Recalculate contrast, each component, and original all-hard index across their existing fixed 5yr blocks without redefining endpoints.
- NEW OUTCOME A (transfer test): worldwide **attempted coups (successful + failed), Powell & Thyne**, `https://jonathanmpowell.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/pt_20260829.csv`, source page `https://jonathanmpowell.com/coups/`. Include dated occurrences from **1950–2024**. Count each explicitly listed attempted coup once per distinct country/date/record; prefer a documented unique ID if present. Successful coups **only** are a prespecified secondary analysis (not an alternative primary endpoint); use source's coding (1 failed, 2 successful). Do not interpret each country-year as just one event or count a country listed in summary format as one event without actual date/status evidence. - NEW OUTCOME B (transfer test): **UCDP intrastate armed conflict onset**, version **25.1** (coverage through 2024), source `https://ucdp.uu.se/downloads/onset/ucdp-intrastate-conflict-onset-251.csv`, from `https://ucdp.uu.se/downloads/olddw.html`. ## Main declared transfer comparisons

- Exactly two **new** outcome tests, each on **15 fixed, nonoverlapping 5yr blocks 1950–1954 ... 2020–2024**: `contrast` vs attempted-coup count; `contrast` vs UCDP intrastate onset count. Both hypotheses are **positive direction only**. One-sided significance threshold 0.025 each (two co-primary outcomes, Bonferroni). If a source cannot be parsed, count not attempted, no replacing outcome.
- As in the parent, subtract the **within-25yr-era means** from exposure and outcome for 1950–74, 1975–99, 2000–24 (five blocks each) before Pearson r. Use **20,000 seeded within-era shuffles of the exposure** for one-sided randomization p: `seed=20261012`. Avoid treating 15 5yr blocks as hundreds of independent daily samples. Note circular shift whole 15-bin astronomical series is a serial-dependence diagnostic, **not** a calibrated p-value. Record rank of actual alignment among 15 rotations.
- Show simple descriptive contrast and components for each co-primary outcome without cherry-picking which component 'wins'. Require directional consistency: outer-only `r>0`, Saturn-only `r<0` for an exploratory 'same split' status; this descriptive condition is **not** standalone inferential validation and does not rescue nonsignificant primary contrast.
- Source/era sensitivity, **preselected diagnostics**: for ERT and each new endpoint, recompute after excluding each 25-year era; report *all* results, including reversal and weak variance. For ERT additionally remove its extreme 1990–94 block, retaining era centering on remaining; compute contrast r. No selective era omission from main inference.
- Cross-endpoint independence caveat: wars, coups and armed conflicts may overlap; two co-primary outcomes are separate measures, not independent replications of the same construct. Historical outcome ascertainment varies with political time/region.
- Negative controls & robustness: correlate contrast with COW 1950–2004 and parent full COW series; compare against its raw Saturn/outer/all-hard correlations. Split the 15-bin years into chronological 1950–1984 (7 bins) and 1985–2024 (8 bins) and report their signs (within split era-demeaned correlation if enough variance; otherwise undefined). Results from different bin widths/offsets or orb tolerances are **not allowed to change verdict**, but may be recorded as exploratory follow-up proposals.
