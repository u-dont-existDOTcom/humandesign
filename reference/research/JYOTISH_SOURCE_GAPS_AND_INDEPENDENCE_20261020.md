## Verified project baseline (October 20)

- The operational V1.4 natal rule family comprises selected Lilly, Ptolemy, Valens and **Phaladeepika** clauses. It is not exhaustive even of those works.
- **Phaladeepika**: original V. Subrahmanya Sastri 1950 English translation acquired as PDF; original full systematic audit and all natal/yoga/dasha mechanisms not implemented.
- **Brihat Jataka** of Varahamihira: N. Chidambaram Iyer 1885 edition previously acquired; NOT yet a source-complete or independent operational Jyotish arm.
- **Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra**: edition identified in existing acquisition registry, both volumes not yet recovered/verified/read; no complete BPHS rule engine.
- **Jaimini**, divisional vargas, shadbala, avasthas, Vimshottari and other dashas, rashi/graha drishti, and yoga qualification are not covered by one source-complete executable Jyotish engine. A lengthy modern Vedic narrative using them does not mean our engine implements them.
- Chinese BaZi and Zi Wei are separate emerging research lines; their existence is not a completed Jyotish comparison arm.

## What each new source should contribute to the empirical test

1. **Exact original rule and negative/exception clauses**, not modern paraphrase selected for a fit. Source statement, translation, commentary and new psychological bridge each get different fields.
2. **Independent mechanism:** new graha period, computed yoga conditional, independent time-lord, or distinctive signification with an independent condition. Repeated 'strong Mercury means intelligence' is not a new independent outcome signal.
3. **Falsifiable target:** exclusive response options with context, frequency/horizon and no latent-alternative rescue; null if source cannot constrain an outcome.
4. **Dependency roots:** identical astronomical state, shared source verse, literal copy, inherited technique and correlated calendar inputs. Score one symbolic evidence group under duplicated roots.
5. **Admissible comparisons:** Western-only, Jyotish-only, their lineage-aware combination, BaZi/ZWDS only after chart parity, numerology-only, HD-only, strongest empirical no-astrology baseline. Identical cohort outcomes and frozen scoring across all arms.
6. **Negative value:** a source predicting the opposite should reduce or split confidence, not be invoked after the answer as a different 'mature/shadow' interpretation.
7. **Generalization boundary:** improvements seen on Hale are development-only because current research already viewed her questionnaire. A genuinely separate interpreter cannot turn her into a novel untouched human validation case.
