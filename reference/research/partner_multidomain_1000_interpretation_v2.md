## Interpretation

The visually striking 2029-2031 overlap should not be treated as strong evidence that Joel and Bee specifically share a future life. Similar or stronger multidomain timing concordance is common in the null pools under this symbolic model.

The 2030-2031 prospective hypothesis remains frozen and may still be observed prospectively, but the null benchmarks substantially weaken any claim that the astrological/HD timing itself identifies Bee as Joel's unusually likely future partner.

## Important limitations

1. Bee's exact birth time and exact Cameroon birthplace remain unresolved.
2. Geography could not be fairly included, so astrocartography/relocation is absent from V2 ranking.
3. No houses, angles, or progressed angles were used for any comparison partner.
4. The state model is a preregistered symbolic rubric, not empirically calibrated to real relationship outcomes.
5. Null partners are age-matched random UTC birth moments, not a demographic sample of actual people.
6. V2 is development work motivated after V1, not independent validation.

## Next legitimate upgrades

Do not retune V2 to rescue the pair.

Potential new-model work must be versioned separately and evaluated against fresh null samples. High-value additions are:

- independently rectifying Bee's birth time;
- exact geography/astrocartography only after time/place uncertainty is reduced;
- houses and progressed angles for all partners using a declared synthetic-location null policy;
- calibrating life-state mappings on known longitudinal human cases rather than hand-set symbolic weights;
- testing whether past known couples rank above nulls before interpreting future couples.
