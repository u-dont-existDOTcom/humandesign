### 3. Birth-year number

### 4. Name Number

- based on the name the person is actually most known by in the relevant context;

## D. Name calculation procedure

Cheiro's own worked examples establish:

1. calculate each commonly used name-part independently;
2. retain that part's compound total;
3. reduce the part to its root;
4. add the **reduced part roots** to form the combined-name compound when the sum is 10+;
5. reduce that combined compound to the final Name root.

Example used by Cheiro:
- John = 18 -> 9
- Smith = 17 -> 8
- 9 + 8 = 17 -> 8

Therefore raw continuous-string totals must NOT be used as Cheiro compound totals.

Because 7+1=8 is below 10, do not invent a combined compound such as 53 for Joel Rosenblum.

Therefore a single person can have different active Name Numbers in different social contexts.

Freeze the context BEFORE testing an outcome.

## I. Birth/name harmony

Cheiro prefers the Name root to harmonize with the Birth Number.

## J. Action/lucky-date method

Basic rule:
- prioritize date-of-month roots in the person's Birth Number series;
- strongest during own or harmonious zodiacal periods;
- stronger when Name Number harmonizes.

For most roots:
- use own-number dates.

For 4/8:
- apply special exception rather than blindly increasing the 4/8 series.

## K. Compound future-date method

For a proposed date:

1. obtain the combined Name root using Cheiro's name-part procedure;
2. reduce the proposed **day of month** (not month+day) to its root;
3. add Name root + date root;
4. reduce this intermediate sum to a root;
5. add the Birth Number;
6. interpret the resulting final compound using Cheiro's 10-52 compound table.

If unfavorable, test another date.

Do not silently replace this with Personal Year/Month/Day arithmetic from modern Pythagorean systems.

## L. Periodicity

Cheiro gives a separate recurrence rule:

- take an important year/date;
- add the digit sum of that year/date to itself;
- resulting year/date is a later candidate of importance.

For birth-year periodicity:
- 1985 -> digit sum 23 -> 2008
- 2008 -> 10 -> 2018
- 2018 -> 11 -> 2029

This is a recurrence generator, not a general probability score.

A sequence seeded from a known event is post-hoc for that event and cannot be counted as independent prediction.

## M. Life-age / ordinal-year recurrence

Cheiro also examines ages/ordinal years of life numerologically (for example "69th year").

Freeze whether an experiment uses:
- chronological age completed; or
- ordinal year of life (age + 1).

Do not switch conventions after seeing an event.

## N. Places, titles, house numbers, colors, health, music, betting

Cheiro's book also contains explicit systems for:
- house and place-name compatibility;
- titles/prefixes;
- colors/jewels;
- music affinities;
- health/herbal claims;
- horse-racing/betting.

These are part of the historical system but are **OUT OF SCOPE by default** for personality/life-event experiments.

They may only be activated by a new explicit experiment version.

Health/herbal claims are historical occult claims, not medical guidance.

## O. Cheiro completeness status

For the research questions currently relevant to this project—personality, name effects, life-event timing, relationship/name compatibility—the calculation methodology is now sufficiently complete to freeze.
