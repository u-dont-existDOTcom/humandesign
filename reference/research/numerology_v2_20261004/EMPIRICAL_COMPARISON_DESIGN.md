## 1. What must be distinguished

There are four different achievements:

1. **Source inventory freeze:** what each supplied author actually teaches, including unresolved alternatives. This is complete within the recorded supplied-source limits.
2. **Operational reading freeze:** exactly how an author-specific reading becomes finite, testable forecasts. It binds interpretation resources, input conventions, branch selection, positive/adverse-condition selectors, hierarchy and output rules. This is a separate prerequisite, not supplied by a mathematical formula alone.
3. **Implementation verification:** independent source examples, boundary tests and blinded synthetic cases demonstrate that the chosen operational reading is executed as specified.
4. **Human predictive evaluation:** untouched cross-person or prospective outcomes test whether those readings discriminate better than controls.

A passing arithmetic test establishes neither step 2 nor step 4. Conversely, a book's omission of a weighting equation does not justify throwing away all its harder-to-code layers. Use a complete, frozen structured-reading procedure, or label the arm incomplete and do not launch the advertised full-system comparison.

## 2. Questions, arms and separate comparisons

Use five principal author arms: Cheiro V2, Decoz current V1, Campbell V1, Jordan V1 and Javane/Bunker V1. Retain Cheiro V1 as an explicit paired **relationship-layer ablation**, not as an extra independent author or a replacement for V2. All six system IDs remain separately addressable.

Evaluate distinct questions rather than one overall impression of accuracy:

| Task | Independent target | What a successful result would establish |
|---|---|---|
| Personality | Prespecified trait/behavior measurements, with participant and independent informant sources kept separate | Out-of-person correspondence between a complete reading and independently measured attributes |
| Event timing | Domain, onset interval, impact and valence recorded independently of the chart | Discrimination of observed event and non-event periods, not retrospective narrative plausibility |
| Relationships | Individually reported relationship quality and prospectively observed outcomes, retaining both partners' reports | Prediction of the specified relationship endpoint, not proof that numerical affinity causes it |
| Actual name changes | Repeated observations before and after a genuinely used new name, with actual adoption dates and circumstances | Observational prediction of change; not, by itself, a causal benefit of changing a name |

The primary study must select one task and one primary endpoint before unsealing outcomes. Secondary tasks and native-resolution forecasts remain prespecified secondary analyses. Do not combine personality resemblance, relationship satisfaction and event hits into an unvalidated grand score.

For owner history, every arm and every analysis is DEVELOPMENT only. Familiar examples used to construct the source audit or an operational reader are not held-out validation cases. In particular, 2008 is moderate, 2018 major and 2029 prospective; no new fit is performed here. A future 2029 observation would be one prospective owner observation, not independent cross-person replication, and needs an event definition and observation window frozen beforehand.

## 4. Inputs and unresolved source branches

Before any reading, record original spelling, chronological name history, component boundaries/roles, pronunciation evidence relevant to Y/W, actual public/private/business use and dates of sustained adoption. Record legal changes separately. Birth data include date and calendar convention; time and place are not invented prerequisites for author methods that do not use them.

Preserve original Unicode. Accent/transliteration, punctuation, hyphen and multipart-name rules absent from a source need an explicit project convention or a missing-input disposition. Do not silently collapse them. Do not infer a pronunciation from spelling when the author depends on sound. Freeze the mask from recorded participant-provided pronunciation information before outcome access.

For each applicable ambiguity ID, the operational manifest must select one of:

- a source-supported branch fixed before outcomes, with a reason independent of outcome fit;
- a finite registered sensitivity set, with every branch reported separately;
- UNRESOLVED, which makes the affected forecast non-admissible rather than silently choosing a favorable branch.

For a sensitivity set, the primary claim must either hold under every registered branch or be explicitly branch-specific with multiplicity control. Report disagreements and the range; never report the best-fitting branch as the system result. Do not average contradictory author outputs or contradictory source branches.

Conditional concepts such as developed versus undeveloped expression, master-number activation, delayed change or unusually early effects require a selector independent of the outcome being predicted. Without one, report the condition as unresolved. “The event proves the adverse manifestation was active” is not a selector. Both favorable and adverse possibilities in a source cannot both earn a correct-prediction reward for the same endpoint.

## 5. Outcome firewall and structured reading

Maintain separate access boundaries:

**Input custodian:** checks birth/name/usage inputs and pronunciation masks; does not choose interpretations from outcomes.

**Reader:** sees only the arm's frozen sources, admitted inputs and prediction window. It receives no biography, prior accuracy narrative, owner fit, participant outcome labels or other arm's output. Ordinary consenting participants are preferable to recognizable public figures; record recognition and exclude recognized identities from the untouched primary analysis by a predeclared rule.

**Outcome coders:** establish event facts, chronology quality, impact and observed non-event periods without names/charts/numbers or forecasts. They reconcile disagreements before unsealing predictions. Self-report and informant accounts remain separately attributable.

**Evaluator:** receives sealed predictions and sealed labels only after both are fixed. It follows the registered scoring code, not an intuitive reading of how well the narrative fits.

A frozen operational reader can be a qualified human procedure or a pinned model/prompt/toolchain. Record its identity, version, instructions, source hashes, decoding configuration and any permitted source retrieval. A model serving two arms must receive fresh separated contexts. Do not claim that two model contexts establish independence equivalent to two human research teams.

For every reading, produce a coverage ledger and a finite prediction-card set. Each card has: system ID and operational version, participant ID, target domain, time window, direction/valence, impact category, source rule IDs, relevant calculated values and conditions, explicit abstention reason if applicable, and creation/seal identity. A second fixed translation step may convert the source reading to the common outcome ontology; that step is a project operationalization, must be frozen and tested, and cannot inspect outcomes.

Broad statements, advice, unbounded eventualities, mutually opposite statements, and statements whose condition cannot be evaluated from admitted inputs are retained in the reading but marked NONSCORABLE before outcomes. Report the proportion of output that is non-scorable. A system cannot gain credit for many plausible sentences while concealing how little it forecast specifically.

## 6. Chronology, impact and observation coverage

Each event record has separate fields for domain, onset lower/upper bounds, valence, impact, chronology quality, evidence locator, coding date and adjudication. Chronology quality uses exactly:

- documentary/contemporaneous;
- strongly anchored memory;
- approximate memory;
- uncertain.

Impact is separately coded, for example minor/moderate/major. A date's membership in a predicted sequence must never affect impact. An anniversary, death or marriage is not automatically a top-major subjective life change. The event's occurrence and the person's subjective impact are separate endpoints.

UNKNOWN is a missing observation state, not an outcome label of zero. For each domain and period, record whether the observation process was sufficiently complete to assert no qualifying event. Retrospective silence is insufficient. Record prospective missing diary periods, withdrawal and loss to follow-up explicitly.

Primary exact-time scoring uses observations precise enough for the selected unit. Approximate chronology may support a predeclared coarser secondary analysis. Do not move an event to whichever month or year earns a hit. When an interval spans possible cells, those cells cannot be certified negative for that event domain solely because a preferred exact date is unknown. Report conservative bounds or a coarser endpoint, not arbitrary precision. A uniform distribution over remembered date intervals would be a modeling assumption, not documentary evidence.

All exclusion and coarsening decisions must be made by coders blinded to forecasts. Publish missingness/coverage by arm-independent participant strata and chronology quality. If no defensible observed-negative periods exist, specificity, false-alarm rates and a full event-discrimination claim are unavailable for that cohort. Do not repair that problem with unlabeled background years.

## 7. Timing units, multiple predictions and false positives

Choose a common primary time unit before outcomes, justified by observation quality. A person-year unit is a reasonable candidate for major-life-event work, but is not automatically the right unit for relationship quality or daily behavior. Preserve all source-native intervals as secondary outputs. An annual benchmark does not establish that an eight-hour prediction works, and a native eight-hour method must not receive 1,095 chances to match a single annual event without a corresponding exposure cost.

For binary domain × person × time cells with observed outcomes, define:

- TP: a positive forecast and qualifying observed event in that cell;
- FP: a positive forecast and confirmed absence of that event in that cell;
- FN: a negative forecast and qualifying observed event;
- TN: a negative forecast and confirmed absence.

Report the full confusion table, precision, recall, false alarms per observed person-time and forecast coverage. The primary error accounting gives **FP and FN equal unit cost**: `(FP + FN) / N_observed_cells`. Also report the transparent utility `TP - FP - FN`, where one false alarm cancels one hit and misses are not free. These definitions are project scoring choices, not author formulas. Compare against identical-outcome-unit controls; raw accuracy alone is inadequate when negative cells dominate.

An abstention is not a negative prediction and receives no hit or TN credit. Report its rate and outcomes separately. Compare arms at a predeclared common forecast-coverage level, with thresholds/ranking rules frozen on development data; retain the full coverage-versus-error curve. Unconditional superiority is not justified by scoring only each arm's easiest self-selected subset. A confirmatory complete-coverage endpoint needs an admitted mapping for every eligible cell; otherwise make only an explicit selective-prediction claim.

An emitted positive interval covers every common-unit cell it intersects under the registered boundary convention, and pays FP cost in every observed negative cell covered. An event spanning multiple cells has one prespecified onset unit; do not reward every month of an ongoing relationship as a new relationship onset. Duplicate source indicators supporting the same card do not create duplicate TP credit. Duplicate major/minor Triangle ages are one endpoint opportunity, not multiple independent events. Predicted domains, impact and valence must match the target contract; a financial loss does not validate a favorable romantic prediction merely because both are consequential.

For event-list matching as a secondary endpoint, use one-to-one matching with prespecified compatible domains and intervals. One actual event cannot validate an unlimited number of overlapping predictions. Wider windows must be charged by forecast exposure or compared to equally wide control windows. No tolerance window may be enlarged after a near miss.

Probabilistic evaluation is optional. The books do not supply calibrated probabilities. A probability adapter must be fitted only on development data and frozen, with a separate version. Then report a proper probability score such as mean squared probability error, `mean((p-y)^2)`, plus calibration and coverage. Do not label a source's “favorable” as an established 80% probability.

## 8. Personality, relationships and name changes

For personality, freeze the construct dictionary before chart/outcome pairing. Campbell's Quiescent Self must not silently become Jordan's outward Personality merely because both sum consonants. Preserve source-specific constructs; admit cross-system comparisons only on common independently defined targets. Include balanced positive and negative attributes and prespecified neutral controls. A participant's preference for an attractive reading is distinct from accurate trait discrimination.

A blinded matching task may compare the participant's reading with matched foil readings, stripping system labels and giving all readings the same presentation and length budget. The complete source calculation still occurs before that common presentation step. Equal presentation length is not permission to compute only one source number. Record the presentation adapter and score discrimination as well as agreement with individual statements.

For relationships, distinguish compatibility at baseline, reported satisfaction, duration and breakup onset. They are not interchangeable successes. The relationship pair, not two independent single people, is the sampling unit; shared individuals across multiple relationships must remain in the same split. Preserve both partners' ratings without choosing the one that fits. No experimental instruction to enter, leave or remain in a relationship is part of this design.

For name changes, record genuine current-use adoption rather than only legal registration. Preserve concurrent changes in marriage, occupation, location and self-presentation. Do not recommend artificial changes to create test cases. Use the author's stated lag only at its actual precision; “better part of a year” is not an exact 270-day rule. Unspecified lags remain unspecified unless an independently frozen project operationalization is explicitly labeled. A matched longitudinal observational design can test forecasts, but cannot isolate a numerical name effect from all accompanying changes. Never call such an association proof of causation.

## 9. Controls, splits and minimum evidence

Partition at the person level before operational calibration. All observations and name variants from a person stay in one split; connected partners/family clusters must not cross development/test boundaries where their shared data could leak. The owner and all source examples used to build methods stay in DEVELOPMENT. Keep the final test cohort untouched until the complete operational manifest and evaluation code are committed.

Use controls addressing distinct alternative explanations, not only shuffled digits:

- non-numerological demographic/calendar baseline fitted on development data only, using declared covariates available equally to the comparison;
- matched or stratified birth/name shuffles that preserve important input structure and observation opportunities, with the shuffle scheme fixed beforehand;
- equal-length blinded generic and mismatched readings for personality/relationship interpretation tasks;
- predeclared time-shift or matched-calendar controls for event predictions, preserving exposure and recording which age/season properties the control retains or changes.

No control preserves every possible structure. State the exact null it tests. Do not use a poorly matched null to claim a specific numerological mechanism. Compare within the same participants and observed windows wherever possible, so unequal event ascertainment cannot create the author ranking.

Estimate uncertainty using people/relationship clusters, not treating thousands of correlated days or multiple layers as independent replications. Report paired arm differences, baseline differences, uncertainty intervals and all registered sensitivity branches. Correct the declared family of confirmatory comparisons for multiplicity; keep additional contrasts exploratory. Do not pick the most favorable task, time resolution, subgroup or author pairing after results.

Before recruitment, specify the smallest effect that would matter, expected event frequency/coverage, independent participant count and a power/precision simulation under the intended clustered design. This audit supplies no outcome-rate estimate from which to invent a defensible sample size. The operational release must bind those values and the stopping rule. A finite engineering pilot can test usability and coding agreement but is development, not evidence of predictive success. No repeated peeking and extending the cohort until significance is obtained.

A credible positive finding needs an untouched cross-person result or genuinely prospective sealed forecasts, adequate observed negatives, superiority to appropriate controls at comparable coverage, and a predeclared practically meaningful effect. A weak or unstable effect is not made strong by a compelling owner anecdote. A null or harmful result remains a result; new source discoveries start new versions and never repair old misses.
