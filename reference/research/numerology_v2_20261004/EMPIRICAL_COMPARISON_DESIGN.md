## 1. Four separate achievements and the launch boundary

A **source inventory freeze** records every major author layer and its unresolved instructions. An **operational freeze** binds exact calculations, interpretation resources, input conventions, branch choices, conditional-expression selectors and the finite transformation from a reading to testable forecasts. **Implementation verification** tests that procedure against source and synthetic examples. **Human predictive evaluation** then uses untouched cross-person or prospective outcomes. None substitutes for the next.

The present audit and design do not authorize participant recruitment, survey changes, paid services, a trial, merge or deployment. Those require a later authorized operational release. Named launch prerequisites are not unfinished reading of the supplied four books.

Five principal arms are Cheiro V2, unchanged Decoz current V1, Campbell V1, Jordan V1 and Javane/Bunker V1. Cheiro V1 is an additional paired **WWYB-delta ablation**, relevant to period-personality and relationship endpoints. It is not another independent author or a new timing method. Each output stays separate; contradictory authors or source branches are never averaged.

## 4. Inputs, ambiguity decisions and scorable residue

Record original Unicode spelling, component boundaries/roles, chronological birth/current/public/private/legal/married/spiritual/nickname usage, sustained adoption dates and available pronunciation evidence. Legal registration and actual use are separate facts. Do not infer a phonetic Y/W mask from spelling where the author relies on sound. Unsupported transliteration, accents, punctuation, hyphens, titles or multipart grouping need an explicit convention or missing-input disposition, never a silent conversion.

Record birth date/calendar convention and chronology quality. For the admitted external-horoscope adapters also collect local birth time, place, timezone and uncertainty/source quality; unresolved time or chart conventions must be propagated. These are not invented requirements for a number calculation that does not use them.

For each applicable ambiguity ID, an outcome-blind custodian using a registered source-precedence rule must choose a supported branch, register a finite sensitivity set, or return UNRESOLVED. Selection must be blind to **development outcomes as well as test outcomes**. Every registered branch is reported. A robust claim must hold across its declared branch set, or be labelled branch-specific with multiplicity control; reporting only the best branch is prohibited.

Developed/undeveloped expression, positive/adverse manifestation, master activation, delayed name effects and early anticipation need selectors based on admitted inputs, not the event being predicted. Without one, the affected statement remains conditional and non-scorable. An event cannot retrospectively prove which expression was active.

Before launch, measure on blinded synthetic/development inputs the fraction of each relevant layer and reading that can become scorable cards under the fixed translator. Report conditional, contradictory, advice-only and unbounded material separately. A thin calculable residue cannot be described as a test of the complete system. This scorable fraction informs operational feasibility and the sample-size/precision calculation, not post-hoc exclusion of misses.

## 5. Reader/output firewall

The input custodian checks source inputs without choosing meanings from outcomes. The author-specific reader sees only exact frozen author resources, admitted inputs and the prediction window—not biography, outcome labels, other arms' output or past accuracy stories. **Owner-aware audit reports, empirical contracts containing owner labels and prior owner-history files must never enter reader contexts.** Give the reader a scrubbed operational instruction packet instead.

Outcome coders establish facts, chronology, impact and observation completeness without names/charts/numbers/forecasts. The evaluator receives sealed predictions and sealed labels only after both are fixed. Recognized public identities risk leakage; prefer ordinary consenting participants and predeclare recognition handling.

A reader may be a qualified human procedure or pinned model/prompt/toolchain. Record the exact identity/configuration/source hashes and allowed retrieval. A model used for multiple arms gets fresh isolated contexts. Separate model contexts do not establish the same independence as separate human research teams.

Each reading has a complete layer-coverage ledger and finite cards: system/operational IDs, pseudonymous participant, domain, valence, impact, onset window, direction, source rule IDs, calculated values, applicable conditions, abstention reason and seal identity. A **frozen arm-agnostic translation rule** assigns SCORABLE/NONSCORABLE; the author reader cannot selectively discard difficult claims. Source-specific constructs are not silently equated: Campbell's Quiescent Self is not Jordan's outward Personality simply because both use consonants.

## 6. Independent observation and chronology

Events carry onset lower/upper bounds, domain, valence, impact, evidence locator, coding date and exactly one chronology quality: documentary/contemporaneous; strongly anchored memory; approximate memory; uncertain. Impact is coded separately from date precision and without numerical predictions. A predicted recurrence year cannot upgrade an event to major.

**The primary analysis set contains only observation-complete domain × time windows**, rated by blinded coders using the same prespecified completeness rule whether or not an event was found. All arms use the identical eligible cell set. A recalled event from an incomplete window does not earn a primary TP while the unremembered neighboring years escape FP cost. Such events belong only in explicitly labelled sensitivity/descriptive analyses.

UNKNOWN is a missing observation state, never a negative. Missing diary periods, withdrawal and unverified retrospective silence stay UNKNOWN. Forecasts in those periods are counted in a separate unassessed-forecast/exposure inventory, not credited as hits or certified absence. The completeness mask is arm-independent and frozen before predictions are unsealed.

The primary time resolution must be chosen from the prospective observation protocol or arm-blind source-quality metadata, not from which grid best fits forecasts. Imprecise onset intervals may support a registered coarser secondary analysis or conservative bounds. Do not move them to a convenient month/year. Cells spanned by unresolved dates cannot be certified event-free merely because one preferred date is unknown. If no defensible complete windows exist, do not estimate primary specificity or a full event-discrimination ranking from that cohort.

## 7. Equal error costs, complete coverage and two timing anchors

The registered cell key includes **participant, domain, valence, impact category and time unit**. Categories must be fixed, sufficiently disjoint and independently coded; an event does not create many copies by satisfying overlapping synonyms. A wrong-valence prediction is an FP for the predicted category and an FN for the event's category under this explicitly declared categorical task.

Within each primary grid, every admitted arm must output a structured positive or negative decision for **every eligible cell**. TP/FP/FN/TN then have their ordinary binary definitions on the same observed cell set. Silence is a negative only where the preregistered complete-card translator explicitly defines it that way; otherwise it is an abstention.

**Any abstention fails complete-coverage primary admission for that arm; it never produces a zero-error primary score.** Report coverage and the failure to supply an admissible primary forecast. Do not impute a convenient sign. Selective secondary comparisons must use the identical input-defined common cell subset for all compared arms, report its coverage and changed target population, and make no unconditional full-system superiority claim. Equal percentages on different cells are not matched coverage.

Primary error is `(FP+FN)/N_eligible`. Each false alarm and each miss has unit cost1. Report the full confusion matrix, precision, recall, false alarms per observed person-time and coverage. The companion utility is **−(FP+FN)**; alternatively the symmetric correct-minus-incorrect score is `TP+TN−FP−FN = N_eligible−2(FP+FN)`. Both order arms exactly like the primary error on a common cell set. The earlier draft's `TP−FP−FN` is withdrawn: it rewards repairing a miss twice as much as repairing a false alarm. All development thresholds optimize the primary equal-cost objective, not that withdrawn utility. Scores whose denominator differs cannot be compared as though they used common exposure.

**No single anchor is allowed to decide the primary timing conclusion.** Evaluate two prespecified views: calendar-anchored and birthday/age-anchored grids at the same registered unit size. A confirmatory superiority claim must meet its direction and uncertainty/effect-size criteria on **both**, reported separately, never averaged and never selected after results. They are correlated robustness requirements, not two independent replications. Use only fully observed cells under each view and use identical cells across arms within that view. A12-month birthday interval can intersect two calendar cells while a calendar interval intersects one; the anchor reversal is exposed by the second view rather than hidden in a single favorable grid.

Within a grid, a positive source interval is mapped by the registered intersection convention to the cells it touches; all observed negative cells covered incur FP cost. Common anchor robustness is required because this discretization can penalize a misaligned interval. Native exact-interval evaluation remains a separate secondary analysis: report actual observed person-time coverage and one-to-one onset matches without rounding a tiny boundary overlap into a full extra year of native exposure. Do not present the gridded benchmark as proof of exact-day accuracy.

Duplicate indicators supporting one card do not create multiple TP credit. Duplicate Triangle ages are one endpoint opportunity. A single event cannot validate unlimited overlapping event-list forecasts; secondary matching is one-to-one with prespecified domains and intervals. Broad windows pay their full registered exposure cost and are compared with equally broad controls. Never widen a tolerance window after a near miss. An ongoing relationship contributes one onset, not a new onset every month.

Optional probabilistic scoring requires a separately versioned adapter calibrated on development data only; no book supplies calibrated probabilities. Then use a proper probability score such as mean squared probability error with calibration/coverage checks. A source adjective such as favorable must not be relabelled80% without that additional model.

## 8. Personality, relationship and name-change implementation

Freeze the common construct dictionary and source-to-target mapping before chart/outcome pairing. Include positive, negative and neutral attributes; do not reward attractive prose as accurate discrimination. A blinded matching task may use same-length, same-presentation actual and foil readings. Complete author calculations occur before the common presentation adapter; equal word counts do not justify calculating only one number.

Relationship quality, duration and breakup onset are different endpoints. Keep both partners' accounts separately, and keep shared individuals/relationships in the same split. No instruction to enter, leave or remain in a relationship is part of the study.

Name-change work observes genuine actual-use adoption, not just legal registration. Record concurrent marriage, work, location and self-presentation changes. Do not manufacture changes for testing or attribute their whole effect to numbers. Preserve an author's lag at its actual precision; “better part of a year” is not a sourced exact270-day rule. A matched longitudinal association does not isolate a causal numerical name effect.

## 9. Controls, untouched splits and evidence threshold

Partition by PERSON before operational calibration; all names and repeated periods stay together. Connected partners/family clusters must not leak across development/test splits. The owner and source examples remain development. Final test outcomes stay sealed until source, operational reader, mapping, scoring and statistical plans are committed.

Controls include constant-negative and development-estimated base-rate forecasts; a non-numerological demographic/calendar baseline using equally available covariates; matched birth/name shuffles preserving declared input structure and observation opportunities; equal-presentation generic/mismatched personality readings; and registered time-shift/matched-calendar event controls. Each tests a stated null. No control preserves every possible structure, and no weak null by itself establishes a numerical mechanism.

Compare paired arms on identical participants/windows. Cluster uncertainty by person or connected relationship group; thousands of days and multiple correlated source layers are not independent people. Report paired differences, baseline differences, uncertainty intervals and all registered sensitivity branches. Correct the declared confirmatory comparison family for multiplicity. Additional subgroups, tasks or time scales remain exploratory.

Before recruitment specify the smallest useful effect, expected observed event rate, expected scorable coverage, required independent participant count, a power/precision simulation and a stopping rule. This audit has no outcome-rate estimate from which to invent a defensible sample size. A finite engineering pilot tests usability/coding agreement only. Do not repeatedly peek and extend recruitment until significance appears.

Credible evidence requires sealed forecasts, untouched cross-person or genuine prospective outcomes, adequate observed negatives, advantage over suitable controls at common coverage and a prespecified practically meaningful effect. A weak association is not made strong by a compelling owner story. New source discoveries create new versions and future tests; they never repair old misses.
