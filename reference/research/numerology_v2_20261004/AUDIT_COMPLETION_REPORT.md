## What was wrong in the earlier simplifications

There is no single interchangeable “Pythagorean” calculation and interpretation system. Shared labels can refer to different constructs, and the same alphabet can be combined or reduced differently. A generic phonetic Y rule is wrong for Jordan. A universal calendar-year boundary is wrong for Javane/Bunker. Treating every44 as the same kind of master number is wrong across these systems.

The birth-name/current-name distinction also does not reduce to “legal name is active” or “Minor means negligible.” The actual source and name-use context control. No one author's rule should be imported into another because it seems to explain a known event better.

The earlier raw53/90 name sums were not Cheiro combined compounds. His component-root procedure leaves the previously reported final roots unchanged, but the raw compound labels were inappropriate. The affected historical notes are annotated separately rather than changing V1 or rescoring old predictions.

The audit also withdrew its own false criticism of a Javane/Bunker Sagittarius diagram after checking the native image. Jordan's displayed10/1 in the relevant example lies within19→10→1; it is not an arithmetic mistake. Those corrections are mandatory in SOURCE_RECONCILIATION.md. The later independent check caught one more audit error: on Jordan's printed page262, the11/2 labelled Reality had been mistaken for the final Pinnacle. The actual Pinnacles are9,3,3,9, agreeing with page263 and the formula. V2.1 withdraws that false source-conflict annotation. An audit error is not evidence against an author.
