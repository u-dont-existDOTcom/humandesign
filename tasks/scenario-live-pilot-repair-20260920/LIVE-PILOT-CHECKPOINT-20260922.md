## Design lessons confirmed in live use

1. Ask only the missing piece after a partial answer; do not replay the whole scene.
2. Do not use inverse/mirror questions as independent evidence when the earlier answer already supplies the dimension.
3. Specify hidden motives, baselines, causes, workload, and environmental conditions when they determine the answer.
4. Do not treat ordinary or tautological responses as trait-discriminating evidence.
5. Context dependence is data; do not force a global trait when the respondent names the conditions.
6. Optional bank entries are a menu, not a quota.
