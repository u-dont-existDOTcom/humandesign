# Live pilot repair — repetition-value item — 2026-09-20

## Finding

The live follow-up:

> When the goal matters a lot to you, what would you usually do with those twenty minutes even if the practice feels repetitive?

is not sufficiently discriminating. It mainly measures whether strong motivation can sustain boring practice, which is not the intended target.

The intended target is `D22.repetition_value`: whether the repetition itself carries any value or reward for the participant, apart from reaching the end goal.

## Disposition

- Do not use the participant's response to the high-motivation follow-up as evidence for repetition value.
- Do not infer novelty seeking from willingness or unwillingness to repeat a boring practice.
- Keep the participant's earlier statement that persistence depends on how much the goal matters as a scoped motivation observation only.
- `D22.repetition_value` remains unresolved until directly elicited.

## Replacement wording

> When you repeat the same kind of practice over and over, what, if anything, do you get out of the repetition itself?

Interpretation boundary:
- “Nothing; I only do it for the result” is a valid answer.
- Enjoyment of refinement, mastery, rhythm, familiarity, noticing small improvements, or another named reward can support scoped repetition-value evidence.
- Do not assume persistence, discipline, boredom tolerance, or novelty seeking from the answer.
- Do not require a recent real episode; a habitual self-report is sufficient.

## Pilot handling

The prior high-motivation question is invalid for the intended facet and should be treated as design feedback, not participant evidence. Ask the replacement only if the distinction remains useful.
