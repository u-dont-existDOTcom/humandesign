# Live pilot repair — STATUS recognition item — 2026-09-21

## Finding

The canonical STATUS prompt:

> Suppose the practical benefits of what you do stay the same, but other people begin seeing it as more impressive. What difference, if any, would that make to you?

failed in live use because it stipulates away the main way increased social regard could matter. The participant correctly noted that if being seen as more impressive produces any consequence they value, that is itself a practical benefit; if it produces none, "no difference" is close to tautological.

Treat the live response as design feedback, not evidence of low status motivation.

## Intended construct

The relevant part of `D19.status_ownership` is whether **recognition or admiration is intrinsically rewarding**, distinct from:
- money;
- access or opportunities;
- control/ownership;
- increased practical impact;
- privacy costs or visibility pressure.

## Replacement question

> When people genuinely admire something you've done, is that rewarding to you in itself, apart from any opportunities or practical benefits it might create?

Optional neutral follow-up only if needed:

> What part of that reaction matters to you, if any?

## Interpretation boundary

- "Yes" can support only intrinsic value of recognition in this context.
- "No" can support only low intrinsic value of recognition as self-reported.
- Do not infer ambition, narcissism, insecurity, social dominance, or career motivation.
- Do not force a hypothetical in which recognition is defined as having literally zero subjective effect; the subjective effect is the construct being measured.
- Keep ownership/control motives separate and use the OWNERSHIP route if still useful.

## Pilot handling

Do not use the participant's criticism as a trait answer. Ask the replacement only if intrinsic recognition value remains useful and unanswered.
