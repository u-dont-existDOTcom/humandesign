# Live pilot repair — R07 burst-energy variant — 2026-09-21

## Finding

The canonical R07 prompt:

> Keep the same worthwhile work, but imagine two unusually intense days followed by a real chance to rest. What would your energy usually be like at the end of the burst?

failed in live use because “unusually intense” leaves major determinants unspecified. The participant correctly noted that the answer depends on how much work was done, heat/sun exposure, and other conditions.

## Disposition

- Treat the live response as design feedback, not as evidence for `D14.burst`.
- Do not infer burst capacity from an answer to an undefined workload.
- Keep `D14.burst` unresolved until workload and environment are concrete enough.

## Replacement live variant

> Imagine two days in a row where you do about ten hours each day of mentally demanding computer work that you find worthwhile. You are indoors at a comfortable temperature, sleep normally between the days, eat normally, and take short ordinary breaks. At the end of the second day, before a longer rest, what would your energy usually be like?

## Interpretation boundary

- This is a prediction about one specified workload, not a maximum capacity.
- Do not generalize to outdoor heat, physical labor, sleep loss, illness, or all work.
- Enjoyment and tiredness can coexist.
- The answer describes the pre-rest endpoint only; recovery requires a separate question.

## Design implication

Work-energy variants must specify enough of duration, task type, environmental load, sleep/recovery and break conditions to avoid making the respondent invent the main determinants.
