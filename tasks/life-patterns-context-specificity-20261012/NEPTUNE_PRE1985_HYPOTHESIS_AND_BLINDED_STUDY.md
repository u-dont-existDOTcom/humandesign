# Neptune angularity, spirituality and substance-use motives — prospective theory audit

**Status: RETROSPECTIVE TRIGGER / DEVELOPMENT ONLY. Not a verified causal or predictive law; never score the person whose anecdote prompted this rule as an untouched test.**

## Origin and temporal provenance

A retrospective report that a mother, immediately after her child's birth, interpreted the chart as forecasting both spiritual interests and drug involvement motivated inspection of pre-birth astrology sources. This prompted hypothesis selection and therefore makes the originating person **development only**, regardless of the mother's purported prediction date. The exact original neonatal horoscope text or contemporaneous written prediction is not available in this task. No identifying owner DOB/place, chart positions or private substance history belong in this shared repo.

The 1918 source *The Message of the Stars*, by Max Heindel and Augusta Foss Heindel, chapter IV (*Reading the Horoscope*), explicitly adopts a **six-degree rule** for planets in the twelfth house near the Ascendant. Chapter XVII (*Neptune, the Planet of Divinity*) uses Neptune for mystical/spiritual faculties and also associates Neptune affliction with possible alcohol/drug involvement. This was a traditional astrological claim already available decades before the reported birth; neither source's existence nor retrospective apparent fit proves it is true.

## Hypotheses must remain mutually separable

| Code | Participant-reported phenomenon | Chart-side hypothesis | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| N-SPIRIT | Sustained voluntary engagement with contemplative, philosophical or spiritual practices, recorded in actual activities and frequency | Neptune in twelfth within <= 6 degrees behind tropical Placidus Ascendant | Not evidence of supernatural attainment, mental health, or spirituality *caused by* a planet |
| N-ALTERED | Deliberately pursuing altered conscious experience by **any** method (meditative or non-meditative) in a declared period | Same chart hypothesis, but a separately coded outcome; do not count it as independent of N-SPIRIT without dependency analysis | Not evidence of using substances |
| N-SUBSTANCE | Any voluntary psychoactive-substance experimentation, with privacy-preserving optional category/frequency reporting; non-use is valid | Same chart hypothesis, distinct outcome. Predefine whether one-time experiment or repeated interest counts; do not alter threshold after looking at answer | Never call this addiction/substance-use disorder |
| N-DISORDER | Clinically significant, impairing pattern of substance use | **NOT AN ADMITTED ASTROLOGY PREDICTION**; requires independent validated assessment and consent | Do not infer from frequency, psychedelic/spiritual motivation, or self-description as “druggie” |

These are **provisional test definitions**, not established associations. A respondent may be spiritual but not use drugs, experiment with substances without mystical practice, do both, or do neither. The logic may not select whichever category makes a chart look more accurate.

## Proposed neutral prompts for an OPTIONAL separate study

1. “Over the past year, have you taken part in activities primarily aimed at contemplation, spiritual understanding, or examining consciousness? If so, which practices and about how often? ‘None’ and ‘prefer not to answer’ are valid.”
2. “Have you intentionally explored unusual states of consciousness? If so, were these through meditation, breathwork, sleep/dream practice, psychoactive substances, or another approach? You may select several, describe your own, or skip. No dose, acquisition or procedure is requested.”
3. “If you chose to explore such experiences, what tended to motivate you—spiritual inquiry, personal wellbeing, curiosity, recreation, connection with others, relief of distress, or something else? What changed over time?”
4. For **voluntary substance-related research only**, separately and without asking for identifiable medical data: “Was this a one-time experiment, occasional exploration, repeated use, or not applicable?” Do not diagnose from the answer.

Keep these optional; do not insert targeted intoxication or spiritually labeled questions into an existing birth-blind assessment without separately pre-registering the new instrument and consent protocol. Chart predicates and theoretical expectation remain invisible to the respondent and blind coder.

## Prospective engineering and statistical admission criteria

- Freeze age/location/time standard, source edition, six-degree orb, house algorithm and ephemeris calculation mode **before** testing any new people.
- Mark Neptune as behind or in front of the Ascendant using signed angular separation; avoid silently changing the traditional twelfth-house criterion to broad first-house angularity.
- Commit survey item wording, behavior coding thresholds, missingness and split-person sample assignment before chart reveal.
- Estimate independently observed baseline rates of spiritual practice, intentional altered states and substance exploration with age/cohort/geography matching. Do not equate a geometrical orb percentage with predictive significance.
- Check trait dependency and multi-comparison selection. Report both false positives (Neptune near Ascendant without outcome) and false negatives (outcome without feature). Compare to randomly shuffled natal charts.
- Keep legacy six-rule AstroHD, Human Design and this historic western-Neptune hypothesis as **separate** arms until an explicitly frozen joint study justifies combination.
- **Do not** count the originating user’s chart or private responses as independent validation; the rule was selected in response to them.

## Interpretive limitation

Modern astrologers still associate Neptune rising with mystical interests, fantasy and possible intoxicants, but the associations lack established predictive validity. Even a striking natal symbol can be found retrospectively among many available planets, signs, houses, aspects, interpretive schools and outcomes. The historical source makes the wording less vulnerable to *temporal invention*, not free from post-selection bias.
