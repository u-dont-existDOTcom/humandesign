# HumanDesign Zi Wei empirical research — prior human studies change the hypothesis testing plan

**Date:** 21 October 2026. **Scope:** bounded source-verified original thesis metadata and abstracts, not full-text systematic literature review or numerical meta-analysis. No user/person private birth records or scored responses in this public document. Existing studies involving people remain **external prior evidence**, not completed or validated HumanDesign predictions.

## Scientific consequence

There is **already real-human empirical Zi Wei Dou Shu evidence**, including a 2018 psychological thesis reporting broadly null relationships between astrologers' chart-based judgments and established psychological measurements, and a 2020 economics thesis reporting higher cigarette-smoking propensity among people allocated to particular Zi Wei-derived personality categories. The two studies have *different outcomes, analyses and evidential limits*. It is scientifically incorrect to count both as support or to combine their reported results numerically without full tables, covariates and measurement definitions.

Both are Taiwan master's theses with confirmed institutional records. The 2018 study is **more directly relevant to our four proposed ordinary psychological traits** than the 2020 smoking study. The latter is relevant to selecting prospective chart-category/behavior rules and co-star classifications but cannot establish independent chart prediction simply from an abstract association.

## Direct Zi Wei study A: 2018 TEO — mostly null with explicit subgroup exceptions

**Official:** 張惠清 (HUAY CHING TEO), *紫微斗數與具信效度心理量表的相關* (*Are traditional Chinese fortune-telling approach, Zi Wei Dou Shu, associated with well-established psychological inventories*), Chung Shan Medical University, Psychology/Clinical Psychology master's thesis, 2018.

- University original record: https://scholars.csmu.edu.tw/entities/publication/0504838a-2de5-459b-9bf3-47ae1c1ea0bf
- Secondary abstract and references: https://www.airitilibrary.com/Article/Detail/U0003-1807201810474500
- The university's archival original file is named **106CSMU5821007-001.zip**, listed as 1.24MB with supplied MD5 **f24a2b9f1640d4e3a45af4bdc6788b4c**, but offers **Request a copy**. File not downloaded, no full source read. Airiti shows Download and abstract but rights/access unverified.

**Abstract-grounded design:** 243 college students completed measures including the Big Five Mini-Markers, parental attachment, family system and parents/siblings satisfaction items. Three experienced practitioners received generated Zi Wei charts and each assessed personality/parent/sibling outcomes through a seven-item astrology evaluation instrument. The researchers correlated practitioners' chart judgments with the independent questionnaire scores. University and Airiti abstracts report **no significant relationships overall or within sex/eastern-vs-western religious groupings**, except a low positive friendliness/agreeableness-domain correlation among religious participants and a marginal such correlation in those who did not believe Zi Wei.

**Do not overinterpret:** “No overall significant correlations” is neither proof no possible astrology model exists nor equivalent to a numerical effect exactly zero. The exceptions must be retained, not erased. However, because there were multiple outcomes and subgroup checks, the subgroup signals require full p-values, primary versus exploratory hypotheses, confidence intervals and multiple-testing correction. None of these or the actual seven questions, birth-hour accuracy, blinded procedures, practitioner agreement, missing responses or subgroup denominators can be checked from the visible abstract. The full thesis or authorized original tables are necessary.

**Implication:** This is independent existing negative pressure on any generic “Zi Wei reads personality” claim. HumanDesign cannot claim to be the first to test these relationships, nor ignore this large directly relevant null study. A planned new study should explicitly compare to a predeclared baseline personality questionnaire and not cherry-pick friendly outcomes after seeing them.

## Direct Zi Wei study B: 2020 Chen — reported smoking association, numeric effect unknown

**Official:** 陳冠升 (Kuan-Sheng Chen), *吸菸行為的「命」傾向* (*The marginal fate effect of smoking behavior*), Tamkang University, Economics and Finance master's thesis, 2020, DOI https://doi.org/10.6846/TKU.2020.00022.

- University original metadata: https://etds.lib.tku.edu.tw/ETDS/Home/Detail/U0002-0207202010434300
- Airiti abstract: https://www.airitilibrary.com/Article/Detail/U0002-0207202010434300
- The university says the 78-page thesis became accessible off campus on **2020-07-14**, but the actual visible Download Full Text button redirects to an institutional **ezproxy/SSO login**. This session has **not accessed the full thesis or its numerical tables**. Do not claim contrary simply because the metadata indicates an open release date.

**Abstract-grounded results:** The author compared Zi Wei innovative/leadership categories with supportive/cooperative categories, reporting greater smoking tendency in the former. Some qualitative interviews linked smokers to preferred solitude; this is interpretive qualitative evidence, not calibrated chart prediction. A separate difference-in-differences analysis of cigarette warning images reported no observed deterrent effect in the quantitative analysis; **that is not an astrology accuracy result**. The source's own stated hypotheses are framed as expected differences, even though the abstract labels them “null hypotheses”; don't reinterpret that language as verified confirmatory preregistration.

**The actual TOC—valuable method map, not actual regression results:** Table 4.1 numerically codes favorable/unfavorable star dignity, Table 4.2 maps star dignity levels, Tables 4.10 and 4.11 examine correlations among single-star and paired principal stars, Tables 5.1–5.9 estimate smoking associations for all 14 principal stars, four broader groups and all star combinations, separately overall and by sex; Tables 5.10–5.11 concern warning images. Its references cite TEO's **2018 mostly null thesis**. This means Chen's analysis model is materially richer than an undifferentiated “smokers are creative” anecdote, but the sheer number of possible analytic comparisons also raises a pre-specification and multiplicity concern; tables and model equations are needed before any judgment.

**Unverified:** actual N, birth record/date/hour quality, how smoking defined (ever/current/daily/number cigarettes), exact chart software/schools, covariate adjustment (age, sex, cohort, income, education, peers, smoking access), effect sizes, standard errors, robust SEs, significance, correcting for many outcomes/star pairs, causal interpretation or out-of-sample validation. A reported association does not in itself show independent accuracy beyond these common nonastrological predictors, and no quantitative result from this thesis has been transcribed.

**Replication design implication:** Once full text available, extract the exact categorical chart rule and test *one frozen* smoking outcome against a strong preregistered non-astrological comparator, ideally with time/sex/cohort/education/family smoking and socioeconomic controls. An original regression fitted on these respondents is development data, **not the independent prospective test sample**.

## Additional independent Western astrology comparison — methods only, not Zi Wei confirmation

- **Tyson (1984)**, *An empirical test of the astrological theory of personality*, Personality and Individual Differences 5(2):247–250, https://doi.org/10.1016/0191-8869(84)90059-X. Publisher abstract describes **15** respondents and nominees choosing among five charts, plus five psychometric personality dimensions; reports no successful horoscope identification or meaningful astrology-vs-self/nominee/psychometric trait correlations. Small sample, different Western astrology system.
- **Carlson (1985)**, *A double-blind test of astrology*, Nature 318:419–425, https://doi.org/10.1038/318419a0. Original publisher abstract states two double-blind natal-personality tests; the brief public source extracted for this checkpoint does not provide numerical outcome scores. Do **not** invent them from the paper's reputation without reading and adjudicating methods, critiques and full analysis.
- **Fuzeau-Braesch (1992)**, *An empirical study of an astrological hypothesis in a twin population*, https://doi.org/10.1016/0191-8869(92)90027-M. Publisher abstract reports 238 respondents among 251 twin pairs, **153 exact**, **65 reversed**, **20 no difference** trait matches; author interpreted as astrology-positive. This is Western twin chart interpretation, not Zi Wei.
- **Ertel & Dean (1996)**, *Are personality differences between twins predicted by astrology?*, https://doi.org/10.1016/0191-8869(96)00112-2. Their critique/reanalysis of Fuzeau's result reports no meaningful excess over chance after examining response stereotypes, description rule adherence, replication and original data discrepancies. It is a substantive counteranalysis, not direct Zi Wei.
- **Fuzeau-Braesch (1996 reply)**, https://doi.org/10.1016/0191-8869(96)00036-0. The original positive-study author disputes post-outcome editing allegations and the reanalysis/replication methods. The debate is contested; do not portray only the critique as an uncontested fact or the original positive hit rate as validated without caveat.

**Generalizable methodological lesson:** Locked descriptions, transparent rejected/missing responses, independent chart calculations, unambiguous prespecified scoring, chart-label/target blinding, decoys, direct psychometric/baseline comparisons, proper multiplicity/uncertainty analysis and replication matter. Astrologically plausible **post-outcome** explanations cannot repair failed prospective predictions.

## Consequences for existing HumanDesign research status

Historical original source research in this repository remains sizeable (Chinese V14 BaZi210+ZiWei586; Western B07bg Lilly13236+Ptolemy1066) but these are documentary source **annotations**, not 15,098 humans, tests or successes. The Chinese natal clock and software synthetic tests are engineering. The draft source lexical four-behavior model presently has overlapping predictors (TaiYang and TianLiang both claim 好济施 helping; QingYang/WenQu auxiliaries conditional/not computed), and no validated probabilities/trait outcomes. Its model options 10/34/42 synthetic coverage are not a real predictive result.

### Next ranked work

1. **Obtain full texts through authorized university/library routes, not authentication bypass.** For TEO, request institutional original ZIP or accessible librarian copy. For Chen, use an eligible university SSO/library/public off-campus legitimate download route. Extract the original 7 practitioner questions; practitioner agreement, date-hour birth source, exact coefficient/SE/CI/p, sample inclusion, n missing; tables 4.1/4.2, 4.10/4.11 and 5.1–5.9; identify which claims can be scored prospectively and which are artifacts. If full text cannot be retrieved, mark blocked at original source and continue bounded protocol work. Do not assert missing details.
2. **Design preregisterable but not yet preregistered replication**, choosing one population and one primary endpoint: either source-to-chart prediction of validated behavioral/personality questions analogous to TEO or **single smoking-category** prediction analogous to Chen (not both as unconstrained after-the-fact alternatives). Declare objective chart input policy, source school, birth-time uncertainty/abstention, chart decoys matched on calendar/cohort/location, and nonastrological baseline trained/calibrated separately. Incorporate social desirability and expectancy controls.
3. **Quantify sample size** from an independently declared minimal worthwhile prediction improvement and plausible baseline, with blind score permutation design; no fabricated effect sizes based on invisible theses. Preserve exploratory analyses but do not re-label a favorable subgroup as confirmatory.
4. **Consent/privacy and genuine untouched subjects** remain human gates. Owner and Hale's previously inspected charts/answers are development illustrations, not test data for final confirmation. No real recruitment/participant scoring authorized from this checkpoint.
5. Continue the original Western Lilly/Chinese Zi Wei/BaZi sources in separate append-only branches only insofar as it resolves **predeclared testable rules**. Avoid another large source-annotation batch as a substitute for actual predictive validation.

**Conclusion from inspected original sources:** There is published Zi Wei human research and it includes predominantly null and some positive author-reported signals, but **no new independently reproducible prospective human predictive success can be verified from the accessed thesis abstracts**. The project should explicitly treat those studies as existing evidence, not claim novelty or clinical/individual diagnostic use.
