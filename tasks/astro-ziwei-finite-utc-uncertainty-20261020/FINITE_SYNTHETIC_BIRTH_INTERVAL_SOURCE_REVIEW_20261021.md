# Zi Wei original-source natal chart interval sensitivity — synthetic UTC-second evidence

**Date:** 21 October 2026. **Source scope:** selected transmitted Zi Wei Dou Shu source-based 14-principal-star positions, no other auxiliary/annual/transformation/relation rules or real human outcomes. **Status:** mathematically reproducible simulation only; not a complete horoscope, validated ancient text edition, birth-time source-quality study, preregistered real-human prediction or moral/medical inference.

## Precise input definition

Each of six experiments begins with a **fictional, known UTC instant interval** and a named fictional IANA timezone/longitude. Every *integer UTC second*, inclusive of both endpoints, is enumerated; no sampling or rounding is used within that restricted grid. This deliberately removes a major unsolved problem—the different possible UTC instants of an *ambiguous recorded local birth clock*, including DST folds, uncertain birthplace or historical timezone version. A real participant may supply only a remembered local hour with error: this tool **cannot** infer, enumerate or authenticate that real interval.

For each integer second, the SHA-pinned original engine converts UTC → historical local clock with explicit IANA fold, runs a known, declared Chinese-calendar reference choice (the same instant on **fixed UTC+08** versus birthplace's historical civil Gregorian calendar date) and named natal two-hour Earthly Branch choice (recorded local civil hour versus **local mean solar longitude**). Traditional Zi Wei *Chinese Lunar New Year* annual reference and transmitted original always-as-next leap-month school are held fixed. Alternative 23:00 Zi-day turnover and astronomical apparent solar time are not implemented. Then the engine calculates 14 **principal** star positions and Life/Body palace, not a full Zi Wei chart. The calculation is source- and independent-software-verified *within these declared conventions* only.

The six interval definitions total **1,206 distinct invented UTC seconds**; each has four declared day/hour conventions, so **4,824 second-policy evaluations**. Identical chart states are compressed into contiguous, completely covered UTC second runs; start/end second, clock fold, UTC offsets and matched source quote/HKO provenance retained. The source output stores a SHA-256 for each normalized chart state, preserving the original overall source status (including two `qualified_source_variant` statuses—never silently rewriting them to fully original). The program's output includes all 18 unique source calendar/hour chart-state records encountered across six experiments.

The **only complete mathematical source-chart invariant** under the entire tested instant×policy grid is the fictional New York DST-fold interval. Its Life palace has *none* of our four candidate source stars, so it yields **no affirmative prediction**. The fictional Hong Kong leap-hour interval also has invariant **source candidate abstention status** despite having two different 14-star layouts; absence of one of four source phrases is not a validated negative claim about personality.

For Dakar's Lunar New Year boundary, the original accepted lunar-year/time conversion can legitimately change the natal star set by one second across **fixed UTC8 midnight** while the birthplace Gregorian date remains unchanged. No claim that a particular school has better astrological predictive validity follows from this.

## Source evidence, limits and failure mode

- Independent historical source-engine code and original HKO response bodies (22 exact files) SHA-pinned to previously accepted `fb6b30b1ca67ff62fecc0c4ea7f3e4d0553640d4`; previous source policy methods record exact accepted SHA pins and 48 artificial policy comparisons. All original Chinese source files/old natal engine remain unchanged.
- Two independently implemented lunar calendars `lunar_python==1.4.8`, `sxtwl==2.0.7` with binary/source-tree SHA required but *not bundled*. HKO original source dates covered in previously frozen observational witness set; not all generated calendar days have an original HKO verification. The experiment does not falsely claim all generated rows have authenticated original official dates.
- The original independent 2057-09-28 double-calendar disagreement aborts **the whole affected synthetic interval** rather than picking lunar 8/30 or 9/1; no partial person's chart output or fallback school.
- Some synthetic source natal chart positions have accepted source status `qualified_source_variant` due to documented source emendations. It is not upgraded to original unqualified text or human predictive certainty.
- The four provisional Zi Wei source phrase→question mappings (WuQu decisiveness, LianZhen quarrelling, TianLiang helping, PoJun competitiveness) remain unvalidated expert translations/psychometrics. Traditional conjunctive/modifying conditions with other stars, auxiliary stars and classical time rules remain unresolved. A chart in which the star is absent gives **no inference that someone lacks a trait**, and a star present with others gives **ABSTAIN** under the earlier deliberately conservative model until a historical combination/contradiction rule is frozen.
- There is **no objective probability** for any real person's actual birth-time uncertainty in this six-interval designed simulation. The full real uncertainty would involve record quality, historical source, IANA tzdb provenance, non-Gregorian calendar crosswalk, possible daylight gap/folds, historic true solar equation, Zi-day school and geographical uncertainty—none inferred from a guessed point location.

It must propagate intervals into the existing UTC-set checker and abstain if source chart feature status is not invariant.
