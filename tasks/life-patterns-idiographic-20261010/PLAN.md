## Method and invariants
- Idiographic inventory: invite an initial few examples, continue toward 10–20 only if willing; concrete behavior+frequency+context+comparative group+why chosen+counterexample; say explicitly ordinary/unusual either is fine and 0 is valid.
- Keep spontaneous count and prompted count separate, and treat self-judged rarity as confidence, not objectively established prevalence. No birth-identification scoring until a held-out preregistered test shows incremental gain. Guard language fluency, recall, modesty, culture, socioeconomic constraints, fatigue and age.
- Multi-choice anchors may be shown by default as *possible ways to answer*; always include `Other / describe in own words`, `depends` and `not sure`, never demand one response; anchor/priming effects tested against open-ended-only control. Voice and text parity.
- Current TF1 A0 maps friend invitation vs solitude partly; prospective new A0 asks intrinsic solitude enjoyment, with a separate optional friend-invitation branch to preserve the old behavioral dimension. Do not falsely treat a stated enjoyment level as actual time spent alone.
- STATUS and OWNERSHIP are separate question constructs. Historical D19.status_ownership was one joint *guide facet* attached to both older routes. Preserve the frozen guide but prevent current/prospective STATUS from inheriting ownership motives; annotate provenance and split the new development facet. Keep any original observed laptop answer independently if relevant, not as recognition evidence.
- Kettle anecdote: do not claim a personality trait from a single behavior; do not imply unsafe appliance uses are advisable or intrinsically more efficient. Experimental example should be generalized, not copy private author experience to public repo.
