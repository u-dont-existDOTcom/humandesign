The preceding three source parts have transcribed source-line records for Zi Wei, Tian Ji, Tai Yang, Wu Qu, Tian Tong, Lian Zhen, Tian Fu, Tai Yin, Tan Lang, Ju Men, Tian Xiang and Tian Liang, with the last V12 note ZWD-R0482 containing only a **Qi Sha navigation prefix** at source codepoint28621. **Qi Sha 七杀**, original source codepoint **28621–29332**, has 22 substantive source lines, including seven palace-specific condition lines and male, female and time-limit poetic text. Original tradition associates the star with authority, coercion, social status, adversity, lifespan and bodily/sex-coded categories, sometimes applying adverse conditions or mitigating combinations. These are *authorial conditional judgements*, not verified behavioural or medical effects.

**Po Jun 破军**, original source cp**29332–30135**, has 23 substantive lines, six palace-condition rows and male/female/time-limit verses. The historical text alternates favourable authority/fortune situations with unfavorable social/health and relationship claims conditioned on co-present stars and other old rules. None are independently scored against actual people or supported as modern clinical or social classifications.

After Po Jun, the original begins **Wen Chang 文昌** (auxiliary literary star) at codepoint **30135**, documented as next-source **navigation prefix only** in ZWD-R0528. Thus there are **45 principal-star substantive original source lines + 1 next boundary source marker = 46 historical annotations ZWD-R0483–ZWD-R0528**, with source exact codepoint/UTF8 offsets and quote/segment SHA verification. Source text for **all fourteen principal stars' individual Life-palace profiles has now been covered through Po Jun**, but this does **not** complete the entire first Life-palace section: auxiliary stars (Wen Chang, Wen Qu, Zuo Fu, You Bi, Lu Cun and more) occupy further original text until the separate Brothers/Siblings heading **===二兄弟===** at cp**37269**.
