## Coverage: natal conditionals, role confounds and measurement uncertainty

**Zi Wei** (紫微): lord/authority/fame and property status rhetoric, dependency on assisting heavenly stars and fortune/malefic combinations, 7 original palace-combination lines, masculine/feminine lifespan/wealth/sex/morality verses, and two large/small life-limit fortune verses. Crucially, a verse “紫微辰戌遇破军” uses a general “encounter” verb, whereas natal conditional table explicitly says “辰戌宫得地与天相同度” (same-degree Tian Xiang). Another table has “丑未宫入庙与破军同度”. **Do not equate the broad classical verb 遇 to precise same-palace 同度 before an independent traditional expert and original model-source check.** This is a contextual semantic disambiguation, not a proven erroneous ancient manuscript or chart contradiction.

**Tian Ji** (天机): being benevolent, strategic/speech/occupational roles, fortunes where companion stars and house/sex contexts vary, six palace-region source lines, historical value judgments of female character and partner status, distinct good/bad/limit verses. These texts mix a claimed trait, profession, ordinal star placement, wealth and mortality in the same line: atomic future predictive target rules require preregistration and may not be retroactively selected.

**Tai Yang** (太阳): explicitly **day-born versus night-born** strength/status, palace location/malefic alternates, health and longevity rhetoric, six source natal-house lines and sex-specific good/bad verses, plus time-limit claims. A true birth “day or night” standard requires a chosen local time/longitude, historical horizon and solar altitude; the separate modern Chinese chart bridge is not a verified original historic clock for this period. Do not automatically treat a 12-hour branch label as sufficient to establish day/night Sun above the horizon without astronomical conversion.

**Wu Qu** (武曲): money, occupation, martial/official vs writing roles, northwestern/southeastern life-context contrast, male/female ancient status words, six palace-region rows, favorable/hostile verses and life-limit timing predictions. The original uses sexist normative labels and fatalistic claims; they must remain source-only and are not acceptable as demographic character inference, clinical risk, modern social/hiring or partner advice.
