## Bounded civil-Gregorian-date → official Chinese lunar date bridge

Implemented `ziwei/dual_witness_civil_lunar.py` with pinned, *optional external* lunar-python 1.4.8 and sxtwl 2.0.7. Calls both independently; if either is missing, at an unexpected version, or disagrees, **fails closed**. It never silently chooses a library. The returned official-calendar date has a Chinese New Year year stem; the distinct Li Chun annual changeover is a required, *unimplemented* separately selected variant. It does NOT convert an individual's local birth timestamp to the Hong Kong/Beijing reference-calendar date, a true-solar birth hour, or medieval original-calendar conventions. The caller must supply a reference calendar day explicitly and provide hour branch independently. No production integration or predictive model activation.

All ten HKO API records (2023, 2025, 2026, 2027, 2028) preserved by *exact original UTF-8 response bytes and SHA256*; their Chinese-text month/day strings are parsed and checked against both library outputs. This includes 2023 leap month 2 boundaries, 2025 leap month 6 start, 2026 Feb16 vs Feb17 Chinese New Year, and 2027 New Year. HKO specifically explains that a lunar New Year Gan-Zhi year can differ from a Li Chun Gan-Zhi year used by diviners; on **2026-02-16** official lunar year is 乙巳 while library Li Chun year is 丙午. Lunar-year selection must not be inferred from astrology source study without provenance.
