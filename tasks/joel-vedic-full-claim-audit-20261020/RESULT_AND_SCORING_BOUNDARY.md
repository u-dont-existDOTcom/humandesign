# Full cold Vedic reading — 129-claim catalog and outcome audit

**2026-10-20 · Research/development, not validation.** The owner specifically reports that the third-party ChatGPT astrologer received only birth chart metadata and charts, **not the owner's questionnaire or personal history**. Accept that premise when interpreting the results. Do not repeat the prior error of treating a cold-generation reading as though its author had seen the answers. The present evaluator, however, already knew the owner's results before extracting claims; the score is not an independently outcome-blind adjudication.

- Three ordered CSV parts, 43 rows each, cover **P001–P129** and are contiguous, with original-reading line anchors and the astrologer's tropical/sidereal/shared arm labels:
  - `claims_source_only_part_01.csv`

  - `claims_source_only_part_03.csv`

## What actually counts as a forecast?

| Class | Entries | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| A | 5 | Relatively assertive directional traits, still without numeric threshold |
| H | 83 | Hedged/possible expressions; no committed probability |
| X | 10 | Opposing possible outcomes explicitly supplied |
| G | 1 | Broad hobby/occupation menu |
| U | 8 | External outcome (status/family/achievement) not measured |
| N | 6 | Normative advice/ideal |
| T | 3 | Metaphysical/symbolic rather than measured prediction |
| F | 13 | Timing-period or future theme, mostly untestable yet |
| **Total** | **129** | Candidate interpretations, **not 129 independent predictions** |

The five A entries all correspond to the owner's self-reports, but two represent the same intellectual/inquiry family, leaving **four broad substantive themes**: inquiry, attraction-linked initiative, work discipline, and generally calm emotional baseline. All are broad and may be common in other respondents.

The more permissive H entries: **25** clear descriptive correspondences, **32** partial, **1** mixed, **2** with counterevidence, **22** unmeasured, **1** effectively nondirectional. These are *not* 25 independently successful forecasts. Most text uses “could”, “may”, “constructive expression”, “shadow”, and alternative development paths.

Aggregate 129-row coding: **S=30, P=32, M=1, C=2, U=41, N=23**. The coder's grade definitions are: S=clear self-report fit, P=partial/adjacent/contextual, M=mixed evidence, C=contrary evidence to suggested direction, U=not tested, N=not an eligible directional prediction.

The owner-facing **private** detailed CSV, JSON, report and formatted workbook contain individual source-to-answer evidence references. Because survey materials are private, those files are not committed. Do not publish raw respondent answers or another participant's questionnaire without a separate appropriate permission boundary.

## Why this cannot yet become a p-value

- Outcome generation was reportedly cold to survey, but this scorer's extraction and coding were outcome-aware, and unambiguous source-only claims are broad and correlated.
- No measured population endorsement frequencies exist for the four traits on a common measurement instrument; a 50% per-claim coin flip is **not** a valid null model.
- Ten explicit contradictory-alternative sets and one multiactivity menu greatly increase opportunities for retrospective resonance without specifying a losing outcome.
- One unrelated respondent's differently worded interview qualitatively endorsed some of the same broad claims; **not** a comparable matched decoy cohort or significant blind match.
- Neither the rare astronomical feature geometry nor the owner's previously owner-fitted AstroHD century rank is independent behavioral validation. The older AstroHD rank has an explicit structural-null audit.
- A fixed full-length reading can be tested properly with several independently written cold decoy readings, multiple similarly assessed people, concealed identities, and true-chart versus nonchart likelihood/discrimination scores.

## Important omissions for future tests

No specific forecast of extremely frequent awe/self-surprise/synchronicity, the timeline of a profound subjective shift in early adulthood, central humor/playfulness, the sharp mental-vs-physical work-stamina contrast, or the actual situations governing taking on other people's needs. These are omissions in descriptive coverage; they are not automatically false assertions.

**Conclusion:** the Vedic interpretation has several genuine cold-reading correspondences and deserves serious scoring. Its *amount of chart-specific accuracy above guessing* is **unmeasured**, not proven zero and not proven positive. The precise next test is blinded true-versus-decoy chart assignment on new people, with identical reading protocols and predeclared behavioral outcomes. This case must remain a development/evaluation example rather than being recycled as fresh confirmation.
