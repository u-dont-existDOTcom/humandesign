# Instruction to the GPT that produced the Vedic reading — make testable predictions

I want to rigorously test whether your original, chart-only Jyotish reading of this person predicts real-life characteristics and events better than generic guessing. You previously received the natal birth data and charts and wrote a long two-part reading, including a conventional Lahiri sidereal interpretation and a separate tropical-sign/Kala-chart interpretation. **Continue in that same chat and use the original reading already in your context.**

## Absolute information boundary

- **Do not ask for, obtain, search for, or infer from** this person's survey responses, biography, websites, publications, social media, prior ChatGPT conversations, memory, connected applications, or any previously learned personal facts. Do not search for the subject's name. Use **only the previously provided birth information, charts, your original written reading, and explicitly identified traditional astrology rules/calculations**.
- If you discover any pre-existing personal/outcome knowledge in your available context, identify its origin and the affected claims; do **not** present those claims as independent chart-only predictions. You cannot certify lack of leakage simply by declaring yourself blind.
- **Do not ask to see the test answers until all predictions are finished, exported, and locked.** Do not solicit clarifying personality or life-history details.
- Do not mention or guess what our survey asked, what answers it contains, or what its results might already be. Choose the forecast subjects from **your own original chart-derived reasoning**, not from any hints I give about the subject.

## Two strictly separated tasks

**PART 1 — Exhaustive original-reading claim inventory (source fidelity).** Examine your *entire* prior reading, including the later deep analysis of dignities, yogas, karakas, nakshatras, Vargas, and dashas. Extract **every distinct proposed factual assertion about this person's disposition, behavior, motives, relationships, life circumstances, talents, strengths, weaknesses, choices, life events and future events**. Give each a stable ID and quote its relevant short original wording or exact internal heading/paragraph anchor. Keep all the negative, uncomfortable, disputed, conditional and mutually opposing interpretations, not only the flattering ones. Deduplicate repeated expressions of essentially one underlying claim and record their dependencies.

Classify each original claim as:
- **ORIGINAL_DIRECTIONAL** — the earlier reading actually chose a side or concrete outcome, with a reasonably observable implication;
- **ORIGINAL_HEDGED** — may/could/potential/shadow/mature expression; a possible outcome, not an actual commitment;
- **ORIGINAL_BOTH_WAYS** — the prior text allowed opposing outcomes without saying which is more likely or specifying when;
- **ORIGINAL_MENU** — many possible hobbies, occupations, abilities or events with no unique preferred outcome;
- **SYMBOLIC_OR_ADVICE** — traditional symbolism, questions to reflect on, virtues to cultivate, metaphysical ideas, or advice rather than an observable prediction;
- **EXTERNAL_OR_FUTURE_UNTESTED** — objective or time-dependent claim not yet testable from an ordinary personality questionnaire.

**Do not retrospectively reword an originally hedged statement as an originally confident prediction.** A new sharper statement belongs in Part 2. Return an inventory count for every class and explicit references tying each scored prediction back to Part 1. If the reading is too long to catalog in one screen, save it to a file or output a complete structured table in ordered installments without dropping sections.

**PART 2 — A NEW, independent, falsifiable chart-only forecast.** Now that the old wording has been preserved, make the *strongest precise predictions you actually judge warranted* by the chart. These are **new commitments made today**, not things the old reading is retroactively credited with predicting. Select a manageable set of informative, nonredundant outcomes (roughly 20–40 if genuinely supported; **do not invent weak predictions to reach a quota**). Include both likely presences and likely absences of traits/events where the chart supports a direction. Explicitly record **ABSTAIN / CONFLICTED / INSUFFICIENT_RULE_SUPPORT** where it does not.

## Every new directional prediction must be a real wager

For **each** independently testable prediction, provide all the following:

1. **ID and chart arm:** `L` for conventional Lahiri sidereal, `K` for the chart's tropical-sign/Kala convention, or `SHARED_GEOMETRY` where the same astronomical feature and inference genuinely apply to both. If a claim requires zodiac or karaka settings not reliably known, mark unresolved rather than selecting whichever interpretation is convenient. Treat competing arms as separate predeclared models, **never a pool of interchangeable explanations**.
2. **Chart basis:** the precise placements, houses, aspects/orbs, dignities/yogas, nakshatra/padas, dasha calculation and original-text anchors relied upon. Specify the relevant traditional rule and source where known; distinguish a classical rule from your own modern psychological extrapolation. Check birth-time/ayanamsa sensitivity when material. A rare-looking aspect is not automatically a behavioral prediction.
3. **ONE observable proposition:** a clear everyday-language forecast of what the person has *actually experienced or usually does*, not what they *could be capable of*, might someday learn, should improve, or would ideally become.
4. **Operational definition and context:** the exact behavior/experience, the relevant circumstances, the reference period or age/date window, and a threshold such as “more often than not,” “at least weekly,” or “at least two distinct occurrences,” where such a threshold makes sense. Do not invent false time precision. Separate ability from frequency, typical behavior from one extreme event, preference from capacity, and subjective experience from objectively verified events.
5. **Complete mutually exclusive answer options:** usually 2–5 neutral options, including a real disconfirming option. When *both A and B* can genuinely coexist, predefine A-only / B-only / both / neither, or specify separate contexts that are objectively testable. An option set that makes all plausible answers count as agreement is invalid.
6. **Preferred outcome and numerical probabilities:** choose **one** highest-probability option and give probabilities for **every** answer option summing to 100%. These are your **uncalibrated subjective forecasts**, not empirically validated frequencies. Express genuine uncertainty. If the source does not warrant a chosen direction, **ABSTAIN** instead of mechanically assigning 51%. Never claim the absence of a scientific population baseline means 50% is the real baseline.
7. **What would prove you wrong:** list the exact response(s) that would count as misses, plus how partial/unknown answers would be handled. No later rescue by “shadow expression,” “not yet mature,” “karma,” another zodiac, a different dasha, or unrelated anecdote unless the triggering context was frozen **now**.
8. **Generalizable rule:** what would be predicted for *another* birth chart lacking the triggering feature (or having the opposite condition), or state clearly when the tradition provides no such comparative mapping. If the same answer would be predicted for nearly everybody, call it generic rather than chart-discriminating.
9. **Dependency group:** label linked or near-synonymous propositions so an evaluator cannot double-count them as separate successes.
10. **Question for an independent interviewer:** phrase a neutral question that does **not** mention signs, planets, your hypothesis, or your preferred answer. Keep all choices neutral, without implying which is desirable.

For life timing, also give **specific start and end dates**, the *single primary event type*, an observable event definition, a probability, and a clearly nonoccurrence outcome. When making multiple timing windows, specify in advance how many total opportunities you are claiming; no retroactive widening of windows or substituting other event categories. Give past historical windows and future windows in separate sets; neither has been tested merely because it describes an evocative theme. Avoid making diagnostic, treatment, death, safety or other high-stakes decisions based on astrology.

## Coverage, internal consistency and honesty checks

- Produce a domain-coverage ledger of the subjects that **your original source reading** actually discusses, with for each domain: number of original claims; number of new committed directional predictions; abstentions; and brief reasons. Do not add entire domains solely because of possible survey contents.
- Identify every original contradictory/alternative formulation and state whether Part 2 resolves it by a **new, explicitly conditional rule** or declines to predict. Do not count either alternative as a hit simply because it can explain what happened.
- Preserve contrary testimonies from *all* chart features considered, rather than presenting supporting evidence alone. A strong opposite testimony can lower confidence or require abstention.
- Provide the **five predictions with the highest subjective confidence**, the **five you expect are most differentiating from a generic no-chart forecast**, and the **five most likely to be wrong** (if five exist in each category). These are rankings **within the frozen set**, not extra predictions.
- Technical clarity: do not combine tropical “exalted Moon/Jupiter Atmakaraka” with sidereal “Moon Atmakaraka/Saturn–Ketu twelfth” into one composite result. Report arm-specific disagreements and their consequences before looking at answers.
- The subject's original narrative and this new forecast may both be studied, but **never treat the new precision as something the first reading had already predicted**.
