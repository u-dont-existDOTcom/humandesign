### Specific problems not to hide

1. The reading's emphasis on Saturnian discipline, sustained craft and decisive Mars-like practical action is **not** plainly supported as a dominant across-context behavioral trait by the questionnaire; some descriptions of slowing, overanalysis, deliberate course changes, and intense but shifting interests pull against it. The frozen V2 is also exposed to this same tension.
2. The Vedic text repeatedly phrases inverse or alternative possibilities (strong emotion but not ease, public involvement plus private retreat, competence plus difficulty receiving help). Such formulations have low falsifiability unless the predicted direction, frequency and threshold are pre-specified.
3. No respondent outcome has demonstrated unusual sound/voice sensitivity, paranormal faculties, actual career prestige, wealth, objective reputation, specific family history, or a dasha-driven dated change. Count these as UNTESTED, not as accurate hits.
4. The Vedic narrative places an approximate 2023 Saturn-return exact date at March 9–10, while the project V2 reports March 6 under its fixed calculation convention. This technical mismatch should be independently resolved before timing comparisons; it is not survey evidence.
5. Our V2 astrology-only mapping explicitly had no affirmative autonomy, immediate-body-signal, emotion-permeability, role-projection, correction-threshold, or value-tension claim. The extensive Vedic narrative covers more psychological territory, but that coverage is not automatically predictive information.
6. Do not compare a multithousand-word reading against an eight-claim freeze by counting resonant phrases. Convert both to equal-length, directional, mutually exclusive claim sets before any controlled scoring.

## Scientifically defensible interpretation

The extended Vedic reading resonates with more topics in the interview, notably autonomy and spiritual meaning that the narrower frozen astrology model deliberately left null. That is evidence of **descriptive coverage** on one known case, not evidence that a Jyotish model predicts personalities better. On domains both texts address, there is large overlap; the independently frozen astrology arm already catches much of the same thematic information.

To establish an advantage, first extract a limited number of objectively disconfirmable, behaviorally operationalized forecasts from each rule system without seeing fresh participants. Freeze chart features, question versions, model prediction probabilities, decoys and a non-astrology baseline; use independent answer raters and score all negative claims. Assess calibration and discrimination across multiple unseen people; repeated chart geometry/numerology should not be treated as independent samples.
