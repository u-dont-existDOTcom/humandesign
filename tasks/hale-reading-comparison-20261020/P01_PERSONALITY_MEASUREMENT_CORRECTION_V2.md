# Measurement correction: from one-event predictions to recurring personality tendencies

2026-10-20 — HumanDesign research, P01, DEVELOPMENT only.

## Owner objection and defect

Owner observed that asking only about the 'most recent' episode does not establish a participant's *overall personality*. This was correct. The five October 20 sealed forecasts (two Western, three Jyotish) were made operational by selecting single, unusually specific tasks; most questions were about a single latest incident. That sacrifices construct coverage and validity. The former J02 study-duration question was a twelve-month measure already, but it still confused a chosen threshold with a stable learning disposition.

The correct scientific distinction is between **event prediction**, **self-reported behavioral recurrence**, **opportunity-sampled action frequency**, and **stable dispositional construct**. They are not equivalent, and an isolated behavior can be a legitimate event test while remaining a poor personality measure. William Fleeson's 2001 and 2015 research on density distributions of states supports preserving within-person variability and between-person differences in typical tendency. Sources: PMID 11414368; PMID 26348598. Existing UDA `patterns/interview-evidence-information-gain.md` explicitly requires preserving recurrence reports, denoting opportunities/denominators, eliciting exceptions only when informative, and forbids invented anecdote quotas.

- **Do not edit or supersede the original exact prediction file:** `~/Téléchargements/predictions.json`, SHA-256 `756a67b6afcee4661c73a03c49ad927d26727032cc4c4c6da7571ffdc617a72d`.

- The new respondent-facing `P01_personality_patterns_supplement_v2_20261020.md` asks only about **five broad, habitual behavioral dimensions** derived from the existing five forecast domains: persistence after setbacks, fact checking before advice, practical helping, voluntary sustained learning, and spontaneous group coordination. It seeks usual prevalence, eligibility, context, exceptions, and temporal change, without demanding exact fractions or examples.
- Each broad question is **neutral to the chart's predicted answer** and includes competing behaviors or mixtures. It distinguishes capacity, preference, actual action, and opportunity. Context dependence is useful data, not unlimited post-hoc forgiveness.
- Reuse already recorded participant recurrence from the 96-answer interview; only ask about missing denominator, frequency or exception boundaries. No re-interview for appearance's sake.
- This is a **new measurement instrument (V2), not a modification of the five frozen October 20 predictions**. Its answers cannot be quietly substituted for the original 'most recent episode' endpoints in a scoring report. Any association of V2 responses with those forecasts is **exploratory/developmental**, because the broadened measurement was created after the original forecast was issued and after this project had seen P01 answers.
- There is **no validated psychometric scale** for these five ad-hoc dimensions yet. The broader 96-question narrative must not be represented as a standardized personality assessment either. On future unseen participants, consider an independently validated multidimensional personality measure as a baseline, and test a fully frozen chart-to-construct mapping rather than equating a historically symbolic phrase to an arbitrary modern cutoff.

## Why a large question list is not required

The V2 supplement intentionally uses five focused questions, not a quota of confirming anecdotes. Respondents may answer in natural-language frequencies. One counterexample or boundary condition is useful when it changes the claimed recurrence, not simply because more details sound scientific. If an existing answer already clearly states the typical tendency and its main exceptions, do not ask for the same information again.

## Evidence and execution limits

- Development-case scope only; no unseen-human predictive validation from this redesign.
- Preserve original forecasts, misses and unresolved domains without post-hoc recoding.
- For actual frequency, prospective randomized/repeated sampling of eligible opportunities is more reliable than self-selected recollections.
- For prospective outcome discrimination, match each behavioral construct to a model derived *without* participant outcome exposure; compare against non-astrological norms and independently calculated decoy charts, while adjusting for correlation across rulebooks.
- No private participant birth details, raw responses or score keys are to be committed to a public repository.
