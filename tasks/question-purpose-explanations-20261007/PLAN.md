# Question-purpose explanations — 2026-10-07

## Owner outcome
Every behavioral question or clarification shown to a participant explains what person-level tendency/distinction it is testing. The explanation must not reveal answer direction, scoring, astrology/Human Design targets, or claim validated diagnostic power.

## Boundaries
- Keep frozen historical v7 question wording unchanged.
- Keep `question_text` exact and separately stored/validated.
- Add participant-facing purpose as separate metadata/presentation.
- Cover active TF1 questions, legacy live probes, fast-review clarifications, and final-synthesis fallback.

## Acceptance
- Active TF1 routes carry a nonempty neutral purpose.
- Every public reviewer clarification carries a nonempty neutral purpose.
- Custom GPT instructions require showing the purpose before every behavioral question, including legacy probes and Railway clarifications.
- Canonical question equality/nonredundancy behavior is unchanged.
- Tests enforce coverage and leakage restrictions.
