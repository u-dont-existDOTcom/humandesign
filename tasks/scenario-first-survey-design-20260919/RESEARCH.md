# Scenario-first survey: bounded existing-work scan

Date: 2026-09-19. Purpose: inform a design brainstorm, not perform a systematic review or validate the Life Patterns instrument.

## Independent conception and search route

The owner's independent idea was captured before external searching in OWNER-CONCEPTION.md. It is scenario-first elicitation of personal responses, not a proposed employment test or a new theory of personality.

SciSpace semantic discovery was used first with a full question about representative situational vignettes, open-ended anticipated behavior/feeling, abstract trait prompts, retrospective reports, social desirability, and real behavior. It surfaced situational interviews, personality-situation assessment, vignette validity, and survey-response bias literature. Discovery summaries were not treated as evidence; some automated summaries were less faithful than their abstracts.

Primary/official sources were then checked through web search/open. Search formulations included cognitive interviewing comprehension/recall/judgment/response; situational judgment would-do versus should-do response instructions; vignette versus real-world behavioral validation; prospective childhood autobiographical memory; and official one-question-at-a-time design guidance.

## Decisive sources

### S1. CDC / NCHS: Cognitive Interviewing

https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/ccqder/question-evaluation/cognitive-interviewing.html

Official question-evaluation guidance, dated April 16, 2024; opened during this pass.

Supports: checking how respondents comprehend a question, retrieve information, judge an answer, and formulate a response. Cognitive interviews are suited to finding question problems and are not automatically population estimates.

Application: test whether a scenario is understood and which assumptions the respondent supplies before treating fluent answers as valid data. Our proposed small owner walkthrough is an informal design test; it is not a completed CDC-style research study.

### S2. Nguyen, Biderman & McDaniel (2005)

Effects of Response Instructions on Faking a Situational Judgment Test. International Journal of Selection and Assessment, 13, 250–260. DOI: 10.1111/j.1468-2389.2005.00322.x.

https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1468-2389.2005.00322.x

Publisher abstract and article page inspected.

Supports: asking for best/worst responses is not the same response task as asking for likely/unlikely personal behavior; behavioral-tendency responses are not immune to faking.

Application: ask what the participant expects they would actually do or feel, not the morally correct response. Transfer limit: an employment-style SJT study does not validate an open-ended personal interview or justify a single correctness score for its answers.

### S3. Hainmueller, Hangartner & Yamamoto (2015)

Validating vignette and conjoint survey experiments against real-world behavior. PNAS, 112, 2395–2400. DOI: 10.1073/pnas.1416587112.

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25646415/
https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1416587112

Primary article text and PubMed metadata inspected.

Supports: some stated-preference designs reproduced real-world attribute effects well in a specific naturalization-voting setting, but performance varied by format/sample and absolute preference levels were not accurately recovered.

Application: concrete hypothetical scenes can be useful, but realism alone does not establish behavioral validity. Do not transfer this study's preference-task results into a claim that the Life Patterns survey predicts individual life behavior. Its best paired format is not a mandate to ask every Life Patterns question as a pair.

### S4. Bauer & Larkina (2014)

The onset of childhood amnesia in childhood: A prospective investigation of the course and determinants of forgetting of early-life events. Memory, 22, 907–924. DOI: 10.1080/09658211.2013.854806.

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24236647/
https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/09658211.2013.854806

Primary study abstract inspected. Published online in 2013, journal issue in 2014.

Supports: early autobiographical events can be forgotten even during childhood; later recall is not interchangeable with the original experience.

Application: do not require exact age-five recall or treat present imagination as a remembered event. Transfer limit: this does not imply that all adult memories from age five are false, or that childhood questions have no value.

### S5. GOV.UK Design System: Question pages

https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/question-pages/

Official guidance inspected during this pass.

Supports: starting with one question per page, asking only needed information, reusing earlier answers, and allowing uncertainty when valid.

Application: one response task per conversational turn. Adapt the principle, not the whole government form pattern: do not reintroduce routine Continue checkpoints contrary to the owner's conversational design.

## Existing-work map and disposition

Reusable: cognitive interviewing for identifying response-process problems; precise separation of question tasks; versioned scenario stimuli; distinction between intended and recalled behavior.

Adaptable/composable: situational vignettes plus open-ended personal reports and optional recalled examples. A few relevant contrast cases can expose conditions without replacing an open-world evidence record with a closed trait taxonomy.

Incompatible as a direct transplant: employment selection tests with an answer-key-defined good response, forced dichotomies used as moral/personality labels, and generalized scores claimed to be valid without evidence in this population and application.

Unresolved: whether this particular owner finds the proposed scenes easier; whether a broader intended population understands them similarly; how reliably a generated follow-up preserves the stimulus and source meaning; how much useful information each turn adds; actual post-implementation response burden and cost.

Primary disposition: COMPOSE + BOUNDED EXPERIMENT. Do not claim a novel psychometric method. The local design problem is an adaptive, non-leading conversation with provenance and participant authority, not proving that vignette methods were previously unknown.

External baseline: a well-written, short recent-event interview prompt, plus the established cognitive-interviewing approach to inspecting comprehension. Compare with the incumbent abstract-pattern entry too, but not only with its weakest phrasing.

## Proposed experiment, not a prerequisite campaign

Draft a small set of scene cores and review them in Chat. If the owner chooses to try them, use a few text-only development examples and watch for missing assumptions, incomplete/compound demands, obvious moral responding, and unsupported interpretation. Do not require recruitment, a validation sample, a model tournament, formal psychometric calibration, or a live VM before this design exercise.

Before a scientific claim, public instrument release, or use of new hypothetical evidence in chart recovery, separately address validation, sampling, routing/wording versions, evidence-mode compatibility, demographic leakage, and appropriate comparison/null design. Do not retroactively present this brainstorm as satisfying those tasks.

## What this scan does not establish

No numerical accuracy estimate for Life Patterns. No proof that scenario-first always beats pattern-first or remembered-episode-first. No evidence that childhood is a cleaner view of a 'true' personality. No basis to infer diagnosis, empathy type, Human Design type, population rarity, everyday frequency, or a causal mechanism from one vignette. No authority to modify the accepted v2 evidence contract in this documentation pass.
