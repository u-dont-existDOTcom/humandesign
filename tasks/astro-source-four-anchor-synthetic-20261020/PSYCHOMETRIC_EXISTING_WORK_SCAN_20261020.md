# Established measurement precedents for four ancient Zi Wei behavioral claims

**Status:** bounded research-before-reinvention scan; original paper metadata/abstracts verified via journal, PubMed and/or institutional source pages on 20 Oct 2026. Neither full proprietary questionnaire forms nor psychometric datasets were imported. This is not a systematic instrument review, expert-translated Chinese source, participant questionnaire freeze or proof an astrological trait exists.

## Purpose

The working source-to-question draft has four Chinese natal text fragments, which can be expressed as ordinary, observational questions: 武曲 *性刚果决* (firm decision-making), 廉贞 *好忿争* (contentious disagreement), 天梁 *好济施* (inclination to help), 破军 *好争强* (competitive striving). All four are from exact source note records and raw Wikitext offsets, **not observed empirical effects of those stars**. We first considered four newly authored single behavior questions simply to test whether the software could seal/survey/score labels. Before building an actual *human* instrument, compare to established psychometric measures and report construct mismatches rather than declaring an ad hoc question scientifically valid.

## Existing work by candidate outcome

### Decisiveness / follow-through — Wu Qu

- Scott & Bruce (1995), *Decision-Making Style: The Development and Assessment of a New Measure*, *Educational and Psychological Measurement* 55(5), 818–831, DOI **10.1177/0013164495055005017**; the General Decision-Making Style (GDMS) framework measures rational, intuitive, dependent, avoidant and spontaneous styles. Original journal page <https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0013164495055005017>; University of British Columbia Decision Making Individual Differences inventory entry <https://dlab.sauder.ubc.ca/sjdm/dmidi/General_Decision_Making_Style.html>.
- Frost & Shows (1993), *The nature and measurement of compulsive indecisiveness*, *Behaviour Research and Therapy* 31(7), 683–692, DOI **10.1016/0005-7967(93)90121-A**, <https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/000579679390121A>. Measures problematic indecisiveness, not simply absence of an ancient source's firmness.
- Germeijs & Verschueren (2011), *Indecisiveness: Specificity and Predictive Validity*, *European Journal of Personality* 25, 295–305, DOI **10.1002/per.786**, <https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/per.786> investigated high-school education-choice commitment; context and age are not interchangeable with general adult decisions.

**Mismatch:** GDMS 'spontaneous' isn't the same as being firmly decisive; indecisiveness/perfectionism is not automatically the opposite of astrological 'firmness'. Our single provisional frequency item about settling on a choice may mix habitual decision style, fear of error, competence, outcome seriousness and cultural values. **Next:** select a validated narrow decision-commitment item family or a predeclared event-log/decision-latency proxy on a development-only cohort; check discriminant validity before human prediction freeze.

### Interpersonal argumentativeness — Lian Zhen

- Infante & Rancer (1982), *A Conceptualization and Measure of Argumentativeness*, *Journal of Personality Assessment* 46(1), 72–80, DOI **10.1207/s15327752jpa4601_13**, <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16370641/>. Original 20-item scale distinguishes **approaching vs avoiding arguments**, with context/state components; neither high argumentativeness nor conflict frequency is necessarily hostile or morally bad.
- Important contested validity: Infante, Rancer & Wigley (2011), *In Defense of the Argumentativeness and Verbal Aggressiveness Scales*, DOI **10.1080/01463373.2011.563439**, <https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/01463373.2011.563439> argues for the scales, but Levine, Kotowski, Beatty & Van Kelegom (2012), *A Meta-Analysis of Trait–Behavior Correlations in Argumentativeness and Verbal Aggression*, DOI **10.1177/0261927X11425037**, <https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0261927X11425037> disputes predictive/behavioral correspondence, finding sparse and inconsistent scale-behavior convergence in reviewed studies.

**Mismatch:** the original Chinese phrase 好忿争 connotes quarrelsome/contentious tendencies, but our question about escalation in disagreements is event opportunity-dependent. Infante–Rancer trait argumentativeness is debate approach/avoidance, not identical to verbal aggression or everyday quarrel frequency. **Next:** distinguish argument seeking vs hostility, consider contemporaneous event logging or confidential blinded other-reports; do not rely on an uncritical single trait score or disregard contested validation results.

### Voluntary helping — Tian Liang

- Carlo & Randall (2002), *The Development of a Measure of Prosocial Behaviors for Late Adolescents*, *Journal of Youth and Adolescence* 31, 31–44, DOI **10.1023/A:1014033032440**, institutional repository <https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/psychfacpub/70/>. Its Prosocial Tendencies Measure (PTM) distinguishes **six** forms: altruistic, compliant, emotional, dire, public and anonymous. Validation samples were late adolescents/college students (249, followed by 40 for retest), not general global adult population.
- A subsequent German PTM-R measurement invariance/reliability study with 1,067 participants, *Measuring Prosocial Tendencies in Germany* (2017), <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5723663/>, found support for six factors and English/German comparison. Cultural/linguistic generalizability beyond studied groups must still be independently examined.

**Mismatch:** 好济施 could include material aid, emergency help, financial generosity or compassionate service. Our question about spontaneous help without return is only one opportunity-dependent slice; income, dependency and social-desirability concerns can dominate. **Next:** select declared PTM-like relevant subscales or behavioral diary outcomes, verify permission and cross-cultural interpretation; do not convert 'good character' into a presumed clinical or moral trait.

### Competition and status striving — Po Jun

- Houston, Harris, McIntire & Francis (2002), *Revising the Competitiveness Index Using Factor Analysis*, *Psychological Reports* 90(1), 31–34, DOI **10.2466/pr0.2002.90.1.31**, <https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.2466/pr0.2002.90.1.31>, PubMed <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11899003/>. Revises a 20-item binary measure into a 14-item 5-point scale with two reliable subscales, **Enjoyment of Competition** and **Contentiousness**, based originally on 213 undergraduates. This is not an objective future competition-event measure.

**Mismatch/dependency:** the source 好争强 may mean seeking superiority or assertive rivalry, but an item about winning games misses non-game workplace status striving. The index's contentiousness overlaps the Lian Zhen argument item, creating a clear **correlated-measurement/dependency risk** if both were counted as independent chart evidence. **Next:** predeclare subscales and their correlations/dependency cap, ensure real activities opportunity and domain are measured, then use external controls and holdout validation, not just high subscale scores.

## Transfer decision and remaining work

**Decision: reuse/adapt**, not invent a new general psychometric scale. Reuse already-present repository person-level blinded score/rank/permutation implementation as an engineering baseline; adapt an appropriately validated measurement battery **only after** source-literal scope, construct mapping, real-world cultural/translation context, license/permission, accessibility, data protection, and birth-chart mechanism are reviewed. The initial four invented single questions remain explicitly **synthetic engineering fixtures**. Neither an official copyrighted scale nor its scoring key has been copied into the prototype.

There are at least four important failure mechanisms before any frozen human confirmatory test: (1) ancient source wording and working English gloss not critically translated; (2) possible influence of other stars, astrological branch conditions and inherited birth calendar/time rules not computed; (3) opportunity and social desirability bias and scale/translation measurement invariance; (4) overlap between argumentativeness/competitiveness and other traits, which must be dependency-adjusted. No validated behavioral probabilities exist; do not treat 0–4 source-support toy values as probabilities or call the engineered toy permutation p-value human evidence.

**Next required independent steps before G04/G05 pass:** consult full original scale methods where accessible; measure/review actual construct fit and use rights before selecting final questions; predeclare item missing/not-applicable handling and any multimethod respondent/observer corroboration; use separate development data to estimate answer marginals and calibrate score/weights; then freeze a verified birth-derived chart model and study packet BEFORE reading untouched consented respondents. Historical source counts and full original Life-palace coverage alone do not justify skipping these gates.
