## Quality boundaries

The ODG's strengths include chronological windows, actual activity versus hypothetical preference, opportunity/duration examples, missing != no, evidence notes, answer-freeze separation and future outcomes apart from completed personality. Defects include arbitrary absolute thresholds, Western salaried-work bias, $ aesthetic-spend proxy, forced A/B, five-factor ranking, ambiguous "creative" exemplars, external-label conflation, duplication, DOB-revealing reference windows and *not yet observable* future predictions. For personality, prefer repeated tendencies + variability, not automatically the most recent event. Historical events can aid interpretation but do not establish stable personality by themselves.
