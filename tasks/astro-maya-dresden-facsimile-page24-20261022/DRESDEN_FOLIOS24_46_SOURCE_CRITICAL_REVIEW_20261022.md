## What is actually known about the text — and what is not

**Direct image evidence only:** original glyph and numeral layouts, visual organization, red dating symbols, ritual/weapon-like painted scenes on the first Venus leaf; **zero** independently translated glyphic source claims or verified personal birth-label predictions. Full source/media witness and failure boundaries are in `DRESDEN_VENUS_P24_P46_VISUAL_SOURCE_WITNESS_V1.json`.

**Separate independent professional interpretations:** SLUB classifies pages 24 and 46–50 as Venus tables dealing with a schematic **584-day Venus synodic cycle** over **104 Haab vague years**, alongside dangerous ritual imagery around Venus morning-star rise; that institutional source is an *interpretation of the original*, not a verbatim glyph transcript. A published academic review of Venus pages reconstructs 236+90+250+8 days for the four phases; later specialists have changed interpretations and caution that the whole glyphic reading/actual use remains debated. Vail and Hernández (2013, quoted at dresdencodex.org) describe p24 as a **preface with calendar anchor and table-multiple/correction entries**, whereas pages46–50 are the repeating table proper.

**Mathematical consistency only (not decipherment):** the published reconstruction has
`5 × 584 = 2,920 = 8 × 365` days for a five-Venus-cycle block;
`13 × 2,920 = 37,960 = 65 × 584 = 104 × 365 = 146 × 260` days for the complete canonical repetition.
The **104 “years” means 365-day Haab cycles, not 104 tropical Gregorian years**. These integer relations are arithmetic, not evidence that all glyphs agree with a given modern ephemeris. They should be independently verified against a second calendar source before computation is promoted.

**Interpretive conflict:** Gerardo Aldana's 2016 `Discovering Discovery: Chich’en Itza, the Dresden Codex Venus Table and 10th Century Mayan Astronomical Innovation` gives a new decipherment of p24, placing corrections in a specific historical context and reporting lower model precision than some earlier interpretations. Independent decipherments must be preserved rather than selecting the most accurate retrospective model. Secondary source https://escholarship.org/uc/item/6cr1s6jd . Related published 2010 calendar reconstruction https://www.scielo.org.mx/scielo.php?pid=S0185-25742010000100002&script=sci_arttext .

This page **is not a modern personality birth-chart**. Venus ritual timing and hazardous augury classes are time/event/collective source claims, not directly supported individual psychological questionnaire domains. Even if the calendrical arithmetic is correct, that alone says nothing about divination's real-world predictive power. **No Mayan original natal personality rule, no direct Maya calendar birth feature, no real-human forward/reverse blind test, and no runtime promotion are accepted here.**
