# Continue a previously completed Life Patterns interview

After the existing Life Patterns Interview GPT has been updated by its owner:

In the original completed ChatGPT interview conversation, type the @ symbol, select **Life Patterns Interview**, and write:

**Continue my completed interview. Please use the answers already in this conversation. Do not start a new questionnaire; ask only material, nonrepeating clarifications admitted by independent review.**

If that original chat is unavailable, open the updated GPT as a **normal saved chat**, attach the completed edited questionnaire DOCX or source-only imported JSON, and say:

**Please import every question and answer from the attached completed survey, preserving its edited-document provenance. Review it first, without re-interviewing me. Ask me only for missing research consent if needed, followed by genuinely useful independent-review clarifications.**

You may skip any clarification, correct an old answer, pause or stop. Do not supply birth dates, times, places, charts or anticipated results during the behavioral review. The GPT should be able to tell you how many existing answers it recovered. Only the later independent quality-review step determines whether any new answer is actually necessary.

The GPT may show an external-service permission card. For an Action that sends your responses to the research reviewer, choose **Allow once** only if you want your completed record transmitted; declining leaves the local saved source unchanged.
