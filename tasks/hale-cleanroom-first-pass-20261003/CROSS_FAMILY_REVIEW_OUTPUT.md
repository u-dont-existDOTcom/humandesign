## 1. Calculation and method errors

**Numerology arithmetic: all correct.** I re-checked Life Path 7, Birthday 28/1, Attitude 29/11/2, Expression 9, Soul Urge 8, Personality 1, Maturity 16/7, the current-name figures 8/7/1, Pinnacles 2/6/8/6 with ages 29/38/47, Challenges 0/4/4/4, Periods 1/1/5 with the 2024 transition, every Personal Year, and every transit letter and Essence for ages 27–43. The only issues are conventions:
- **Mental transit fallback.** With no middle name, the surname supplies both the Mental and Spiritual letters. That counts BAYSAN twice in every Essence.
- **Master numbers reduced.** Attitude is 29/11, and Personal Years 2025 and 2034 are also 11 before reduction. Any reading of "11" has been removed by convention.
- **Karmic debt 16 on Maturity.** Not every school flags karmic debt on Maturity.
- Y in BAYSAN is counted as a consonant. That is correct, because it follows the vowel A.

**Astrology. The sidereal conversion checks out** (Lahiri about 23.78°), and so do the night-chart call, the tropical whole-sign houses and the transit/progression dates I spot-checked. The problems:

1. **Probable fingerprint miscode.** For `lilly/lord:10/benefic_conjunction = 0`: the MC is in Leo, so the 10th lord is the Sun, and the Sun is conjunct Venus at 2.6°. That should score 1, unless the rule excludes conjunctions where the benefic is combust. The fingerprint is probably 2/6, not 1/6.
2. **Combustion is missing from every strength table.** Venus is 2.6° from the Sun, so it is combust in all three traditions. Mars is 7.95° from the Sun: combust for Lilly (8.5° limit) and Jyotish (17°), and under the beams in Hellenistic terms. Mercury is 15.6° away, so under the beams. Mars's strength (exaltation, sect, Ruchaka-type) is overstated as listed.
3. **Sect is only scored when it helps.** In a night chart Saturn and Jupiter are both contrary to sect. Saturn, the strongest planet by Lilly (13), is the out-of-sect malefic in the Hellenistic reading. The packet hides this real disagreement between traditions.
4. **Lilly's atomic flags don't match his table.** In Lilly's table the 3rd house scores **+1**, not −1; it is the Moon's joy. The atomic lists also leave out peregrine status, combustion and orientality, so the native totals can't be reproduced. For example, the Moon gets +5 for being angular, −5 for being peregrine in Leo, and further adjustments must explain the total of 0.
5. **The tight-aspect list leaves out the angles.** The Moon is conjunct the MC (0.56°) and Jupiter squares the MC (0.92°). With Sun and Venus opposite in Aquarius, this forms a **fixed T-square** with Jupiter at the apex and the empty leg at about 12–13° Taurus. The packet never names it.
6. **Natal Jupiter and Saturn are left out as transit targets.** This misses (all derived, verify):
   - the Saturn return, around early March 2023, single pass;
   - Uranus opposite Jupiter in mid-2021 to early 2022, which falls on the T-square's empty leg;
   - Saturn opposite Jupiter around April 2029;
   - Pluto square Jupiter in 2031–32;
   - the Jupiter return around 2029–30.
7. **Jyotish timing is absent** (no Vimshottari dasha), so the packet can't claim timing agreement across traditions.
8. **Fragile placements:**
   - Sidereal Mercury at 29.56° Capricorn is 0.44° from Aquarius. Other ayanamsas, such as Raman, put it in Aquarius, in the 5th house.
   - Venus is about 1.8° before the Regiomontanus IC, so a few minutes of birth-time error flips it between the 3rd and 4th houses.
   - The Lot of Fortune (derived) is at 0°09′ Taurus, so its sign flips with under a minute of birth-time error.

**Angle- or house-sensitive (minutes of birth time matter):**

8. **The private and domestic sphere carries weight.** The 4th house is stacked in both systems: Aquarius in tropical whole-sign, Capricorn in sidereal. This matches the retreat_privacy and sanctuary_sensory votes.
9. **Jyotish with a Libra lagna (derived):**

10. **Null:** emotion_permeability, autonomy_direction, role_projection and similar domains have no testimony. Leave them null.
