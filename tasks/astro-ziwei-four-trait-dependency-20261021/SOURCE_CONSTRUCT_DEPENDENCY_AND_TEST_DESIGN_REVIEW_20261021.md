## What the source claims support, and what they do not

The exact accepted first Life-palace text with the four original wordings 性刚果决, 好忿争, 好济施 and 好争强 contains **multiple direct textual analogues** for the proposed behavioral constructs. Exact original helping wording 好济施 appears for BOTH main stars Tai Yang and Tian Liang, and a similar but reversed 好施济 in auxiliary You Bi. The word 果决 appears for Wu Qu, in a Wen Qu passage **only when named co-stars accompany it in a favorable house**, and in Qing Yang's auxiliary description with temple/grade context; the latter Qing Yang lines have original raw source SHA evidence but have not yet been accepted into a source registry for a full auxiliary chart algorithm. Lian Zhen's quarrel phrase differs from a stronger daily-disputes statement conditional on auxiliary Qing Yang. Po Jun's general competition phrase differs from a historical multi-malefic fallen-palace statement about fighting/illness; those health terms cannot become medical claims. See the independently accepted lexical and exact natal pair-grade source reports at source Sha pins in the JSON.

This dependency study maps exactly **11 prespecified historical phrase occurrences** into textual context, with five explicitly identified source overlaps/dependencies. No English word equivalence is certified; “generosity,” “decisiveness,” “argument,” “competitiveness” and aggressive violence are different or overlapping constructs and may measure different things across language/culture. The two adjacent source phrases on aggression and historical fighting/disease are *explicitly separated from the four questionnaire targets* rather than quietly counted as evidence for contentiousness/competition.

Prior source original natal grade texts: **15 same-palace historic pair-grade rules** with 13 unordered star pairs matched 20/22 selected-star co-located synthetic layouts; they mostly described social rank/wealth/fortune conditional on year stems, not quantified behavioral reversal. A suitable behavioral rulebook must not infer numeric effects or make a co-star disappear by choosing an astrological “good/bad” exception after questionnaire responses.

## Comparison of deliberately unfinished model choices using 120 NONHUMAN charts

One independent accepted 312-row source-vendor chart parity file is grouped by synthetic Gregorian date and two-hour Earthly Branch to **120 distinct artificial source chart states** (not a random birth cohort or a prevalence estimate). Only major-star layouts have been independently compared, not positions of Qing Yang/Wen Qu/You Bi.

| Development-only structural definition | 120 source-chart sample coverage |
|---|---:|
| A. Original four stars, only a sole Life-palace selected star and all raw vendor comparisons agree | **10** |
| B. At least one of initial four candidate **major** stars present in Life, independent of co-stars (unverified conditional source rules) | **34** |
| B+. B but add Tai Yang as a possible second *historical source* for helping, with day/night and temple qualifications still unresolved | **42** |

B+ adds 8 artificial configurations not covered by B. However, **2** original synthetic layouts have BOTH helping-phrase main stars Tai Yang and Tian Liang; those should contribute at most *one conceptual questionnaire target*, not be treated as two independent pieces of person-level evidence. The 120 charts break down by B+ construct appearances: **78** have zero of the four proposed constructs represented by the five main stars; **39** have one; **3** have two distinct constructs (not statistically independent scores). No construct source absence means a person lacks the trait. A/B/B+ are exploratory alternatives, NOT co-primary registered hypotheses, and none can be chosen based on outcome matches.

The broadest model is not necessarily best. It has more opportunity to generate a positive-sounding statement for almost everyone, and it does not solve the original source dignity/day-night/auxiliary/year-stem qualifications, horoscope time uncertainty, cultural measurement validity or baseline prevalence. New source discovery *before* new participants is a development model update, never a retrospectively validated prediction. Any real confirmatory study must freeze ONE selected narrow model, scoring, decoy, multiplicity corrections and all proper/questionnaire-calibrated probabilities with separate development data, then test on untouched new people.

## Current go/no-go

The linked machine-readable readiness file still has **10/10 blocking human study gates**: bounded acceptable birth years/time sources, verified independent calendar/clock/chart, original source model/conditional priority, human questionnaire mapping and instrumentation, scoring/calibration, matched chart decoys and nonastrological baseline, private consent and data policy, cryptographically frozen inputs before responses, untouched independent cohort/power, and prespecified null/negative disclosure. The existing four artificial-trait questions are only engineering fixtures; the original lexical claims require specialist translation, dependence adjustments and appropriately validated multi-item/prospective behavior measurements. No real-person recruitment is authorized from this artifact.

The present deliverable is therefore a **candidate source dependency graph** with explicit calculated-main-star versus unavailable-auxiliary features, old clinical/moral/social stereotypes excluded, all source quote IDs and branch hashes, plus the finite synthetic chart coverage. It has zero human behavior accuracy and no production model promotions. The next useful work is to complete **independent conditional source-rule validation and auxiliary placements**, draft a genuinely falsifiable limited-source forward design, and seek owner/participant consent only after all other technical/scientific gates are ready. Continuing to add source counts alone is not proof of predictive success.
