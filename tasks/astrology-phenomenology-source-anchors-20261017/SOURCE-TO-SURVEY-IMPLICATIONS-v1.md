# Traditional chart claims versus Life Patterns measurements — source audit v1

**Date:** 2026-10-17
**Status:** Source-grounded *historical-claim* audit, not an astrology validation result or questionnaire release.
**Inputs:** Ptolemy *Tetrabiblos* III.13, and the ninth/tenth-house introduction in Lilly *Christian Astrology* printed p.55 (1659 edition). The original Life Patterns V7 scenario bank and an optional eight-item experience-and-meaning pilot are compared without editing or scoring any participant answers.

## Why the questionnaire missed the owner's examples

The V7 interview was constructed primarily to distinguish **anticipated actions and decision processes in realistic scenarios**, with an AstroHD/Human Design-oriented mapping path. That is not equivalent to a **source-first inventory of natal astrology's claimed domains**. This is a methodological gap: a scenario about correcting a group plan can assess someone’s response to an error, but it does not measure their frequency of being deeply startled by life. A question about whether community service seems worthwhile cannot replace a question about whether life itself feels meaningful.

The earlier **26-domain chart-topic to V7 comparison** classified nine candidate domains absent, seven only proxy-measured, seven partially measured, two directly sampled, and one inappropriate for an ordinary personality survey. Those counts describe a hand-built first-pass list. **They do not mean 26 is the total number of things a complete natal chart tradition claims.** The earlier matrix sometimes listed a source under a related modern concept without making explicit that the modern concept was an extrapolation. This v1 ledger makes those links auditable rather than treating a related topic as a direct rule.

## What these actual texts discuss

**Ptolemy, Book III, chapter 13:** The chapter considers rational and sensory/emotional qualities through Mercury, the Moon and specific planetary configurations. It also lists religious dispositions and ritual practices; interest in mysteries, omens and interpreting dreams; philosophical/scientific inquiry; attention to the gods; art, music and beauty; and changes in one's mind or character. Some claims are explicitly conditional on which planets dominate, dignity, zodiac-sign class, stations or relative position. A single broad “spirituality planet” would misrepresent Ptolemy's method.

**Lilly, introductory ninth- and tenth-house significations, p.55:** The ninth-house list includes religious occupations, dreams and visions, books, learning and journeys. He additionally associates Jupiter in the ninth with religious devotion. The tenth-house list concerns trade, profession and public office. This page is an introductory house-topic and *horary-associated* account, not the full natal-rule inventory. The hosted Wikisource text is marked **not proofread**. Use the text to identify candidate subjects and source locations, not as complete independent proof that any one characteristic is predicted in a real person.

The reproducible ledger `PRIMARY-SOURCE-CLAIM-ANCHORS-v1.json` contains **17** anchored traditional claims across those two sources, each with a location, a required chart condition or topic, and an existing V7 coverage classification. `SOURCE-HTML-CHECK-RECEIPT-v1.json` records actual source-page readback, page digests and bounded phrase checks. One Ptolemy phrase spans an embedded printed-page label; a failed contiguous string check is **not** treated as absence of the source wording.

## Six specific measurements that remain hypotheses rather than direct translations

| Modern proposed measurement | What the checked tradition actually covers | Direct quantified rule found? |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency and intensity of **awe/wonder** | Aesthetic interests, learning, reverence and religious disposition | **No** |
| How often one is genuinely **shocked by oneself/others/world** | Curiosity, changeability, mind and character | **No** |
| **Sudden revelation** followed by changed conduct | Ordinary change of mind, versatile dispositions, intellectual inquiry | **No** |
| Feeling that a **higher power guides events** | Devotion, piety and religious/mystical practices | **No** |
| Frequency of noticing **synchronicities** | Reading/interpreting omens and dreams | **No** |
| Personal **sense of life's meaning** and perceived wider contribution | Religion, philosophical ideals, purpose-like commitments and vocation topics | **No** |

“No” means these two checked passages do not give a direct frequency/intensity rule for that modern measurement; it **does not** establish that no other classical/modern astrology author discusses it. Importantly, recognizing a meaningful coincidence is a participant's report of an experience and interpretation—not evidence of a supernatural cause.

## Recommended measurement path

Retain the new items **outside any existing participant interview** and outside any frozen chart scorer. For an optional separate pilot, begin with no more than **four** ordinary-language prompts: self-surprise (EM01), awe/wonder (EM03), sudden insight versus action change (EM04), and personal versus social meaning (EM08). Offer the others only if relevant and unanswered: surprise in others/world (EM02), felt predictability (EM05), perceived guidance (EM06), and meaningful coincidences with later reassessment (EM07).

A response of “never,” “I do not believe in such guidance,” “I only realize things gradually,” or “I do not know” is complete evidence. Separately capture *frequency*, *intensity*, *interpretation*, *later reappraisal* and *impact on actual behavior* where possible. An illustrative anecdote should be optional, never a compulsory three-example ordeal. Do not confuse vivid imagination, eventful life circumstances, religiosity and a particular sense of awe.

Before claiming any **astrological predictive ability**, a different research gate is needed: map each actually cited source rule to a calculable natal feature and a prospectively frozen direction; test against independent respondents and non-astrological controls with unknowns intact. Random chart labels, matched alternatives, known-mapping confounds, and multiple testing must be handled in advance. The same person whose experiences suggested an item cannot also supply an untouched validation win.

## Remaining corpus and version boundaries

This is a **narrow source verification pass**. It does **not** reproduce the full corpus of Lilly Book III, Dariot, Ptolemy Book IV, Hellenistic nativities, Jyotish sources, or the ongoing Lilly source-record extraction lane. Future passes should scan those source traditions by *explicit chart condition → claimed subject or event → testable observation type → existing survey coverage*, keeping natal judgements separate from introductory horary house meanings. Preserve sensitive medical, death, violence or intimate-life claims as historical research subjects, not as psychometric or clinical predictions.

The original 26-domain coarse matrix remains archival descriptive coverage; **this source-anchored v1 ledger governs what can be called an explicitly attested rule in the two checked texts**. No accepted V7, AstroHD reduced model, canonical HD V4.3 chart/mapping, live GPT, independent review or participant data was changed.
