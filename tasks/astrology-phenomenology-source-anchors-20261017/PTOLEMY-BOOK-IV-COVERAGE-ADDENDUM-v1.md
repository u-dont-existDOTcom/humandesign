# Book IV addendum — natal life outcomes versus scenario questions

**Scope:** Ptolemy, *Tetrabiblos* IV.2–IV.10; three public source-text segments. This addendum expands the two-source III.13/Lilly source audit without changing its earlier accepted entries.

Book IV treats nine additional major topics: material fortune, honor/social rank, occupation, marriage, children, friendships and hostility, foreign travel, mortality, and timing of life events. Those are **historical natal chart claims about actual life circumstances**, not automatically the same objects as questions about how someone would respond in an imagined social situation.

The original 79-question V7 interview has scenes about whether to spend money, how to manage work, whether to pursue a romantic attachment, and how to organize a trip. Those may clarify **preferences and reactions**. They do not establish someone's real financial history, livelihood, marital transitions, caregiving or travel biography. Nor is a participant obliged to disclose sensitive events merely because the original tradition addresses them.

Two particularly important boundaries follow. **First**, a chart-topic catalogue is broader than any one psychometric questionnaire and should be built source-first. **Second**, the chart's claim to depict actual life events is a different empirical hypothesis from its claim to depict subjective awe, spiritual guidance or sudden realization. These need different outcome evidence and prospective controls.

The machine-readable matrix, `PTOLEMY-BOOK-IV-LIFE-DOMAINS-v1.json`, assigns one native source chapter to each topic, records only related V7 scenario families, and flags potentially sensitive topics as noncompulsory or excluded from routine psychological interviewing. It is not a substitute for extracting the exact astrological predicates from Ptolemy's original rules, and is **not** evidence that any rule predicts an event.
