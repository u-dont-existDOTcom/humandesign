## Outcome

The original six scenario cards were not a complete behavioral-profile survey. Against the frozen first rubric they represented 8-13 of 60 information checks per fictional person and completely described only 0-2 of the 23 primary domains. An expanded scenario bank reached 14-17 primary domains. Targeted follow-ups brought all ten constructed examples to 60/60 initial checks. A subsequent broader V3.6 source audit identified 13 additional requirements; after separate persona-extension freezing and additional questions, all ten constructed examples reached 73/73 explicit checks.

The final candidate has 55 available question nodes covering 23 primary and eight supplementary behavioral areas. The ten answer-aware replays selected 51-53 questions each, 520 in total from 550 authored answer rows. This is not a mandated survey length. The remaining burden is material and human answerability is unmeasured.

Important distinctions repaired include persuasion ability versus desire, own emotional baseline versus effects of another person's mood, ordinary work pace versus bursts versus overload, external urgency versus chosen commitments, natural ability versus trained skill, and what restores trust or permits re-entry after withdrawal. Unknown, declined, inapplicable, generic normative, and process-feedback answers do not become positive trait evidence. Scenario premises do not become biography.

## Interpretation limits

These are manual fictional interviews authored and assessed in the same Chat context. Character briefs and the initial rubric were frozen before walkthroughs; the later extension was frozen separately before its answers. This is not independent or held-out evaluation, not ten live app runs, and not evidence of human questionnaire validity. Deterministic checks validate source links, routing, hashes, and selected failure cases; they do not independently establish semantic accuracy.

Coverage of all 73 explicit behavioral checks does not demonstrate exact natal AstroHD chart, date/time, planetary, center, gate, line, or authority recovery. No birth charts were assigned or decoded. The broader mapping audit and Survey-v2 recoverability protocol preserve further model-scope and decoding requirements. No inference VM contact/wake, production implementation, deployment, paid inference, or participant collection occurred.
