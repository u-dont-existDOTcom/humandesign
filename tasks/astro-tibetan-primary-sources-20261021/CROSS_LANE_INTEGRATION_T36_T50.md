## Newly separated calculational systems and outcomes

### 1. Consultation-hour and nine-sector material (T36–T37)

After the previous nine-sector mewa query chart, White Beryl associates **household** with vitality, **land** with destiny and **tombs** with luck for element comparisons against the hour. “Hour inhibits subject” and “subject inhibits hour,” along with the two enhancement directions, are distinct outcomes; five same-element categories add separate interpretations. The 5×5 geomancy lookup associates location relative to a house (center/right/rear/front/left) and the five elements with geometry, color and doors; this does **not** prescribe safe construction choices.

This chapter also uses witnessed clothing, wine/food, hairstyle, illness circumstances, house geometry, barley-vessel fill and body posture. These are **consultation-time observations**, not variables inferable from a subject's birth data. Barley half-full appears in multiple interpretive contexts; do not manufacture a single mutually exclusive predictive rule. Earlier query nine-square sectors 8/9 disagree between prose north/northeast and printed table northeast/north (existing TIB-CONFLICT-007).

### 2. Illness chapter / pine-tree systems (T38–T41)

The “nineteen pebbles” comprise 16 from natal vitality/body contrasted with current year/month/day/hour (relation plus sector for each) plus 3 calendrical markers (lunar day, weekday, lunar mansion), under historical pebble-color conventions. The eight lunar-day goddess durations sum to thirty days: six groups of four plus two groups of three.

There are **15 canonical pine-tree starting points** and **two additional school-specific variants** (Narapa and Dungpa), not seventeen equivalent core rules. Different starting animal/element years, offsets ±13 and ±5, forward/backward counting and alternate family-member substitutions require their own policy keys. The prose and embedded Moonbeams disagree on counting direction for starting point **3 (felt hat)** and **13 (gatehouse)**: TIB-CONFLICT-008/009, unresolved. The source itself reports incorrectly colored dots in the living/dying-eye illustration (editorial error TIB-PLATE-CORRECTION-010). Historical medical, demonic or ritual claims have no clinical validity and must not direct therapy, prognosis or safety decisions.

### 4. Thirty Chinese/Tibetan computational charts (T47–T48)

White Beryl Chapter26 and the pictured manuscript number **30 charts consecutively**. Its introduction proposes separate Chinese/Tibetan sets of 15 whose chart names do **not** exactly match the continuous set. *Moonbeams* presents another noncontiguous mapping: chapter indices 1–7 = Chinese 1–7; 8–14 = Tibetan 1–7; 15–22 = Chinese 8–15; 23–30 = Tibetan 8–15. The full list of 30 names/roles, the Moonbeams mapping and separate 15 Chinese/15 Tibetan animal-year/element-relation starting points are in T48; all remain witness-labeled and not conflated.

In the elaborate method there are **eight source comparison positions for each of the thirty charts (30×8=240 basic positions)**. Their directional source list covers four, seven and eleven animal-sign offsets and two identity-sector comparisons; *Moonbeams* conditions direction on **masculine/feminine birth-year polarity, not the native's sex**. Pebble-color encoding in this family is mother 3 white; friend 2 white; identity Earth/Water 1 white; son **1 white plus 1 black**; identity Wood/Iron/Fire 1 black; enemy 2 black. Pebble multiplicity means 240 base positions is NOT always the literal physical count of separate white/black stones. Royal, monastic, women's, child, household, livestock, illness, funeral and ritual chart domains vary considerably; this is not one trait-discrimination survey model.

White Beryl attributes further detail to a rare work *'Phrul-gyi mig-tshags* preserved in the Potala archive. This original has **not** been read, and source completeness for detailed prognoses therefore remains limited.

### 5. Thirteen Bal-mo lding-skor charts (T49–T50)

The simplified set uses **five ordered personal axes** per chart—vitality, body, destiny, luck, numeric square—so **13×5=65 simplified positions**. The same source reports a larger 2,920-pebble ancestry and an abridged **872=371 basic+501 symbolic** procedure; do not equate this with the distinct 2,920 public funeral counting scheme solely because its total is equal.

The color convention differs materially from the 30-chart system: **son is black** (not one white and one black); mother, friend and Earth/Water identity white; enemy and Wood/Iron/Fire identity black. T50 preserves all 13 source chart names, roles, starting animals and count directions where explicit. The chart-10 starting point and the chart-13 count direction are **unresolved**, not filled in by Chinese sign-cycle intuition. Roles include royalty, teachers, ritual specialists, adults/young people, estates, valuables and cattle.

The editor's conclusion (PDF400) stresses that full implementation of the illustrations without living oral instruction is exceptionally difficult, and Moonbeams explicitly leaves destructive/hostile techniques to other sources. Neither statement proves predictive efficacy, and the latter is not permission to invent missing rules.
