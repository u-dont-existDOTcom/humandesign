He then assigns possible instigators to neighbors, relatives, hired servants, drink-sellers, nurses and other categories, or invokes divine power. The source gives **no independently identified witch, afflicted patient, clinical record, accused person, actual supernatural event, or tested geographic identification**.

**Historical physical-risk material.** His remedies subsection discusses dangerous domestic rituals involving fire/heat, household objects, urine and other bodily samples, cattle hair and ashes, and lunar-phase conditions. These practices can cause burns, contamination, fire or unnecessary animal handling and must not be interpreted as verified remedies. Lilly asserts repeated experiments and a sympathy theory but supplies no controls, follow-ups or independently authenticated clinical outcomes.
