# B02s lesson disposition

- Historical source wording, original-volume errata and handwritten marginalia stay separate.
- Avoid blame inversion in claims about sexual conduct: distinguish deceived from culpable, preserve explicit denial and nonconsensual branches. These are documentary source distinctions, not validated assessments of people.
- One printed scope-extension sentence near a chapter end requires its own unit when source completeness is the owner's outcome.
- Maintain both independent first-pass review windows and informed follow-up provenance; do not mislabel the latter as blind evidence.
- Existing Universal Development Architecture source-provenance rules already cover these; no new universal promotion required.
