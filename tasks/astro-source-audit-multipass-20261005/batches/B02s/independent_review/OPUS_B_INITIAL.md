# Lilly 1647, Ch. XLIX: review of RULES.json (B02s, records R2009–R2107) against the page images

**Result:** RULES.json misstates the source in several places. The worst problems are a wrong zodiac sign in R2028, wrong outcomes in R2018, an invented contrast in R2048, and several dropped clauses that protect the woman: denials that anything happened, a force clause, a free-will clause, and Lilly's warnings about false reports. The list below has 53 numbered issues.

I transcribed all 11 designated images before opening RULES.json. I looked at no other files.

## B. Issues

### PDF344 / p. 310

1. **[Med] R2009.**
   - *Problem:* The sentence starts on p. 309 (PDF343, not provided), so "Saturn" cannot be checked. The other half of the rule is about the Lord of the seventh being afflicted, not a planet in the seventh: "judge the like if the Lord of the seventh suffer the same afflictions, that then the quesited shall be the occasion of strife."
   - *Fix:* Add PDF343 to the locator, flagged as unverified. Rephrase as the Lord of the seventh suffering the same afflictions, so the quesited causes the strife.
2. **[Med] R2010.**
   - *Problem:* A clause is missing: "♄ ♂ or ☋ in the ascendant, idem, if the question be asked by the man."
   - *Fix:* Add it, keeping ☋ and the condition that the man asks.
3. **[Med] H1 has no heading record.**
   - *Problem:* There is no record for "Who shall be the cause of their Strife, or the authour of their Good." Because of this, R2018 (gifts) is filed as a "disagreement" rule.
   - *Fix:* Add a heading record and re-file R2018 under the "Good" part of this subsection.
4. **[Med] R2011.**
   - *Problem:* A condition is missing: "and be in the ascendant or seventh house."
   - *Fix:* Add it.
5. **[Med-High] R2013.**
   - *Problem:* "In the fourth" carries on from "an Infortune in the tenth". It means an infortune placed in the fourth house, not an affliction of the fourth house's ruler. The record also drops "either a Divorcement".
   - *Fix:* "Infortune in 4th → Divorcement, or willingness to it, or hindrance in Dowry."
6. **[Med] R2017.**
   - *Problem:* The source says "if the ☽ doe aspect, *or* be in ☌ with ♄ or ♂", so any aspect counts, not only conjunction. The man/woman split also only applies "if this ☌ be in the tenth or fourth".
   - *Fix:* Add the aspect alternative and the 10th/4th condition.
7. **[High] R2018.**
   - *Problem:* The record reverses the square's meaning and invents an opposition rule. The source says "☽ in □ of good Planets, by dead men", which means gifts come by way of dead men, not "harder gain or quarrels". The record also drops "☽ in ☌ of good Planets, promises good by their owne industry and labour".
   - *Fix:* △/⚹ of good planets → gifts from friends; □ → by dead men; ☌ → by their own industry.
8. **[Low] R2019.**
   - *Problem:* The record loses the hedge and an alternative: "notes a probability of separation or long disagreements".
   - *Fix:* Keep both.
