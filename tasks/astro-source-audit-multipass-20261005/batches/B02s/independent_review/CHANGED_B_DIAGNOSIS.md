## Outstanding HIGH and MEDIUM errors, with narrow corrections

- **H1. R2064 (PDF349, p. 315, lines 9–13).**
  - *Problem:* The record still drops "though the woman love some martial man, **yet he cannot bring her under his Yoak**". It also adds "rather than willingly guilty", which is not printed and implies something happened.
  - *Fix:* Restore the printed sentence and delete the invented phrase.
- **H2. R2120 (PDF347, p. 313).**
  - *Problem 1:* "hath been formerly **deluded**, if she be with the ☊" (she was deceived) has become "earlier alleged error" (her own fault).
  - *Problem 2:* "she hath formerly offended, and is still guilty, nor will she amend hereafter" has been softened to "continued suspicion".
  - *Problem 3:* The print says "the same may be said of ♂, if he be in place of the ☋; yet ♂ imposeth not so much malice". So Mars stands in for ☋ with less malice; it does not soften ☋.
  - *Fix:* Restore all three as printed. Keep the print's tension with R2121 rather than smoothing it over.
- **M1. R2010 (p. 310).**
  - *Problem:* The repair replaced the old content instead of adding to it. "the ☽ in her fall, or at □ or ☍ with ♄ or ♂, or any Retrograde Planet, if the ☽ then behold the ascendant, noteth brawling ever moved by the woman" is now in no record.
  - *Fix:* Restore it as its own record, and flag that "idem" has an ambiguous referent.
- **M2. R2021 (p. 310, last lines).**
  - *Problem:* The print says "if he be a **heavy Planet**", meaning a slow, superior planet. The record turns this into "heavily afflicted".
  - *Fix:* Use "heavy Planet".
- **M3. R2041 (p. 312).**
  - *Problem:* It drops "fixed and well disposed, it signifieth **she is a Virgin**" and "or evil Planets be in fixed Signes beholding them". It also says every configuration leads "only" to a doubt.
  - *Fix:* Restore both clauses. "A doubt of Legerdemain" belongs only to the adverse branches.
- **M4. R2067 and R2126 (p. 315).**
  - *Problem:* "**♂ Lord of the seventh**, or ☽ Lady of the seventh, in ♈ or ♏" means Mars *is* the seventh lord. Both records read it as Mars *with* the seventh lord.
  - *Fix:* Restate the condition with Mars as the seventh lord.
- **M5. R2075 (p. 316).**
  - *Problem:* "or with ☊, idem" (she hath none) is missing. The record says node cases are "handled distinctly", but no record handles this one.
  - *Fix:* Add the clause.
- **M6. R2119 (p. 313).**
  - *Problem:* The trigger "If the angles be fixed Signes, and the Lord of the ascendant or ☽ in moveable or common" is missing, and so is "**is still tempted**". "Declined" is not printed.
  - *Fix:* Add the trigger and "is still tempted"; remove "declined".
- **M7. R2066 (p. 315).**
  - *Problem:* It drops "perchance the woman had a Lover **before she knew her Husband**". In this adultery section that timing clears her.
  - *Fix:* Restore it.
