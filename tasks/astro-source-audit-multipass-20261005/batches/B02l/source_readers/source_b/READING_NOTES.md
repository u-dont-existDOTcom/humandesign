## Coverage and the whole argument

### Side and member: B001-B006

The section contains several different constructions, not one clean two-sided lookup. The first assigns both right and upper body to an unfortunate/afflicted sixth lord above the earth, explicitly enumerating houses 12 through 7. The next says under the earth, houses 1 through 6, **or** vitiated in a diurnal sign, and still prints **superiour and fore-part**. The nocturnal-sign branch assigns the back part with elided conditions. The feminine configuration has two planetary actors and a sign/house qualification on the second actor. The first-person masculine/above-earth rule adds a different requirement from the initial general rule.

The upper/middle/lower mapping concerns **that Member**, rather than an automatic division of the whole body. **Few**, **middle**, and **lower degrees** have no stated numeric bins. The source does not license decans, three equal thirds, or replacing lower with later or smaller without an explicit interpretation. Whether the degree mapping inherits the preceding masculine/above-earth conditions is left visible.

### Body, mind, or both: B007-B024

The preliminary assignments distinguish spirit, internal/external members, and animal faculties. Later rules use overlapping but nonidentical sets: Ascendant, Sun, Moon, lords of the luminaries' houses/signs, the Moon's dispositor, the Ascendant lord, and the Ascendant lord's dispositor. Their identity and degree-versus-ruler distinctions matter. The whole argument cannot be preserved by collapsing everything to three generic planet scores.

The phrasing **or two of them at least** leaves a quantifier problem. The Saturn list has alternative rulerships, lunar separation, placements, and aspects; these are not cumulative requirements. The following planet is visibly Jupiter and the body-only assertion says **never** of the mind. The Mars rule visibly includes a sextile, while the twelfth-house phrase has unresolved attachment. The comparison rule has both directions but no metric or tie rule. The tormented-soul condition has both a punctuation problem in the debility list and an omitted subject for **and free**. The compressed Sun/lord clause and the later Mercury pronoun switch are likewise preserved.

Mental, bodily, moral, and natural-philosophical vocabulary remains historical language. It is not translated into modern diagnoses, measured clinical endpoints, personality labels for actual people, or medical causal claims.

### Nativity, eclipse, and luminaries: B025-B029

The natal callback is explicitly conditional on having the patient's nativity. Degree/sign matching and **at time of the sicknesse or neere it** are not assigned an exact orb or exact time window. The annual **revolution of the World** is a distinct reference frame; its eighth-house sign must not be replaced by a natal or question eighth house. The next rule uses a sign rising at first falling sick **or** demand which contained an infortune **in the nativity**; a current infortune in the Ascendant is not an equivalent condition.

The close Moon-Sun statement requires **not above six degrees** and a **not-yet-passed/not-yet-exactly-conjoined** phase. Aries or Leo reduces rather than abolishes the asserted misfortune. The twelve-degree statement is separately recorded as **little danger**, not no danger, a guarantee, or a greater-than-or-equal threshold. No interpolation for six-to-twelve degrees or probability is supplied.

### Crisis: B030-B037

The timing method prioritizes the first hour the patient **took his bed**, as nearly as obtainable. Only if unavailable does it substitute the first hour when physician judgment was required. Rectifying the Moon to that hour is an instruction without a full computation or precision definition here.

The 45-, 90-, and 135-degree alteration rule is explicitly for disease **not chronick, but acute** and says **neer upon** those times. It does not supply 180 degrees, all multiples of 45, an exact clinical event time, or chronic applicability. The goodness or badness of the crisis further depends on the lunar aspect and its target. The negative branch's increased pain and little medicine benefit continue across the page boundary and are included.

The later first-person adverse-aspect paragraph claims no remembered failure but gives no denominator or case log. The favorable-aspect clause describes what the narrator **pronounces** to the patient. That communication is not an independently recorded recovery. The final solar-aspect clause has a negative exception concerning power/dominion in the disease, with an unclosed parenthesis and uncertain pronoun. Its claimed effect is an enlightened mind, not a bodily cure.

### Recovery timing: B038-B045

Recovery is already expected before timing is estimated. The method gathers aspect distance, houses of both bodies, signs of both bodies, disease quality, and discretion. A movable-sign aspect ordinarily uses days; swift motion **and** angularity increase the narrator's certainty. For common signs he explicitly withholds a mechanical choice of months, weeks, or days until considering the nature and possible duration of the disease.

The report of the Ancients preserves **weeks or months** and **months or years** as alternatives. House/sign equivalences do not specify an override policy when indicators conflict. The top of PDF302 further requires the Moon's speed, sign, and quality to be mixed with the principal significators. No weighting or priority formula emerges from the complete argument. The degree measure itself is not explicitly defined as raw longitude distance or distance to aspect perfection.

The ingress report requires the Ascendant lord to leave its **question-time sign** and enter a sign where it has essential dignities. Its outcome is recovery **or** a sensible improvement. The separate sixth-cusp example uses **28 degrees and a common sign**, and predicts the disease will **vary in less than two weeks**. It is not a general >=28-degree threshold, a guarantee of recovery, or a dated case.

### Full upper bridge: B046-B047

The remaining paragraph promises clearer explanation through a figure or two in the later practical part. These are forward dependencies, not charts actually present in the owned span. It then explains the desire to give readers more variety of judgment, contrasts the burden of sickness with the delight of health, and claims to have translated and inserted the Hermes Iatromathematicks, described as esteemed and necessary to judgments of this house. All of that text through **House** is included before the horizontal divider.
