## Qualifications that govern the reconstruction

- Application versus separation in the second-house example distinguishes present lack of money from recent damage. Magnitude is tied to the afflicting planet and aspect; opposition is worse **herein**, not declared universally worse in every judgment.
- Strong essential dignity, swift motion, and a good house support the outward-accident judgment. The Saturn/Mars square/opposition branch excludes sixth-lord and Moon-dispositor roles and retains “not well, yet not perfectly sick.”
- Poison suspicion in the Saturn passage is conditional and modal. The ninth-lord/sixth-house occult account also requires pre-existing mistrust. Neither passage supplies proof of poisoning, witchery, or a modern diagnosis.
- “Usually,” “may,” “more assuredly,” “almost,” “some,” “many times,” and “ordinarily” remain meaningful. Conversely, the absolute claim that Jupiter “never” oppresses the mind is recorded as an absolute source claim, not silently softened.
- Body/mind rules distinguish lights and ascendant places from their rulers and dispositors. Comparatives such as “more in the Minde then in the Body” do not mean mind only. Explicit negatives such as “not in the Body” cannot be omitted.
- Moon within no more than six degrees **before** Sun conjunction is the adverse condition; Aries/Leo lessens it. The separate twelve-degree statement says “little danger,” with no stated direction or “at least” threshold.
- Crisis timing prefers the first bed-taking hour as closely as obtainable, with the first request for a physician’s judgment as fallback. The 45/90/135-degree rule is for **acute, not chronic**, disease and is approximate in event timing. No 180-degree interval or fixed calendar conversion is supplied.
- Crisis judgments distinguish deterioration and poor medicine effect, comfort or an interval of ease, and mental relief. These are not interchangeable with death, permanent cure, or quantified drug efficacy.
- Recovery timing starts only after recovery has already been judged. Lilly ordinarily uses degrees as days in movable signs, with more certainty when swift and angular, but explicitly uses discretion for common signs. The ancients’ common-sign weeks/months and fixed-sign months/years are alternatives, not a compulsory single unit.
- The final paragraph requires synthesis with Moon motion/sign/quality. A dignity-changing ascendant lord may coincide with recovery **or** felt benefit. The 28-degree common-sign **sixth-cusp** example says the disease will **vary** in **less than two weeks**; it does not expressly promise recovery.
