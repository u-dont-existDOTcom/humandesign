## Historical source outcome separation

First Lilly speculates on whether reported dreams correspond to future real events. A Jupiter **or** Venus occupying the ninth house, potent and unafflicted, is alleged to indicate true/corresponding dreams. Infortunes occupying ninth allegedly indicate deceitful/untrue dreams. The author explicitly permits **ambiguous dreams, sometimes true and sometimes false**, when nominal benefics are weak or afflicted; he says no belief/credit should follow from such uncertain dreams. With **no ninth-house planets**, the ninth cusp sign and ruler form a fallback, not a presumption that the person never dreams.

An internal unnumbered subsection *Of the quality of Dreames* then assigns **subject matter**, not accuracy, by planetary signifier: Saturn agriculture, digging, caverns, treasure, burials, fears and beings called devils/spirits; Jupiter church/gentry/honours; Mars contention, war, horses, fire, dogs and hunting; Sun money and magistracy; Venus merriment, garments, young people, pictures and scents; Mercury books, travel, accounts, writing, money and children; Moon water, navigation, sea dangers and flying. The Moon joined by Saturn is separately alleged to intensify frightening dreams. These categories overlap and cannot be treated as precise psychological diagnoses or supernatural observations.

**Critical distinction:** the dreamer's remembered content, the dreamer's belief in a dream, independently confirmed later real-life event, and objective prediction of an unobserved event are different test endpoints. The source contains no independent prospective dated dream reports, target registry, time window, matched comparator or false-prediction denominator. The dream-symbol material also cannot establish a real person's sleep disorder, psychiatric condition, spirituality or occult ability.
