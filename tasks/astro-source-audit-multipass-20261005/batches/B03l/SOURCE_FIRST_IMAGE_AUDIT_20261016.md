## Chronology discrepancy and disaggregated outcome

**Lilly's original sentence on PDF436 literally prints `27th of April, 1644`,** whereas the original horary figure is `17. Apr. 1643`. Independent Civil War sources describe Reading surrender on **27 April 1643**, not 1644. This strongly supports a historic printing error in the surrender sentence, but the original witness stays `1644` and the independently supported interpretation stays `1643`, in separate fields. Original date is NEVER edited to force an exact prediction.

Lilly also explicitly says the town was delivered **three days after the time he had limited**, although treaty talks began around eight days after the question. A forecast of possession of Reading is **not** scored as an on-time success because negotiations began near the deadline. Separately, historically documented agreement, surrender/withdrawal, and contemporary claims about munitions are not one undifferentiated outcome.
