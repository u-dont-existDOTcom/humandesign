## Historical content and interpretive hazards

The author's intellect rules are highly conditional. Mercury and Moon are treated as paired significators, with allegedly favorable conjunction or sextile/trine, distinct unfavorable no-aspect/opposition cases, and a **square treated as having some wit but a rougher fancy**. Relative planetary strength, house and sign, Moon's waxing condition, reception, nodes, Mercury's speed/orientality/combustion and dispositor are described as modifiers. Mercury mixtures with Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus and Moon produce sometimes contrasting traits.

The text also applies hostile moral and cognitive stereotypes to various astrological conditions, including derogatory labels, purported speech impairment and claims concerning intelligence. Its premodern soul/brain and four-humour physiology is not neuroscience. None of these can responsibly diagnose cognitive disability, assess a person's intelligence or character, or establish medical predictions.
