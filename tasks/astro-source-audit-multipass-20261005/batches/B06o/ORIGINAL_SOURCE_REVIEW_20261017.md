## Substantive source and falsification boundaries

Lilly explicitly changes the principal *men's-marriage* natal indicators Venus/Moon to **Sun/Mars** for women while retaining seventh house, ruler and occupants. He says to consider barren/fruitful/double-bodied signs, benefic/malefic nature, strength and house placements before judging marriage. Yet he later explicitly uses **Venus in seventh with Saturn fourth**, **Sun with Moon in masculine signs**, and **Venus together with a dispositor in common/tropic signs** for specific outcomes. These are not mistakes to repair by applying the initial Sun/Mars replacement globally; they are original-source exceptions.

The occurrence of a marriage is a separate claimed target from desire, purported willingness, speed or ease, and alleged period gender-norm judgments. The author proposes favorable fertility/strong/benefic configurations versus adverse or barren/weak/malefic testimony; then uses awkward old stereotypes about women refusing marriage or becoming allegedly refractory.
