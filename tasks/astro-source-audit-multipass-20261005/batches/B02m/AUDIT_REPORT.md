## 1. A missing “no” reversed a source statement

The Scorpio/Saturn passage says **“no retention of Urine.”** The recovered draft instead recorded urinary retention. The corrected record preserves the negative, alongside the same passage’s bladder-stone, swelling and discharge descriptions. This correction establishes what the page says; it does not settle the intended modern physiological interpretation or diagnose a person. [PDF310 / printed276; R1684; TEXTUAL_CHECKS.json.]

## 2. This section does not use one interchangeable chart context

The opening discusses conception and birth, including planetary assignments to bodily members. It later changes to first illness or lying down. A request for the physician’s judgment is a fallback when the precise original onset hour cannot be known, not a free choice among times. The conception/birth and illness-question material therefore need separate applicability conditions. [PDF302–304 / printed268–270; R1651–R1664.]

The local sensory table assigns the right eye to the Sun, left eye to the Moon, hearing and ears to Saturn, brain to Jupiter, blood to Mars, smell and taste to Venus, and the tongue and throat/lung-pipe wording to Mercury. It does not print a sex-dependent eye reversal here. These assignments are historical source assertions, not modern physiological facts. [PDF303 / printed269; R1652; REFERENCE_TABLES.json.]

## 3. Lunar speed, change in speed, light and aspect are separate conditions

The introductory passage asks whether the Moon is swift and increasing in light, whether both conditions coincide, or neither. Another passage says **decreasing in light and motion**. “Decreasing motion” is not silently replaced with “below mean speed”; the latter numerical comparison is not supplied here. The Saturn sign catalogue inherits its own decreasing-light-and-motion condition, while the Mars/Aquarius row specifies slow and waning, and Mars/Pisces increasing light and swift motion. [PDF304–305,307,315; R1665–R1669, R1676, R1700–R1701.]

A severe Saturn rule has an explicit exception: disease vigour remits immediately after **opposition to Saturn**. The text does not say that remission after any Saturn contact is equivalent. Likewise, the earlier Mars rule requires the post-Sun-conjunction sequence, increasing light and motion, a Mars square or opposition before the Sun opposition, and no intervening benevolent aspect. Counting “Mars square” alone would lose the operative restrictions. [PDF304–305 / printed270–271; R1666–R1668.]

## 5. Opposing medicine recommendations must remain visible

On printed272, certain fevers and lung disorders are attributed to Saturn and Mercury, followed by a recommendation for refrigerating medicines. On printed273, disease origins assigned to Saturn or Mercury are instead given heating and softening remedies. Both passages are retained; neither the planets nor the remedy polarity is silently changed to create consistency. The Mars/Scorpio row also contains its own heating/comfort prescription rather than simply repeating the broad Mars-cooling rule.
