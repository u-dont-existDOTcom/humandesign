## Qualification and evidence limitations

- Separate original historical assignments of ascending/first-house querent, seventh-house opponent, eighth house opponent's friends, and ninth house opponent's third; no automatic symmetric modern scoring algorithm is inferred.
- The first-house malefic branch alleges poor conduct and poor grounds for war; the benefic alternative alleges justification. These are **not moral or historical findings** about any real army.
- The second-house adverse reading requires its original exception-sensitive dignity and hard-aspect conditions; the author's brief `contrary` is not expanded into unqualified victory.
- Mars in the third is contrasted by dignity: possible provisions vs original insulting allegations about soldiers. Fifth/sixth/seventh house clauses differ by their own actor and object. The fourth-house historical terrain descriptions are not recommendations for selecting terrain.
- Several original glyphs (sixth, tenth and twelfth house) are uncertain, so specific node/planet identities are unassigned; no fixed modern ephemeris or numerical orb inserted.
- An eighth-house source claim about casualties and injuries is historical-only; no clinical effectiveness or forecast data. The ninth-house beneficial planet is linked to alleged misinformation: source polarity preserved, not repaired as a positive honesty signal.
- The eleventh-house malefic passage distinctly allows *fidelity but inexperience*, so cannot be misrepresented as general treachery. Twelfth-house conditions similarly describe opposing forces but are unverified.
- The unnumbered *If the Armies shall fight* subsection orders: angular bodily union, else hard aspect, else third-planet transfer by hard aspect, followed by **weightier-planet reception exception** switching fight to reconciliation. No algorithmic weights or probabilities are supplied.
- These historical claims must not be used as real-world military intelligence, personal honesty diagnostics, conflict advice, medically predictive judgments, or proof that astrology works. Zero authenticated battle cases and zero empirical predictive tests.
