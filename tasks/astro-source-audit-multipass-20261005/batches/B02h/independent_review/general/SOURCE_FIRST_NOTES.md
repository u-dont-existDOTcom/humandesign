## A. Scope and agreement: PDF221–222

A01. The preamble assigns questions about the querent's brothers, sisters, kindred, unity/concord with them, peace with neighbors and their good/bad condition, and the prosperity of a short journey to this house. It is a topic/scope statement, not a chart-conditioned outcome.

A02. L1 signifies the asker. L3 signifies the brother, sister, or neighbor asked about.

A03. The unity/concord list is disjunctive. Lilly says any of these: L3 is a benevolent planet; OR L3 is in the Ascendant; OR a fortunate planet is in H3; OR L3 and L1 are in SX/TR within the orbs of either planet; OR L3 and L1 are in mutual reception; OR L3 casts SX/TR to the cusp of the Ascendant; OR L1 casts SX/TR to H3. The result is unity and concord between querent and the specified brother/sister/neighbor/kinsman. Do not require every favorable condition together. The explicit orb qualification belongs to the planetary L3/L1 aspect branch; it is not numerical here. The cusp-aspect directions are different branches.

A04. Fault attribution: `if a Fortune be in the Ascendant or the Lord of the Ascendant behold the cusp of the third, and the Lord of the third doe not aspect either the Ascendant, or be in aspect with the Lord thereof`, the querent is of good condition/no default; defect lies with the other person. The source's compound punctuation should remain visible: the natural reading combines one of the favorable querent indications with L3's lack of either reciprocal contact. Do not change `behold` to a specified favorable aspect, or reduce this to a Fortune-alone fault test.

A05. Saturn OR Mars OR South Node locally in the Ascendant is a historical character/fault attribution to the querent. No essential-dignity exception is given in this Ascendant clause.

A06. Saturn OR Mars OR South Node in H3 gives little good from the specified relatives/neighbors UNLESS in their own essential dignities. The expectation is worse if peregrine, retrograde, combust, OR in any malevolent configuration with any other planet. These worsening predicates are alternatives, not an all-required group. Even apparent unity at the time of the question need not continue; Lilly says hatred or grumbling `usually` arises afterward, not an unconditional temporal law.

A07. Saturn OR South Node in H3: neighbors characterized as clowns and kindred as covetous/sparing. Mars in H3: kindred treacherous and neighbors thievish. The claim is `most assuredly` when the relevant body is out of essential dignities. Preserve the scope as this historical characterization of the persons asked about.

## B. Absent brother and other absent persons: PDF223–226

B01. The question Ascendant and its lord signify the querent. Cusp H3 becomes the absent brother's Ascendant; H4 his substance house; continue in order. Lilly then tells the reader to consider L3's condition, house, aspects from good/evil planets, aspect type, and corporal conjunction. This is an instruction to evaluate the configuration, not a single outcome.

B02. In the initial H3 scenario: L3 in H3 AND unfortunate planets have no SQ/OPP to him -> brother in health. High-resolution inspection resolves the glyph after the square as OPP (two circles), not CONJ.

B03. Malignant planets SQ/OPP S WITHOUT reception -> lives and is in health, but great perplexity, discontent, sorrow. This follows B02 before the explicit move to H4, so retain the H3/aspect-reading context rather than confidently universalizing it to every placement.

B04. The same malignant SQ/OPP WITH reception -> great distress, but he will evade it with ease and free himself from the present sad condition. Reception mitigates the stated distress; it does not mean no distress. Direction and mutuality are not specified.

B05. Fortunate planets SX/TR S WITHOUT reception, OR SQ/OPP S WITH reception -> good health and well content to stay in the current place. The two combinations must not be inverted or reduced to generic benefic contact.

B06. Fortunate planets SX/TR S WITH reception -> health and the stronger claim that he lacks nothing to make him happy. Keep distinct from B05.

B07. L3 in H4, explicitly identified as the brother's own second, WITHOUT aspect of malignant planets -> endeavors to obtain estate/fortune in the country where he is when the figure is erected. It is endeavor, not already acquired wealth. `Without aspect` is not restricted to SQ/OPP here.

B08. L3 in H5 AND joined with L5, `with reception of a Fortune or not`, PROVIDED L5 is not impedited grievously -> health, jocund/merry, likes the local company. Retain the unusual phrase about reception of a Fortune and its optionality; do not silently make L5 a Fortune or require reception.

B09. Continuation across PDF223–224: if the body with whom the brother's significator is in CONJ is a Fortune, OR in SX/TR with reception, the good-condition statement may be made `more safely`. This is increased confidence in that local H5 context, not a new certainty scale. The punctuation does not settle whether reception modifies the conjunction branch as well as the SX/TR branch; do not invent mutual reception.

B10. L3 in H5 void of course, OR in perfect CONJ with an unfortunate planet WITHOUT reception AND that unfortunate planet itself impedited -> argument of health indisposition, `crazy` (historical word), and discontent with location. Keep the H5 context and distinguish the void-of-course branch from the impedited-malefic conjunction branch; do not require a malefic conjunction for void of course. The exact grouping around H5 is prose, not formal notation.

B11. Immediately afterward: brother's significator in `other houses which are [obscured/cancelled word] naturally ill (as the sixth, eighth and twelfth houses are)` -> not well pleased, yet no hurt will come of it. The printed word between `are` and `naturally` is heavily inked/cancelled in this copy and cannot be confidently restored as `not` or omitted. Preserve the textual uncertainty. A clean assertion about all naturally ill houses or all other non-ill houses would exceed this image evidence.

B12. S in H8 AND corporally joined to a Fortune OR joined by SX/TR -> not very well, but not so ill as to doubt well-being; nevertheless indisposed. Reception is not stated as a requirement in this clause. Keep the first placement as common context for the contact alternatives.

B13. S joined to evil planets by bad aspects `and out of the sixt house` -> infirm. This old phrasing should not be translated into `outside H6` as a geometric negation; its precise attachment (the afflicting body's place / aspect's origin) is not fully explicit. An alternative is L6 in H3 -> same infirmity judgment, UNLESS that lord has dignities in the sign AND is in those dignities.

B14. Conditional on the brother already being found ill: L3 CONJ L8 OR entering combustion -> `it's likely` he will die of that infirmity. This is a qualified historical mortality claim with a pre-existing illness premise, not certain death or an unqualified combustion rule.

B15. S in H7 -> still in the same country to which he went, not yet departed; stranger/sojourner; neither well nor ill, `so so`. There is another heavily cancelled short word before `went`; it does not license a new location or timing claim.

B16. S in H8 -> he fears he will die. More fear if S combust, OR CONJ L8 in H8, OR SQ/OPP the Infortunes `out of the eighth`. The outcome is the absent person's fear; do not upgrade it into death. Keep the explicit location phrase and its older usage rather than silently repairing the geometry.

B17. S in H9 -> gone from the first destination to a further country; OR, if capable, entered a religious order; OR employed by religious men; OR possibly, according to his quality, employed on a journey far from the prior abode. These are alternatives with capacity/status/probability qualifiers, not simultaneous events or a guaranteed clerical profession.

B18. S in H10 AND CONJ a Fortune or SX/TR a Fortune, ESPECIALLY with reception -> employment/office/command in the country, good estimation, credible manner of living. Reception strengthens the claim, rather than being the only permissible branch.

B19. H10 continuation on PDF225: joined to Infortunes, OR SQ/OPP them, OR otherwise impedited by them, OR combust in H10 -> `it may be feared he is dead`. Keep the H10 context and this tentative present/past mortality concern separate from B14's likely future death and B16's own fear.

B20. S in H11 joined to Fortunes by any good aspect; OR CONJ L11 -> safe at a friend's house, pleasant/merry. H11 remains the surrounding placement context. Bad planets afflicting him in that house OR casting malevolent beams to him -> malcontent, displeased with present condition.

B21. S in H12 joined to Fortunes WITH reception AND those Fortunes not impedited -> dealings with horses/great cattle, grazier, master of horse, hostler, drover, etc., ACCORDING TO the person's quality. Preserve alternatives; do not claim all occupations together, and do not make the source a universal profession rule.

B22. H12 adverse paragraph: `unfortunate in the twelfth, or in bad aspect with the infortunes, or in aspect with the Lord of the eight, or Combust` -> discontent, fear of never seeing home again, and Lilly's `probable` death there. L8 aspect is not narrowed to bad aspect. House context and the source's string of alternatives must not be replaced by an unconditional any-house death claim.

B23. S in H1 -> frolic/merry, extremely pleased where he is; loved and respected there.

B24. S in H2 -> `probable` inability to come away, either prisoner or an act preventing departure. If S retrograde he will make hard efforts to escape whenever opportunity appears. This is conditional effort/opportunity, not guaranteed successful escape.

B25. Lilly calls this a key to other absent-person questions: absent father -> H4 as that person's Ascendant and proceed round twelve houses; absent child/son/daughter -> H5 Ascendant, H6 second, H7 third; servant -> H6 Ascendant, H7 substance. The father paragraph says `the second House from the Ascendant of your Question, is the substance of the quesited; the third from that ... his Brethren; the fourth his Father`. Keep that wording/context rather than replacing it with a clean modern formula not literally supplied here.

B26. Explicit counterbalancing statement on PDF226: although every house has its sixth, eighth and twelfth, in every person asked about the SIXTH HOUSE OF THE FIGURE signifies infirmity, the EIGHTH death, and TWELFTH imprisonment. Lilly adds that rules must be varied and this is the art's master-piece. This is not permission to silently turn every 6/8/12 reference; record both statements and the source's qualification.

## C. Reports, news, intelligence, fears: PDF226–227

C01. Attribution: Ancients differ; some assign these questions to H5; others to triplicity lords of signs ascending/descending on cusps of H3 or H5. This is reported plurality of methods, not Lilly's settled use of one of them.

C02. Lilly's own declared experience is specifically the late wartime context. Moon in H1/H10/H11/H3 AND separated by benevolent aspect from ANY planet regardless of its house lordship AND then applying by SX/TR/CONJ to L1 -> he found report/rumor true and always tending to the good of Parliament, whether the report sounded good or ill. The original image includes SX as well as TR and CONJ; OCR drops the SX. Do not generalize `Parliament` into universal querent victory without labeling an adaptation.

C03. If at figure erection Moon applied to L7 by any good aspect, he judged `we` had the worst and the enemies the victory. This is the same historical partisan wartime account; it is not generic proof that all rumors are false or bad.

C04. Moon void of course -> news of no moment, `usually` vain or mere lies and soon contradicted. Preserve usually; inconsequence and falsity are not identical.

C05. Moon and Mercury SQ or OPP AND neither one nor the other nor both cast favorable SX/TR to the ascending degree -> news false and purposefully reported to frighten `us`. The no-favorable-contact condition is essential, and the targets are the ASCENDING DEGREE. It is not every Moon–Mercury hard aspect.

C06. Question time: when Lilly himself first heard the news/rumor; if someone else propounded it, the very moment it was proposed. This is a timing procedure, not an outcome.

C07. On whether the report will harm the asker: Jupiter OR Venus in Ascendant, OR Moon/Mercury in their essential dignities AND TR/SX to L11 -> no detriment to the asker. The dignities-plus-L11 aspect belongs to the Moon/Mercury branch, not automatically to Jupiter/Venus in Ascendant.

C08. Harm conditions: L6/L8/L12 in Ascendant OR in bad aspect L1; OR Mars/Saturn retrograde in Ascendant, OR in evil aspect L1, OR casting SQ/OPP to ascending degree. The punctuation leaves the retrograde adjective's reach into the latter Mars/Saturn alternatives less than formal; preserve its position rather than erase it or assert a unique broad scope. Harm to querent IF the report concerns that person; if about the Commonwealth, damage to ministers/parties. Do not erase this relevance condition.

C09. Within that harm/Commonwealth interpretation, IF a named planet signifies the mischief: Saturn -> country friends plundered, corn/cattle lost; Mars -> straggling parties cut off; Mercury -> letters miscarried/intercepted; Sun -> chief officer/commander distressed; Jupiter/Venus -> gentlemen, friends, supporters. These are damage-type examples conditioned on the planet being the significator, not malefic claims about every occurrence of the planet. Lilly says to vary rules according to the question.

## D. Rumor rules attributed to the Ancients: PDF227–228

D01. Consider L1 and Moon, which is angular; OR Moon's dispositor angular AND in fixed sign; OR any of these in succedent house AND fixed sign; OR in good aspect, explicitly SX/TR, of Jupiter, Venus, or SUN -> rumors true and very good. Preserve Sun in this local favorable list. The punctuation leaves the reach of `and a fixed Signe` in the angular branches somewhat open; do not conceal this by claiming the prose supplies one formally unique grouping.

D02. L1 afflicted by Infortunes OR cadent -> contrary judgment, THOUGH strong in its sign. Sign strength does not negate the stated exception.

D03. `For the most part` rumors true when figure angles are fixed (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius) AND Moon and Mercury in fixed signs, separating from Infortunes and applying to a fortunate planet IN AN ANGLE. Do not omit the angular recipient, fixed signs, separation/application sequence, or probabilistic wording.

D04. Ill rumors hold true if fourth AND tenth angles fixed AND Moon `received in them`; qualified as `in some sort verified`. Do not make `received in them` a definite mutual-reception formula or substitute a modern resolved placement test.

D05. Under evil news/bad reports/unlucky intelligence, if either Fortune in Ascendant OR Moon `ufortunate` (visible printed spelling in the high-resolution original) -> strong argument rumors false and turn rather to good than evil. The word visibly begins with `u` in this copy; whether intended as an abbreviated/misprinted `unfortunate`, as `fortunate`, or missing text is not settled by the image. Record it as a material textual ambiguity. Do not silently normalize to Moon fortunate or use the alternative as a clean machine condition.

D06. Retrogradation of Mercury OR Mercury otherwise afflicted, OR of the planet to whom Moon applies, OR to whom Mercury applies; `and above all, if either of those two be Lords of the Ascendant` -> ill rumors vanish and convert to good. Exact attachment of the shared affliction/retrogradation predicate and the antecedent of `those two` is not completely explicit. Preserve the source's ordering and qualifier instead of inventing a determinate set of three/four normalized bodies. The topic remains ill rumors.

D07. L1 under Sun's beams OR combust -> matter kept secret; few ever know its truth. This is secrecy/epistemic limit, not proof of truth or falsity.

## E. Counsel/advice: PDF228

E01. Context: neighbor/kinsman/friend visits to advise/persuade; erect the figure at the moment they FIRST begin to disclose the matter. This differs from C06's first-hearing/proposed-question rule.

E02. Fortune in MC/H10, expressly Sun/Jupiter/Venus; OR North Node there; OR Moon applying to L1 -> honest heart and advice intended for the recipient's good. North Node and Moon application are separate favorable options. The Moon's aspect type and reception are not specified; do not add them. The house context attaches to the fortune/node placement, while Moon's application is its own alternative.

E03. An Infortune, expressly Saturn/Mars/South Node, in the corresponding H10/MC context -> deceitful intention and lying. Keep the nodes distinct and the local house context.

E04. Haly is explicitly the source of the further claim: Ascendant sign movable AND L1 in movable sign AND Moon in movable sign -> deceitful visitor intending to entrap. Preserve all three conditions and the attribution.

## F. Whether siblings exist, unity again: PDF229

F01. Lilly says this question is better resolved from the querent's proper nativity; he nevertheless offers rules claimed from experience. Keep that preference/caveat.

F02. Fruitful sign on H3 cusp: Cancer/Scorpio/Pisces; ALSO Aquarius/Sagittarius/Gemini, though these are less fruitful -> may judge brethren/sisters. High-resolution glyph check confirms the less-fruitful group and must not be overwritten with a standard modern list.

F03. Brother(s): masculine sign on H3 cusp AND its lord in masculine SIGN OR HOUSE, OR in aspect with a masculine planet. The original word is `house` (long-s type), not OCR's `houre`. Retain the conjunction/alternative syntax rather than introduce planetary-hour rules.

F04. Sister(s): feminine sign AND feminine planet in H3, OR significators in feminine signs/houses AND CONJ/application to feminine planets. Preserve the alternative configurations and source plurals. Application does not here specify an aspect type.

F05. Counting doctrine is ATTRIBUTED AND REJECTED: `some say` the number of planets in the house or aspected by L3 equals the number of siblings; Lilly regards demanding such particulars from a question as too scrupulous. This cannot be promoted as Lilly's approved count rule.

F06. Unity in present/future discerned from LAST aspect of L3 and L1, OR placement of benevolent/malignant planets in H1/H3. Fortunes in H1 -> concord from querent; fortunes in H3 -> from brother/sister/kindred. `Last aspect` is not automatically their next application.

F07. Saturn/Mars out of essential dignities in H3, OR South Node in H3 -> strong argument of cross/untoward relatives, no unity, continual discord/wrangling. The South Node alternative is not silently made subject to the Saturn/Mars dignity qualifier. Historical characterization only.

## G. Short journey: PDF229–230

G01. Scope: twenty/thirty/forty miles OR far enough to go and return in a day, or at latest the next. This is Lilly's practical definition, not a modern distance boundary requiring an exact numerical cutoff or both duration and mileage.

G02. Consider L1 at question proposal. Text begins `see if he be swift or slow in motion`, followed by a favorable disjunctive list: L1 in any dignities OF L3; OR L1 in H3; OR L1 SX/TR/CONJ L3; OR L1 SX/TR/CONJ a benevolent planet situated in H3; OR Moon applies to L3; OR Moon applies to ANY planet in H3; OR Moon in H3; OR Moon SX to ascending sign; OR Moon SQ `in Signs of short ascensions, in any house whatsoever`; OR Moon swift in motion. Lilly says `all, or any` are arguments of going on the short journey with good success.

G03. Do not invert the dignity direction: it is L1 in the dignities of L3, not L3 in L1's dignities, and not necessarily mutual reception. Do not require the Moon's recipient in H3 to be benevolent. The benevolence qualification belongs to the earlier L1-aspect-to-H3-planet branch.

G04. Literal `swift or slow` is an observation instruction with awkward connection to the favorable list. It should not be silently rewritten as a clean rule that either speed is favorable, nor should `slow` be silently deleted from a purported transcription. Mark interpretation uncertainty if operationalizing.

G05. Moon's square branch retains the source's short-ascension-sign and any-house qualifiers. The text does not restate its target after the preceding SX-to-ascending-sign clause, so a normalized target/hemisphere rule would need to be identified as an interpretation. No geographic hemisphere convention is supplied here.

G06. Intended direction: consider H3 sign, L3's sign, and Moon's sign; judge by which is strongest in essential dignities where it is. If the principal significator is in a northern sign, journey intended north; analogously the rest `with their due limitations`. No complete sign-to-compass lookup or ranking/tie algorithm is supplied in this span. Preserve that dependence and absence rather than manufacture one.
