**Complete substantive contents:** The initial numbered knowledge/profit question allows a distinction between possessing skill and making productive use of that skill. Two unnumbered sections follow: (a) whether another person's claimed wisdom/science is genuine, and (b) the conditions/status of four, five or more absent people, using a sequential ordered list of names and Moon applications. A third unnumbered section asks **to which part of the heavens/world one should travel**, with quadrant directions and a separation between fortunes expected at the destination (eighth) and fortunes expected upon return (second).

**Evidence boundary:** Every skill, financial, travel and absent-person judgment is historical astrology prose. The work contains no objectively documented case, independently authenticated prospective prediction, scientific evidence for safe travel direction, or real medical/missing-person finding. Original-source assertions are *not* validated predictive rules.
