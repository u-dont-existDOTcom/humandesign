## Actual historic text and source qualifications

The unnumbered introduction asks how **many** enemies/adversaries the native supposedly has. Lilly offers alternative testimony from planets occupying the seventh or twelfth houses or opposing natal Sun or Moon. Multiple planets in seventh/twelfth or both lights opposed are alleged to mean many adversaries; **absence of planets in those houses** allegedly means few or none. Other alleged positive witnesses include the first-house ruler in the twelfth or the twelfth ruler in the first, and equivalent reciprocal first/seventh house lord placements.

The source also equates the northern lunar node's supposed effects with traditional Jupiter/Venus benefic analogues and the southern node with Mars/Saturn malefic analogues. Its final exception says that when the twelfth ruler does not aspect the Ascendant ruler, this is an argument **against** large numbers of enemies. Thus the text does include explicit negative and non-occurrence cases, although no numerical thresholds, age interval, probability or independent human outcomes are supplied.

The following printed CLIII concerns *who or what kind* of enemies supposedly exist, which must not be inferred from this count source. Nor may a legal dispute, unpopular acquaintance, actual violent threat or harmful person be deduced from a natal chart. Historical zodiac labels and nodal glyphs are not established empirical mechanisms.
