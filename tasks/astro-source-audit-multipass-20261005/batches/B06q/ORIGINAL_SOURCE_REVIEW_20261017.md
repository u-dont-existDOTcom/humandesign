## Count methods and source contradictions

Lilly proposes that the number of children rises with fertile-category planets located in fifth or eleventh house and in source-defined fertile zodiac signs, while fewer eligible planets or barren signs imply fewer children. He gives two distinct arithmetic modifiers: source fruitful planets in **double-bodied (bicorporeal) signs supposedly double the number**, whereas those in **fruitful signs supposedly triple the number**. Because sign classifications can overlap and the author specifies neither reference count, overlap priority, nor tie-breaking, these cannot be converted into a unique or demonstrably accurate count from this passage alone.

The *Whether Male or Female* subsection separately assigns Jupiter, Saturn, Mars and Sun to alleged masculine classification and asks for masculine signs/houses/degrees. For female children he uses Moon/Venus, occidental status and feminine sign/house/degree testimony. Historical astrological categories are not biological fertility or sex forecasting evidence.

In another subsection Lilly shifts to **children's survival, fame, happiness, obedience and benefits to parents**, using planetary potency and essential dignity; weak planetary positions supposedly reverse the outcome. Another distinct rule attributes **mutual parent-child love or hostility** to good or bad aspects between the lord of fifth and lord of first. Neither child count nor sex nor survival nor subjective love can rescue a failed prediction of another outcome in an empirical test.

The source then quotes *Aphorismes of ALBUBATER concerning Children*. Examples allegedly predict early child death from Saturn/Mars fifth or eleventh; Sun ruling fifth joined with a malefic; Sun/Saturn/Mercury in source ambiguous sign/MC placement opposed or square Moon; Mars opposite Jupiter or Jupiter opposite Saturn; Mercury first and Saturn west. More death-order/child mortality allegations follow on PDF640 involving fifth lord in eighth or source glyph/sign plus adverse planets. None are medically validated, and original Albubater textual attribution has not been independently checked.

## Substantive inter-author disagreement

At the end of the chapter Lilly says that later astrologers have **departed from Ptolemy's child-judgment procedure**, which he describes as considering the **tenth, eleventh, fourth and fifth houses**, their signs and occupants. This is an explicit historical methodological disagreement with Lilly's earlier fifth-house-first procedure, not independent replication or validation of either.
