## Substantive semantic boundaries

1. The initial capture passage includes unusual attachment of a conjunction to both first/fourth lords and several placement alternatives; house exclusions 12, 8 and 6, and the one-way Moon reception are **not omitted**.
2. An apparently favorable capture signal involving the fourth lord not beholding its house is overridden by an express seventh-lord-in-fourth exception; do not repair this away.
3. The author alternates capture, possible treason, governor injury and uncertainty of continued resistance. These are original **assertions**, not demonstrated events or predictions.
4. Two astrological symbols printed near the fourth-house statements remain glyph-unresolved. No modern planetary/node identities, sign-to-fort mapping, or degree orb are invented.
