It asserts Wednesday July 16, 1645 (inferred English Julian/Old Style), with a separate Saturn planetary-hour glyph. PDF324: source infers **spleen**, wind-colic, melancholy obstructions in bowels/small guts, small fevers and remiss pulse, head disturbance and unrestful sleep. It writes **[All which was true.]** This is **Lilly's own retrospective attestation**, not an independently verified medical finding. The source distinguishes the celestial diagnosis from the patient's independently reported symptoms only as printed; no modern condition can be inferred with confidence.

The author states he advised the sick person to make peace with God and put his house in order, because by *natural causes* he did not perceive that the man could live beyond **ten or twelve days**. This is author-reported prediction content, not a verified prospectively frozen forecast.

Four printed reason clauses are separately sequential:
1. Sun as temporal luminary at question in perfect square to Saturn sixth lord, in fixed signs, with a parenthetical Latin phrase (preserve as printed, no inferred calculation).
2. Ascendant afflicted by Mars bodily present, naturally ill and accidentally almost ruler of whole eighth.
3. Moon near **Cauda Leonis** and afflicted by the cross influence of Mars in the house signifying life (ascendant). Do not silently convert Cauda Leonis to the dragon's tail or a particular star without author citation.
4. Moon had separated from sextile of Mercury ascendant lord in long-ascension signs, which is said to be *more properly a square*, and transferred virtue to Jupiter lord of eighth. This is a source qualification, not modern ephemeris parity.

The author then writes **the sick died the 28th of July following**, 12 calendar days after the chart's July16 date. This is **author-reported** only, with no independently authenticated death certificate or proven prediction freeze. Subsequent Mercury/Sun/Saturn and Moon/Sun/first-cusp/12th-cusp calculations in the prose are **post-outcome explanations**, not more prospectively preregistered predictions. Keep the outcome distinct from the initial ten-or-twelve-day assertion.

The source contains exactly **one enquiry, one chart and one reported patient outcome**. Do not count each explanatory clause or symptom as a second observed person, prospective win, or replicated case.
