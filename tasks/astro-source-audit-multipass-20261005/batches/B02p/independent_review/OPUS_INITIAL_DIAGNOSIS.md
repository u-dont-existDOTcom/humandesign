### 1.3 Ambiguities to log rather than repair

- **A1** "that house and Signe" (J3): the sentence subject is the ascendant sign, but the nearest antecedent is "the eighth house".
- **A2** "require" here means seek or inquire.
- **A3** "earthly, melancholly" modifies "Signe", not the Moon.
- **A4** "who *was* the temporall light … and *is* (fons vitalis potentiæ)": the tense splits case-specific from general doctrine.
- **A5** "crosse influence" means adverse influence. The text puts ☽ and ♂ both in ♍, so this is not an aspect.
- **A6** "his vertue": most plausibly ☿'s (the Moon is "her" at J9), but this is not stated.
- **A7** "more properly a □": the convention behind this is not stated.
- **A8** The ten-or-twelve bound has no stated anchor (the 7:22 question time or the later persuasion), and inclusive vs exclusive counting is not stated.
- **A9** "All which": the bracket closes a period that begins at J1. Its scope is most naturally the conditions in l.2–7, but it is not formally delimited.
- **A10** "□ of ♄": the text doesn't say whether this is radical or transiting Saturn.
- **A11** "⊙ the cusp of the twelfth" is elliptical, and "at time of the Question" is not repeated.
- **A12** "14. of ♒" against the wheel's "13 32" is probably an ordinal degree. Don't harmonize them.
- **A13** "Cauda Leonis" literally means the Lion's tail. It is a different term from the ☋ at J5.
- **A14** The text does not say who asked the question.
- **A15** Copy-specific manuscript marks: "6th House" in the top margin, an underline at "require the Disease", and the ruled line at "♒ being".
