## What the author attributes to celestial conditions

At the outset, **Virgo rising** is described as especially afflicted by the **bodily presence of Mars**, which the author also partly associates with the eighth-house rulership. For disease location and causes he considers the sixth-house **Aquarius**, the sixth ruler **Saturn in Taurus** (described as fixed, earthy, melancholic and of Virgo's triplicity), and the **Moon near Mars in the ascendant**. An additional sign/glyph said to affect the sixth-house Aquarius is not securely read and is deliberately not normalized. [PDF323 continuing on PDF324; R1773–R1777; U05–U06,U10]

From these testimonies the author infers spleen problems, wind-colic, melancholy bowel obstructions, small fevers, a slow/remiss pulse, head disturbance and restless sleep. The next bracketed claim, **“All which was true”**, is **Lilly's own retrospective corroboration**. This is not an independently documented examination, another patient, or an objective confirmation of the historical horoscope's accuracy. Modern diagnoses are outside the source-audit contract. ## The unusual ten-or-twelve-day account

Lilly says he encouraged the patient to put his affairs in order and make peace with God, stating he could not perceive natural grounds for living longer than **ten or twelve days**. That statement is followed by four rationales: the Sun's asserted perfect square to Saturn, the sixth ruler in fixed signs; Mars physically at the ascendant and substantially associated with the eighth; the Moon near **Cauda Leonis** and afflicted by Mars; and a lunar separation from Mercury's sextile, judged **more properly a square in signs of long ascensions**, followed by virtue transferred to Jupiter as eighth lord. All four are preserved with their named conditions and original-terminology uncertainties; they are not four independent patient outcomes. [PDF324; R1782–R1787; U07–U11]

The text then reports that the patient **died on 28 July following**. July 28 minus July 16 is **12 calendar days**, the upper end of Lilly's earlier interval. This is a real arithmetic correspondence *within the printed account*. Crucially, both the prediction and the outcome appear in a book printed in 1647 describing events in 1645. No independent death record, pre-outcome manuscript, timestamped prediction freeze or denominator of missed cases has been established. It therefore cannot be counted as a validated prospective 12-day forecast. [PDF323–324; R1783,R1788–R1789; CASE_EVIDENCE.json; U14–U16]

The remaining Mercury/Sun/Saturn and Moon/Sun/cusp descriptions occur **after the death statement**. They are presented as retrospective explanatory transits, not as original pre-frozen targets. The final degrees and aspect calculations have not been reproduced against verified historical ephemerides or an independently implemented house system. [PDF324; R1790–R1792; U17–U18]
