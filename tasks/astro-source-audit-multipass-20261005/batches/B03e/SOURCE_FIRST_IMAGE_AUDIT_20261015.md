## Documentary vs prediction/effect boundaries

- The narrative explicitly states the woman revealed more facts after the first reading. Later assessments could therefore incorporate participant feedback and cannot be reconstructed as one frozen prospective prediction from the book.
- The astrologer **advised approaching another individual**; this social intervention might affect the later marriage independently of horary astrology.
- The "within twenty days" match is asserted by Lilly, but no independently verified marriage register or counterpart account accompanies this chapter. The historical account is not a blinded, prospective, independent validation.
- Preserve calendar distinctions: chart date June 16, planned intermediary approach June 19, claimed Sun–Saturn aspect June 27, and claimed Jupiter–Sun conjunction June 29. None was independently reconstructed from chart data.
- Initial Sun–Saturn sextile **without reception** was explicitly weak/negative; Jupiter reception/translation-of-light is subsequently used to explain a positive reported outcome. Do not silently flatten all aspects into successes.
- The hypothetical questions about longevity, wealth, character and long-term compatibility did not occur as measured outcomes. Treat them as **counterfactual source assertions**, not successes.
- Anecdotes about personal appearance, third-party identity, jealousy, honest conduct and rival marriage are not independently verified individual psychological traits.
- Chart graphics, not OCR text or modern self-selected ephemeris calculations, control original symbolic readings. Uncertain time notation and glyphs remain unresolved until independent image reading.
