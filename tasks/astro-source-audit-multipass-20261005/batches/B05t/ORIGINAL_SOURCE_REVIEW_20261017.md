## Historical source content

Lilly claims all or a majority of strong sibling significators imply favorable sibling conditions, long life, wealth, honor and potential benefit to the native. He asserts weak sibling indicators imply contrary outcomes, and mixed indicators mean some siblings may enjoy moderate fortune while others encounter hardship. No actual siblings, longevity follow-up, objective finances or social data are supplied to establish the correctness of these allegations.

The source gives two additional *relative* sibling conditions using traditional planetary sex attributions: if the overall significators are strong, Moon fortified and Mars weak, sisters allegedly fare better than brothers. The reverse condition, Mars strong and Moon weak/afflicted, allegedly favors brothers in position and resources. The two comparator cases must retain **both** parts of each compound condition, and should not be treated as human sex, health, wealth or relationship prediction evidence.
