## Structural and interpretive restrictions

The original distinguishes **body/conjunction**, **sextile or trine**, **square or opposition**, **terms** and cusp/stellar positions. It offers several fundamentally different possible event families: bodily health and old humoral labels, inherited property and gifts, commercial or official advancement, reputation, subjective contentment, partnership/marriage, childbirth, family illness or death, litigation/debt, travel, loss of office and injuries. **These cannot be interchangeably scored as a single prediction.**

**Jupiter** body/soft aspects may allegedly give good health, fortune, patronage, favor, or marriage if age/circumstances permit. He explicitly adjusts output relative to natal/social context, e.g. clergy benefices, tradesmen profits, courtiers promotions and kings receiving resources from subjects. Jupiter hard aspects can instead be associated with complaints, adversaries and modest economic loss, while Jupiter terms receive another sometimes favorable set of claims. Birth class, essential strength, radix condition, house and zodiac sign matter.

**Mars** body/hard aspects allege conflict, financial or medical misfortune, dangerous injuries and sometimes death; serious source claims require additional hyleg/anareta, sign, harm-related and benefic mitigating prerequisites. Friendly Mars aspects allegedly bring martial preferment or successful crafts while also suggesting spending or impatience; there is no simple Mars=evil conclusion.

**Sun** and **Venus** sections similarly make distinct positive/negative source claims by contact, social status and physiological circumstances. The Sun offers office/royal favor and family-health alternatives, while adverse aspects allegedly have eye, father, financial or travel problems. Venus has old claims about delight, adornment, social connections, trade, marriage and children, with negative alternatives involving scandal, health and jealousy, none clinically or morally defensible.

**Mercury** body/friendly directions cover scholarship, oratory, commercial bargaining, writing or accounting, and other paths often conditioned on the native's age and social status; hard directions claim litigation, alleged fraud/defamation or loss of study; terms can be favorable. **Moon** body and positive directions depend partly on original natal lunar fortitude/radix; soft directions allegedly bring family and partnership, travel or success, while difficult directions cite mother/partner or economic difficulties, water hazards and illness. The lunar nodes, Part Fortune and planet-specific contacts are separate source paths, not repeated lunar results.

For the **house cusps**, the source separately treats Ascendant directed to second (property), third (siblings/travel) and fourth (family/estate) among other claimed outcomes. Extremely consequential old harm/death assertions are kept as historical descriptions without personal application.

## Historical fixed-star table and its uncertainty

The original final source section names sixteen fixed-star/asterism entries across **PDF699–702**, including spear-staff of Bootes, Arcturus, lion-tail, a bright ship star, girdle of Orion, lion-heart, Lesser Dog, Crater, Virgin's Spike (Spica), lion-knee and neck, Hydra, Cratch of the Crab, Pleiades and the **two Aselli at separate original longitudes**. Source labels, printed zodiac degree and minute when visible, exact page and uncertainty are preserved in `FIXED_STARS_1647_CLIX_ORIGINAL_TABLE_V1.json`. Some original minutes are missing and some historical names identify groups rather than modern single stars. No modern ephemeris alignment, original coordinate epoch, precession/parallax or star-proper-motion calculation has been validated.

The old alleged outcomes are wildly diverse—wealth, exceptional honors, new social acquaintance, shame, illness, water/animal dangers, injury, occupational changes or worse—and often pair favorable and unfavorable alternatives under the same star. Lilly also urges joint consideration of concurrent natal positions and other planetary testimony. None is reliable modern medicine, legal analysis, travel safety or moral assessment.
