## Original historical conditions and competing travel prediction methods

The initial question combines prior ninth-house significators, particularly Moon and Mercury, with mutual reception, aspects, natal house placement or house rulership/exchange of first and ninth, solar contacts to Moon or Mercury, and planets in mutable signs. The original contains an unresolved aspect glyph after one mutual-reception clause; it is NOT guessed or made executable. The author separately asserts that more positive testimonies predict frequent journeys, fewer signs predict less travel, none suggest remaining in the native country, and movable zodiac signs mean quick/profitable travel while fixed signs mean longer stay with little profit. Subjective intention, trip occurrence, trip frequency, time away, moving abroad and profit are distinct outcomes with no operational unit or lifespan cutoff.

The internal geography subsection gives several **alternative direction methods**. A structured reference `TRAVEL_QUARTER_HISTORICAL_METHODS_V1.json` preserves **seven** distinct methods without pretending they form a deterministic validated forecast:

- Lilly's source assigns Sun/Saturn → **East**, Venus → **South**, Jupiter → **North**, Mars/Moon → **West**. Mercury follows the direction of the planet receiving its aspect; in one conjunct-luminary condition without sufficient essential dignity, the author says it predicts **nothing**.
- Traditional zodiac triplicities instead map **fire East, air West, earth South, water North**.
- A different passage Lilly explicitly attributes to **Ptolemy** uses Sun/Moon in oriental houses 10, 11, 12, 1, 2, 3 → **East or Southeast** and occidental houses 9, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4 → **West or North**. Lilly notes its disagreement with other authors; the exact Ptolemy original passage has not been collated.
- Mars/Moon with northern or southern ecliptic latitude, when strong, are separately alleged to indicate travel toward **North or South**.
- Lilly then says to use the majority of testimonies, without weights, eligibility rules, an explicit tie policy, angular tolerance or no-match threshold.
- For transport, source sign categories fire/earth predict chiefly **land**, water/air predict **water or mixed travel**, quadruped signs **horse/coach**, human signs **on foot**. These historical transport categories cannot be trivially translated to air flights, cars or contemporary multimodal trips.
- Additional mutable/fixed and plurality of aspects allegedly predict trip duration, frequency, ease and finances—again separate from destinations.

These methods can visibly contradict each other on one chart. A future independent test must specify one method or a frozen composite **before** seeing travel histories, must include all failures, and must compare against geographic and socioeconomic base rates. The source offers no demonstrated successful prospective human cases in these pages.
