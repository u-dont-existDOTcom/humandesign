## Original conditional accounts

Lilly treats the **Sun and Venus in daytime nativities** as one parent-to-parent relationship source pair, with aspects or mutual reception allegedly supporting marital concord. A favorable Venus approaching combustion is linked to a period stereotype that a wife seeks to please her husband. Venus retrograde, peregrine, unfortunate and separating from Sun is given as an unfavorable opposite alleged to imply disobedience. At night, Lilly instead selects **Saturn and Moon**; their favorable aspects allegedly predict unity while square or opposition allegedly predicts ongoing conflict.

An additional alleged conflict criterion is the **opposition of the tenth-house ruler and fourth-house ruler**. Lilly assigns blame to the mother for a malefic in the tenth, or poor paternal character for a malefic in fourth.
