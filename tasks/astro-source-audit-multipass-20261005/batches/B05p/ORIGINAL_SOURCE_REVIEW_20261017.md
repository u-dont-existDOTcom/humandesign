## What Lilly actually asserts and the limitations

The method treats an estate significator as favorably disposed if it is naturally benefic or located in dignities of a benefic despite an intrinsically malefic planetary nature. The converse may be adverse. Claimed lawful versus indirect sources also depend on absence/presence of hostile aspects, combustion, retrograde motion, peregrine status and strong versus weak testimonies.

Lilly explicitly allows *both* lawful and indirect acquisition in a mixed natal figure, rather than a single categorical outcome. He lists four consideration dimensions: intrinsic planetary nature, sign/dignity, combustion, and retrograde motion, while also invoking discretionary mixture, plurality of testimony, and aspects to other planets. A simplistic “malefics mean crime” algorithm would contradict his qualifications.
