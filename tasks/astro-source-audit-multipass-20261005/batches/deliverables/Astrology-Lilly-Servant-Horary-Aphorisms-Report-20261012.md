## Servant–master judgment

For this 1647 horary question, **first house, first lord and Moon represent the servant**, **tenth house and tenth lord represent the master**, whatever his condition. The author tests joining between the two lords by body or aspect, with and without reception. An exact conjunction **by degree and minute** means quick, easy freedom in the source; separation by a few minutes is only **'an argument'** the person is **'as good as freed already'**, not a recorded release. If that first relationship is absent, apply analogous conditions to the **Moon**, and if both servant significators lack the relevant relation with the tenth lord, apply analogous joining/separating conditions to the **Sun**. The source's broad word *conjunction* across body/aspect is preserved rather than harmonised with a narrower later definition. [PDF330; R1853–R1859; U03–U07]

The text groups **both examples** under a **'determinate and not absolute'** question: release from the present master and ever gaining freedom from his power. It does **not** contrast the two examples or define an absolute question. It lists alternative departure indications: ascendant lord **cadent from an angle without beholding ascendant**, the printed alternative **'or is in aspect with any Planet in an Angle'**, or to a planet beholding the ascendant. That **'or'** may instead continue **'no aspect'** as a negative **'nor'** and, read positively, may contradict the direct-angular no-release clause; the source condition is unresolved, or in **third/ninth** (or joined to a planet there). Parallel Moon conditions can substitute. The contrasting denial spans PDF330–331: Moon or first lord in **first, tenth, seventh or fourth**, or joined with a planet there that is **direct**, prevents freedom; **retrograde** gives freedom but slow/difficult. Malefic corporeal conjunction, square or opposition afflicting the angular ascendant lord, or **'if he is entring combustion'**, are further negatives. Whether **entering combustion** inherits the preceding angle restriction remains unclear. None is a witnessed historical slave release, and none is present-day legal or human-rights guidance. [PDF330–331; R1860–R1870; U04–U08]

## General horary aphorisms 1–16

The first eight demand an acceptable **hour lord–ascendant lord nature or triplicity agreement** (1), warn differently about **early versus late degrees** (2), warn about tenth-house afflicted Saturn/Mars or a node glyph and astrologer's credit (3), reject trivial or unprepared questions (4), give the **Moon special weight as alleged planetary-virtue transmitter** (5), treat Saturn/Mars as symbolically cold/hot, with the explicitly Moon-involved **mutual reception of Moon and Saturn/Mars**, not just any significators (6), distinguish Jupiter/Venus application by sextile/trine and essential dignity (7), and instruct **'hope well'**, not guaranteed success, with fortunate significators while fearing worse with infortunes (8). [PDF332; R1877–R1884]

Rule (9) says a **void-of-course Moon** is less obstructive in **Cancer, Taurus, Sagittarius or Pisces**; retain all four. **Moon separating** from a planet marks the past (10), **applying** by a good aspect **from the Moon's good house to a good planet** marks the matter's present prospects (11); application to a planet in fall is an adverse separate condition (12). **Retrograde or first-station** significators show discord (13); malefic significators make bad worse and good incomplete/difficult (14); slow motion delays (15); aphorism16 actually reads **'cast not any aspect unto them'** before claiming the indicated evil is lessened; this printed negative is **not** silently corrected to favourable aspects. Its further clause concerning fortune significators is also ambiguous, so no symmetric algorithm is invented. [PDF333; R1885–R1892; U16–U17]

## Aphorisms 17–28

The numbered rules retain benefics' **cadency, poor dignity, no ascendant aspect or retrogradation**, with reception as a qualifier (17); contrast reception of malefic and benefic significators (18); call a peregrine planet **'malicious beyond expression'**, with an analogy of a noble person **scorning to hurt** his opponent (19); then give a Saturn/Mars dignity-and-angular-position positive exception **as the continuation's body reads** (20). At the page333–334 join, however, the printed page333 **catchword is 'or'** while the next body reads **'and Angles'**. The exact conjunctive condition is therefore uncertain. A fortune without essential dignity does not wholly perform (21), both fortunes and infortunes weak prompts **deferred judgment** (22), an afflicted question significator combust or in solar opposition allegedly signifies **no good and cannot perfect** (23); two infortunes **'joyned'** by a broadly worded contact/aspect produce little good or probably intensified evil (24), and a first lord without dignity and cadent indicates no hope (25). [PDF333–334; R1893–R1901]

Rule (26) specifies **within twelve degrees** of Sun as under the beams and weak **'let it be in what Signe it will'**, but **within sixteen arcminutes** as **cazimi**, strong. Rule (27) says oriental **Saturn/Jupiter/Mars** suggest earlier resolution, occidental later; **Venus/Mercury reverse that direction**. Rule (28) assigns fixed signs stability, common signs possibility without final conclusion, movable signs sudden resolution either way; historic examples concern house foundations, journeys and moderation. These are not exact tested calendar times, probabilities or safe high-stakes prescriptions. [PDF334; R1902–R1904; U19–U21]

## Aphorisms 29–43

Rule (29) treats contact of first lord/Moon with **Head or Tail of Dragon** as harmful, conditional on house; (30) links pending eclipse position with ascendant or significator degrees; (31) treats an impeded Moon as generally difficult and warns about the querent's life specifically **'if it be in going to WARRE'**, then offers separate journey and wooing examples. The initial draft **wrongly read WARRE as a lunar WANE condition**; this has been corrected against PDF335. (32) discusses a significator opposite its own house, illustrated by Mercury in Sagittarius/Pisces; (33) traces obstructions through the planet's house lordship/location; (34) distinguishes angular/succedent/cadent positions; (35) treats Moon conjunct Sun/combustion as worse than ordinary malefic aspects; (36) discusses an infortune and a significator when **BOTH** have stated negative conditions, while the grammar of those conditions remains unsettled; (37) requires significators' conjunction **and a congenial sign**; (38) distinguishes interruption by frustration or prohibition **before** perfection; (39) uses a **fortune-like glyph** in a particular house for gain/harm, with glyph identity left source-qualified. [PDF334–335; R1905–R1915; U22–U27]

The last four clauses (PDF336) say an **'unfortunate Planet' (afflicted, not automatically a natural malefic)** in the seventh threatens marriage disagreement **unless that same planet is a significator at birth** (40); an afflicted eighth lord **in the eighth** relates to separate woman-death and deceased-person debt claims (41); dignified Jupiter/Venus in house-linked matters suggest benefits, with third-kin, fourth-father/land, fifth-play examples (42); a Dragon-tail-like glyph is said to signify harm, scandal or slander through its house, with the author's strong **'it seldome failes'** preserved as a claim, not an observed frequency (43). These are source claims, not independently documented marriage, death or debt predictions. **CHAP. XLIX** begins only **below** the next divider and is not extracted. [PDF336–337; R1916–R1920; U28–U31]

## Original-image independent review: corrections and unresolved contradictions

A fresh source-image reviewer read all **nine** original-page/errata images and all **69** draft records, producing **FINDS_ERROR**. Three meaning-changing corrections were supported by the original: **aphorism31 = 'going to WARRE', not a waning Moon** (original PDF335/printed301); **aphorism6's reception exception names the Moon and Saturn/Mars** (PDF332/printed298); and **aphorism40 says 'unfortunate Planet' rather than necessarily a naturally malefic one** (PDF336/printed302). Two additionally unresolved source conflicts are now recorded: the **'or' catchword on PDF333 versus 'and Angles' body on PDF334** in aphorism20, and the positive-sounding **angular 'or is in aspect'** in XLVIII which can contradict an angular/direct no-release clause. Aphorism16's source-literal **'cast not any aspect'** is preserved even though the author then describes mitigation of evil. Smaller exact-qualification and page-scope repairs are recorded against original page images. The reviewer initially saw section identities for image navigation before reading the images, so independence was substantial but not absolute. One reviewer comment that \`evidence/\` paths were unavailable was limited to the intentionally isolated \`source_images/\` staging folder and is NOT a defect in repository sources. This documentary review makes no test of whether the astrology predicts reality. [PDF330–336; independent_review/OPUS_INITIAL_DIAGNOSIS.md; independent_review/FINAL_RECONCILIATION.json]
