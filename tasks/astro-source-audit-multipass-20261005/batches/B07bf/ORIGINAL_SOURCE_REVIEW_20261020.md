### Leakage: voyages reported BEFORE any supposedly inferred judgement

The original printed folio758 contains a **marginal past-tense note**, concerning the merchant personally: he has “thrice been beyond Sea,” with **two Barbary trips and one to Holland**. The prose then derives a travel inclination from a Mercury–Moon trine, reception and movable/common signs. Whether Lilly's merchant actually took those voyages is *not independently verified*, but the author explicitly **has the reported outcome** when describing the astrology. This is a **known-information example**, not a successful prospective forecast, independent retrospective blind chart-to-response hit, or 3 newly discovered voyages. The three separately annotated notes intentionally carry the `DISCLOSED_PREEXISTING_OBSERVATION_NOT_CHART_INFERENCE` evidence type and an expected future scoring exclusion.

### Forecast and interpretive claim classes, all unverified

The source proposes from third/ninth house and the Mercury–Moon relation a tendency to travel, intervals between voyages, and favoured **southwest** destinations. It considers a southeast direction but expressly discourages it unless a Saturn condition applies. The travel-motive paragraph points to a desire to accumulate wealth or personal interest in foreign places, with a speculative commissioned task from someone in authority.

Under *Success in Travels* it forecasts material and reputational gain, referencing the star Spica, the ninth cusp and supposedly beneficial Venus. **At the same time**, Moon–Mars and Mars–Venus configurations allow risks of fire, shipwreck, robbery, thieves or sailors during **unspecified unfavourable astrological directions**. It recommends postponing travel unless a stronger benevolent direction contradicts Mars. Both the hazard and its overruling mitigation are *conditional and unspecified in time*: do not turn them into a definite dated tragedy, guaranteed travel safety or medical/commercial recommendation.

The last section subdivides countries by signs: Capricorn associated with general safety; Pisces with fortune; Libra with commerce and esteem; Sagittarius with ventures involving personal travel or commercial stock. Each is further **geographically qualified**, e.g. Capricorn south/west, Pisces northern cities, Libra west/south and Sagittarius east/northeast. These are historical schematic territorial associations, not reproducible modern actual country lists, independently corroborated favourable investment destinations or evidence of specific journey outcomes. The text defers judgment about dreams and religion partly to the native, then adds the assumption he will follow the faith of his upbringing. Record both the nonprediction and limited generic religious conjecture.
