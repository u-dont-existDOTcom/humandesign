## Whole-span reconstruction before rule drafting

### S01. Chapter XL — Whether a Woman be with Childe or not (PDF263–264; printed229–230)

The question is asked by a woman. Lord1 and Moon signify the querent; fifth house and lord5 signify conception, **if any**. Lord1 in fifth **or** lord5 in ascendant, free from impediments, argues she is conceived. A further route is translation of lord1's virtue/disposition to any planet in an angle; reception between the recipient and lord1, stated in either direction, makes this more certain. The clause's punctuation does not justify requiring mutual reception rather than either of the expressly named directions.

When the planet receiving lord1's disposition is cadent, Lilly infers grief and regards supposed conception as more likely sickness. If conception does hold, he judges a poor end, especially with Aries/Cancer/Libra/Capricorn rising, a malevolent planet in an angle, or South Node in the locally stated context. He then distinguishes South Node in fifth (usually abortion) from South Node in ascendant (extreme fear and mistrust of it). Do not turn the latter into another categorical abortion sign.

In contrast, when the **ponderous** recipient is in a good house explicitly given as second/eleventh/ninth, not conjunct Infortunes, and Moon free, the conception ends well and the woman is delivered safely. The carryover on PDF264 adds lord5 in the ascendant free of misfortune; retrogradation, combustion, and being with South Node are the listed impediments. The phrase "or not with" is awkward and must not become a free-standing favorable South Node absence divorced from lord5's ascendant placement.

### S02. If a Woman doe Conceive with Childe of more then one? (PDF264; printed230)

First inspect whether Gemini/Virgo/Sagittarius/Pisces rises. Then see whether both Jupiter and Venus are in the rising sign, the fifth sign, or in any of the twelve signs **except Leo**. On the printed construction, this is a probability of two children, not certainty. The scope of the broad sign alternative is not silently narrowed to common signs or angular houses. With North Node accompanying Jupiter and Venus in ascendant or fifth, three are **possible**. If none of these are in ascendant/fifth, inspect their sextile/trine to the degree rising or fifth cusp; this is another probable multiple conception/pregnancy route. The antecedent of "these Planets" after the node clause is not fully resolved.

If a fixed or movable sign occupies ascendant or fifth, and Sun and Moon are therein—he explains fixed/movable signs and fifth/first house—he calls one child a certain argument. Preserve the grammatical uncertainty about whether both luminaries must share one sign/house versus the stated classes/locations. There is no supplied rule resolving a figure that triggers both the two-child and one-child routes. Before venturing a judgment, consider the rules carefully and learn whether multiple births are natural/usual in her family. This is a source instruction to inquire into family background; no modern predictive value is claimed.

### S03. If Male or Female; marginal heading Masculine Planets (PDF264–265; printed230–231)

Inspect ascendant and its lord, fifth sign and its lord. The six listed signs Aries/Gemini/Leo/Libra/Sagittarius/Aquarius import male; the other six female. In the specific conflict lord1 in a masculine sign and lord5 in a feminine sign, turn to Moon: inspect her sign and whether she applies to a planet in a masculine sign. If so she supports the masculine significator and a boy is judged. The reverse conflict and tie mechanics are not specified here.

Saturn/Jupiter/Mars/Sun are called masculine, Venus/Moon feminine. Mercury follows the sex of a planet he aspects or conjoins; alternatively, when oriental to Sun he is deemed masculine and when occidental feminine. The author does not state precedence when Mercury's association and solar orientation conflict. This is the author's binary historical system, not a modern biological or gender classification.

### S04. How long the Woman hath been Conceived? (PDF265; printed231)

Inspect Moon, lord5, and lord of hour. Select whichever is "neerest from the separation of any Planet" and consider the type of separating aspect. Trine → fifth **or third** month; sextile → second **or sixth**; square → fourth; opposition → seven months; conjunction → one month. No algorithm selects the alternative month or defines "nearest" in temporal versus arc terms. Preserve the given order 5/3 and 2/6, not a silently sorted single outcome.

### S05. Of the time when the Birth will be? (PDF265–266; printed231–232)

Consider when Mars and Sun conjoin lord5, Moon, lord of hour, **or the greater part of them**; he says that time of conjunction shows the hour of birth. The exact grouping/simultaneity and which set supplies the majority are ambiguous. A supporting technique uses the Fortune which in the question beholds the Part of Children: observe when it applies to the quarter of heaven holding the Part. Direct the Part by ascensions to the fifth cusp degree, Jupiter's degree, and Jupiter's aspects, especially if Jupiter lies between Part and fifth. Part applying to the relevant ascensional degrees and entering their orbs gives delivery, at one day per degree. This is source-directed ascensional work, not a warrant to substitute ordinary zodiacal transit timing or invent an orb.

Also inspect disposition/application of the planet to which the Part is directed, before the conjunction of lord5 and lord1 in ascendant or fifth; birth is about that time. There is a real attachment/ordering ambiguity in "before the conjunction", which does not authorize removing it. Additional routes: significator of the question changes sign; or lord5's distance from fifth cusp, counting one month per sign. Use other testimonies and judge by the majority of significators that most nearly concur. No numerical weighting/tie-break or exact-versus-inclusive distance convention is supplied.

Part formula is explicit and same by day/night: take Mars → Jupiter and project from ascendant. The clockwise/counterclockwise convention of "from ... to" is not redefined in this local paragraph; no standalone longitude implementation is asserted here.

### S06. Whether the Birth shall be by day or by night? (PDF266; printed232)

Inspect ascendant and lord1, Moon, planet in ascendant, lord5, fifth sign. Majority in diurnal signs → day, contrary → night. If significators disagree, choose the essentially strongest and judge by that. An alternative projects from the ascending degree the degree-distance between the selected planet and fifth cusp; a diurnal ending sign → day and the contrary → night. He supplies no essential-strength tie algorithm, no distance orientation, and no instruction for absent/multiple ascendant occupants.

For a related sex/day judgment inspect lord5, Moon, lord of hour, Part of Children, its dispositor. Majority in masculine signs → male and birth by day. Equal testimonies → twilight. The text does not expressly state the female/night converse in this last sentence; do not invent it. Whether the equal-testimony clause covers just this list or earlier day/night lists is not explicit.

### S07. Unnumbered "Some say" conception variants and malefic qualification (PDF266–267; printed232–233)

This is reported doctrine with an explicit "Some say" introduction; named author attribution is unavailable. Inspect lord1, lord5, Moon's dispositor, Moon. Application among these + Moon in common sign + ascendant common + significators angular, **or** a fortunate planet in first or second, gives pregnancy; otherwise not. The scope of the latter "or" against the preceding conjunction of conditions is not perfectly explicit. A direct planet conjunct Moon is another positive route. Jupiter/Venus in fifth, Moon in fifth applying to Jupiter/Venus, or a Fortune cazimi Sun, is a strong argument. Do not substitute generic combust for cazimi.

Infortunes placed as above instead of Fortunes provide no sign of conception. If conception was already assured before asking, the same adverse pattern is instead presented as proof of abortion. This prior-fact condition changes the outcome; do not merge with infertility. Mars's affliction is associated with flux of blood; Saturn's with sickness/fear/frights/excess wind and water. ### S08. Unnumbered mother-after-birth and infant-life paragraphs (PDF267; printed233)

If asked about the mother's state after birth, identify Moon's final planetary application before she exits her present sign. The recipient's nature, heavenly position, and fortitude describe the mother's condition after birth.

Lilly then reports his practice concerning mother's safety/condition **when pregnancy is evident**: ascendant free and lord1 neither separated from nor applying to a bad aspect of lord8/lord4; **or** Moon fortunately applying to a Fortune, Sun, or a good aspect of an Infortune. He says he never doubted the mother's life and does not remember failing. Preserve the disjunction and do not strengthen an autobiographical recollection to independent or quantified validation. The grammar leaves uncertain whether ascendant-free also qualifies the lunar alternative.

Lilly reports judging infant short survival when lord5 is in eighth, has no essential dignities in its sign, and has any aspect good or bad to lord8 or lord4; he says his prediction was always true. Separate historical report from verification. He further claims births at the very change of Moon seldom outlive the next full Moon; birth at the moment of full Moon has a very probable death at next new Moon. His stated rationale is that earthly light comes from the two luminaries and lasting life is not believed possible when both at birth are afflicted intrinsically or by Infortunes. Preserve "seldome", "very probable", and "I ... beleeve", not certainty. No supplied clinical evidence, exact phase tolerance, or modern biological inference follows.

### S09. Whether Unity is like to be betwixt the Infant and the Parent, or betwixt the Parent and any of his Children of elder Yeers (PDF268–269; printed234–235)

Lilly says this is better resolved from the nativity, but offers horary because few can judge one. Inspect lord1, Moon, lord5. Reception and application between lord5 and lord1, in tenth/eleventh/fifth/third/ninth/first/second, indicate love and unity. Alternatively Jupiter or Venus beholding both house cusps indicate unity/concord. No aspect kind or exact placement assignment is specified.

Lilly's practice first examines Saturn/Mars/South Node in fifth; his next clause says "if those two Planets, or any Planet who is posited in the house be Peregrine" and attributes the unruly/noncompliant child's conduct wholly to the child/young man/maid. Retain the peregrinity scope problem and node/planet distinction; do not reduce the whole clause to any fifth-house malefic. Saturn/Mars/South Node in ascendant instead assign responsibility to the inquiring parent. Mars is said to be too lordly, overawing and restrictive; Saturn too austere, dogged, close-fisted, demanding disproportionate service/duty/obedience/attendance, without encouragement; South Node too jealous/mistrustful, credulous about lies/calumnies and vain reports. These are specific historical attributions, not accepted diagnoses.

He says this last part of judgment can apply to other questions and reports reconciling parents and children through it. He expressly asks astrologers to deal fairly and truthfully wherever the fault lies. This qualification prevents rewriting the passage as unconditional deference to the parent. The scope of "last part" and "any other Question" is not exhaustively defined; no universal modern counseling method is inferred.

### S10. Chapter XLI — Of Embassadours or Messengers (PDF269–270; printed235–236)

Lord5 represents ambassador; Moon also signifies. Planet receiving lord5's or Moon's application shows cause of embassy; judgment may use both recipient planets. An application from a Fortune by square/opposition/conjunction, with reception or collection/translation by a planet, and that planet lord10 or in tenth, indicates business to king on honor, high affairs, or urgent occasion. If the received/collecting/translating planet is lord11, purpose is renewing a league of friendship between nations. Pronoun attachments and exact logical alternatives in the first sentence are not rewritten as a single executable test.

Lord5 unfortunate in seventh + lord1 and lord5 square/opposition + Mars's malicious aspect to either/both: no likely unity/contentment, mutual trivial provocations, no expected solid peace and probability of variance. For fairness/falseness/evasion of ambassador, inspect lord5's good/ill condition, its aspect to ascendant/lord1/lord11, and house placement. Lord5 essentially dignified in tenth is nevertheless read as excessive concern for own prince's honor and overconfidence in own ability. Do not simplify essential dignity to invariably positive diplomatic conduct.

Mercury square/opposition lord5 gives alternative outcomes: insufficient commission, countermand/contradiction by prince's dispatch, dishonest secretary, or ill-received message. Generally, ambassador significator sextile/trine lord1 or ruler of the sign assigned to the kingdom under consideration means benevolence toward the kingdom and sincerity, **whether well dignified or not**. No national-sign table is supplied here; do not invent one or let the general condition erase separately stated warning routes.
