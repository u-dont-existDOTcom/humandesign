## Whole-boundary reconstruction

The source progresses from the general question of ever having issue, to a long-childless married woman's capacity to conceive, to when children may arrive, then repeats broader man/woman fertility testimony. It narrows to having children with a named partner, then to the woman's suspected current pregnancy, then a man asking without the woman's knowledge. The final subdivisions treat sex, survival and twins. These are distinct question contexts and outcomes. “Have Children,” capacity to get/conceive them, actual present conception, future conception, survival, fetal movement and sex must not be pooled as one outcome. No adoption proposition appears in this assigned span; interpreting “have Children” as adopted children would be an added interpretation, whereas several passages expressly concern natural causes, conception, getting children or coition.

The passage uses multiple testimony families and does not supply one universal score. It explicitly says to attend to age and natural/hereditary infirmity, seldom conclude without two testimonies, judge by the major testimonies in one pregnancy subsection, treat some starred testimonies as additions, distinguish essential dignity from merely accidental hope in one reception rule, and retain an anonymous angular-receiver variant even after stating a negative no-aspect conclusion. Those qualifications belong with their local rules; they are not a license to invent thresholds or weights.

## 1. CHAP. XXXIX — “If one shall have Children, yea or no?”

**PDF256–257 / printed222–223.** Context: a man or woman long before marriage, or an ancient bachelor or maid, asks whether ever to have a child or children.

- Initially consider whether the fifth-house sign or ascending sign is fruitful; the named fruitful signs are Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces. The grammar says to consider these signs and then considers the following aspect. It does not cleanly state whether fruitful signs are an indispensable conjunctive gate for every subsequent positive rule.
- The ascendant lord (whatever sign it occupies) or Moon in aspect with the fifth lord by conjunction, sextile, trine or square is favorable, provided the planet to which the fifth lord applies, or with which it is in aspect, is free from combustion and other accidental or essential misfortunes. The source expressly observes that conjunction is not properly an aspect. The claim is that the querent shall have issue before death, not necessarily soon. Opposition is not in this particular list; later “any aspect” passages should not silently replace this list.
- Similarly, fifth lord in the ascendant, or Moon or ascendant lord in the fifth, is a strong argument of children/issue.
- If neither ascendant lord nor Moon applies to fifth lord, rendering virtue or light to one another, or translation or collection by/from the principal significators, still supports issue, but later than under the first mode of judgment. “Rendering,” translation and collection remain distinct named mechanisms; no here-defined numerical delay.
- After all that, consider the planet receiving the disposition of the significators: freedom from retrogradation, combustion or cadency gives great hopes of issue. This is a separate receiver-condition check, not an assertion that every earlier promise survives every impediment.
- A further rule compares the Moon's separation and application: the planet from which it separated is lord of the fifth sign counted from the sign containing the planet to which it applies; those two planets must also aspect each other. This testifies children/a child. The direction of this derived fifth-sign relation matters.
- No aspect between those two planets makes issue unlikely. However, **“some say”** that even when those planets/significators do not aspect, an angular planet to which the Moon applies allows issue. Preserve the adverse statement and the anonymously reported exception as two source variants, not a resolved consensus.

## 2. “If a Woman aske, whether she may conceive?”

**PDF257 / printed223.** Context: a married woman long without children asks if likely to conceive.

Positive alternatives, as printed in one list: ascendant lord in seventh; fifth lord in first; first lord in fifth; fifth lord in seventh; seventh lord in fifth; Moon “with him”; good planets in ascendant; good planets with fifth lord; good planets in any angle. She may then conceive. “With him” has an uncertain antecedent, most immediately the seventh lord; do not invent a resolved association or convert “with” into a newly defined orb.

If none of those testimonies concurs and barren signs plus ill planets occupy the former places, the source gives both a current and future negative: not presently conceived and will not conceive hereafter. If good and bad planets are mixed, she may **perhaps** conceive or have children, but they will not live. These are separate qualifications, not a binary fertile/infertile classifier.

Cancer, Scorpio or Pisces in ascendant or fifth permits children. Leo or Virgo there signifies neither presently with child nor **hardly** later: the qualifier is weaker than an absolute future denial. For women long childless asking this question, inspect whether the nativity originally denied children. The text instructs this consultation; it does not here state an explicit numeric priority or an unconditional natal veto algorithm.

## 3. “At what time, or how long it may be ere she have a Childe?”

**PDF257–258 / printed223–224.** Prerequisite: according to natural causes she may have child/children, and the querent wants approximately when.

The fifth lord's house gives this exact, unusual table: first/ascendant → first year; second → second year; tenth → third year; seventh → fourth year; fourth → fifth year. Preserve **second house** as printed. Do not replace it with a more familiar house or fill missing houses.

Qualify with sign and motion: swiftness in a movable sign hastens time somewhat; a double-bodied sign does not manifest so soon; fixed signs prolong. Regardless of its sign, a significator swift in motion **and direct** hastens the business and makes accomplishment sooner. No quantitative conversion is supplied for these adjustments. No clear origin date for “first year” beyond the question context is separately specified.

## 4. “Whether the Querent shall have Children, be he Man or Woman that asketh?”

**PDF258–259 / printed224–225.** The leading package requires fortunate planets beholding the ascendant; ascendant lord in ascendant, tenth, eleventh or fifth; Jupiter well placed together with the ascending triplicity lord; and “he” not combust or retrograde. This permits the man to have/be capable of getting children; for a woman, to conceive and not be naturally barren. “He” most immediately follows the triplicity lord, but attachment to that planet alone versus the Jupiter/triplicity package is grammatically uncertain.

- Ascendant lord in fourth or seventh, with Jupiter in a good house, indicates a child a long time after asking.
- Ascendant afflicted/infortunated by malevolent planets, ascendant lord in an evil house/place, and Jupiter cadent, in eighth, combust, or insufficiently removed from sunbeams: few children, sickly, hardly any surviving. Preserve this package and alternative Jupiter impairments.
- Unfortunate Moon is a great sign of nonconception **or** no capacity to conceive.
- Fortune in fifth or benign aspect to its cusp gives hopes and strong testimony of a child in a short span.
- Infortune in fifth, ill dignified, combust, retrograde, slow etc. gives no children. The punctuation lists impairments without an explicit Boolean connective or complete list. Contrary condition: that infortune direct, swift, oriental, and in any essential dignity signifies children; more if Jupiter, Venus or Sun sextiles/trines it from good houses.
- Nearer fortune to the ascendant means earlier expectation of children; more remote means longer time. The source does not specify the distance metric or a numerical scale here.
- **“Others observe”** Jupiter in ascendant or fifth in a nonbarren sign as an argument for a child. Glyph verified separately at 300 dpi.
- There is also strength in “the Lord of the house”: angular, with reception of the lord of that angle, or in eleventh or fifth with like reception, is sure testimony of children. The antecedent of “the house” is unclear in this paragraph; preserve rather than silently assigning fifth lord, ascendant lord or Jupiter's dispositor. Reception direction is likewise not given as an explicit dignity formula here.
- General qualification **“In all Questions concerning Children”**: attend to querent's age and natural/hereditary infirmity, and **“seldome conclude without two testimonies.”** “Seldome” is not an absolute never; independence/weight of testimonies is not defined.

## 5. “If a Man shall have Children by his Wife yea or not, or of any other Woman whom he nominates.”

**PDF259–260 / printed225–226.** Also expressly covers a woman asking about issue with a named man. It concerns children by that named partner, not a generic fertility claim divorced from the partner.

Examine ascendant, its lord and Moon. Ascendant lord **or** Moon joined to fifth lord supports issue by that party. Failing that, translation from fifth lord to ascendant lord supports children after some time. Ascendant lord or Moon in fifth, or fifth lord in ascendant, permits children. If none of these, consider whether ascendant lord, Moon and fifth lord join a planet more ponderous than themselves; it collects “both their lights,” receives disposition, and indicates whether child/children **if any** live. The list contains three named significators but says both lights; preserve this ambiguity, and do not turn the conditional survival inquiry into unconditional conception.

Collector free from impediment → children live; collector retrograde, combust, peregrine or otherwise unfortunate → children do not live long, nor do parents take comfort from them.

After this consider Jupiter as natural significator of children: in first, third, fifth, ninth or eleventh, free from every misfortune, woman shortly conceives, **perhaps** at the first coition after asking or a little after; author calls matter as good as done. Do not turn “perhaps” into a fixed one-coition timing rule. Venus in fifth without impediment plus another fortune there hastens to very sudden conception. Jupiter impeded in the aforesaid houses → either not conceived, or, if conceived, no completion and abortion.

Venus unfortunate through Saturn/Mars, under sunbeams or combust → no conception, **unless** a fortune is in fifth; then more assuredly already with child or shortly to be, but there is fear of mischance before birth. Keep present/future alternatives and feared-loss modality. Saturn or Mars, especially south node, in fifth, or Saturn/Mars opposition to fifth, makes it **seem** she is not with child; square of infortunes to fifth **seems** to hinder conception. These are not identical outcomes or categorical statements.

## 6. “Whether she is with Childe or not.” — margin “Shee is.”

**PDF260–262 / printed226–228.** Context: woman suspects pregnancy, asks for truth, figure erected for time of her demand.

Positive testimonies:

- Ascendant lord **or** Moon beholds fifth lord by any aspect or translation.
- Ascendant lord **and** Moon in fifth, direct and free from malevolent aspect of infortunes.
- The preceding infortune category is expressly broadened: do not rely wholly on Saturn, Mars or south node as the only unfortunates. Consult the actual figure. Lord of sixth, eighth or twelfth, whatever planet, afflicts fifth lord, ascendant lord or Moon by square/opposition. Marginal clarification calls Saturn/Mars planets, not the node; preserve this terminology distinction.
- Jupiter generally in first, fifth, eleventh or seventh, without aspect to Saturn/Mars when they are slow or retrograde. “They” appears to be Saturn/Mars; retain uncertainty instead of applying the motion condition silently to Jupiter.
- Ascendant lord or fifth lord aspects angular planet with reception and renders virtue to it.
- Moon in reception with an angular planet **essentially fortified**, else not. Accidental dignities in this kind of judgment give hope, not real assurance. Do not erase this explicit limit on assurance or flatten essential/accidental fortification.
- Ascendant lord amicably aspects ascendant from a good house; or Moon in seventh beholds seventh lord in eleventh; or Moon in eleventh beholds seventh lord in seventh. These three alternatives remain explicit.
- Ascendant lord received in house, triplicity or exaltation, with receiver also having like dignity in house, triplicity, exaltation **or term** of the received ascendant lord. This is an asymmetric written reception list; not a generic dignity rule with interchangeable face/term/exaltation.
- Moon renders virtue/light to a planet in fifth **or** having essential dignities in fifth.
- Moon applies to ascendant lord or fifth lord in first or tenth, and that lord is not cadent from its own house or exaltation.

### Embedded explanation: “A Planet cadent from his owne house.”

**PDF261 / printed227.** This is sign-relative, independent of mundane house placement. Mars in Aries, his own house, is angular in this sense in any of the twelve houses; Mars in Taurus is succeeding; Mars in Gemini is cadent from its own house. Apply the principle to the rest; every planet is angular in any of its own houses. The source mentions exaltation in the preceding rule but gives the worked illustration only for domicile. It gives no explicit tie-breaking scheme for a planet with multiple domiciles.

### Starred addition to other testimonies

**PDF261–262 / printed227–228.** A marginal asterisk says **“These added to other testimonies.”** It marks a run of supplementary testimonies, beginning with Moon's dispositor and hour lord in angles. Preserve the additive/corroborative character; exact end of the asterisk's scope is typographically uncertain.

- Moon's dispositor and hour lord angular.
- Mars in the sign of seventh → newly conceived, understood only if Mars well fortified.
- Saturn in seventh → she is quick/the infant moves.
- Jupiter in seventh → male child.
- Sagittarius or Pisces in seventh → girl, **when the rest of the significators are equal and do not balance the judgment**; source repeats the conditional instruction. Do not normalize away the unusual Sagittarius/Pisces claim.
- Moon in fifth applying to Jupiter or Venus “argue the same.” Nearest antecedent is a girl, but the pregnancy-focused context means this is a real antecedent ambiguity, not settled by this extractor.
- True conception if ascendant fixed with a fortune therein, **or** fifth lord strong in ascendant or tenth. The source's “You may ever predict” is recorded as its assertion, not endorsed.

## 7. “If the Man aske unknowne to the Woman.” — “Shee is.”

**PDF262 / printed228.** A distinct questioner/knowledge context, retained without inventing consent or social conclusions.

Fifth lord beholds an angular planet with reception; **or** hour lord, fifth lord, Jupiter, Venus, Sun, Moon, Mercury **or north node** in fifth fortunate; **or** fifth lord in seventh; **or** seventh lord in fifth. This is the positive list. Commas and terminal “or” in the body list plausibly make these individual alternatives, but the exact all/any grouping is not formally stated. “Fortunate” scope across the list is not mechanized. No code-ready predicate will be silently invented.

## 8. Same section — “She is not.”

**PDF262 / printed228.** Printed negative cluster: Jupiter or Venus impeded; Venus joined to Saturn or Mars, and “they” combust, retrograde, slow, or in Leo/Virgo/Capricorn; Saturn or Mars in fifth, square/opposition fifth lord. These denote no conception, **or danger of abortion if other significators are more prevalent and testify conception**. Preserve the shift from no conception to threatened loss under stronger positive testimony. The punctuation does not provide an unambiguous Boolean grouping for all these clauses, and “they” is not perfectly resolved.

Another cluster: ascendant lord joined to a retrograde planet, or a planet in a cadent house, or received by a retrograde or combust planet; no aspect or translation of light between fifth lord and ascendant lord; **“judge by the major testimonies.”** The conjunction of this no-aspect clause with the preceding alternatives is unspecified; do not treat the whole cluster as an absolute contradiction of positive testimony.

## 9. Same section — “Male or Female.”

**PDF262 / printed228.** Ascendant lord, fifth lord and hour lord masculine, and Moon in masculine sign, degrees or quarter, note a male; contrary, a female. “Degrees” and “quarter” are alternatives as printed and not defined in this local span; no invented definitions. “Contrary” is retained without a newly created mixed-testimony rule.

## 10. Same section — “Whether it shall live?”

**PDF262 / printed228.** Fifth lord retrograde, combust or cadent from its house/exaltation presages death, **“& e contra.”** Ascendant lord, hour lord, fifth lord all or most unfortunate is argument of death. Saturn, Mars or south node in first or fifth, **and Retrograde**, denotes same. The retrograde predicate is printed after a list including a node; do not delete it on modern astronomical assumptions or manufacture a special exemption. These are historical survival claims only.

## 11. “Where suspition is had of Twins” — margin “Twinnes.”

**PDF263 / printed229, above the divider only.** Prerequisite: suspicion of twins and a question on that subject. Textual package: ascendant common, fortune in it “or the fifth or first House,” and Sun and Luna in common signs; **or** sign of fifth a common one and fifth lord in a common sign. Twins may be judged. Preserve the placement alternatives, Sun **and** Moon condition, and the second fifth-sign/lord alternative. The punctuation and repeated first-house placement leave the grouping of ascendant-common, fortune-placement and luminaries partly ambiguous. Common/double-bodied classification is a source term, not a new sign table supplied from memory.
