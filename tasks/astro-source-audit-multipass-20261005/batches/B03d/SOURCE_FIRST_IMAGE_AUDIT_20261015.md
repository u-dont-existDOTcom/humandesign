## Material uncertainties and interpretation controls

- **Different question types**: whether there are unnamed adversaries vs whether a specific nominated individual is an adversary; the first source pathway uses the twelfth and the named pathway seventh, with a marginal exception for relations.
- Seventh lord's direct twelfth house is not identical to a twelfth sign counted from first lord or Moon. The clause involving intermediate planets and missing reception has uncertain attachment across OR branches.
- A separating vs applying hard aspect changes the author's alleged timeline of conflict; do not replace both with one timeless personality label.
- Under the general question, the source permits Moon/twelfth-lord hard aspects with or without reception; do not copy the opposite reception prerequisite from the named-person branch.
- The author's marginal “Often and ever by me found true” is *his own anecdotal assertion*, not independent evidence. His claims of masked hostility, concealed conspiracy, and unreconcilable enemies are unverified and potentially harmful if applied to contemporary individuals.
- Multiple planets in seventh is an authorial enemy-count claim with no independent rate, and ill-dignified planets in second are a different financial interpretation. A square allegedly permits reconciliation but opposition without reception uses categorical language; neither is a measured probability.
