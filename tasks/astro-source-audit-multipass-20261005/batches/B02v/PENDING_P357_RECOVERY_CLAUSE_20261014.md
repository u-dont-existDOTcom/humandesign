# Pending printed p357 clause-completeness correction (original PDF391)

**Status:** source-image finding, NOT applied to the current 402-unit draft or U55 archive.

The printed `Aphorismes concerning Recovery` passage says:
`when one of them applyeth bodily to the Planet that signifieth recovery; the thing stole shall then be recovered`. Here `one of them` refers to the Moon or lord of the ascendant from the preceding clause. The printed continuation specifies different temporal behavior for retrogradation versus direct application.

**Provisional R2762** currently says the Moon or ascendant lord's bodily application `is said to give the time of recovery`. That captures the timing role but **omits the source's explicit affirmative return-of-goods consequent** for this condition. A later correction should retain both aspects, without turning this historical claim into an independently observed successful retrieval.
