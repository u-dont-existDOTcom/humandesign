# Corrected date in Lilly’s worked sick-doctor chart

**Original source:** William Lilly, *Christian Astrology* (1647), Wellcome original PDF320 / printed286, below the divider beginning Chapter XLV. Full-volume SHA256 `2cb53e20e122ffc6e47917ba10241f6c49687cd7a4696f3b5db655a2a1aab28b`.

**Correction:** The chart caption reads **`♃ 8°. Maij.`** beneath **1645**, followed by **`6:15' P.M.`** and **`hor: astr:`**. The first glyph is **Jupiter’s sign ♃**, representing Thursday, **not the numeral 24**. The case is therefore captioned **Thursday 8 May 1645 (Julian / English Old Style)**, not 24 May 1645, which an earlier B02n continuation note erroneously stated. Julian 8 May 1645 corresponds to Gregorian 18 May 1645 and fell on Thursday, corroborating the weekday glyph. The printed hour is retained in its original convention; no unsupported modern timezone or birth-time conversion is inferred.
