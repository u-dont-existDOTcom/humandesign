# Project-local source-audit lessons: Lilly XLV

The Jupiter weekday glyph next to `8 Maij` was initially mistaken for `24`. Preserve the original glyph and separate contextual Julian calendar interpretation from what is actually printed. Cross-check the weekday; do not infer a modern time zone.

A single historical chart, author observations, patient complaint as relayed, astrological inference, therapy suggestion, and the author's reported death form ONE case with different evidence classes. The printed duration interpretation follows the death report and cannot be counted as a prospectively frozen forecast.

Early-modern `out of his Termes` and `out of Angles` remain ambiguous; do not convert these into Boolean negative dignity/angle rules. Partial diagram observations are not modern ephemeris parity.

**Review-packet discoverability:** When a reviewer is restricted to Read-only access with no directory listing, the prompt must name *exact relative file paths*, especially the next chapter's boundary: here `evidence/boundary-pdf323.jpg`. Merely saying PDF323 is supplied produced numerous unsuccessful `.png` guesses. Record what pages the independent reviewer actually read, rather than equating source-file presence with review coverage.

Pixel parity, hash checks, mutation probes, structural tests and cross-family reviews are bounded source-fidelity work, NOT clinical efficacy or predictive validity. No universal policy change or new acceptance gate is introduced by this local lesson.
