## First-hand reading, preserving the source claim structure

The heading concerns a sick doctor and asks the disease and whether curable. The center of its image has **1645; ♃ [Jupiter weekday] 8°. Maij; 6:15' P.M.; hor: astr:**. Thus a **Thursday 8 May 1645 Julian** chart, **not May24**. Julian Thursday8 May corresponds to Gregorian Thursday18 May in that century. Neither the planetary weekday glyph nor the old date supplies a modern time-zone label. The diagram central aspect line uses glyphs and Latin abbreviations, which cannot be treated as a complete automatically verified ephemeris export.

The ascendant sign is **Scorpio**, with the **Chele/Claws** fixed stars reported near it but without an evil planet afflicting the ascendant by position or presence. The author therefore turns to the sixth house and finds **Saturn in its fall** affecting the illness house.

The source gives **Aries = head**, **Saturn in Aries = breast**, **Mars the ascendant ruler in Leo = heart**, with page113/244/245 backward references. Mars is said to have recently separated from a **dexter square of Saturn**, the author calling the relevant signs cardinal, and Mars being in **Cancer at the time of that square**, said to signify breast and stomach. These independent time contexts (current chart placement versus prior aspect-time placement) must not be collapsed. The author concludes head/breast/heart/stomach and a **melancholic obstruction** in breast or stomach.

Saturn is assigned main disease authorship: in its own terms, with the Moon in its house applying to it; melancholy and dry diseases are hypothesised in head/breast. The writer directs readers to a page244 catalogue rather than repeating every potential disease. Mars lord ascendant in Saturn's terms, Moon outside his terms applying to a **square of Sun**, Sun in Mars's terms: **choler** is named a secondary cause.

The writer says **when he came to speak to the patient** he reported head pain and rumbling; silence/dullness/melancholy; little sleep, a dry cough; breast and heart pain and weakness; complexion black/yellow, possible jaundice. The patient allegedly also had wasting consumption, great weariness throughout joints, and difficulty urinating with **red gravels**, pain in secrets/bladder. Moon in an **airy sign**, Scorpio rising and another Moon-sign placement are invoked as explanatory tokens; a glyph or traditional body correspondence should remain a literal source claim, not repaired to what another chart might suggest.

The author says he had **little medical knowledge** and suggested the doctor prescribe for himself medicines described as gently **hot, moist, cordial**, allegedly to prolong life. He cites Moon in the fourth in sextile to Saturn as arguing illness until death. He says the patient **died the fourteenth of August following**. The death date is the author's own retrospective testimony, **not independent verification or evidence of a prospective blind prediction**. No individual outcome in this passage may enter a validation cohort without external dated evidence and a prediction freeze.

A later subsection asks whether the disease would be long or short. Saturn slow/ponderous as main significator; angles fixed; Sun and Moon in fixed signs, in square, out of angles and in terms of an infortune; Mars lord ascendant in a fixed sign. These are presented as converging evidence of duration. **Mars's antiscion near Sun** is additionally called an affliction to the luminary of the time. No exact orb, house system implementation, Julian calendar conversion, event-timing arithmetic or trustworthy clinical prognosis is supplied by this prose.

## Editorial hazards to preserve

1. Chart's weekday ♃ is not the number24. Distinguish 8 May Julian from 18 May Gregorian and do not give a new clock timezone.
2. Single reported doctor, single chart, repeated symptoms and several testimonies: **one case**, not separate patients or independent prediction successes.
3. Retrospective claim of death has no documented answer-key blindness or independently verified source.
4. Source headings 'What part ...', 'From what Cause...', 'Whether long or short?' must remain distinct without counting an unnumbered heading as new chart.
5. The later aspect (Mars separated from Saturn) explicitly refers to an earlier time when Mars was in Cancer; do not force that into the chart's present sign of Leo.
6. The medicinal advice described in the original source is historical documentation, not a recommendation for current patient care.
7. Source authorial case count = 1 and chart count = 1. The next CHAP. XLVI is a different physician question and diagram dated July1645; stop at divider.
