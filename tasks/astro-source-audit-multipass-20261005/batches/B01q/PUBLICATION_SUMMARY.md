## Useful additions

The geographical chapter preserves four directional triangles, eight outer/central groups,24 individual-sign refinements and twelve summary rows containing73 entries. The reported transmitted total72 and narrative/list discrepancies remain explicit. Central regions add the opposite triangle and Mercury; they do not simply replace the parent quarter. Historical population stereotypes are source assertions, not current demographic facts or a classifier.

City-founding Sun/Moon/centres, especially the Ascendant, are primary. The natal-Midheaven alternative is conditional on missing foundation times; main-text officeholder/king wording and Robbins's founder/prominent-person explanation are separately attributed.

The eclipse chapters keep four questions separate:region, time/duration, affected classes and event quality. Local ordinary seasonal hours differ from the equinoctial duration hours. The symbolic rule maps each equinoctial hour to a year for a solar eclipse or a month for a lunar eclipse; it does not supply a complete civil-date or contact convention.

The three angular reference cases distinguish onset during a four-month period after the eclipse from intensification during a third of the full event duration. Trigger phases require the event-producing planetary role and an aspect to the causal signs. Intensity is not a good/bad score, and abatement is not automatically cure.
