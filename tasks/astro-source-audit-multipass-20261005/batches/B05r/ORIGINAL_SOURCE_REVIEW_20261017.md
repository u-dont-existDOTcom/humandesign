Lilly says that for general questions about sibling count one should primarily examine **the nativity of the first-born child**, since its chart allegedly shows the siblings to be born subsequently. If that chart is unavailable, he allows another native's chart for the conditions and qualities of siblings. This is not a demonstrated demographic forecast and contains no control for family planning, infant survival, cohort or geographical fertility patterns.

The passage introduces three separate questions: whether siblings exist, what their condition is, and whether siblings will live together in amity and concord. These are different outcomes; the following numbered chapters must be examined separately rather than importing their rules into this introduction.
