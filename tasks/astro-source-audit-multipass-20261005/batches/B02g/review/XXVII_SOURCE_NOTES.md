## Source obligations, conditions and alternatives

Abbreviations here only: L1/L2/etc. = lord of the named house; F = Part of Fortune; Moon is the Moon. These notes are paraphrases of historical horary doctrine, not empirical findings or practical recommendations.

### Wealth and means (PDF201-206)

1. XXVII asks whether a querent will be rich or have a competent fortune, by what means, when, and whether it continues. For a *general* wealth question without reference to a particular prospective benefactor, the Ascendant, its lord, and Moon signify the querent regardless of station. The 2nd cusp/sign, its lord, occupants, aspects to its lord/cusp, and F's sign, heavenly position and received aspects must be considered. F and both nodes emit no rays/aspects.
2. General favorable signs: all planets angular; or planets succedent, direct and swift; or planets in good houses, direct, and at least moderately essentially dignified. These are general testimonies, not complete standalone quantitative forecasts.
3. PDF202's connected alternatives: L1 or Moon corporally joined L2; L1 and Moon having friendly aspect to L2; Jupiter/Venus trine or sextile or conjunction F; L2 in Ascendant; Moon or L1 in 2nd; another planet translating L2's light/virtue to L1; beneficent planets sextile/trine Ascendant cusp or F; a fixed star of Jupiter/Venus nature ascending with 2nd cusp; F conjunct/near such star; Jupiter, Venus or North Node in 2nd with no infortune aspect to them; all planets direct and faster than their mean daily motions. References visibly include pp. 57, 61, **65**, 69, 72, 76, 80.
4. Result phrased as competent riches/sufficient subsistence and freedom from fear of poverty. Estate size follows the major testimonies, requiring careful examination of relevant significators and the querent's quality/capacity (*Quoad capax*). No numeric vote count, probabilistic threshold, or monetary quantity is supplied.
5. Means-of-wealth judgment follows an already sufficient judgment of subsistence/riches. L2 in 2nd -> own labor/industry; L2 Ascendant -> unexpected fortune or little labor. If L2 or Moon promises substance through their aspect to one another, inspect the house aspect comes from and Moon's ruled house. If neither promises substance, examine F's house and the house its dispositor rules.
6. The *assisting/promising* planet being L1 -> own diligence; for a mean/mechanical man, hands, invention, care and labor. Being L2 -> own stock, management and buying/selling what naturally fits his life or the planet's nature, considering the sign.
7. L3 must fortunate L2, 2nd cusp or F: honest neighbor, kin/siblings if any, journey, or removal toward the quarter from which L3 gives the good aspect/conjoins the substance significator. Do not reduce to siblings alone or omit the favorable contact.
8. Favorable planet/significator being L4 *or placed in 4th*: living father's aid, aged person/grandfather, farm/grounds, purchase of property, management of ancestral stock, or kin's loan. Father's assistance is conditioned on living.
9. Wealth-promising L5: gentleman's games/sports; capable courtier's embassy/message; ordinary man's victualling house, inn/tavern/bowling alley or keeper/porter work. Separately, strong L5 promises something from father's estate or match-making for any querent. Historical class qualifiers remain source qualifiers.
10. Sixth-house passage begins: L6, significator, or assistant planet in 6th **and its sign humane** -> good servants and their profitable labor. Following contextual alternatives: king/prince subjects' subsidies/loans; noble/gentleman leases and steward/bailiff management; farmer/husbandman small cattle; scholar physician's salary. Do not detach these from the successful-means context or silently make this practical vocational advice.
11. L7 must fortunate L2, 2nd cusp, F or a 2nd-house planet: rich/good wife or loving woman's assistance; for a gentleman sword/war or legal recovery; bargains; trade acquaintances if merchant. Preserve alternatives.
12. L8 fortunating those significators: testamentary legacy; unexpectedly enlarged wife's portion; **or** unforced, unforeseen settlement in another country and prosperity there. The relocation alternative must not disappear into inheritance alone.
13. L9 giving virtue/fortunating F, L2 or cusp: sea voyage if **Cancer or Pisces** on 9th cusp **and that sign's lord there**; wife's brother/allied person/neighbor of her earlier residence, or religious person/minister assisting vocation. An earth sign on 9th cusp with its lord there supports relocation to sign/quarter's place and dealing in that place's commodities. Do not generalize Cancer/Pisces to all water signs.
14. 10th route alternatives: fortunate L2 in 10th; L10/L2 reception; or L10 benevolently regarding L2, 2nd cusp, occupant or F. Means: service of king/prince/noble/master etc. Young, poor querent: craft according to 10th sign/lord, **if capable and fit**. Educated and desiring preferment: office/public employment.
15. Benevolent fortunating L11 -> friend's recommendation or merchant/courtier/great person's servant, unexpectedly beneficial employment/fortune.
16. Fortunate planet casting the previously described aspect **in 12th** -> great cattle/horse races; imprisonment/prisoners if 12th sign humane; **Taurus, Capricorn or Aries** -> cattle; **Virgo** -> corn. Source adds mix judgment with reason. These are placements of the fortunate planet, not simply L12's lordship.
17. The author's “most assured” *horary* wealth-and-continuance testimony: L1, L2 and Jupiter conjoined in 2nd, 1st, 10th, 7th, 4th or 11th; failing conjunction, applying sextile/trine with mutual reception; even applying square/opposition **with reception** permits an estate with much labor and intervening difficulties, more abundance than want. No calibrated confidence is supplied; conjunction placement clause should not silently be rewritten as a universal numerical strength rule.

### Obstruction (PDF206-207)

18. This inquiry is needless where the chart already signifies obtaining an estate; it is for a denied great fortune when cause is requested. Inspect planet obstructing/most afflicting L2, F, 2nd cusp, Moon or F's lord/dispositor. L1 culprit -> self; L2 square/opposition to F or 2nd cusp -> insufficient money/stock for employment; L3 -> kin unhelpful/burdensome or malicious neighbors capturing trade/underselling; extend through houses by the preceding scheme.
19. Essential dignity **or** good-planet aspect **or** a benevolent house may make malefic L2/F-dispositor a wealth significator. Conversely afflicted/impedited Jupiter/Venus (or significators as aforesaid, in the source's wording) can obstruct. Natural benefic/malefic type is not sufficient by itself.
20. South Node in any house denotes harm/impediment in that house's things. In 2nd source specifies own folly, non-thriving or proper neglect; 3rd ill/beggarly/peevish kin; analogical extension to others.

### Ordinary demands, borrowing and pledges (PDF207-208)

21. L1/Moon querent, L2 own substance, 7th/L7 the one asked for repayment/loan, 8th/L8 that person's substance. Applies to ordinary peers/community; expressly exempts kings/princes/nobles who pay slowly and escape ordinary law. Higher-status demand gets the distinct 10th/11th scheme below.
22. L1 or Moon joined L8, or joined/aspecting a planet in 8th, with fortune in 8th **or fortunate aspect**, supports money obtained, loan lent or pledge restored; benefic in 8th need not have reception. With malefic in 8th or as L8, reception of L1/Moon supports desire; no reception means hardly/ever obtaining, or only with regrettably great labor. This is weaker than an absolute “never” statement.
23. L8 in 1st or 2nd and L2 receiving him makes success **probable**. L7 or L8 in 1st/2nd with neither having reception of L1/L2/Moon gives denial/prejudice. The latter “have reception of” wording is less directionally explicit than “L2 receive him”; preserve this uncertainty if machine-normalizing direction.
24. L1 **and** Moon joined to a benefic having dignity in ascending or intercepted sign -> success. Any of them joined to malefic with dignity in Ascendant and receiving L1/Moon -> success. L1 **or** Moon joined to a fortunate planet well placed in 10th/11th -> perfection without reception. Do not erase the printed and/or switches without recording the source form.

### Payments from superiors (PDF208-209)

25. Applies to demands where querent is much inferior to payer, not merely a generic debt. Ascendant/L1/Moon querent, 2nd/L2 own substance; 10th/L10 superior, 11th/L11 superior's substance.
26. L1 or Moon joined L11 or an 11th-house planet that is benefic and **not in any measure impedited/ill disposed** -> owed salary/debt/money obtained. Moon **and** L1 joined malefic receiving them in essential dignities -> payment with much solicitation, weary visits, fears/distrust. Any aspect between significators with one malefic and no reception -> source says never obtains. Scrutinize actual essential dignities, mutual reception, and the specific dignity of reception.

### Timing (PDF209-210)

27. First identify the planet to which L1 or Moon applies/conjoins and which signifies accomplishment. Source mentions sextile/trine, benefic or otherwise, with or without reception, then degrees lacking at query to partile aspect/conjunction. Its literal conversion is: **both cadent -> days; succedent -> weeks; angular -> months**. Must judge whether the business can plausibly finish in the unit; long matters can take years instead of months, especially L1/Moon/other significators angular. Do not “correct” the order using a different traditional timing rule.
28. Mixed places: angle+succedent -> months; succedent+cadent -> weeks; angle+cadent -> months. No exact rule for competing planets, tie breaks, retrograde interruptions, calendars or ambiguity-free application geometry is provided here.
29. Attributed to “some of the Ancients”: completing planet and L1 in same sign at question, conclusion at degree/minute bodily conjunction if L1 more ponderous, reception or no reception. If L1 lighter and hastening to conjunction, the other planet must receive L1. Without reception, no accomplishment **unless** significators in an angle at conjunction, or in one of “his own houses,” especially his joy. Pronoun ownership/one-versus-both need caution; no invented parsing certainty.
30. Named joys are **Aquarius-Saturn, Sagittarius-Jupiter, Scorpio-Mars, Libra-Venus, Virgo-Mercury**. These are sign/domicile joys in this passage, not the separate mundane-house joy scheme.
31. Lilly's own observation: single reception by exaltation without other testimony does not profit; reception by domicile with benefic significators **usually** perfects, even square/opposition and beyond expectation; sextile/trine phrased more certainly. Distinguish received ancient rule from his observation; do not convert usual success into guaranteed success.
32. Lilly's further time observation: benefic, Moon or lord of sought thing in Ascendant **and essentially dignified there** -> distance in degrees from Ascendant cusp to body gives number of time units. Days with a movable sign **and a business capable of days**; otherwise months/years according to sign, quality and business nature. No complete fixed/common/movable lookup, wrap convention, threshold, or unique calendar date is supplied.
