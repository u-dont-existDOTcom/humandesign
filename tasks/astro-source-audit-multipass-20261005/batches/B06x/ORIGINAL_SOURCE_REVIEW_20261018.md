## Exact ranking of source testimony

Lilly gives four classes of indicators in an explicitly ordered form: **first**, the luminaries, especially the **Sun**, together with planets aspecting the lights **within the mediety of their orbs**, particularly the Sun; **second**, the Lord of Midheaven; **third**, the planets located at the Midheaven; **fourth**, *regal* fixed stars of first or second magnitude situated near the cusps of the tenth or first house. These are four source-ranked families, not independent human examples or computed percentile weights.

The question concerns honours, preferment, dignity and estimation in historical social terms. The introduction supplies neither numerical aspect-orb values, specific fixed stars or allowed distances from the cusps nor operational definitions of prestige or promotion.
