**Procedural rule:** Starting from an estimate-based natal figure, find the LAST NEW Moon before birth. If the last relevant syzygy was FULL Moon, take the zodiacal degree of the luminary (Sun or Moon) ABOVE the horizon at that opposition. At that selected longitude, evaluate traditional *essential dignities* and select the planet with the greatest rights. Compare that planet's position in the chart to the estimated **Ascendant** and **Midheaven**. If closer to Ascendant, retime/recast so that the Ascendant takes the selected planet's zodiacal longitude; if closer to Midheaven, set the MC to that planet. When two planets are tied in essential dignities, Lilly prefers the one whose position is nearest the Ascendant. Neither the historic dignity score nor the natal house positions have been independently ephemeris/house-recast.

**Retrospective merchant demonstration:** Lilly says the prior merchant natal figure was already rectified by *Accidents*, with original estimate differing from that adjusted chart by no more than approximately one ascendant degree. His selected prior syzygy was **Sunday 15 September 1616**, a full Moon four days before birth around **11 AM**. He gives the Sun, above the horizon, as **2°32′ Libra**, awards Saturn the greatest essential dignity from exaltation, triplicity and term, and by closeness to rising cusp suggests the Ascendant ought to be **9°02′ Capricorn**. He adds that treating Venus as the greater dignity holder would give a different, possibly compelling coincidence. This is comparison to **his own retrospectively adjusted chart**, not an independently certified actual birth instant. **Comparative reliability and rejection:** Lilly explicitly says **neither Animodar nor Trutine of Hermes is as sure** as rectification by life accidents, even while giving this Animodar illustration. He proposes the preliminary methods for children before significant dated life events become available.
