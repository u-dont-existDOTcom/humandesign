# Lilly original 1647 printed CIII — alleged infant survival and longevity indicators

Primary witness: first edition William Lilly, *Christian Astrology* (1647), Wellcome b30338724 complete original PDF SHA256 `2cb53e20e122ffc6e47917ba10241f6c49687cd7a4696f3b5db655a2a1aab28b`. CIII, Of the space of Life, or whether the Native is like to live long, or not** begins original PDF559/printed525, continues original PDF560/printed526 and ends on PDF561/printed527 **above a horizontal divider**. Beneath that divider begins separate printed **CHAP CIV** on the Hyleg/Apheta and killing/interficient planet. Original scan page561 is shared between chapters; no text is duplicated or merged.

**Historical procedure and scope:** Lilly advises reviewing the ascending degree, geniture ruler, five hylegical places and planetary condition before making a lifespan direction. He also proposes checking parents' natal charts for children's significators. He treats favorable rising sign/degree, benefic sextiles/trines, essential dignity, rapid planetary motion and favorable Ascendant ruler as indicators of longevity, and combustion, retrogradation, malefic aspects and various unfavorable house positions as indicators of short life. Separate conditions apply to Sun in daytime and Moon in nighttime births. All are *author-reported historical beliefs*, not observed child survival evidence or medical risk estimates.

**Contradictory source exceptions:** The printed author ascribes excess moisture to full-Moon births and dryness to new-Moon births and treats both as unfavorable, yet reports an exception from 'the Ancients': near-exact conjunction/opposition of luminaries with Jupiter or Venus on the Ascendant could indicate survival and activity/success (while still allegedly not reaching old age). Replacing the benefic with an infortune reverses the authorial conclusion. Preserve the conditional positive branch, negative branch and limited-old-age qualification rather than coding lunar phase as categorically unfavorable.

**Additional negative branches and safeguards:** Original PDF560–561 discusses severe luminary afflictions and combustion of the Ascendant ruler; many planets in houses 6/8/12; malefics near rising point; planets below horizon without dignity; nodes aligned with Mars/Saturn in fourth; day-Mars-in-Aries and night-Saturn-in-Capricorn above horizon; Moon/Mars/Saturn contacts; and allegedly maternal difficult labor. These are not diagnostic claims. In the closing passage, Lilly explicitly instructs the reader to consider **all significators and not pronounce death rashly**. That caution is part of the historical source, but it does not make natal mortality prediction safe or scientifically credible.

**Separate next chapter:** The opening below the PDF561 divider names Hylech/Hyleg/Apheta as the life prorogator and announces an interficient or 'killing' planet. Its three navigation annotations are not counted as rules belonging to printed CIII.
