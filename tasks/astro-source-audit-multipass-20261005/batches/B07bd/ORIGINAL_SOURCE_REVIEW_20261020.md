## Explicit historic claims, not confirmed outcomes

The source answers **whether this known merchant will marry** affirmatively (folio754), using a seventh-house Cancer/fruitful-sign premise, Moon near fifth/pleasure, a Moon-Mars sextile, Venus as a strong almuten, and conditional Saturn and cusp geometry. The same chapter predicts only small obstacles to seeking a wife, but allows interference from the woman's father, her relatives, a churchman or a brother-in-law. This is historically contingent astrological/social reasoning, not observed evidence.

Under a distinct heading on folio755, it says marriage should come **after age 29**, following Saturn's revolution. It does **not** give an exact marriage calendar year; the author explicitly says one could only know it through directions. For number of marriages, an initial Moon/Mars/Mercury reading “doe promise but one,” yet Venus's sign and purported Cazimi plus the Moon's subsequent aspects override it. Lilly concludes **exactly two wives** and **two additional “Sweet-hearts”**. These source-internal conflicting indications and the author's discretionary final preference are not a verified 4-event life series.

The first wife is described as shorter or average, relatively lean, fair and light-haired, scarred or blemished, strong-willed and frugal, with possible temporary quarrels. The second is described as taller, slim, reddish/dark-eyed, modest, religious, with a **mole on the forehead near the left eye**. These are specific historical claims, but no independent observer has verified either actual spouse. They do not support reliable modern personality or appearance profiling.

The wives' wealth section on folio756 predicts property, inheritance or fortune, but obstacles in obtaining it. It says **“almost three moneths, or perhaps so many Termes”** before a possible transfer—a crucial *explicit ambiguity* rather than a preregistered precise timing. The geography section forecasts first wife east/southward and not of the merchant's home shire, second wife far west but English, without modern birthplace coordinates or independent spouse records.
