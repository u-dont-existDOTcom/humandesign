## PDF282–283, printed 248–249: duration carryover and continuing signs

- PDF282 ends its duration discussion by saying common signs prolong infirmity, except Pisces on the sixth cusp, which Lilly says he has always found equivalent to a movable sign. PDF283 supplies the completion `it equivalent to a moveable Signe`. This is an expressly experiential authorial exception, not a general empirical validation. The preceding movable-sixth/Moon/no-other-impediment qualification belongs to reader A's range.
- Moon applying by ill aspect to the ascendant lord: disease increases.
- Moon in sixth in ill aspect with Venus: the source assigns self-caused disorder and bad diet. The following Venus-in-Scorpio clause attributes a sexual infection to contact with an `uncleane woman`. If a woman asks, the source instead mentions excessive Whites or Reds, or disease occasioned by her own folly. Preserve the sexist and moralizing nature as historical attribution, with the grammar linking these clauses left visible; do not modernize them into validated diagnoses.
- Sixth lord applying to ascendant lord by square or opposition: disease is increasing and not yet at its height/full growth. Sixth lord in eighth or twelfth is an ill argument and great presumption of more affliction before relief.
- Unfortunate planet in sixth moving out of one sign into another: disease will speedily alter. To judge when, the source counts degrees remaining before sign exit as as many months, weeks, or days according to the sign's nature and quality. This paragraph supplies no determinate unit-selection table.
- Sixth lord retrograde, combust, in eighth or twelfth, and square/opposition/conjunction Saturn, Mars, eighth lord, or fourth lord: much infirmity and a long continued and sudden change from better to worse, if not death itself. Punctuation does not supply a modern unambiguous Boolean grouping.
- Sixth lord in eighth and eighth lord in sixth, with sextile or trine between them: the printed sentence reads `you shall not doubt of the death of the Patient at that time`, but immediately explains that nature is not yet overcome or so weak that the sick cannot overcome it. Preserve that source tension; do not silently rewrite `death` or resolve the historical sense of `doubt`.
- Sixth lord trine/sextile/conjunction Jupiter, Jupiter in ninth, and Moon separating from sixth lord to Jupiter there: an `assured argument` that prescribed/already taken medicines made the party very sick while taken/operating, acted effectively in outward parts, and afterward brought comfort and improvement to the parts first afflicted. The sentence is retrospective or inferential historical content, not a clinical case report.
- Saturn, Mars, or any unfortunate planet in sixth threatens great danger; its qualifier continues onto PDF284.
