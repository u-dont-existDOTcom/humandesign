## Lower-severity findings (wording, scope, hedges)
- **R2197** (p.325 ll.2–4): reads as if common signs *indicate* dead things. In the original, dead vs. living is the condition and "within the house" is the result. Also restore "Ditches" and "quick or living things, or Catttle".
- **R2204** (l.26): the original says only "they are kept close"; remove the added "IN POUND".
- **R2201** (ll.14–15): the statement narrows "if you find none of these" to "separations", which contradicts its own note.
- **R2216** (p.326 ll.22–23): "the direction is replaced" is the candidate's addition. The original says only "in Closes or Pound, or under lock"; whether "east-ward" still applies is not said, so label it.
- **R2219 and R2294** (p.327 ll.5–6): restore "going away-ward" in the watery cadent clause.
- **R2224** (p.327 l.16): "new hills/land" should be "in new land, or ground full of hils".
- **R2234** (p.328 l.24): the one who recovers is the querent ("the Querent shall recover"), not the Lord of the ascendant.
- **R2235** (l.26): "applying to first" should be "apply to the Lord thereof".
- **R2241** (p.329 l.9): "zodiacal" is a guess; the original says only "in what quarter the ☽ is". Label the ambiguity and restore "or intendeth to goe".
- **R2250** (ll.33–34): restore "be found or come againe".
- **R2245** (ll.20–21): restore "in his first or second station".
- **R2236:** omits "by reason of her quick motion" and "and be recovered".
- **R2254** (p.330 ll.9–11): this method concerns only "he … from the place where he went from"; remove "OR THING LOST".
- **R2259** (ll.18–19): the original reads "having relation to the thing lost, and whether it be in a Town, or in the Fields"; the fugitive-or-thing-lost choice is invented.
- **R2265** (l.26): restore "contented to be separated for ever".
- **R2286** (p.326 l.8): restore the hedge "for usually". "Same quadrant as the querent" is an inference; the original only says "the same Quadrant".
- **R2253:** keep "according to probability" on the news alternative.
- **R2208:** "angular-quarter" is not in the original.
- **R2266:** the Thief heading is the phase boundary; keep it only as non-countable boundary metadata.

## Ambiguities that candidates resolved instead of labelling
- **R2200:** in "chiefly if the Lord of the sixt be cadent, or that the Cattle are dri[v]ed away or stolen", the last part could be an alternative outcome or a second condition. The "v" is damaged in print.
- **R2202:** in "in the eighth, in □ to any infortune in the fourth", only a comma joins the two parts, so it may be one joint condition or two separate ones.
- **R2205:** in "if they be found", "they" may be the Lord of the sixth, ♃/♀, or both.
- **R2225 and R2288:** "Lord of the twelfth and principall Significator" may be one planet or two.
- **R2262:** it is unclear whether "☉ under the earth" also applies to the "♀ Orientall" clause.
- **R2209:** "but one Signe between them" may mean within 30°, or one sign lying between them.
