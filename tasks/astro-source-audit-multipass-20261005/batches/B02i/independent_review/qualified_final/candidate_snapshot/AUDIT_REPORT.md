## 1. Mislaid objects require the owner and the kind of question

For one's own missing goods, Lilly begins with the second-house lord. For a brother's or sister's goods he uses the fourth; for the father's, the fifth; and for the mother's, the eleventh. The later paragraphs literally return to “lord of the second.” The source invites continuing according to the party, but this chapter does not restate a complete general substitution algorithm. The reference code therefore supplies the four explicit owner examples and records the limit.

Location depends on additional context. A tenth-house placement can point to a mechanic's shop, a gentleman's hall or dining room, or the husbandman's usual room. Air, fire, earth and water produce different descriptions, and an outside location must already be indicated before the bridge, stile, tree or elevated-ground alternatives apply. The watery clause also requires the former significators to be strong. Removing these prerequisites would turn a qualified location description into a broader claim than Lilly wrote. [XXXII, printed202–204 / PDF236–238; records R1010–R1016.](RULES.json)

Lilly first gives an hour-lord method attributed to earlier astrologers and then says he has not found it very exact. Its first branch names the tenth and eleventh houses. The twelfth is not silently inserted to complete a quadrant, and the treatment of angular endpoints remains unspecified.

His alternative is introduced for something missing or mislaid **inside a house and not stolen**. It considers eight sign slots: Ascendant, Ascendant lord, fourth cusp, fourth lord, Moon, second cusp, second lord and Part of Fortune. The greater number of testimonies chooses a quarter. The following direction table also expressly mentions fugitives, so its stated scope is recorded separately from the narrower introduction to the eight-slot procedure.

| Main quarter | First sign | Second sign | Third sign |
|---|---|---|---|
| East | Aries: east | Leo: east by north | Sagittarius: east by south |
| West | Libra: west | Gemini: west by south | Aquarius: west by north |
| North | Cancer: north | Scorpio: north by east | Pisces: north by west |
| South | Capricorn: south | Taurus: south by east | Virgo: south by west |

The code preserves those verbal directions. It does not invent exact azimuths, weights or a tie-break. If the same planet fills several named roles, the raw slot count and the repeated physical body are both visible; the count is not relabelled independent evidence. Lilly's playful glove/book experiments remain an unspecified experience account, with no individual charts, dates, count of attempts or failures. ## 2. Purchase, land quality and rental use different house roles

The same house has different meanings within these neighbouring subsections. The tenth is especially easy to miscode:

| Context | First house | Fourth house | Seventh house | Tenth house |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Purchase | Buyer | Property | Seller | Price |
| Assessing land | Tenants or farmers | Soil, land and buildings | Herbage, small plants, crops | Timber and trees |
| Rental or hire | Tenant | End of the undertaking | Lessor | Profit |

The purchase frame also includes the planet from which the Moon separates for the buyer, the planet to which it applies for the seller, and the Moon among the property significators. These are source-specific assignments, not a universal one-house/one-topic dictionary. [XXXIII, printed204–208 / PDF238–242; R1023, R1032, R1044.](RULES.json)

Lilly permits a purchase to conclude after square or opposition application/translation, with argument, threatened breakdown and delay. Acquisition and ease of acquisition are distinct. The subsequent purchase-perfection paragraph has compressed reception and placement alternatives; an isolated mention of the Moon does not unambiguously state its house. The extraction retains that uncertainty instead of supplying a placement from habit. A broker-mediated bargain is separately conditional on the relevant house occupations being absent and the Moon transferring the fourth lord's light to the first lord.

The land-quality rules also have ordering requirements. The tenth lord is consulted for timber only when the tenth has no occupant. Direct and retrograde motion produce different claims about trees, tenants or prior depletion. The source's “own house, viz. the fourth” explicitly identifies mundane house four; substituting zodiacal domicile would change that statement. The Saturn water/flood example adds proximity to the sea before its sea-bank concern. [XXXIII, printed205–208 / PDF239–242; R1026–R1043.](RULES.json)

In rental, the favourable first-lord aspect is described as within a moiety of that lord's own orbs, preferably to the actual ascending degree. The last favourable Ascendant glyph is Part of Fortune, unimpeded there. It is not the north-node glyph used in the earlier property-success list. The adverse seventh-house occupant clause expressly excludes a planet that is itself seventh lord. Regretting an existing rental, declining a proposed rental and taking it but promptly passing it to someone else are separate stages and outcomes. [XXXIII, printed208–209 / PDF242–243; R1045–R1051.](RULES.json)

## 3. The father's estate chapter includes an express limit on timing

The father is assigned to the fourth, while the father's personal estate or movable goods are assigned to the fifth. That distinction matters when “estate” could otherwise be read as land alone. The source gives a second/fifth-lord reception statement together with an exchange of their house placements; it does not justify replacing the whole clause with any generic mutual reception.

Lilly distinguishes requesting present means from waiting for a later provision. If the father appears reluctant, one instruction is to wait until the relevant planet leaves its sign. If the querent cannot wait, a narrower alternative requires directness, swiftness, oriental position and the stated favourable contacts. Immediately afterward Lilly says observing these times does **not by itself enforce the father's mind or will**. His positive claim is that the father's inclinations are more benevolent then. Both halves are retained.

Retrogradation is also contextual. The fifth lord's retrogradation can indicate part of the intended estate being wasted or otherwise assigned, while application by retrogradation between the second and fifth lords can indicate a sudden receipt. The latter sentence does not specify unambiguously whether one or both lords must be retrograde. A universal retrograde penalty would erase this distinction. [XXXIV, printed210–211 / PDF244–245; R1052–R1065.](RULES.json)

## 4. Relocation and waterworks combine practical descriptions with historical doctrine

The relocation chapter assigns different alleged reasons for moving to different house lords: sickness or servants, slander, declining finances, reputation or trade, the house itself, and neighbouring competitors. A marginal note adds that a house may stand inconveniently for trade. That practical alternative remains alongside the author's supernatural explanation of unlucky houses.

Lilly's statement that he remembers no regrets and received thanks or rewards is an aggregate memory report. It supplies no enumerated sample, denominator or independent confirmations. His discussion of angels, curses, expiation and afflicted families is preserved as theological opinion and historical explanation. It is not converted into empirical evidence or a newly generated remedy. [XXXV, printed212–214 / PDF246–248; R1066–R1080.](RULES.json)

For bringing water into ground or a house, the opening favourable combination includes Saturn direct, swift and oriental, specified Moon houses, and the absence of a Mars aspect. The source later mentions a receiving benefic “ascending in his latitude,” whose precise numerical convention is not defined locally. That phrase is not replaced by northern zodiacal position.

The printed rain-producing list is **Cancer, Leo, Aquarius and Pisces**. Leo is included; Scorpio is absent. Replacing it with the usual water-sign list would be an editorial alteration. The adverse tenth-house testimony concerns pipe or bank failure, disrupted flow and poor design; its sentence continues onto the following page. [XXXVI, printed214–215 / PDF248–249; R1081–R1087.](RULES.json)

## 5. Treasure presence, identity, recovery and depth are different questions

Lilly first asks whether the querent hid their own goods, who owned them, what relation the hider had to the querent, whether the treasure is unidentified, and whether a specific mineral is being sought. A question about a coal mine already contains the coal category; an answer about coal cannot then be counted as discovering that category from the chart.

The presence rules differ from the acquisition rules. Qualified Saturn or Mars placements can signify treasure, despite their usual unfortunate classification. The text then states that a planet in its own house or essentially dignified and acting as significator is not unfortunate. The neighbouring requirements concerning directness and impediment, and earlier qualifications of essential dignity, remain visible rather than being cancelled by that broad statement.

The treasure-quality instructions first combine the treasure significator with the seventh lord when distinct, then give an absolute seventh-lord alternative with unclear negation/affinity wording. Both are retained. The material lists distinguish dignified and weak cases; the original weak-mining paragraph names Saturn, despite the text layer's misleading glyph rendering.

The final group is expressly attributed to Alkindus. It includes presence, acquisition, location, depth and digging-time instructions. Depth increases with progress through the sign, but no feet, metres, equal thirds or degree-to-depth ratio is supplied. The helper therefore implements an ordinal comparison only. ## 6. One property purchase, with prior knowledge and several dependent episodes

The one actual diagram is dated **31 March 1634, 6 Hor. P.M.** It gives no minute, second, latitude or timezone. The narrative says the author is writing while living in one of the houses in1647. Before describing his judgement, he says he was fully resolved to buy and already knew his money could not be called in without six months' notice. Those are disclosed inputs.

| Stage | What the source reports | Evidential distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Before the question | Intention to purchase; six-month notice requirement | Prior knowledge and preference |
| During negotiation | Many meetings; £530 demanded without reduction | Reported conduct and asking price |
| Between beginning and conclusion | Rival purchaser and seller's daughter's assistance | Dependent episodes within this enquiry |
| Twelve days after, as narrated | A friend lends £500 | Reported loan; exact Mars/Jupiter timing antecedent remains qualified |
| 25 April | Bargain made, associated with Venus–Sun conjunction | Agreement date; no independent advance-date freeze supplied |
| 17 May | £530 paid and conveyance sealed, associated with Venus–Moon conjunction | Completion date, distinct from agreement |
| At narration | Financial injury, yet no regret because of attachment and prior history | Financial evaluation and satisfaction are different endpoints |

The competitor margin expressly says this happened after Lilly began and before he concluded. A later competitor therefore does not itself contradict the narrower statement of no other purchaser at the original moment.

The account also compares five bodily marks with chart features, reports the houses as old but strong, and expects many leases to outlast his life. The marks are retrospective self-reports, while later structural durability and lease-lifetime expectations lack confirming endpoints in these pages. The final denial of occupational and marital claims attributed to Wharton is retained as polemical autobiography; the underlying dispute has not been adjudicated. ## 7. Exact arithmetic preserves discrepancies instead of repairing the chart

The numeric table has twelve cusps, seven planets, both nodes and Fortune. Planetary signs are classified as inferred from the drawn sectors and cusp intervals; the sign is not reprinted beside each body. Mars and Saturn have explicit retrograde marks. Absence of a motion mark on another body is not silently converted to a verified direct-motion assertion. Fourth and tenth cusps give18° but omit minutes, which remain `null`.

The most substantial arithmetic discrepancy concerns Fortune. Lilly's earlier selected formula is the same by day and night:

**Fortune = Ascendant + Moon − Sun, reduced around the zodiac.**

Using Libra13°45′, Virgo10°38′ and Aries20°56′ yields **Pisces3°27′**. The diagram visibly prints **Pisces12°27′**. The difference is **9°00′**. Neither the source coordinate nor the calculation is replaced to make them agree. The cause remains unresolved. The targeted errata check found no entry for printed219 correcting it. The earlier reported alternative nocturnal formula is a different convention, not an automatically authorized repair.
