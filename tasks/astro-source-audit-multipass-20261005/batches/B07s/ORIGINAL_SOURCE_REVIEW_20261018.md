## Source corrections and exceptions preventing flattering/harmful overclaim

1. Lilly describes alleged **Sun-to-opposition-of-Venus** outcomes but immediately notes Venus cannot be separated from the Sun by more than **48 degrees**, discusses an extreme age qualifier, and instructs readers to understand the passage as **Sun-to-square-Venus** rather than automatically count a feasible opposition. This is an original-source computation objection, not independently verified modern primary-direction geometry or individual fertility claims. Keep conjunction/sextile/trine/square/terms separate.
2. He likewise admits uncertainty about ordinary **Sun-to-opposition-of-Mercury**, speculating very long life; it cannot be admitted as an executable direction or a forecast on the authority of this passage.
3. **Jupiter sextile/trine** promotion is said to act only *slenderly* when natal Jupiter lacks essential dignity. **Jupiter adverse** includes a conditional recovery in reputation and estate if natal chart not wholly opposed. **Mars body** contains positive military preferential treatment even while describing adverse humoral and bodily conditions.
4. The extreme Sun-to-Mars adverse language is specifically conditional for alleged mortality upon a **climacteric year, Mars as Anareta and Sun as Hyleg**. None of these original 1647 timing calculations, hyleg/anareta, era astronomy or clinical implications have been independently established.
5. **Venus terms** are said not usually to consummate a marriage unless Venus already signifies a wife in the nativity and/or additional profection, annual revolution or benefic-aspect conditions apply. **Venus body** marriage and family outcomes are conditioned on natal Venus dignity, social class, age and existing family circumstances.
6. **Mercury body** combines learning/business/potential patronage **with both gain and loss**, reputational misinformation and many changing studies. **Mercury benefic aspects** paradoxically bring busyness *without great profit*, sometimes travel without success. Do not score each occurrence as independent uniformly positive support.
7. **Moon body/adverse aspects** make severe period illness, moral and gender claims, alongside conditional office if natal Moon is well dignified. The North Node claims office/patronage with explicit substitutions for non-monarchical local governments; South Node negative fever rhetoric requires another ill direction for the strongest claim. Part of Fortune names gifts from those of higher rank.
8. Twelve separate house directions must not be omitted: 4th benefics versus Saturn detriment, 5th malefic negation, 9th watery-sign sea journeys with malefic pirate/shipwreck **or** benefic safe passage, 10th office beyond normal birth class **or** conditional custody/exile, 12th alleged enemies/captivity **but also** gains from horses/oxen. These are conditional old-world scenarios, not present-day survival guidance.
