## Historically asserted categories and important literal distinction

The source proposes a spouse from another location if marriage indicators occupy the ninth or third houses or are peregrine/lacking essential dignity. **Lilly expressly writes county, not country**, so an initial result concerns being born outside the same local administrative county rather than necessarily abroad. The author then suggests an exchange of ninth and seventh house rulers as another condition for a wife born away from the native's home country/place, and adds unrelated stereotypes of her religion, resources and virtue when significators are benevolent and strong.

In a different rule, **Venus must be ruler of ninth** and occupy either first or tenth house to suggest a wife born in *another country*. A subsequent original line involving the Sun and another planet/astronomical glyph in or aspecting the seventh is not unambiguously transcribed and is explicitly held unresolved. Sun in third is a separate alleged nonlocal-wife indication.

Lilly then directs attention to the zodiac sign of the seventh cusp, the sign occupied by seventh ruler, Venus/Moon signs, and their celestial quadrants, and claims the greatest number of concordant testimonies makes the judgment more certain. He gives **no metric converting natal house/quadrant factors into kilometers, county boundaries, passports, spouse residence at marriage, or direction/bearing on Earth**. His moral/religious/social descriptions are unsupported period classifications rather than trustworthy facts about actual individuals.
