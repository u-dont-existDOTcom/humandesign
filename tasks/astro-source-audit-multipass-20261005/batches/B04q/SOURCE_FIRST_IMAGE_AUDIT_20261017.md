**Question and chart:** Lady asks when her husband imprisoned will be released. Lilly gives the seventh ruler **Jupiter** to the husband, describing it retrograde in Cancer and recently trine Sun; Moon applies to sextile retrograde Saturn, then trine Jupiter with favorable reception. The astrologer reports telling the woman she need not ask friends of the King or others for release. This advice crosses physical PDF505→506.

**Chronological confound:** Lilly predicts the husband **either was [already] discharged, or would be discharged within three days**, by a solar-associated commander with assistance and money. He immediately reports that, in fact, both the release and the seizure of the garrison occurred **the day before the question** and attributes them to an honest Parliament colonel who gave funds and necessities. This account is self-reported; we cannot establish whether Lilly or his client already knew the event or whether the release genuinely preceded the question, but the source itself prevents a clean forward-only prediction score. The difference between reported release, garrison capture, provider identity, money and necessities is preserved. Finally Lilly says retrograde exalted Jupiter in a movable sign trine Sun ruler fourth explains short imprisonment, without independently verified custody period.
