## Historically asserted success, hazards and regions

Lilly's favorable travel source needs the **travel significators**, not unrelated planets, to be potent, in beneficial aspects **or** in their own domiciles. The author alleges travel without danger, prosperity, friendship and honors. He expressly qualifies honors by the person's social class, so absolute status and relative improvement are not interchangeable outcomes.

Weak travel significators, difficult aspects and malefic house placements are alleged to indicate trouble or danger. The source offers a range of *different* speculative subtypes: imprisonment if twelfth-house factors dominate; treachery of relatives/neighbors or ordinary thieves under third house; Saturnine poverty and money shortage; Mars-associated harm by thieves/soldiers. None of these is based on observed travel outcomes, and the source itself supplies no forecast horizon or crime/accident base rates.

The original then classifies travel harm by planetary/zodiac conditions: moist signs allegedly indicate showers/water/navigation or isolated places; **Saturn in fixed signs EXCEPT SCORPIO** is connected with falls or windstorms; movable signs with lightning/thunder/air changes; human-shaped signs with theft/deception; bestial signs with animals, falls, earthquakes or building collapse. **Scorpio** is handled in its own source case for *flux* or poisoning. Alleged drowning has the additional conjunction of a watery sign, affliction by malefics **and** violent fixed stars. Saturn with Lord Ninth in twelfth is a separate alleged death-while-traveling claim, whereas ninth ruler placed in Saturn's own house is a different claimed robbery case. These period-era medical, accident, criminal and mortality associations are not safety advice for a real person.

An internal unnumbered subsection, *What Region or Country will be best or most fortunate unto the Native*, proposes historical realms/cities attributed to the signs associated with first, tenth, eleventh and second houses, as well as Jupiter/Venus and an **unresolved third glyph**. Allegedly unfavorable regions are assigned by signs of seventh, sixth, eighth and fourth, especially when malefics occupy them without favorable Jupiter/Venus aspect. A later Saturn/Mars/other-glyph source disfavor condition is also retained with the original glyph unresolved. The author explicitly says the mapping of countries/kingdoms/cities to zodiac signs can be found in **Part I printed page 93**, which has NOT been collated in this batch. No real country selection, compass direction, geographic coordinates or modern place rankings follow from these paragraphs.

The source's strong language to *avoid* purportedly adverse regions is itself a historical claim, not a valid travel recommendation. No human journeys, safety records, actual map of source-claimed places, economic returns or decoy-comparison tests were examined.
