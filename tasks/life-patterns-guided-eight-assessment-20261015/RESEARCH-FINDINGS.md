## How to interpret the new responses

New **descriptive specificity** does not automatically mean new **between-person discrimination**. The statements below are item-method findings written *after* inspecting the developer participant's answers. They are NOT an independent coding validation or evidence of a natal relationship.

| Route | Current evidence decision | Next instrument decision |
|---|---|---|
| **G01** | A conditional information-gathering sequence plus a concrete process example. More specific than selecting all methods; not demonstrated distinctive. | Pilot a real first-action/verification contrast with availability and task difficulty controlled |
| **G02** | Audience-sensitive explanation with a case. Largely ordinary communication adaptation; likely low discrimination. | Redraft around a specific communication decision and listener response, or keep as qualitative context |
| **G16** | Conditional difference between curiosity despite slow progress and fulfilling commitments after interest fades. Source has an actual task context. | Candidate for blinded motivation-vs-obligation tradeoff pilot |
| **G23** | One concrete instance of detecting and acting on a group quality issue. Does not establish an invariant 'first thing noticed'. | Replace abstract first-notice ranking with a specific observed issue/initiative/action question |
| **M05** | Conditional descriptions of cues, unease and uncertainty that overlap conceptually. Adds little identifiable behavioral contrast. | Suspend chart scoring; retire or test a narrowly preregistered timing distinction elsewhere |
| **G20** | Real resource-use example but several purposes are compatible and no meaningful opportunity cost is established. | Replace hypothetical resource preferences with a real allocation/foregone-alternative contrast |
| **M01** | Describes response to requests to hurry as dependent on legitimacy, actual cost and relationship. Mainly hypothetical illustrations. | Pilot matched requests with objective deadline, cost to comply and motive separate |
| **M03** | Conditional principles of feasible promises and changed priorities; broadly normative, no concrete costly choice. | Retire as scored item unless an uncoached new-person pilot finds a real discriminating contrast |

**Qualitative analyst triage, not a statistical scale:** four routes provide promising conditional/episode evidence (G01, G16, G23, M01), two give source context but weak discrimination (G02, G20), and two show an item-design problem likely to defeat additional probing (M05, M03). These classes do not imply that the first four are established distinctive traits.

## Key lessons for the source-to-target study

1. **Question completeness is achieved.** All eight requested clarifications were actually supplied. Do not ask this participant to repeat them merely because a field such as "recent example" is empty where another sufficient context field is present.
2. **Information gain and chart specificity are different.** A detailed process example can be valuable for case description without distinguishing this person from ordinary comparison groups.
3. **Context dependence is not contradictory behavior.** The four multiple-choice labels often describe sequential stages or complementary conditions, not independent trait votes.
4. **Respect respondent instrument feedback.** Criticism that an item elicits obvious answers is a question-quality finding, not evidence of skepticism, nonconformity or a natal configuration.
5. **Stop digging into low-value generic items.** Repeated questions to force a discriminating answer create fatigue and researcher demand effects. For M05 and M03, redesign on unfamiliar respondents or retire.
6. **Separate route stability from construct stability.** An item named G23 should not keep the implied psychometric target "always notices X first" when its useful source content concerns noticing and acting on a quality issue in a particular group.
7. **Keep the old 84-turn reviewed record frozen.** This supplement is a development clarification, not a new main interview submission, not an independent replication and not eligible for chart scoring.
