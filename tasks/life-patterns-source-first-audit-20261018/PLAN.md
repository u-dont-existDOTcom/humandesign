## Source reliability and missing coverage boundaries

Not a full natal tradition corpus: Hellenistic natal sources, Jyotish beyond the four *Brihat Jataka* XV verses, full modern astrology and unreviewed Lilly natal folios remain outstanding. A scanned table of contents is not a chart rule; an unvalidated traditional assertion is not an empirical prediction. Overarching project empirical tests remain subject to prospective freezing, controls, uncertainty and independent participants.

## Design decision

New questions are development candidates; no compulsory additions or deployment without answering whether distinct, respondent-applicable measurement value and burden justify them. The previously installed eight questions remain unchanged. Study existing MADRE dream questionnaire, CAQ creative-achievement measure and event-history-calendar methodology as reuse/adapt baselines rather than copying a copyrighted questionnaire.
