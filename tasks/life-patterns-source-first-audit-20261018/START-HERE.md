## How to use

Retain the current eight experience questions. Offer topics rather than 16 consecutive questions; permit none. Check previous participant sources before deciding whether a question is genuinely still needed: a related hypothetical does not imply the actual event or experience was measured. For an optional pilot, the highest-priority topics are DREAM, BELIEF, RENUNCIATION, BELIEF_CHANGE, ART, MYSTERY, TIMELINE, VOCATION and HOME. No more than five selected topics in one session.

**Boundary:** This is a survey-design candidate set, not the full world history of astrology and not evidence astrology predicts any item. Sensitive health, mortality, confinement and reproductive claims are not mandatory life-history questions. No live Railway, GPT, participant source answer or frozen score is changed.
