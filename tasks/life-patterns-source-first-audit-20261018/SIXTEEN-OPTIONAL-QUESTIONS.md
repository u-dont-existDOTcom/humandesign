# Sixteen optional Life Patterns question candidates

**2026-10-18.** Research candidates only; these are NOT in the currently
deployed survey and have no natal predictor or scoring rule.

Ask no additional question merely to reach a quota. Check whether the
person already answered each area, let them select topics, allow full
decline, and normally limit to at most five optional topics per session.

## DREAM — Dreaming and what dreams mean to you

**Priority:** high. **Type:** phenomenology. **Time frame:** typical_last_3_months. **Privacy:** ordinary_optional.

**Ask:** In a typical recent month, how often do you remember dreams after waking up, if ever?

**Optional follow-up:** If you remember them, do particular subjects recur, or do dreams ever influence how you think or act while awake? You can say no or skip.

**Measure separately:** recall_frequency, vividness_when_remembered_optional, usual_emotional_tone_optional, meaning_assigned_optional.

**Avoid interpreting as:** Dream recall frequency is not a claim that dreams foretell events. Do not infer dream content from beliefs about omens.

## BELIEF — Worldview and regular spiritual/religious practice

**Priority:** high. **Type:** self_report_worldview. **Time frame:** current_and_past_year. **Privacy:** protected_beliefs_fully_optional.

**Ask:** What, if anything, plays a regular part in your life: religious observance, meditation or other spiritual practice, philosophical reflection, a secular worldview, or none of these?

**Optional follow-up:** Do these activities reflect deep personal conviction, routine, community belonging, curiosity, or something else?

**Measure separately:** actual_current_practice_type_optional, practice_frequency_optional, current_self_description_optional.

**Avoid interpreting as:** Do not infer religious identity from a sensed higher power, awe or synchronicity. No identity is assumed; refusal is complete.

## BELIEF_CHANGE — Important changes in your beliefs or convictions

**Priority:** high. **Type:** autobiographical_change. **Time frame:** lifetime. **Privacy:** protected_beliefs_fully_optional.

**Ask:** Have your most important beliefs or convictions stayed broadly stable, shifted gradually, changed suddenly, or changed several times across your life?

**Optional follow-up:** If something changed, what would you consider the most significant shift and roughly when?

**Measure separately:** change_pattern, approximate_age_period_optional, self_reported_change_domains_optional.

**Avoid interpreting as:** A change of mind is not necessarily a religious conversion, a revelation or evidence of being guided. Do not force a narrative.

## RENUNCIATION — Deliberate renunciation, retreat or initiation

**Priority:** high. **Type:** optional_life_course_role. **Time frame:** lifetime. **Privacy:** protected_beliefs_fully_optional.

**Ask:** Have there been any significant periods in your life when you deliberately stepped back from worldly goals, possessions, status or ordinary social roles, whether through spiritual/religious commitment, simplicity, retreat or something else?

**Optional follow-up:** If so, was this a formal commitment or more a personal way of living, and did it last or later change?

**Measure separately:** applicability, chosen_commitment_vs_external_constraint_optional, approximate_age_period_optional, duration_optional, formal_initiation_optional, later_return_optional.

**Avoid interpreting as:** Voluntary renunciation and formal initiation are distinct from generalized spiritual experiences, introversion, depression, poverty or enforced isolation. This is not an assumed religious identity or a medical state.

**Source note:** Varahamihira Brihat Jataka Chapter XV verses 1-4, Sanskrit and secondary English translation; wording uncertainties noted. Four natal yoga conditions are historical assertions, not verified chart predictions.

## ART — Making things creatively versus appreciating beauty

**Priority:** high. **Type:** two_independent_axes. **Time frame:** past_year. **Privacy:** ordinary_optional.

**Ask:** Over the past year, how often have you actively made, written, designed, performed or invented something for its own sake? Separately, how often have art, music, beauty or aesthetics been important experiences for you?

**Optional follow-up:** If they differ, what do you create yourself and what do you mostly appreciate or enjoy?

**Measure separately:** creative_production_frequency, aesthetic_appreciation_frequency, example_domain_optional, creative_achievement_optional.

**Avoid interpreting as:** Enjoying art and producing it are different; school/work outputs need not be excluded or automatically counted. Avoid confusing achievement with aesthetic preference.

## MYSTERY — Interest in hidden, philosophical or unusual questions

**Priority:** high. **Type:** interest_and_method. **Time frame:** past_year_and_long_term. **Privacy:** ordinary_optional.

**Ask:** How often, if at all, do you actively explore unresolved philosophical questions, hidden or mysterious subjects, divination, scientific puzzles, or other ways of investigating what you do not yet understand?

**Optional follow-up:** When you encounter an unusual claim, what tends to convince you it is true or mistaken?

**Measure separately:** actual_activities_frequency, subject_area_tags_optional, evidence_evaluation_style_optional.

**Avoid interpreting as:** Interest in omens, interest in science, personal intuition and beliefs about causes are different. Do not collapse into an occult-or-not binary.

## TIMELINE — Actual turning points and life phases

**Priority:** high. **Type:** life_event_timeline. **Time frame:** lifetime. **Privacy:** fully_optional_life_history.

**Ask:** If you look across your life, what are up to three changes or turning points that most clearly divide it into a before and after? You may say none or decline.

**Optional follow-up:** For each event you choose, was the change mostly in external circumstances, your understanding/identity, or both?

**Measure separately:** event_description_optional, approximate_age_or_year_optional, external_event_vs_inner_change_optional, life_domain_optional, confidence_in_date_optional.

**Avoid interpreting as:** Do not require trauma, death, health events or intimate details. A major inner transformation is distinct from actual marriage, job, family or residence events. Not a live forecasting instrument.

## VOCATION — Actual work, calling and long-running roles

**Priority:** high. **Type:** life_history_and_identity. **Time frame:** current_and_lifetime. **Privacy:** ordinary_optional.

**Ask:** What kinds of work or contribution have actually occupied the most of your life so far? Has there been one lasting vocation, several distinct phases, or no clear occupation or calling?

**Optional follow-up:** If an occupation or calling has changed, what changed and around what period?

**Measure separately:** actual_role_history_optional, felt_vocation_separate_optional, approximate_transitions_optional.

**Avoid interpreting as:** Actual jobs and public roles are not inferred from how mentally draining computer work feels or a volunteer hypothetical. Unemployment or unpaid care are valid contexts.

## HOME — Home, family roots and belonging

**Priority:** high. **Type:** experiential_belonging. **Time frame:** lifetime_and_current. **Privacy:** fully_optional_private_relationships.

**Ask:** Where, with whom, or in what situations have you felt most at home or a sense of belonging, if anywhere? Has that changed over your life?

**Optional follow-up:** Do family relationships, chosen community, or places themselves play the biggest role, if any?

**Measure separately:** belonging_context_optional, period_changes_optional, kinship_vs_place_distinction_optional.

**Avoid interpreting as:** Do not presume supportive parents, siblings, a family home or a stable residence. No personal identification details required.

## KINSHIP — Actual sibling and extended-family roles

**Priority:** medium. **Type:** life_history. **Time frame:** lifetime. **Privacy:** fully_optional_private_relationships.

**Ask:** Without naming anyone, what role—if any—have siblings or extended relatives played in your life, and how has that changed?

**Optional follow-up:** Would you say these ties have been central, occasional, absent, or too variable to summarize?

**Measure separately:** applicability, actual_relational_role_optional, change_over_time_optional.

**Avoid interpreting as:** Not everyone has siblings or safe family relationships; skip is not an absence of empathy or attachment.

## BONDS — Actual long-term friendship and partnership patterns

**Priority:** medium. **Type:** relational_life_history. **Time frame:** lifetime. **Privacy:** fully_optional_private_relationships.

**Ask:** Looking at your actual close bonds over time, have long friendships or partnerships tended to last, change, end and begin again, or follow different patterns?

**Optional follow-up:** Would an answer about friendship be different from one about romantic partnership?

**Measure separately:** friendship_stability_optional, romantic_history_optional, approximate_phase_change_optional.

**Avoid interpreting as:** Do not force relationship trauma, marital status, sexuality or specific partner details. Existing hypothetical romance reactions are not actual relationship histories.

## TRAVEL — Living elsewhere and encounters with different cultures

**Priority:** medium. **Type:** life_history. **Time frame:** lifetime. **Privacy:** ordinary_optional.

**Ask:** How much have actual travel, relocation, languages or living among different cultures shaped your life, if at all?

**Optional follow-up:** Has this been recurring over many years or concentrated in one period?

**Measure separately:** actual_vs_desired_travel_optional, duration_or_phase_optional, felt_impact_optional.

**Avoid interpreting as:** Desire for travel and one hypothetical itinerary are not evidence of actual journeys. No precise addresses requested.

## STATUS — Changes in public responsibility or recognition

**Priority:** medium. **Type:** actual_role_history. **Time frame:** lifetime. **Privacy:** ordinary_optional.

**Ask:** Have public responsibilities, influence or recognition actually been a major or changing part of your life, or mostly not?

**Optional follow-up:** Is that different from how much you personally care about recognition?

**Measure separately:** actual_roles_optional, historical_changes_optional, desire_for_recognition_separately_optional.

**Avoid interpreting as:** Separate actual status and responsibility from whether praise feels rewarding (which TF1 already asks).

## RESOURCES — Broad material circumstances over time

**Priority:** medium. **Type:** private_life_history. **Time frame:** lifetime. **Privacy:** fully_optional_financial_privacy.

**Ask:** Without giving amounts, have your material circumstances generally been stable, improving, worsening, cyclical, or too varied to summarize?

**Optional follow-up:** Do financial security and material possessions matter to you in the same way, or differently?

**Measure separately:** overall_life_course_pattern_optional, approximate_phase_optional, objective_vs_subjective_security_optional.

**Avoid interpreting as:** No specific income, account data, inherited amounts or debt disclosures requested. Windfall spending preference does not establish actual wealth.

## PARENTING — Caregiving and parenting when applicable

**Priority:** medium. **Type:** role_and_life_history. **Time frame:** current_and_lifetime. **Privacy:** fully_optional_family_privacy.

**Ask:** Has caring for children or other dependents been part of your actual life? If so, how central has it been and has your role changed?

**Optional follow-up:** If care matters to you, is it a chosen role, a practical obligation, or both?

**Measure separately:** role_applicability, role_centrality_optional, phase_changes_optional.

**Avoid interpreting as:** Do not assume children, fertility, caregiving or family plans. Never turn natal fertility/death claims into medical predictions.

## PLAY — Playfulness and spontaneous enjoyment

**Priority:** medium. **Type:** frequency_and_style. **Time frame:** past_year. **Privacy:** ordinary_optional.

**Ask:** During ordinary life over the past year, how often have you been playful, amused, silly, or spontaneously drawn into enjoyment, if at all?

**Optional follow-up:** Does playfulness usually arise with other people, on your own, or in both settings?

**Measure separately:** frequency_optional, typical_context_optional, social_vs_solitary_optional.

**Avoid interpreting as:** Enjoyment is not necessarily awe, creativity or optimistic mood. Playfulness is historically adjacent, not an established natal predictor of frequency.
