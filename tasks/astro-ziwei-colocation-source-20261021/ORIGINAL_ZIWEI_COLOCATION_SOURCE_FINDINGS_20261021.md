## Explicit natal same-palace grade rules in the same original source

From accepted original source notes `ChineseAstro-10SRC-Life` through `ChineseAstro-13SRC-Life`, we SHA-verified 15 exact source lines containing an unambiguous **`与[星名]同度`** co-location expression, a specified branch/palace and historical conditional statements. They describe **13 distinct unordered major-star pair types** across the four nominated primary stars. All original full line/codepoint/UTF8 byte offsets and quote SHA are included in the machine-readable audit, not just short modernized paraphrases.

- WuQu with TianFu in Zi/Wu (R0293), QiSha in Mao/You (R0294), TianXiang in Yin/Shen (R0295), PoJun in Si/Hai (R0296), TanLang in Chou/Wei (R0298).
- LianZhen with TianXiang in Zi/Wu (R0340), PoJun in Mao/You (R0341), TianFu in Chen/Xu (R0344), QiSha in Chou/Wei (R0345).
- TianLiang with TaiYang in Mao/You (R0469), TianJi in Chen/Xu (R0470), TianTong in Yin/Shen (R0472).
- PoJun with LianZhen in Mao/You (R0511), ZiWei in Chou/Wei (R0513), WuQu in Si/Hai (R0515).

Two source pair families have **reciprocal historical source observations**. WuQu+PoJun at Si/Hai: R0296 says `壬戊生人财官格`, R0515 says `戊生人福厚`; these are *rank/wealth/fortune* conditions, not an empirical modifier of decisiveness or competitiveness. LianZhen+PoJun at Mao/You: R0341 says `乙辛癸生人吉`, while R0511 says `乙辛癸生人利，甲庚丙生人不耐久`. The conditional favorable birth-stem group and a separate less favorable group are preserved; **do not flatten** into "pair is generally good/bad" or choose whichever clause seems to explain a life. TianLiang's grade row R0469 additionally distinguishes Mao as `入庙` from You as `得地`; R0472 distinguishes Yin as `入庙` and Shen as `陷地`. Equal pair names do not imply equal historical place/year dignity.

**Critical negative finding:** NONE of these 15 literal natal palace pairing/grade lines contains any of the four exact behavioral intro phrases `性刚果决`, `好忿争`, `好济施`, `好争强`. They overwhelmingly assert income/official rank/status/fortune, usually under year-stem or additional benefic conditions. They **do not explicitly state that a co-located star reverses, increases or decreases any of our four proposed questionnaire outcomes**. The presence of a grade rule establishes textual co-location coverage, not behavioral scoring weights or actual prediction accuracy.

## Multi-star narrative caveats cannot be reduced to one partner

Seven additional exact source passages R0290, R0291, R0331, R0339, R0465, R0506 and R0508 mention extra stars/benefics/malefics and conditions. The three most behaviorally relevant are:
- `ZWD-R0331` LianZhen: `与擎羊同是非日有`, a historically transmitted claim about daily disputes when accompanied by Qing Yang *auxiliary* star. The source may motivate a **future conditional behavioral prediction**, but our accepted auxiliary placement/translation and real validation are not ready. It is NOT proof LianZhen alone predicts argument escalation.
- `ZWD-R0339` LianZhen: `天相同宫能化其恶`, a broad claim TianXiang moderates adverse influence. **Not** an explicit instruction to reverse or subtract from the precise `好忿争` quarrelsome frequency target.
- `ZWD-R0508` PoJun: `遇廉贞羊陀火铃于陷宫，争斗疾病`, a **multi-factor fallen-palace, additional-malefic** claim, not a consequence of PoJun+LianZhen alone; historical illness and violent social claims are NOT validated medical knowledge.

Likewise WuQu R0291 needs a whole adverse cluster and PoJun R0506 speaks generally of TianLiang/TianFu mitigating adverse influence, not a numeric change in the competition trait. Source record R0508 ends midcontinuation in the transmitted source line, another reason not to promote it to an unqualified prediction without neighboring textual collation.

## Methodological implications

There are at least two *development-stage candidate models* whose merits would need to be settled **before seeing any new people's responses**: (A) a narrowly conservative sole-star model, abstaining on all co-stars, versus (B) a limited original-intro *star-in-Life presence* hypothesis for each behavior, with co-present stars recorded as separate covariates rather than inventing modifiers—potentially allowing 34/120 synthetic star-bearing layouts (including 22 paired). The source does not obviously require A's exclusion, but B cannot be declared correct either without independent historical interpretation and psychometric testing. All four source hypotheses require birth-chart/date/time source accuracy and full conflict/conditional mapping before real scoring. If both models are compared, predeclare a primary hypothesis and multiplicity/sensitivity plan; don't choose the one with better observed individual matches.

The four provisional questionnaire items are still unvalidated development-only prompts. Existing established instruments in the prior psychometric audit cover decision styles, argumentativeness (contested trait→actual-behavior validation), prosocial tendencies and competitiveness (partially overlapping contentiousness). Source sex-coded, status, lifespan, disease, disability or moral worth claims must not be treated as clinical or social truths.

**Next high-value task:** independent original-language/specialist review of which parts of the four introductions are unconditional and what exact pair/trine/opposition/benefic clauses *explicitly* modify a behavioral trait (not rank). Develop a source-graded compact **hypothesis rulebook**, clearly separate unresolved historical status clauses and numerical probabilities, then a frozen source→question mapping/controls on development data without inspecting untouched final participants. Do not prioritize endless original annotation counts over an actual falsifiable predeclared test; human readiness gates remain open.
