# Competing limited Zi Wei source hypotheses — design alternatives, NOT preregistration

**Purpose:** interpret the negative source finding that co-location grade texts do not explicitly reverse any of the four introductory psychological traits, while avoiding fabricated co-star rules. This is a design note, not authorization to recruit or score humans.

| Candidate | Predeclared star eligibility idea | Current finite synthetic SOURCE configurations | Scientific weakness |
|---|---|---:|---|
| A — strict sole selected star | Positive source trait hypothesis ONLY if exactly one selected major star appears in Life **without any other major star**; abstain otherwise | 12/120 structurally; only10/120 additionally all raw vendor comparator variants agree | Extremely narrow and not a clear requirement of the original text; selection may bias coverage |
| B — source introductory star present | For each historical source intro trait, use its presence as a provisional directional hypothesis even with other major stars present, record all co-star conditions **without** invented behavioral weights; abstain when nominated star absent | 34/120 layouts have at least one selected star, including22 with other major stars;3 with two selected | Original multi-star conditional text not exhaustively checked; trans-cultural translation and overlapping constructs; not calibrated |
| C — grades as psychological multipliers | Apply natal grade/rank/wealth or moral adverse categories as +/− weights to decision/help/competition/argument scores | **Not computable** from original evidence | Source does not justify these personality weights: **REJECT as current model**, not a test-ready branch |

A and B are **not equivalent** and must never be selected after seeing scored human questionnaires. For A or B, no selected star does NOT mean the person lacks the trait; represent it as no astrologically-supported hypothesis, not a negative prediction. If both selected stars co-locate, both source traits may be predicted under B but they are not statistically independent observations without explicit dependency adjustment. Original place/year-stem/benefic-mitigations may limit even B; source excerpts and independent translator/school expert check required.

Before a real human pilot, independently calculate full birth-to-chart policies with documented timezone/longitude, historical exact-birth-clock provenance, LiChun vs official Lunar New Year and leap-month 2057 conflicts, then freeze verified field scope and interpretation rules. Select validated multi-item or event-log behavioral measures (development-only answers for calibration), explicit baseline frequencies, control matched decoy charts, random seeds, missing data/power, timestamped predictions before responses, consent and data protection. **No human study has been preregistered and no human astrology predictive evidence has been obtained.**
