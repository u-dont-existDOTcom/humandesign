# Life Patterns: Experience & Meaning pilot v1

**Status:** A standalone, optional research pilot. Nothing here is part of the live GPT or Railway participant survey, and none of these questions affects a chart score.

**Open `EXPERIENCE-MEANING-PILOT-v1.html` in a browser.** You do not need to run a server, install anything, or sign in. Accept the separate opt-in if you want to try it. You will see four brief questions: surprise about yourself, awe, sudden realization, and two separate ratings of life meaning and perceived contribution.

After those, optionally choose any of four topics: surprise about people/world, life predictability, sensed guidance, and meaningful coincidences. These topics are not required. Every card allows an explicit skip or uncertainty, and you may describe the circumstances in your own words if useful.

At the end, choose **Export answers as JSON** to save responses. There is no autosave or upload. You can **import your own JSON** on the opening screen later and edit previous answers. The HTML pilot runs locally and does not need network access. Existing complete interview records are not loaded or overwritten; if an earlier response already addressed a question exactly, the offline page can mark it already covered with a reference, although live prior-source review would require independent checking.

The `SURVEY-FIRST-DESIGN-AND-TEST-PROTOCOL-v1.md` explains what the questions distinguish, the next validation steps, and why missing answers must not be interpreted as "never." The two JSON source artifacts specify every item and the broader missing-domain inventory.

This is a development experiment, not a validated psychological test and not evidence that astrology predicts these experiences.
