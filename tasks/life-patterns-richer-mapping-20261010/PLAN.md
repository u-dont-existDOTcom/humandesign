## Required distinction

1. Behavioral elicitation: what an interviewer question can *observe* from a participant, with conditions, alternatives, source quotes and uncertainty.
2. HD/AstroHD theoretical target prediction: what a frozen chart feature is predicted to correlate with, cited to actual theory source; not scientific validation.
3. Scoring: a separate, versioned method connecting independent behavioral codes to chart candidates. No retrospective tuning on known owner outcomes. No automatic evidence just because two descriptions sound similar.

## Method
- Keep participant-facing bank and separate model-target bridge physically segregated.
- Audit all 25 existing questions for intended behavioral distinction, missing contrasts, confounds, overbroad wording, feedback status, and whether any source-anchored chart hypothesis is *eligible* or blocked by question mismatch.
- Source each chart claim from a theory-publisher page or clearly mark old owner-conditioned repository mapping as DEVELOPMENT, NEVER as confirmatory source. Keep nonmapped items explicitly unscored.
- Draft only question revisions that add meaningful distinctions, counterexamples and manageable recall scope. No forced choice, no leading HD labels, no duplication or quota filling. Scope alternatives by context (stakes, audience, intensity, etc.).
- Include correct negative/unknown scoring: chart absence is not evidence for opposite behavior, and absence of a response is not contradiction. Cap dependencies; preregister probabilities or symbolic scores before chart reveal. Require out-of-sample cohort, matched nulls and non-HD baseline.
- Benchmark survey item content-validity process against COSMIN item relevance/comprehensiveness/comprehensibility and cognitive interviewing; exclude psychiatric/clinical claims.
