# Decision: stronger behavioral measurement before more birth-chart scoring

## Answer to owner

Yes, the current system needs a richer **predeclared** source-to-target mapping to support a genuinely new birth-time ranking test. But the immediate failure is not simply too few rule clauses. A question may be perfectly understandable yet yield a high-base-rate, context-rational, or mixed-construct answer that does not discriminate any chart hypothesis.

The live TF1 v1 question bank has 25 active interview prompts with empty planning_targets arrays. Keep them empty in the participant interviewer: chart-side hypotheses belong in a separate blinded codebook, NOT in the prompts or interviewer’s selection heuristics. The previous AstroHD V1.5 bridge has six binary clauses and five respondent domains, and the earlier frozen V4.3 survey crosswalk failed actual recorded birth-time recovery. The owner’s 84-turn record is largely the old 79-turn interview plus updates, so another rank on that source would not independently validate anything.

## Immediate output delivered

- 25 question-to-behavior contracts with distinct contrast, ambiguity/condition risks, defect or scope limit, and reason not to claim chart inference.
- 12 source-anchored *theory* hypotheses with explicit feature predicate, positive/counterevidence and abstain requirements; no probabilities, weights or inference claims.
- Ten potential links only. Nine require more appropriate question wording/answers; the near-aligned Root-urgency question is still unscored pending prospective calibration and frozen chart evaluator. Fifteen questions remain unmapped for birth ranking.
- Sixteen current-route prospective wording revisions (12 drafted in this audit, four retained from the earlier versioned v2 proposal), plus four new missing-construct questions. These are NOT active.
- Separate unchanged AstroHD six-rule baseline reference and source-extraction requirements for an expanded astrology arm. No additional astrology rules or independent bits invented.
- Searchable full HTML and markdown question audit installed alongside the existing researcher guide. The live GPT, Railway, frozen v7, active TF1 v1, completed participant review, recorded owner DOB and actual answer wording are unchanged.

## Preferred next experimental pass

1. **Content/answerability gate:** Run cognitively interviewed naive participants on the candidate wording while blind to chart features. Ask what each question means, what conditions they require, and whether they can give a counterexample. Record both successes and failures; refine only before sealing a new version.
2. **Coded-evidence gate:** Have at least two blinded coders independently classify actual answers against the neutral behavior codebook, with explicit unknown/conditional labels. Measure coding agreement and revision effects; do not disclose any chart data.
3. **Source-validity gate:** Separately inspect official Human Design theory statements and applicable astrology primary books. Commit exact machine-chart predicates and defensible conditional psychological claims. If a source is too vague to support an answer-polarity test, record it as non-scoring.
4. **Freeze:** Fix question version, behavior coding, chart feature engine, model features, dependence caps, numeric weights or calibrated conditional probabilities, null and matched decoy universe, and stopping criteria. Preserve files/hashes and keep results concealed.
5. **Untouched human test:** Collect never-used respondent interviews and consented birth data; compare the new HD bridge, the existing frozen 5-domain AstroHD six-rule baseline, and simple non-HD demographic/personality nulls on identical new participants. Report rank/interval, negative and missing cases, rather than cherry-picking the best cohort.

## Limitations and integrity

- No evidence yet that 12 source claims are true about people or birth dates. Jovian Archive is evidence of what Human Design **asserts**, not independent behavioral validation.
- No new respondent cohort, independent coding test, numeric likelihoods, model freeze or genuine birth-rank run was carried out here. Source-complete classical astrology expansion remains a separate research lane.
- Do not use the known owner's chart to decide which of these traits/links to score; it is an already-exposed development case.
- Do not invent high/low trait dichotomies simply to extract labels from a qualitative interview. Risky item patterns are identified and corrected prospectively, not retroactively scored as supportive.

## Baseline methodological comparison

Content-validity dimensions are adopted from COSMIN: relevance, comprehensiveness, and comprehensibility (<https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40562250/>). Cognitive interviewing and concern about forced uninformative items follow the COSMIN study method (<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5891557/>). These are methodological references, not evidence that the present test is psychometrically validated.
