## Methodology for arbitrary-choice control

### Specification curve analysis

Simonsohn, Simmons & Nelson, “Specification curve analysis,” *Nature Human Behaviour* 4 (2020), 1208–1214:
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-020-0912-z

The method explicitly targets results that depend on defensible but arbitrary analytical choices by identifying all theoretically justified, statistically valid, non-redundant specifications and showing results across the full set.

### Multiverse analysis

Steegen, Tuerlinckx, Gelman & Vanpaemel, “Increasing Transparency Through a Multiverse Analysis,” *Perspectives on Psychological Science* 11(5), 2016:
https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1745691616658637

The method evaluates all reasonable processing/analysis choices to reveal how much conclusions depend on arbitrary choices.

Disposition for AstroHD: **adapt** these robustness methods to astrology's legitimate interpretive plurality.

## What is already solved

- Externally sourced astrological rule systems exist.
- Multiple traditions already define planets, houses, aspects, dignities/strengths and interpretive relationships.
- Research methodology exists for showing sensitivity to multiple defensible specifications rather than selecting one convenient analysis.

## What remains project-specific

1. Convert source text/rules into machine predicates without changing their meaning.
2. Map source-defined meanings into the project's behavioral evidence ontology while candidate/target blind.
3. Normalize evidence so verbose traditions do not win by having more textual rules.
4. Compare traditions without owner-tuned cross-tradition weights.
5. Define negative controls for source-to-domain mapping flexibility.
6. Validate on fresh participants.

## Strongest baseline

Primary V1.3 baseline:
- independent tradition models;
- equal contribution per measured behavioral domain within each tradition;
- no project-authored salience/directness coefficients;
- report each tradition separately;
- specification-curve distribution across legitimate variants.

Secondary arms:
- source-native historical scoring tables exactly as published;
- existing V1.2 V1.1-derived weights for comparison only.

## Novel remainder

The novel part is **not astrology itself**. It is the provenance-preserving normalization/evaluation layer:
- source rule -> blinded behavioral domain mapping;
- one-domain-one-vote dependency normalization;
- cross-tradition robustness reporting;
- mapping-permutation negative control;
- candidate-universe birth-time recovery evaluation.
