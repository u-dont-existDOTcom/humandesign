# Dual-scorer owner calibration objective — 2026-09-23

Status: owner-required refinement of the open owner recovery calibration.

## Owner outcome

Use the repaired scenario survey as one target-blind behavioral measurement source, then determine what birth neighborhoods are recovered independently by:

1. the historical V1.1 merged Human Design + Western scorer; and
2. the clean candidate-unexposed V4.3 / NetInformation Human Design scorer.

This does **not** mean forcing the survey or either scorer to reproduce the known birth target. The neutral measurement is completed and frozen first. Both scorer-specific behavioral translations are then derived from that same frozen evidence and frozen before either birth search is opened.

## Existing crosswalk capacity

The existing post-freeze owner crosswalk requires:

- 19 distinct neutral domains for the historical V1.1 merged-cluster family;
- 16 distinct neutral domains for the clean V4.3 information family.

All of those canonical domains are already present in the current 31-domain neutral survey contract. Therefore dual-family evaluation does not itself justify adding new survey domains or another broad redesign. Follow-ups remain limited to material repaired measurement gaps.

## Comparison protocol

- Freeze the completed neutral profile.
- Translate it independently into the V1.1 merged input profile and the clean V4.3/NetInformation input profile.
- Freeze/hash both translations before opening either scorer's birth-search result.
- Run V1.1 using the historical declared recovery procedure.
- Run clean V4.3/NetInformation using its declared exact-state universe/ranking rule.
- Report the recovered birth date/time neighborhoods, target ranks, and relevant score gaps separately.
- Do not average the models or use one model's result to retune the other.
- The candidate-exposed V4.3 Moon/Mars carrier refinements are secondary descriptive diagnostics only, not the primary V4.3 comparison.

## Interpretation

If both scorers recover the same neighborhood, that is stronger development evidence that the survey preserved behavior relevant to both model families. If they disagree, retain the disagreement: because the scorers differ materially, it diagnoses model/crosswalk behavior rather than automatically proving a survey failure.
