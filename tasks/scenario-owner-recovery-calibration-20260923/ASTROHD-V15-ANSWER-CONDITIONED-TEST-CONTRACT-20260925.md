Date: 2026-09-25. Current owner request: complete the missing survey-to-ranking work so another person can test the method. This is an implementation and first external-person testing boundary, not a promise that another person's birth date will rank first.

## Preserved method

The six V1.4d astronomical clauses, their source-module hash, and the generic source-map hash remain unchanged. They were selected using the owner's known case. Five clause instances use Lilly's published strength/house/aspect criteria; one uses Phaladeepika's Venus directional-strength criterion. This does not establish that Lilly originated the criteria, or that the historical sources supplied six modern personality predictions. The equal-vote composition and the behavior-to-condition interpretation are separate modeling choices.

The earlier exact-century signature result is historical evidence: nine maximal minute samples on 1985-01-29. A new-person answer-conditioned result is not automatically that same result. Selecting six conditions from hundreds can still overfit. The advantage over the previous NetInfo architecture is inspectability and a small falsifiable specification, not established out-of-sample superiority.

## Answer-conditioned comparison

`reference/research/astrohd_v15_behavioral_bridge.json` declares the modern behavioral hypotheses, positive/opposing poles, context limits and multi-domain alternative explicitly. No new person's birth date or ranking was used to define it. For a present clause, confirmed compatible evidence contributes +1 and an explicitly incompatible pole contributes -1. Missing clauses, mixed/unknown evidence and nonapplicability do not become invented contrary predictions. No continuous feature coefficient is fitted.

The broad inherited work-energy-range label is not assigned an invented positive pole. It remains unscored. Saturn uses its existing privacy/solitude connection. Romantic connection is used by three clauses; these votes are not statistically independent. The all-positive historical-signature baseline is reported separately so identical answer signatures are visible rather than presented as new discriminatory information.

## Existing interview and review

The existing Life Patterns application remains available. The new `/birth-test` route uses nine unmodified prompts from scenario bank v7 for the five interpretations actually represented by the six clauses. This is a focused adapter, not a replacement for the full questionnaire or a claim to measure all its domains. Existing interview text can be imported rather than asking established questions again.

Free text, back navigation, skipping, mixed and unknown answers are preserved. Optional interpretation receives neutral definitions, original question context and participant responses; it is not supplied with structured birth/chart fields. Basic birth-key marker screening rejects obvious contamination, but is not a semantic guarantee of blinding. Exact evidence quotations are checked against the supplied responses. Suggestions remain fallible and must be reviewed. Manual choices are explicit participant self-reports, not fabricated evidence that a prior scenario proved the statement.

## Test order and scoring scope

1. Collect or import responses with adult-participant permission.
2. Review the neutral interpretations before seeing a ranking.
3. Specify the birthplace and freeze the profile, source-answer hash, model/bridge/scorer identities and seeded decoy panel.
4. Calculate all candidate scores with that fixed profile and fixed model.
5. Enter the documented actual local birth time and time zone only to evaluate its rank against the frozen panel. No target-aware optimization is performed.

The primary panel is 999 uniformly sampled minute instants from the prior 1926-08-24 through 2026-08-24 century window, followed by the actual instant comparison. The 99 and 9,999 panels are secondary nested prefixes of the same 9,999-candidate reservoir. These are not exhaustive century searches, calibrated probabilities, or 1,000 independent participants. Identical feature vectors remain tied. Uncertain birth times are labeled, not silently made exact.

Changed answers invalidate the previous displayed ranking and require fresh interpretation review. Source and profile revisions retain old frozen results in the export history. A later result after rankings have been seen is exploratory; it does not overwrite the first test. The fixed six rules are not refitted on an answer edit.

## Privacy and receiving surface

Raw responses are retained in the participant's browser. Optional interpretation sends the chosen responses to the existing configured research model with explicit consent. Candidate scoring receives only the reviewed neutral profile and birthplace. Runtime result records use unguessable handles and bounded memory; handles expire after two hours or restart. There is no public list of participant records. Exported data remain private unless the participant chooses to share them.

Deploy as an additive route on the existing Life Patterns service, preserving existing authentication, provider settings and the original interview. No new paid service or provider substitution is required. Before promotion, verify direct answer-to-score tests, original-score reproduction, source-quote rejection, actual browser edit/review/freeze/reveal behavior, and the deployed endpoint. Source edits or a green helper test alone are not usable-product completion.
