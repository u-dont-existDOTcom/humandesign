### Small source-first takeaways, not independent natal interpretations

1. Tedlock p95–99 **warns against treating the 260-day cycle as if it must have a culturally canonical “first day,” or each sign as simply good/bad**. Lowland historical and Momostenango contemporary practices are similar but not identical.
2. Reingold–Dershowitz p216 **defines mathematical correlation GMT 584283** for a calendrical date-conversion algorithm. It does not define a measured **personality** effect or settle 584285 and Indigenous living local continuity without a second original witness.
3. Vail/Aveni Ch5–7 distinguish **prophetic/divinatory calendar almanacs, ritual timing, and astronomical tables**. These require different potential empirical outcome types (individual natal trait vs ritual/electional event vs celestial ephemeris). Treating all as birth-sign personality astrology would contaminate an eventual blinded test.
4. None of these four books has been read exhaustively in this owner-input pass. Later worker should read originals at exact page anchors and verify image-backed codical rows against scans before turning interpretation into a rule; **no Maya behavioral rule has been frozen or accepted into the runtime**.
