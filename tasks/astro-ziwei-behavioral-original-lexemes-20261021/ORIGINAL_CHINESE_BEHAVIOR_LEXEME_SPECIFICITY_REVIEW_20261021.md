## Reproducible source-to-chart overlap and dependence

The independent, previously accepted Iztro 2.6.0 original/source 312 synthetic vendor-comparison row JSON was retrieved by exact immutable Git source SHA, grouped to its **120 structurally distinct artificial Life-palace SOURCE chart configurations** (ten chosen modern dates × twelve hour branches, duplicate computational sex/leap variants collapsed only after source chart identity was checked).

In those 120 finite purpose-built SOURCE charts, TaiYang appears as a Life-palace principal star **10** times, TianLiang **8**, and **both together 2** times; their union has **16**, not 18, configurations. WuQu appears 9 times. The 2 joint TaiYang+TianLiang configurations share the same literal helping phrase but are NOT two independent trait observations, nor do any of these 120 charts have actual human survey answers. This sample is not a calendar/birth-population prevalence estimate. Auxiliary WenQu/YouBi/QingYang positions were NOT computed in this main-star-only comparator.

A source-derived reverse chart-matching or forward prediction system must not count every mention of the same helping behavior in multiple source-star descriptions as independent confirmation, especially when two stars may occur in the same Life palace. More importantly, an absent selected star does NOT predict the absence of the trait. Source text includes unquantified conditions and occasional multi-star references; there are no empirical conditional effect sizes.

## What this changes about the pilot

The earlier four source phrase→behavioral-question prototype remains **exploratory and unregistered**. This analysis establishes **a new failure mode**: lexical “specificity” or source quote uniqueness cannot be assumed; one purported behavior may be ascribed to several major/auxiliary stars or conditional combinations. A single pair of star descriptions may say essentially the same behavior at different positions, while conditional source statements require other star combinations and palace dignities.

Recommended next safe work is a **source-graded claim dependency and explicit eligibility resolver**, combining the source intro passages, conditional old same-palace rules and exact lexical overlaps without translating fortune/rank/health grade rules into invented psychological weights. Each candidate should include clear source provenance, all computational dependencies (major vs auxiliary, required benefic/malefic, year stem and palace, original calendar/clock school), conditional versus unsupported/ambiguous status, and a fixed abstention default until verified. Follow the existing psychometric instrument scan: GDMS is not identical to decisive commitment; argumentativeness scale predicts behavior imperfectly; prosocial scales have six facets; competitiveness overlaps contentiousness. No invented calibrated probabilities. Genuine blinded consenting humans and matched decoy/non-astrology baselines remain unstarted.

**Actual independent human predictive validations: 0.** No individual diagnosis, fate claim, gender/moral category or medical advice can be inferred from the historical text.
