# Full-survey import and continuation repair

Version: 2026-09-27 / candidate 1. Scope: measurement recovery, not an astrology interpretation or a new short survey.

## Owner outcome and preservation

Preserve usable full behavioral evidence for one shared, source-grounded model across people. Development fitting remains permitted. The historical selected rules, narrow pilot, source meanings and previous results are unchanged. This repair does not require each tradition to work separately and does not impose a six-rule ceiling on a future shared model.

This is an Iteration-lane repair of collection/provenance, not a validated instrument, production deployment or completed two-person fit. Its basis is the existing full protocol and evidence guide. No new psychometric taxonomy, weighted coverage score or completeness threshold is introduced.

## Distinguish four judgments

1. Evidence usability: what the actual wording supports in its stated circumstances.
2. Mapping admission: which exact neutral distinction, if any, that evidence can support.
3. Provenance and permission: source type, review, corrections, consent and exposure history.
4. Recovery performance: what a separately versioned model subsequently achieves.

A useful interview can have partial mappings. A well-formed export can have poor measurements. Neither a high question count nor a rare fitted chart match establishes measurement adequacy. There is no required percentage of facets, question quota or universal demand for remembered examples.

## Import without erasing or fabricating evidence

Keep the received document and its checksum privately. Preserve every rendered question and answer exactly as received, with stable imported-turn IDs. Identify the source as an edited record when it says it was edited; do not call it a verbatim original conversation. A participant may confirm the received wording without turning it retrospectively into an original transcript.

Recover original route IDs, corrections and review from the original conversation when available. Do not fabricate missing IDs. Use null for unknown consent, review or historical blinding instead of treating missing metadata as false, true or participant refusal. Missing JSON is a serialization problem, not proof that the answers are unusable. An alternative format is admissible after a documented source-preserving import.

Do not publish raw responses, participant coding, birth information or their content-derived hashes to public Git. The current supervisor seeing birth information does not establish that the original interviewer saw it; neither does omitting it from a later packet restore the supervisor's blindness. Keep exposure stages separate. The recovery interviewer must receive no birth information, charts, predicted answer directions or ranks.

## Semantic admission, not keyword matching

For each proposed mapping record: source turn IDs and exact answer quotes; original conditions and time frame; evidence kind; proposed neutral facet; supported scope; unsupported extensions; and review status. Canonical route IDs may remain unknown. An off-bank answer can be admitted through explicit contextual-equivalence or narrower-scope review. Exact wording is not a validity requirement, and topical similarity is not equivalence.

A mapping must not supply a missing contrast, frequency, cause, motive or bodily timing from a prompt, theory or adjacent answer. Reported experience is self-report, not observed performance. A hypothetical premise is not participant biography. Retain historical/current differences, learned strategies, conditional alternatives, uncertainty and counterevidence. Multiple follow-ups about the same behavior do not become independent votes.

Retain supplementary evidence even when no current facet is adequate. Do not force it into a convenient slot or silently discard it. Unknown remains unknown, not the negative pole. A subjective word can be sufficient for a scoped self-description; ask for clarification only when different meanings would materially change the proposed coding, not to demand philosophical proof.

Examples constructed for this repair: declining a task because one's arm is in a cast does not establish low persistence under ordinary ability; enjoying applause does not establish waiting for invitation before entering a role; silence because a train is passing does not establish conflict avoidance.

### What editing and confirmation cannot establish

The edited question is a report of context, not verified evidence of the exact prompt originally shown. When a mapping depends critically on a condition present only in that edited question, do not use it as a verified matched-scene condition without an original trace or a new respondent account that states the condition. Retain provisional/narrower descriptive evidence instead. Conditions explicitly stated in a respondent answer can support its scope even when the question or canonical route is unavailable.

A missing hedge, exception or correction is not evidence that none existed. Editor-written summaries, headings and labels are not respondent utterances. Confirmation can acknowledge the gist of the received respondent text; it cannot retrospectively verify original question wording or create wording-level evidence for negotiation/framing. New substantive clarification becomes a dated new turn. Check material items selectively, not by requiring all answers to be repeated or confirmed one by one.

Metadata completeness means that each relevant field is present with an attested / unknown / not-applicable status and its source, not that every status is affirmative. Unknown consent allows private preservation and draft review, not public sharing or promotion into the consent-requiring participant fit. Unknown earlier blinding remains an exposure flag, not a failed import or fabricated clean baseline.

Determine what remains unresolved from all preserved respondent evidence, not merely from currently credited facets. Different source formats can create apparent between-person differences; retain source type in the shared-model feasibility review. Disclosing a supervisor's birth exposure does not make their admission decisions blind: final neutral admission and adaptive question selection for this continuation belong to a fresh birth-free reviewer/interviewer. The birth-aware supervisor's source comparison remains a provisional audit, not an independently blinded mapping result. No external model receives private participant data without the applicable authorization.

After review the highest permitted status is reviewed_for_development_fitting, with scope and exposure limitations—not validated. Review of a public policy is not review of the participant's private answers.

## Resume rather than restart

First reconcile source/provenance and only ambiguities needed to admit the next question. Do not display an interpreted personality summary before new questions; keep the normal neutral evidence review at the end, reducing avoidable consistency priming. Do not make the respondent re-answer clear usable reports. Recover existing research consent when available; otherwise obtain the missing permission once. Explain that additional questions address distinctions the record did not establish, not that earlier answers were wrong.

Use the full bank as a menu. For every next turn, privately record:
- exact canonical route ID, or explicitly labeled exploratory/adapted route;
- the existing answer IDs establishing any antecedent;
- the unresolved neutral distinction;
- why plausible different answers would materially change that distinction;
- the relevant conditions held fixed or varied;
- the exact final rendered wording and its admission result.

Then ask one question. These fields are a planning/admission record, not a lengthy participant-facing checklist. Do not promote a proposed route merely because a facet is empty. Apply the existing final-wording admission before rendering. One repair attempt is allowed under the existing protocol; suppress an inadmissible route after that, preserving the unknown.

Prefer a new everyday setting once a distinction is resolved. Do not spiral through neighboring coping, purpose or relationship questions merely because they are easy to ask. Do not pursue the topics of the currently selected six rules exclusively. Do not use a chart, missing vote, poor rank or desired recovery result to select questions.

Matched contrasts remain matched. If a workload comparison is used, its baseline, activity, duration, food, sleep, breaks and recovery remain explicit. If role entry or audience adaptation is proposed, obtain both sides of the applicable contrast unless already present. Do not manufacture those contrasts from generic narrative resemblance.

An existing off-bank response that already supplies equivalent conditions can remove the need for a canonical prompt. A missing distinction that is inapplicable, private, redundant or low-information remains unassessed. Partial inputs can support an explicitly limited exploratory analysis; they must not be labeled a full-survey test, and their omissions must not be filled with favorable predictions.

## Source-preserving closeout

Provide a concise neutral summary with source-turn references, conditions, earlier/current distinctions and unknowns. Ask whether any item is materially inaccurate. Append corrections rather than deleting history. Record the participant's actual review response, including disagreement or refusal; never manufacture assent.

Export all imported and new turns. Each new turn includes canonical/adapted route identity, antecedent references, exact question, exact answer and question-admission metadata. Evidence records include exact source quotes, observation, scope, conditions, evidence type, candidate facet IDs, unsupported extensions and review status. Coverage states are answered / partial / unassessed / inapplicable / skipped / not reached after natural stop; they are not a score.

Keep collection/record review separate from model mapping review. Preserve null values for unknown earlier consent or exposure. Freeze the new reviewed version before any subsequent birth-data comparison. Do not claim a pre-reveal freeze for an earlier record without evidence. Later corrections remain permitted and versioned; calling them exploratory does not prohibit them.

A deterministic validator can check structure, source links, quote containment and declared statuses. It cannot certify semantic equivalence, respondent truthfulness, completeness or predictive validity. A structure-only pass must never set semantic_validated=true.

## Immediate launch

Attach the self-contained companion packet and the received response document to a fresh interview-only conversation, or use the original interview conversation if its exact history is available and not birth/chart-contaminated. Do not attach birth data, rankings, the case audit or a selected-rule scoring map. The packet includes all three exact full-survey authorities; it is not a reduced question list.

Start by reconciling the received record and missing permission/review, not by asking the opening question again. Continue only through admissible unresolved distinctions, stop naturally or when requested, and return the reviewed export. The research team then decides whether the supported profile is sufficient for the next explicitly scoped analysis; missing coverage alone is not an automatic veto.

# Scenario-first interview v6 — live-pilot redesign

Version: scenario-bank-v7.0-20260922. Status: text-only redesign candidate after one owner live pilot. This is not an application deployment, a validity claim, chart scoring, or full AstroHD recovery.

## Purpose

Help the respondent describe how they usually respond to concrete situations, including the conditions that change the answer. The bank is a menu, not a quota. A missing facet is not a reason to ask a weak question.

No chart, birth data, expected answer, fit score, or target-theory prediction may select, phrase, admit, or interpret a question.

## Before every next question

1. Respect stop or pause immediately. Save any answer already included, then ask nothing else.
2. Save the exact answer and exact rendered question privately before moving on. Raw answers and content-derived identifiers stay out of public Git.
3. Read the whole reply. Preserve substantive answer clauses, uncertainty, conditions, corrections, and process feedback separately.
4. Identify one concrete unresolved distinction that could change the scoped description. If none exists, do not ask another question.
5. Check the antecedent. A conditional follow-up requires the exact response type it names; a nearby topic or empty coverage field is not enough.
6. Check premise sufficiency. The respondent must not have to invent the main motive, baseline, cause, intensity, workload, environmental condition, relationship frame, or other determinant needed to answer.
7. Check discrimination. Suppress questions whose ordinary answer is nearly forced, tautological, or too universal to distinguish anything useful.
8. Check redundancy against the full transcript. Paraphrases, positive/negative mirrors, and already-supplied causes or limits are not new evidence.
9. If the reply already supplies part of the target, acknowledge it and ask only the missing piece. Do not replay the whole scene unless the referent is otherwise ambiguous.
10. Ask one response task. Do not bundle action, motive, mood, body state, history, and justification into one turn.

### Final rendered-question admission

A route selector, planner, coder, or other model output is a **proposal, not admission**. Before any proposed question is shown to the respondent, re-run the admission checks on the exact user-facing wording that will be rendered. This applies especially to generated narrow follow-ups that are not verbatim bank questions.

The final wording must independently pass all of these checks:

- **context binding:** if the antecedent is nonadjacent, re-name the scene or repeat the material conditions in the question itself;
- **premise sufficiency:** do not make the respondent invent other material determinants of a broad choice;
- **construct discrimination:** at least two materially different plausible answers must exist that would change the scoped description;
- **nonredundancy:** the answer is not already present or recoverable from prior words;
- **construct alignment:** the wording actually isolates the intended neutral distinction rather than a broader decision;
- **single response task:** the rendered question still asks one answerable thing;
- **expected information gain:** given the existing answer history, the plausible responses are likely to resolve or materially change the scoped description rather than merely restate an obvious feasibility condition, add a weak preference, or make the description slightly nicer.

If any check fails, **do not show the proposed question**. Repair it once using only neutral context; if the repair still does not pass, suppress the route and preserve the facet as partial/unknown/unassessed as appropriate. A missing planning target is not permission to weaken this gate.

Concrete regression: after a respondent has conditionally accepted a fixed group schedule when feasible, do not ask whether “anything else” would affect whether they join the group. Joining can depend on many unspecified factors and the ordinary answer is nearly forced. Even a cleaner question about whether some scheduling choice “would be nice” should be suppressed when the likely result is only a mild preference layered onto an already-known feasibility boundary. If a retained work priority remains materially useful, ask only when plausible answers would materially alter the scoped description; otherwise leave the facet partial and move on.

## Handling partial, conditional, and critical answers

A respondent saying “it depends” is not a failed answer. Preserve the named conditions. Ask a narrower contrast only when it can change the description and the needed conditions can be specified without inventing biography.

When process feedback and an answer coexist, save both. Example: “that question is vague, but I’d probably ask them why” contains design feedback plus a behavioral answer.

If the respondent asks what a premise means, repair the premise before interpretation. If one clarification still leaves the scene unanswerable, preserve unknown/inapplicable and move on.

If a reply supplies action but not mood, say what was supplied and ask only mood. If it supplies a reason, do not ask WHY again. If it supplies the cause of a developmental change, do not ask R12 merely because R12 exists.

## Scene sufficiency

A hypothetical scene may simplify reality, but it must include every factor that materially defines the intended contrast.

Do not use vague determinants such as “poor progress,” “intense work,” “useful features,” or “a method that works” when the missing degree or cause is what determines the answer. Specify the relevant obstacle, workload, trade-off, friction, or rationale.

Do not hide the respondent’s motive inside the premise. If a proposal could reasonably arise from different motives that would change the answer, state one motive explicitly or use separate matched variants.

Do not credit a rationale, value, comparison, or leverage point merely because the stimulus supplied it. When the construct is how a respondent frames or negotiates something, credit only answer-originated selection, wording, concession, objection handling, or trade-off.

Do not stipulate the very intention, preference, trust, value, or other respondent state that a route is meant to measure. A premise may hold external conditions constant, but the target internal state must remain answer-originated.

Do not ask respondents to name virtues of an unspecified incumbent system. Either supply a concrete old/new trade-off or use a real familiar system they identify.

For workload questions, specify enough of task type, duration, breaks, sleep/recovery, and environmental load to avoid making heat, illness, physical exertion, or time-on-task silent confounds.

For multi-day or multi-week workload routes, state what happens on nonwork days and whether any longer recovery period occurs. Do not leave weekly rest/recovery to respondent invention, and avoid workloads whose ordinary answer is so forced that the route loses discrimination.

For a matched variant, hold every material non-target determinant constant across the compared scenes. If intensity or duration is the intended change, keep task modality, environment, sleep/food assumptions, and other answer-determining conditions fixed unless another change is explicitly part of the construct.

For promise/follow-through scenes, specify a bounded feasible remaining task, enough available time, ordinary baseline energy, and any competing obligation when those factors would otherwise determine the answer. Do not interpret an unspecified burden as will or commitment.

For resource-purpose scenes, normalize the amount when the set of realistic uses depends materially on scale. A vague “extra money” amount cannot support comparison of motives across respondents.

## Construct boundaries

Ordinary behavior is not automatically trait evidence. Leaving a fully adequate method alone, practising something boring only when strongly motivated, or avoiding an unaffordable ticket should not be promoted into novelty, discipline, or intuition constructs.

Repetition value is activity-specific. Ask about repetition only inside one concrete repeated activity, and do not generalize from one activity to repetition in general without compatible evidence from additional contexts.

Inverse wording is not independent corroboration. If an earlier answer says greater safety increases closeness, do not ask whether lower safety reduces closeness merely to fill the opposite field. Ask only if a genuinely additional asymmetry or weakening route remains useful.

For risk cues, classify the antecedent before transfer probing: direct constraints (unavailable, unaffordable, prohibited), direct hazards, and familiarity-dependent pattern deviations are different evidence. M06 is for the third class only.

When a matched or transfer probe removes the information source that made an earlier cue meaningful, do not add an unexplained replacement cue such as surprise, map knowledge, directions, or geographical intuition. Either remove the source cleanly or ask directly for the boundary under which the original cue would carry meaning.

For bodily-signal routes, a specific bodily/felt reaction must actually be reported. If no bodily/felt reaction is reported, suppress R04, M10, SIGNAL-DEPENDABILITY, and SIGNAL-NOT-FOLLOWED. A brief, fleeting, or inconsistent reaction is still an eligible antecedent: the downstream routes are what distinguish duration, cross-choice consistency, perceived usefulness, and override conditions.

For recognition/status, ask whether genuine admiration is intrinsically rewarding; do not define away all valued consequences and then treat “no difference” as meaningful.

## Routing and follow-ups

Prefer a different everyday setting after one useful distinction is resolved. Do not exhaust a family because neighboring routes exist.

Follow-ups must earn their turn by changing the possible description. Empty planning targets, symmetrical opposites, and curiosity are not enough.

A route that claims audience adaptation or another context difference must itself elicit a comparison across at least two matched contexts, unless a prior answer already supplies both sides. One audience or one context cannot receive comparison credit from an imagined contrast that was never answered.

When the respondent delegates resolution to another person or source, preserve that as their resolution strategy. Do not force them to personally verify what they explicitly said they would send back for group/source agreement.

When external structure is accepted conditionally, preserve the feasibility condition. Do not ask an adaptation probe if the answer already states willingness to adapt when feasible.

A particular helping/responsibility follow-up requires a particular need or helping choice. General social presence is not enough.

An outreach follow-up requires an actually wanted connection or opportunity. Absence of new contacts does not create a social deficit.

## Evidence and interpretation

Keep hypothetical premises, usual-response self-report, retrospective recollection, actual events, normative views, and process feedback distinct.

Credit only what the answer supports in that exact context. Do not turn one response into a global trait, mechanism, diagnosis, skill score, moral judgment, or population rarity claim.

When one route can feed different facets depending on its antecedent, record the antecedent-to-target mapping explicitly and credit only the context-matched facet. When a route intentionally covers only a narrow slice of a broader facet, mark that partial scope in route metadata and preserve the same boundary in the evidence guide.

Self-rated usefulness is not objective accuracy. A predicted unfamiliar response is not observed performance. Physical affection is not automatically sexual desire.

Developmental reports can include approximate life periods if volunteered, but do not request birth dates or use demographic clues to select questions.

## Ending

Stop when no remaining route is both admissible and expected to add a useful nonredundant distinction. Do not continue to fill coverage.

A natural stop means the current behavior-only interview is saturated under this bank; it does not mean the person is fully described, AstroHD is recovered, or the survey is validated.

Exploratory questions stay outside canonical routing and evidence credit unless a separate mapping review explicitly authorizes them. They must not reopen a naturally stopped canonical interview merely to create coverage.

On resumption, recover the exact private transcript and last state. Do not ask successful questions again unless a correction or genuinely new contrast requires it.
