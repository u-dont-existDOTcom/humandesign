# Full-survey import and continuation repair

Version: 2026-09-27 / candidate 1. Scope: measurement recovery, not an astrology interpretation or a new short survey.

## Owner outcome and preservation

Preserve usable full behavioral evidence for one shared, source-grounded model across people. Development fitting remains permitted. The historical selected rules, narrow pilot, source meanings and previous results are unchanged. This repair does not require each tradition to work separately and does not impose a six-rule ceiling on a future shared model.

This is an Iteration-lane repair of collection/provenance, not a validated instrument, production deployment or completed two-person fit. Its basis is the existing full protocol and evidence guide. No new psychometric taxonomy, weighted coverage score or completeness threshold is introduced.

## Distinguish four judgments

1. Evidence usability: what the actual wording supports in its stated circumstances.
2. Mapping admission: which exact neutral distinction, if any, that evidence can support.
3. Provenance and permission: source type, review, corrections, consent and exposure history.
4. Recovery performance: what a separately versioned model subsequently achieves.

A useful interview can have partial mappings. A well-formed export can have poor measurements. Neither a high question count nor a rare fitted chart match establishes measurement adequacy. There is no required percentage of facets, question quota or universal demand for remembered examples.

## Import without erasing or fabricating evidence

Keep the received document and its checksum privately. Preserve every rendered question and answer exactly as received, with stable imported-turn IDs. Identify the source as an edited record when it says it was edited; do not call it a verbatim original conversation. A participant may confirm the received wording without turning it retrospectively into an original transcript.

Recover original route IDs, corrections and review from the original conversation when available. Do not fabricate missing IDs. Use null for unknown consent, review or historical blinding instead of treating missing metadata as false, true or participant refusal. Missing JSON is a serialization problem, not proof that the answers are unusable. An alternative format is admissible after a documented source-preserving import.

Do not publish raw responses, participant coding, birth information or their content-derived hashes to public Git. The current supervisor seeing birth information does not establish that the original interviewer saw it; neither does omitting it from a later packet restore the supervisor's blindness. Keep exposure stages separate. The recovery interviewer must receive no birth information, charts, predicted answer directions or ranks.

## Semantic admission, not keyword matching

For each proposed mapping record: source turn IDs and exact answer quotes; original conditions and time frame; evidence kind; proposed neutral facet; supported scope; unsupported extensions; and review status. Canonical route IDs may remain unknown. An off-bank answer can be admitted through explicit contextual-equivalence or narrower-scope review. Exact wording is not a validity requirement, and topical similarity is not equivalence.

A mapping must not supply a missing contrast, frequency, cause, motive or bodily timing from a prompt, theory or adjacent answer. Reported experience is self-report, not observed performance. A hypothetical premise is not participant biography. Retain historical/current differences, learned strategies, conditional alternatives, uncertainty and counterevidence. Multiple follow-ups about the same behavior do not become independent votes.

Retain supplementary evidence even when no current facet is adequate. Do not force it into a convenient slot or silently discard it. Unknown remains unknown, not the negative pole. A subjective word can be sufficient for a scoped self-description; ask for clarification only when different meanings would materially change the proposed coding, not to demand philosophical proof.

Examples constructed for this repair: declining a task because one's arm is in a cast does not establish low persistence under ordinary ability; enjoying applause does not establish waiting for invitation before entering a role; silence because a train is passing does not establish conflict avoidance.

### What editing and confirmation cannot establish

The edited question is a report of context, not verified evidence of the exact prompt originally shown. When a mapping depends critically on a condition present only in that edited question, do not use it as a verified matched-scene condition without an original trace or a new respondent account that states the condition. Retain provisional/narrower descriptive evidence instead. Conditions explicitly stated in a respondent answer can support its scope even when the question or canonical route is unavailable.

A missing hedge, exception or correction is not evidence that none existed. Editor-written summaries, headings and labels are not respondent utterances. Confirmation can acknowledge the gist of the received respondent text; it cannot retrospectively verify original question wording or create wording-level evidence for negotiation/framing. New substantive clarification becomes a dated new turn. Check material items selectively, not by requiring all answers to be repeated or confirmed one by one.

Metadata completeness means that each relevant field is present with an attested / unknown / not-applicable status and its source, not that every status is affirmative. Unknown consent allows private preservation and draft review, not public sharing or promotion into the consent-requiring participant fit. Unknown earlier blinding remains an exposure flag, not a failed import or fabricated clean baseline.

Determine what remains unresolved from all preserved respondent evidence, not merely from currently credited facets. Different source formats can create apparent between-person differences; retain source type in the shared-model feasibility review. Disclosing a supervisor's birth exposure does not make their admission decisions blind: final neutral admission and adaptive question selection for this continuation belong to a fresh birth-free reviewer/interviewer. The birth-aware supervisor's source comparison remains a provisional audit, not an independently blinded mapping result. No external model receives private participant data without the applicable authorization.

After review the highest permitted status is reviewed_for_development_fitting, with scope and exposure limitations—not validated. Review of a public policy is not review of the participant's private answers.

## Resume rather than restart

First reconcile source/provenance and only ambiguities needed to admit the next question. Do not display an interpreted personality summary before new questions; keep the normal neutral evidence review at the end, reducing avoidable consistency priming. Do not make the respondent re-answer clear usable reports. Recover existing research consent when available; otherwise obtain the missing permission once. Explain that additional questions address distinctions the record did not establish, not that earlier answers were wrong.

Use the full bank as a menu. For every next turn, privately record:
- exact canonical route ID, or explicitly labeled exploratory/adapted route;
- the existing answer IDs establishing any antecedent;
- the unresolved neutral distinction;
- why plausible different answers would materially change that distinction;
- the relevant conditions held fixed or varied;
- the exact final rendered wording and its admission result.

Then ask one question. These fields are a planning/admission record, not a lengthy participant-facing checklist. Do not promote a proposed route merely because a facet is empty. Apply the existing final-wording admission before rendering. One repair attempt is allowed under the existing protocol; suppress an inadmissible route after that, preserving the unknown.

Prefer a new everyday setting once a distinction is resolved. Do not spiral through neighboring coping, purpose or relationship questions merely because they are easy to ask. Do not pursue the topics of the currently selected six rules exclusively. Do not use a chart, missing vote, poor rank or desired recovery result to select questions.

Matched contrasts remain matched. If a workload comparison is used, its baseline, activity, duration, food, sleep, breaks and recovery remain explicit. If role entry or audience adaptation is proposed, obtain both sides of the applicable contrast unless already present. Do not manufacture those contrasts from generic narrative resemblance.

An existing off-bank response that already supplies equivalent conditions can remove the need for a canonical prompt. A missing distinction that is inapplicable, private, redundant or low-information remains unassessed. Partial inputs can support an explicitly limited exploratory analysis; they must not be labeled a full-survey test, and their omissions must not be filled with favorable predictions.

## Source-preserving closeout

Provide a concise neutral summary with source-turn references, conditions, earlier/current distinctions and unknowns. Ask whether any item is materially inaccurate. Append corrections rather than deleting history. Record the participant's actual review response, including disagreement or refusal; never manufacture assent.

Export all imported and new turns. Each new turn includes canonical/adapted route identity, antecedent references, exact question, exact answer and question-admission metadata. Evidence records include exact source quotes, observation, scope, conditions, evidence type, candidate facet IDs, unsupported extensions and review status. Coverage states are answered / partial / unassessed / inapplicable / skipped / not reached after natural stop; they are not a score.

Keep collection/record review separate from model mapping review. Preserve null values for unknown earlier consent or exposure. Freeze the new reviewed version before any subsequent birth-data comparison. Do not claim a pre-reveal freeze for an earlier record without evidence. Later corrections remain permitted and versioned; calling them exploratory does not prohibit them.

A deterministic validator can check structure, source links, quote containment and declared statuses. It cannot certify semantic equivalence, respondent truthfulness, completeness or predictive validity. A structure-only pass must never set semantic_validated=true.

## Immediate launch

Attach the self-contained companion packet and the received response document to a fresh interview-only conversation, or use the original interview conversation if its exact history is available and not birth/chart-contaminated. Do not attach birth data, rankings, the case audit or a selected-rule scoring map. The packet includes all three exact full-survey authorities; it is not a reduced question list.

Start by reconciling the received record and missing permission/review, not by asking the opening question again. Continue only through admissible unresolved distinctions, stop naturally or when requested, and return the reviewed export. The research team then decides whether the supported profile is sufficient for the next explicitly scoped analysis; missing coverage alone is not an automatic veto.
