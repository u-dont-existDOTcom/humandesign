# Revised scenario bank — second review

Design candidate v4. The pilot is paused. The 55 original nodes and 20 optional probes below are a bank, not a fixed 75-question interview. No claim of shorter burden or new semantic validity is made.

Imagine each situation on an ordinary day. Answer with what you would usually do or feel, not what you think you ought to do. Say what changes your answer when it matters; a real example is welcome but not required.

Do not show interpretation notes or coverage labels to the respondent.

Admission: The scene fits or the respondent knowingly chooses to imagine it; target information remains useful and unanswered.

Interpretation limit: Action is not motive, responsibility or durable value tension. Use WHY only when reason is missing; keep all motives the respondent supplies.
