# Optional shared-topic WhatsApp follow-up delivery plan

## Scope and source

For a previously interviewed volunteer who wishes to answer a separate development supplement, provide the neutral source questions from `reference/custom_gpt/releases/life-patterns-shared-topics-v1.10/knowledge/SHARED-LIFE-TOPICS-v3.json` (the same bytes are in the cumulative GPT 1.10 ZIP). No theory labels, chart attributes, birth details or predicted traits appear in the participant text.

This is a **candidate, participant-optional** selection tailored against existing behavioral themes. It does not assert chart blinding, participant consent or independent review. A lightly edited source-only interview is not a verbatim transcript and does not by itself prove consent.

## Short voluntary sittings

Each sitting contains at most five *base* optional questions; a participant may choose fewer or decline. The order is a suggested sequence, never mandatory. All wordings remain byte-for-byte those of the source bank; optional clarifications are only asked if needed, at most two justified conditional probes in a sitting.

- Sitting 1: `DREAM`, `TIMELINE`, `VOCATION`, `HOME`, `BELIEF_CHANGE`
- Sitting 2: `MYSTERY`, `BONDS`, `TRAVEL`, `LEARNING_HISTORY`, `PLAY`
- Sitting 3: `RENUNCIATION`, `STATUS`, `RESOURCES`, `PARENTING`, `SENSORY_ENJOYMENT`
- Sitting 4: `ANGER_REPAIR`, `RECIPROCITY`, `CONTENTMENT`, `UPBRINGING`, `PRIVACY`

Three other bank domains (`BELIEF`, `ART`, `KINSHIP`) may be adequately represented by earlier practice, creativity and family-boundary narratives; do not mechanically reask them. Some selected domains can also have partial prior evidence and should be skipped if adequately answered or if the participant considers them duplicative.

## Consent and preservation

The participant message first requests permission to use *new* optional answers in the research and says that every item can be skipped, marked uncertain or not applicable. WhatsApp replies or voice notes are independent source evidence; preserve their original text/audio, date and any explicit consent, rather than replacing original utterances with edited paraphrases. Do not send a whole twenty-question packet at once: offer only one sitting, and follow up only if desired.

Avoid public repository storage of personal replies, identifying names, contact details or birth information. A text packet for the owner was saved separately to the owner's OS Downloads folder, not committed to the public repository.

After an existing GPT/Railway final submission, the current backend permits exact retries only; do not imply that a later WhatsApp exchange was automatically appended to the finalized record. If the earlier interview's blinding or consent is uncertain, preserve that uncertainty in any analysis.

## Admission

These are unscored development responses. The optional questionnaire can improve factual coverage but does not create independent predictive validation of any astrological or Human Design system.
