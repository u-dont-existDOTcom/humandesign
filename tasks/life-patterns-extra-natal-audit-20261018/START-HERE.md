## Use

These seven new items are research candidates, *not seven additional mandatory questions*. The initial best opportunities are actual education/learning history, how long anger persists and relationships repair, sense of contentment versus wanting change, and giving **and** receiving support. Childhood caregiving and private self-disclosure are particularly optional, and everyday sensory pleasure is probably a short follow-up rather than another module.

A natal source describing a domain is **not** evidence its chart rules predict that domain. All earlier participant answers and the live GPT/Railway survey were left unchanged. No new chart scoring was attempted.
