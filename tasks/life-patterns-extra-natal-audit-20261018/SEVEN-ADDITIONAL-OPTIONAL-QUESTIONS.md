# Seven more optional Life Patterns question candidates

These are **research-only proposals**, not questions already installed in the
existing GPT or Railway completion. Do not ask all seven automatically.
Select a topic only when important and not already answered adequately.
Allow the respondent to decline or stop without scoring the refusal.

## LEARNING_HISTORY — Education Learning History

**Priority:** 1 (1 is most useful for the initial pilot).

**Ask:** Across your life, what subjects, skills or crafts have you actually spent substantial time studying or learning, if any? Was this mainly formal education, self-directed study or hands-on experience?

**Optional follow-up:** Which subjects have stayed important over years, and which were brief interests?

**Record separately:** actual_sustained_learning_topics_optional, formal_vs_self_directed_vs_practical_optional, period_and_depth_optional.

**Why this is different from earlier questions:** How one learns or solves problems is not the actual history of what one studied and practiced.

**Privacy/interpretation rule:** No IQ estimate, reading-level ranking, academic pedigree gate or claim about actual intelligence.

Historical translated source IDs: V-I-MERCURY-EDUCATION, D-II-JUPITER-MERCURY-LAW, BJ-XVIII-LITERACY-MATH

## ANGER_REPAIR — Grievance Duration Repair

**Priority:** 1 (1 is most useful for the initial pilot).

**Ask:** When something genuinely upsets or angers you, how long does that feeling usually matter to you: briefly, hours, days, much longer, rarely, or very differently depending on the situation?

**Optional follow-up:** If a relationship continues, can you forgive or reconnect before the feeling has fully gone, or does it tend to work differently?

**Record separately:** typical_duration_and_variability, time_to_resume_normal_contact_optional, forgiveness_vs_trust_vs_reconciliation_optional.

**Why this is different from earlier questions:** Emotional baseline and reaction to an unreliable friend do not directly measure the duration of anger or capacity for repair.

**Privacy/interpretation rule:** No forced re-telling of harm or abusive experiences; boundaries and forgiveness are not automatically opposites.

Historical translated source IDs: BJ-XVI-ANGER, BJ-XVII-FORGIVING, BJ-XVII-LINGERING-ANGER

## RECIPROCITY — Giving Receiving Help

**Priority:** 2 (1 is most useful for the initial pilot).

**Ask:** In your actual close relationships or communities, how often do you meaningfully help others, and separately how often do you seek or accept help yourself?

**Optional follow-up:** Does giving come more easily than accepting support, or is it the other way around? Both or neither are valid.

**Record separately:** giving_frequency, receiving_frequency, practical_vs_emotional_optional, conditions_that_change_the_balance_optional.

**Why this is different from earlier questions:** Responding to one group request is not the actual two-way history of providing and accepting support.

**Privacy/interpretation rule:** Frequency of giving is not proof of virtue. Receiving help is included as a design choice, not directly quantified by the cited classical claims.

Historical translated source IDs: V-I-JUPITER-BENEFICENCE, D-II-JUPITER-VENUS-GIVING, BJ-XVI-GENEROSITY, BJ-XVII-FAMILY-HELP

## CONTENTMENT — Contentment Satisfaction

**Priority:** 2 (1 is most useful for the initial pilot).

**Ask:** When your basic situation is acceptable, how often do you feel genuinely content with things as they are? Separately, how much do you feel drawn to improve, change or pursue something else?

**Optional follow-up:** Can you feel content and still strongly motivated to grow, or do those experiences usually pull in different directions?

**Record separately:** frequency_of_contentment, frequency_of_desire_for_change, context_or_time_changes_optional.

**Why this is different from earlier questions:** Contentment differs from existential meaning, resources available, and desire for recognition.

**Privacy/interpretation rule:** Treat motivation and contentment as independent axes; avoid labeling contentment as passivity or ambition as greed.

Historical translated source IDs: BJ-XVI-CONTENTMENT

## UPBRINGING — Formative Caregiving Arrangements

**Priority:** 3 (1 is most useful for the initial pilot).

**Ask:** If you want to describe it, did you grow up mainly with one stable set of caregivers or did your living or caregiving arrangements change significantly?

**Optional follow-up:** If something changed, was it an important turning point for you, or simply a normal part of your childhood?

**Record separately:** stable_vs_changed_vs_unknown_or_decline, approximate_period_optional, impact_on_later_life_optional.

**Why this is different from earlier questions:** Actual early caregiving arrangements are not measured by where a person now feels at home.

**Privacy/interpretation rule:** No assumption of separation, adoption, trauma, parental death, illness or special household type. Respondent may omit any details.

Historical translated source IDs: D-I-UPBRINGING, D-I-PARENT-SEPARATION, BJ-XVII-EARLY-SEPARATION

## PRIVACY — Privacy Self Disclosure

**Priority:** 4 (1 is most useful for the initial pilot).

**Ask:** In everyday relationships, how much of your inner thoughts, feelings and personal life do you generally share, and how much do you prefer to keep private?

**Optional follow-up:** Does this depend strongly on trust, subject or the other person's response?

**Record separately:** typical_disclosure_range, relationship_context_optional, actual_sharing_vs_desire_optional.

**Why this is different from earlier questions:** Reliability and adapting one's speech do not directly measure privacy versus openness.

**Privacy/interpretation rule:** The old source's 'secretive' passage is pejorative; it is neither evidence of lying nor a moral judgment. Do not ask what actual secrets are.

Historical translated source IDs: V-I-SATURN-PRIVACY

## SENSORY_ENJOYMENT — Everyday Sensory Pleasure

**Priority:** 5 (1 is most useful for the initial pilot).

**Ask:** What everyday things bring you noticeable pleasure through the senses—such as food, music, scent, warmth, colors, textures or being in natural places—if any?

**Optional follow-up:** Do you regularly seek these experiences out, or mostly notice them when they happen?

**Record separately:** domain_specific_enjoyment_optional, frequency_and_usual_intensity_optional, nature_vs_art_vs_food_optional.

**Why this is different from earlier questions:** Awe, music appreciation, sensory tolerance and everyday pleasure are not identical; much of this may be a follow-up to ART or PLAY rather than a new question.

**Privacy/interpretation rule:** Do not interpret food pleasure as a nutritional or mental-health diagnosis. Prefer a conditional follow-up over a full independent module.

Historical translated source IDs: V-I-VENUS-PLEASURE, BJ-XVII-FOOD, BJ-XVII-GARDEN
