## Important source-bound examples

- G15: Ordinary engaging work and intense physical work are different exposure levels. Tiredness after enjoyable demanding tasks is not a negation of engagement.
- G17: Practical importance and expected social welcome are separate correction conditions, not two opposing traits.
- D0: Instrumental repetition to prevent mistakes may coexist with pleasure in skill refinement in some contexts.
- G04: The V3 free-text answer adds a conditional troubleshooting decision rule. This explains all-four selections but does not prove uniqueness.
- G23: No evidence of a stable first-notice category. All-four is unknown/context-dependent, not four separate trait votes.
- M01: Historic anchored behavior under an unchanged actual deadline versus several broad V3 pressure reactions; needs matched contexts to resolve.
- STATUS: Recognition must never be scored from the historical joint laptop-ownership evidence facet.

## Independent reviewer comparison

The frozen reviewer admitted six behavioral evidence items. Four V3 questions are only **partially related** to those old constructs: adapting to group demands, why someone repeats details, correction thresholds, and recognition value. Two V3 prompts measure something materially different: willingness to persuade versus success when attempting persuasion, and life-phase changes in sociability versus post-interaction need for company/solitude. **Zero exact independent behavioral replications** and **zero new natal predictions** are established.

A separate, versioned facet alignment file lists the six precise old facet identifiers, V3 routes and reasons without source quotes.

## Next step / GPT decision

1. **Do not silently replace the existing production GPT interview yet.** It has an accepted, versioned, consented 84-turn submission and one functional collection/reviewer protocol. A development-only questionnaire should not rewrite existing results or masquerade as independently calibrated scoring.
2. Develop an opt-in V3 cognitive pilot with new people, without chart labels. Compare prompt-alone, example-scaffold and example-plus-context variants, with attention to six weak and two tense items. Keep meaningful negatives, no-stable-default and missing answers.
3. Measure real answerability, independent coding consistency, specificity, time, uncertainty and information gain. Length of answers and number of checked examples are not traits or chart accuracy.
4. Freeze an improved instrument and a separate respondent-blind behavioral coding codebook before adding it as an opt-in version of the **same** GPT; the private GPT Editor requires an owner action, and an old Railway Action submission must not accept novel pilot types without a versioned contract.
5. Test astrology/HD chart matching only with untouched participants and preregistered source-anchored chart predicates, baselines and decoys. Reusing this owner's chart and responses is model development, not new validation.
