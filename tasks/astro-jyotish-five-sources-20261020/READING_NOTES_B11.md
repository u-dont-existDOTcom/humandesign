## 1. Whole-chart Nabhasa patterns: explicit precedence and different temporal scope

The uploaded Kalyana Varma `Saravali`, **Chapter 21, physical PDF pp43–45**, distinguishes **32** detailed Nabhasa patterns while reporting that Yavanas knew **1,800**. It divides them into 20 Akriti, 7 Sankhya, 3 Asraya and 2 Dala groups (Ch21.1–6). This is a **source-author's count**, not independently reconstructed 1,800 formulas. The 32 are not all independent because the patterns overlap.

- **21.7, PDF43:** when an Asraya and another Nabhasa yoga coexist, the latter explicitly prevails and the Asraya result does **not** count as another supportive prediction.
- **21.11–18, PDF43–44:** Akriti describes seven-planet occupancy of consecutive houses, trines, quadrants and alternating signs; Dala and Asraya use benefic/malefic or movable/fixed/dual conditions.
- **21.19, PDF44:** Sankhya offers seven possible classes according to whether the **seven classical planets** occupy 1 through 7 different signs. This feature can be computed without fitting any personality descriptions.
- **21.20–21, PDF44:** the text explicitly says Nabhasa outcomes hold across life **independently of dasha periods**. Do not label these as special-event year predictions unless a separate timed rule is supplied.

The outcomes mix status, occupation, family, moral labels and wealth. They cannot be turned into many independent hit counts or applied as cultural/moral assessments.

## 2. Mūla Daśā subperiod arithmetic differs from ordinary fixed Vimśottarī assumptions

`Saravali` **Chapter 42, PDF118–121**, gives its **own** system of distributing a major period among eligible planet sublords.

- **42.1:** a conjunction with main lord is allocated half; positions in fifth/ninth one-third, seventh one-seventh, fourth/eighth one-fourth (English rendition).
- **42.2–4:** more than one planet in a group triggers a strongest-planet choice, and other named historical authorities offer angular/succedent/cadent or strength ordering.
- **42.5:** the source instructs expressing shares over common denominator and normalizing them to the parent period. A naive model granting each eligible body its full fraction would double count time; fractions are **relative weights**, not independently absolute allotments.
- **42.12–53:** six other sublords for each of seven classical major lords gives 42 described ordered-pair outcomes (social, occupation, financial, familial and historical medical topics). These are **text paragraphs**, not 42 independent real-world successes.
- **42.54–57:** the text itself modifies the descriptions according to planetary strength, debilitation, combustion and the major lord's relationship to the ascendant and Moon sign lord.

Before date-scoped tests this specific Mūla Daśā period/strength system needs implementation separately from Vimśottarī and Jaimini sign periods. There is no authorization to select whichever historic period system matches a known person.

## 3. Extremely dramatic dignity claims can have impossible antecedents

`Saravali` **Chapter 45, PDF123–124**, describes planet-specific dignity outcomes and the number of simultaneously exalted/own/debilitated planets. Ch3.35–36 (PDF8) specifies:
- Sun exaltation = **Aries** (0–30° zodiac), Mercury exaltation = **Virgo** (150–180°). These sign placements require at least **120° of ecliptic-longitude separation**.
- Sun debilitation = **Libra** (180–210°), Mercury debilitation = **Pisces** (330–360°). These require at least **120° separation** as well.
- In physical geocentric astronomy Mercury remains close to the Sun; the maximum apparent Mercury elongation is approximately **28°**, as also stated by the European Space Agency's BepiColombo background: `https://www.esa.int/esapub/bulletin/bullet103/grard103.pdf`.

Therefore two of the English translated text's extreme all-seven-planet conditions **cannot occur** for an actual correctly computed sky:

1. **45.23–26:** *all seven exalted* → eventual emperor.
2. **45.38–41:** *all seven debilitated* → extreme deprivation.

A uniform tropical-to-sidereal zodiac offset leaves the Sun/Mercury relative separation unchanged. The original compiler may have listed these extreme patterns systematically for rhetorical completeness; we are **not** labeling them a measured false positive. They are **empty antecedents**, provide zero discrimination, and their absence from every birth chart cannot be counted as repeated successes. See `IMPOSSIBLE_EXTREME_DIGNITY_RULES_B11.json`. Do not extrapolate this impossibility to every six-planet or own-sign pattern.
