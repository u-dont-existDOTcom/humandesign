## 1. A direct positive-versus-negative 11th-house source collision

The uploaded Bhasin *Sarvārtha Cintāmaṇi* PDF pp212–213, eleventh-house **v2** says gain can follow (A) eleventh lord in angle/trine **or** (B) a malefic in eleventh. The same section **v10** says a malefic in eleventh gives loss, with an additional loss condition affecting its lord. This is not an extrapolation from a later author: a searchable Sanskrit e-text at [VedicPupil Ch8, subchapter 3 vv2 and10](https://vedicpupil.in/library/books/sarvartha-chintamani/chapter-8) visibly retains a positive eleventh-malefic alternative and a negative eleventh-malefic alternative. The two indications overlap for the same invented sign arrangement, e.g., Aries Lagna with Saturn in Aquarius (eleventh; lord-eleventh located there, not an angular/trinal house). No transparent priority between the two is given in this **pair of verses**. A possible omitted strength/exception from other verses or a manuscript variant must be researched, not supplied post hoc. This is a **source-level conflict**, not an empirical refutation of astrology; no wealth prediction is issued.

## 2. An independent translation mismatch: movement is not hunting

The same uploaded Bhasin **tenth-house v29**, PDF208, interprets *movable navāṃśa AND movable rāśi* as hunting, and explicitly says fixed or dual navāṃśa does **not** yield hunting movement. The [online Sanskrit/e-text 8.1.29](https://vedicpupil.in/library/books/sarvartha-chintamani/chapter-8) instead differentiates **movable division → movement**, **fixed → stability**, **dual → hunting excursion**. For a dual navāṃśa, the English and online Sanskrit interpretations yield opposite hunting claims. The printed Sanskrit page image itself was not inspected; the electronic text may share an editorial lineage with other web transcriptions. Do not count ordinary travel as a successful prediction of hunting, and do not flatten the sign modes.

## 3. Professions are not a single determinate mapping in *Jātaka Pārijāta*

The uploaded Sastri *Jātaka Pārijāta*, Adhyāya XV **vv43–50**, scanned PDF924–930, separates a person's **source of money** (parents, spouse, others) from the **work by which money is earned** (10th-house and navāṃśa mechanics). In its note after v43, the translator presents one approach choosing the **strongest** indicator, but also reports Bhattoṭpala citing Gargi for considering **every relevant indicator**, not merely the strongest. This disagreement belongs to the *commentarial note* layer, not a universal printed Sanskrit precedence rule. Multiplying all possible income categories until one matches a biography would be wrong; so would selecting whichever one matched after seeing occupation.

The occupation lists themselves mix translation and later gloss. **Medicine** appears among Sun-governed livelihoods in the translated **XV.44**; **doctors and apothecaries** are placed in the expanded *translator's Notes* after the Mars-related **XV.46**, rather than in that translated verse. In the uploaded Rath *Jaimini Upadeśa Sūtra* course PDF48, a **modern author's** Kala Graha table assigns medicine to Saturn. Those are at least three different source layers and calculation logics; they are not independent confirmation that a doctor's natal chart was predicted precisely.

## 4. Source-specific observables and pre/post-event timing

- Uploaded Bhasin PDF204–208 Ch8 treats **public utility, official authority, honor, fame and duties** as different tenth-house matters. PDF212–213 uses the **eleventh** for revenue/wealth. The tenth-house employment domain must not silently absorb all five outcomes.
- Uploaded *Sārāvalī* Ch30 PDF72–76 describes social rank, undertaking success and honor for planets in tenth. This is **not** an industry code or reliable modern occupation title.
- Uploaded Kerr Portuguese BPHS TXT around line881 lists the tenth as authority, livelihood, honor and overseas life; the eleventh as salary/prosperity. Line-window 1430–1504 includes source-specific positive and negative cases. A second-eleventh lord exchange is connected specifically to accumulated wealth **after marriage**. Bhasin eleventh-house **v3** on PDF212 has the same exchange as a **generic wealth-gain** configuration, also naming an alternate both-lords-angular trigger. Generic wealth gain versus after-marriage gain are distinct predeclarable targets, not extra hits for one favorable circumstance.
- Uploaded Rath PDF47–48 ties some occupational labels to **daily Kala Graha/directional timing** and gives an anecdote about completed service preceding travel clearance. A retrospective anecdote does not validate natal profession prediction, and the method's input clock/direction cannot be replaced with natal-only data.
