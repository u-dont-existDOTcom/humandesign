## Why the testability distinction matters

Traditional texts often contain five different forms of claim, requiring five different observational/ethical methods:

1. **Independently observed historical events**, e.g. age prosperity began (*Sarvārtha* II.114–116), actual property deed (*Sarvārtha* IV.54–56), profession/degree (*Jātaka* XI.81–86), institution initiation and departure (several schools). Each demands a frozen *target* and independent dated records, plus social opportunity baselines.
2. **Repeated prospective experiences**, such as dreams (*Sārāvalī* 38.16–21) or time-specific changes in livelihood (*Parāśara* Sudarśana section74). These need prospective diaries and careful placebo/seasonality controls rather than retrospective narratives.
3. **Behavioral frequency or habit**, e.g. meditation, japa, charitable work; must specify hours/recency/opportunity and should never be translated into proof of metaphysical enlightenment.
4. **Reverse birth reconstruction using extra inputs**, e.g. horary time, name pronunciation, querent posture (*Sārāvalī* Ch52). This is a distinct experiment from predicting outcomes with independently supplied natal data.
5. **Metaphysical, historical moral or high-stakes claims**, including afterlife realms, purported mokṣa truth, medical mortality, sexual family allegations: their literal truth is unscorable or unsuitable for person-specific profiling. Source text can be studied without applying it as clinical or moral judgments.

`EMPIRICAL_TEST_FEASIBILITY_MATRIX_B21.json` records 14 specific families: eleven prospective *candidate* methods (some exceptionally difficult or not natal-forward) and three clearly excluded/non-chart-only classes. Each records source locators, minimum data, mathematical/translation blockers and practical baselines. **This is a design inventory, not a request to modify the live questionnaire or enroll people.** It intentionally refuses to treat self-declared mystical attainment as externally verified and keeps dream astrology separate from existing Ayurveda dream taxonomy.

## Experimental priority is not the source's reported accuracy

We have **0** independently verified real-human predictive successes and **0** astrology runtime rule promotions. Neither a greater number of claimed outcomes nor mathematical complexity automatically increases expected accuracy. The most intellectually defensible future comparison would select externally measurable outcomes, use verified birth times, a completely frozen source text/calculation/question battery and realistic population and participant-matched decoys. It must report all misses, low-frequency signatures, contradictions and null tests.
