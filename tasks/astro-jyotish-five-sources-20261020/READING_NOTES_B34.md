### One traditional verse is transmitted twice and translated differently

- **English 35.57 / PDF99:** Moon with Jupiter in **5th**, Mars and Mercury in **12th and/or 3rd**, plus Moon/Mars/Mercury in own navāṃśas, not debilitated, Mars/Mercury not combust.
- **English 35.143 / PDF104:** states Mars, **Moon** and Mercury occupy **12th/3rd**, *and* Moon with Jupiter occupies **5th**. Taken strictly and assuming exclusive houses, Moon is required to be in two houses. The source author's Sanskrit cannot be declared impossible on this English phrasing alone.
- The older Sanskrit printing catalogued as Pandurang Javji 1928, digitized on [Jain page157](https://jainqq.org/explore/009887/157) and [page166](https://jainqq.org/explore/009887/166), contains **essentially the same stanza at Ch35.58 and Ch35.144**, with minor word-shape differences (the compound naming Moon–Mercury–Mars begins `śaśi-budha-rudhira-`). Both end with Jupiter in the fifth with Moon and a ruler-fame outcome. **These are not independent yoga predictions** and the textual doubling should be counted once per unique astronomical condition.

The Sanskrit's precise distribution of Mars/Mercury versus Moon's dignity/location requires grammatical criticism; **the earlier English 57 offers one coherent interpretation**, but is not silently promoted as uniquely canonical. A modern [VedicPupil gloss](https://vedicpupil.in/library/books/saravali/chapter-35/verse-57) says the planets are *not* in twelfth/third, contrasting with the supplied English affirmative Mars/Mercury location; its automatic explanation is neither original verse nor independently validated repair.

### Source-quality alarm: internet copies can introduce fake missing/duplicated verses

The [Central Sanskrit University online transcription](https://www.csu-lucknow.edu.in/e-books/saravali/p36.html) at chapter35:
- lacks a complete labeled verse **57**;
- contains a **malformed 58**, duplicating earlier words;
- duplicates the Vargottama-Moon stanza as both **61 and 62**.

[Sanskrit Wikisource](https://sa.wikisource.org/wiki/सारावली) repeats these apparent electronic mistakes; similarity should not be treated as independent manuscript agreement. The **older printed Jain-Quantum source** has *distinct* verse57 (Moon in an astronomical water-lily-related navāṃśa), verse61 (watery rāśi/navāṃśa Moon plus benefic Lagna), and verse62 (Vargottama Moon). In this passage, **the older printing is the safer local witness**, even though both electronic transcriptions present plausible Devanagari overall.

This is a separate failure mode from the verified **English Leo fifteenth-vs-Sanskrit fifth** in B33. Both show why one website labeled “original Sanskrit” is insufficient for high-stakes exact-rule extraction.
