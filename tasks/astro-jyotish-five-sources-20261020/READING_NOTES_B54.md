## New cross-book source lineage confirmed physically

1. **Official authority:** *Sarvārtha Cintāmaṇi* Chapter 8 tenth-house **verse 15**, 1929 PDF168/printed150, and *Jātaka Pārijāta* Adhyāya XV **verse 60 in the 1922 printing**, PDF319/printed923, share a distinctive opening `आज्ञास्थानाधिपे सौम्ये` and the promising-authority ending, but vary in benefic association wording (`शुभदृष्टियुतेपि` versus `शुभयुक्तेक्षितेऽपि`) and the title/result (`आज्ञाधिपो` versus `आज्ञधरो`). The shared formula is a **literary dependency**. This does **not** count as a second successful prediction or establish a direction of copying.

2. **Conjunction versus aspect:** The more consequential near-parallel is *Sarvārtha Cintāmaṇi* tenth-house **verse 17** (same 1929 printed page150), `आज्ञाधिपे मन्दयुते रन्ध्रनाथेन **संयुते**`, and *Jātaka Pārijāta* XV **verse 61** (1922 printed p923), `आज्ञाधिपे मन्दयुते रन्ध्रनाथेन **वीक्षिते**`. The former says the eighth lord is **conjoined**, while the latter says the tenth lord is **aspected** by the eighth lord. The rest of the conditions mention Saturn, a cruel divisional placement or angle, and an adverse official-orders outcome. **Do not silently translate the two Sanskrit predicates as the same chart configuration.** Exact aspect style, lords, condition grouping and strength are not frozen; in-source morality allegations are never labels for real people.

3. **Edition numbering:** The 1922 printed *Jātaka Pārijāta* calls the above verses **XV.60 and XV.61**, whereas the currently accessible VedicPupil transcription calls the same two Sanskrit clauses **XV.59 and XV.60**. The offset is **verified only for these aligned clauses**, not assumed globally across the chapter. This is why edition + physical page + Sanskrit anchor must travel together in all source IDs. The B52 near-verbatim *Bṛhat Jātaka* X.1 / *Jātaka Pārijāta* XV.43 livelihood formula remains a separate source dependency family.

## Other original-source differences material to prediction design

4. **Foreign trade is narrower than migration:** The printed 1922 *Jātaka* XV.57, PDF318/printed922, treats **Mars with Venus in the tenth counted from the Moon** as a historical indicator of a merchant trading in foreign lands. That target is **actual commercial activity**, not simply residence abroad, an international trip, or being paid from abroad. If ever tested, commercial records, opportunity to trade, and observed years would be needed. Do not use one relocation to validate both commerce and migration predictions.

5. **The same historical chart condition can describe several businesses:** *Jātaka* XV.57–59, PDF318–319, includes the Mars/Mercury scientific/scholarly livelihood wording and the Saturn/Venus cosmetic-powder/crafts/commerce category. Sanskrit `śāstra` does not automatically mean a narrow modern laboratory-science profession. Several crafts or commercial activities presented as alternative outcomes cannot become a unique occupation match chosen retrospectively.

6. **Multiple sources of wealth are part of classical discussion:** *Bṛhat Jātaka* X.4 says wealth may be assessed in several ways in connection with strong planetary indicators, and Sastri's original *Jātaka* notes near XV.43, scanned PDF312/printed916, cite Bhattoṭpala reporting Gargi's dissent from choosing only the strongest professional planet. This is source-history context, not a canonical conflict-resolution procedure or empirical accuracy measurement. There are already broad category overlaps (B52); do not multiply duplicate transmissions into independent successes.
