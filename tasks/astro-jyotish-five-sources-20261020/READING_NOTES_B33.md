## Most consequential numerical correction

The English source **Ch35.159, PDF104** attributes potential rulership to Jupiter in twentieth of Sagittarius, **or** Sun/Mercury in **fifteenth** of Leo. The corresponding Sanskrit **Ch35.160** clearly reads `सिंहस्य पञ्चमांशे`—**fifth** part/degree of Leo, not fifteenth. Evidence from two publicly accessible witnesses:

- [Central Sanskrit University Lucknow *Sārāvalī* Ch35 Sanskrit](https://www.csu-lucknow.edu.in/e-books/saravali/p36.html), lines 812–814, records `धनुषि च (त्रिशे)विंशे` for Jupiter and `सिंहस्य पञ्चमांशे` for Sun/Mercury, with **`त्रिशे` (thirtieth)** inserted as a **parenthetical alternative** to **`विंशे` (twentieth)**.
- [Jain Quantum Sanskrit printing transcript](https://jainqq.org/booktext/Saravali/009887), around text line 2370, reads **`धनुषि च विंशे`** (twentieth) and **`सिंहस्य पञ्चमांशे`** (fifth), with no printed `त्रिशे` in that displayed witness. The printed-source lineage/independence of both digitizations is unverified.

The same widely circulating modern English translation also appears on other websites, repeating Leo **15th**. A copied English reading is **not** an independent Sanskrit witness for that number. We must preserve *both literal versions* in data, mark the English numeral likely erroneous, and **not select a variant based on known chart results**. Whether ordinal "fifth part" defines 4°–5° of Leo or an exact 5° longitude is a further mathematical-semantic question. The present source comparison **does not resolve the boundary convention** or assert an exact point of zero temporal measure.
