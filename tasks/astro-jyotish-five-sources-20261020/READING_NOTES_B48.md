## Source discrepancies with substantial model effects

1. **18.30: disjunction replaced with interval.** Portuguese `entre os 22 e 27` can be read as the continuous range 22–27. Sanskrit `dvāviṃśe saptaviṃśe'bde` names **22 and 27** as separate alternatives. An interval admits four extra ages **23,24,25,26**, dramatically expanding the chances of a retrospective match. Preserve both, reject silent broadening for source-driven scoring.
2. **18.33: ninth from ninth placement arithmetic.** Portuguese parenthetically identifies ninth from ninth as **seventh**, although an inclusive whole-sign ninth from ninth equals the **fifth**. Sanskrit online also supports fifth. The added Portuguese Rahu-in-either-house wording does not match the narrower or ambiguous Sanskrit `taddvaye rāhusaṃyute` straightforwardly; mark this separate as text grammar unresolved.
3. **18.34: placement mismatch.** Portuguese describes Venus in **ascendant** and seventh lord in its own house. Sanskrit's `bhāgyāj jāmitrage śukre` places Venus **seventh from ninth**, which equals the **third**. The remainder of the Sanskrit (`tad-dvūne`) is ambiguous in these witnesses, so a source-canonical computational rule is not frozen.
4. **18.38: disagreement in high-stakes numeric claim.** Portuguese renders historical spouse-loss years **12 or 21**, whereas Sanskrit's `dvādaśaika-ūnavimśābde` gives **12 or 19**. Not an empirical death-risk forecast, not evidence of a manuscript variant without an independently checked edition.
5. **18.40–41: explicit marriage ordinals.** Sanskrit uses `prathamaṃ` (first), `dvitīyaṃ` (second), `tṛtīyakaṃ` (third) and offers 10th, 22nd and 33rd years under ONE antecedent configuration. Portuguese indexed transcription is malformed around the tenth *year* but preserves the three numbers. Verses 18.22–23 instead say simply marriage and qualify with `prāyaśaḥ` (generally), without specifying first marriage. Thus the B47 coactivated 5/9 vs 7/11 pair has a **disjoint single-first-marriage hypothetical**, but whether the Sanskrit author intended a single indexed event is unresolved. It is not a logically unconditional contradiction.
6. **18.21 can suggest multiple marriages but does NOT automatically resolve B47.** On the synthetic B47 sky, Venus is exalted in the dual sign Pisces and its sign-lord Jupiter exalted in Cancer, satisfying the first two prerequisites. We must not assert a genuine v21 multiple-marriage trigger or use its potential to reconcile 18.22 and 18.23 post hoc.
