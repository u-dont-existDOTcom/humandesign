## Findings

1. **Verse 18.33 has materially rival chart predicates.** R. Santhanam's *Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra* English volume I (p.168; OCR transcription of printed book at StudyLib) explains Venus in fifth with Rāhu in **either fifth or ninth**. The EnjoyLearningSanskrit English gloss instead adds **the lords of both fifth and ninth** conjoined with Rāhu. The visible Sanskrit `taddvaye rāhusaṃyute` does not explicitly name the two *lords*; neither source's resolution should be silently promoted as the only possible parse. These readings are **not nested**: synthetic Aries Lagna with Venus in Leo, Rahu in Sagittarius, Sun in Aries and Jupiter in Gemini activates the Santhanam reading only; Venus Leo, Rahu Capricorn, and both Sun and Jupiter Capricorn activates the lords-reading only. Ten no-biography source diagnostic tests pass; source scoring prohibited. [Santhanam text](https://studylib.net/doc/27858738/maharishi-parashara---brihat-parasara-hora-sastra--vol.-1-) · [Sanskrit plus second gloss](https://enjoylearningsanskrit.com/scriptures/parashara/chapter-18/verse-33/).

2. **Verse 18.34 contains a newly detected *Sanskrit* one-character textual variation.** The named-encoder proofread SanskritDocuments e-text has `तद्द्यूने` (*tad-dyūne*); E-Bharatisampat has `तद्द्वूने` (*tad-dvūne*). `dyūna` is attested as the seventh house in Varāhamihira's *Bṛhat Jātaka* 1.16, making the former lexically intelligible; whether the latter is an error or different source reading is **unresolved** without Sanskrit printed plate/manuscript evidence. Both agree first half places Venus *seventh from ninth* (third from ascendant). Santhanam's and Portuguese glosses placing Venus in ascendant conflict with that first half. Even after recognizing `dyūna`, the antecedent of `tad` in the second clause is unresolved; **no canonical predicate selected**. [SanskritDocuments Ch18](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_misc_sociology_astrology/par1120.html) · [E-Bharatisampat Sanskrit](https://www.ebharatisampat.in/read_chapter.php?bookid=NDE4NjYyMTkwMjQxMzUy) · [Bṛhat Jātaka 1.16](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/brihat-jataka-by-varahamihira-sanskrit-english/d/doc1501579.html).

3. **Verse 18.38's number is still a genuine *source-vs-translation* discrepancy, not a proven separate Sanskrit recension.** SanskritDocuments and the Sanskrit e-texts inspected give historical spouse-loss years 12 or **19**, versus published Santhanam English and Kerr Portuguese 12 or **21**. The Santhanam printed English transcription resolves that 21 is not uniquely Portuguese; the base Sanskrit used by the translator remains unverified. This is historical archive material, **not a personal health/death prediction**. [Sanskrit](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_misc_sociology_astrology/par1120.html) · [Santhanam English](https://studylib.net/doc/27858738/maharishi-parashara---brihat-parasara-hora-sastra--vol.-1-).

4. **Verse 18.40 has contradictory English/Hindi renderings on one site.** VedicPupil English says the *duration* of first marriage is ten years; VedicPupil Hindi says the *first marriage happens* in the tenth year. The surrounding verse 18.41, Santhanam printed translation, and other Sanskrit-context translations favor age-at-event: second at 22, third at 33. We record the site's language split without counting it as independent testimony. Explicit ordinals here do **not** impose a global priority on generic vv22-34. [English](https://vedicpupil.in/library/books/brihat-parashara-hora-shastra/chapter-18/verse-40) · [Hindi](https://vedicpupil.in/hi/library/books/brihat-parashara-hora-shastra/chapter-18/verse-40).
