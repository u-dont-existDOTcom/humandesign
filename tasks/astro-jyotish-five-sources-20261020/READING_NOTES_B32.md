Two observed edition/text-layer errors:
1. **Chapter47, PDF129** visibly prints an impossible descending title **44–15** for the Pisces-decanate paragraph. The [Sanskrit printed source, chapter46 verses44–45](https://www.csu-lucknow.edu.in/e-books/saravali/p47.html) explicitly ends that section with independent vv44 and45. Our source-reading repair **affects only the verse-number count**, not the literal uploaded book text or high-stakes mortality content.

Because numbering itself differs in other Sanskrit recensions, the result is **2,532 numbered verse units for this particular English edition**, not an established cross-manuscript number or proof that all verses are source-critically interpreted.
