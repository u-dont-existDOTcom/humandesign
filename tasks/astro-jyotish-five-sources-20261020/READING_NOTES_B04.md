# JYOT B04 — worldview, family complexity and non-personality life-event domains (2026-10-21)

**Scope:** newly inspected BPHS Portuguese Karakamsha/Arudha section (source text lines 2623–2805) and *Saravali* Chapter 34 (PDF90–94), 22 additional line/verse-located documentary notes in `SOURCE_CANDIDATES_B04.json`.

## A materially different enlightenment claim is present

The uploaded *Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra* Portuguese text, under "Efeitos da 12ª dos Karakamsamsa" (line **2801**), explicitly distinguishes:
- auspicious/inauspicious expenditure from 12th-from-Karakamsha;
- a benefic or Ketu configuration described as reaching heaven after death;
- a separate conditional promise of **complete enlightenment** (*iluminação completa*) with Ketu in specified twelfth-house circumstances (the alternative reference to a '2nd house' is unclear in this Portuguese rendering);
- the *negative* mirror case, Ketu in twelfth linked to malefic, described as not reaching complete enlightenment;
- deity preferences or worship corresponding to planetary combinations.

This **is an explicit birth-chart rule family purporting to describe complete enlightenment**, in addition to *Sarvarth Chintamani* VII.42, VIII.5 and *Jataka Parijata* XV.25 already located. The old model's spirituality coverage therefore requires **multiple separate original rule candidates**, not a generic '9th house means spirituality' approximation. None of these rules validates actual enlightenment in a particular person. The Portuguese line is translated from an English intermediary and is not an independently collated Sanskrit verse; metaphysical endpoints cannot be taken as externally observed facts.

Other previously untracked BPHS sections:
- **line2747:** Venus aspecting Ketu in Karakamsha allegedly forecasts initiation; Saturn aspecting Ketu can instead signify penance or an inauthentic ascetic, underscoring alternative readings and the unfairness of imputing moral labels to people.
- **line2763:** fifth-from-Karakamsha Mercury indicates an ascetic **or** someone managing/controlling people; this disjunction greatly dilutes falsifiability unless fixed beforehand.
- **line2789:** ninth-from-Karakamsha benefits/malefics allegedly distinguish devotion during youth from later hypocrisy: **life-course transitions and age** are part of the stated claims.
- **line2767:** planet-defined vocational specializations distinguish author, scholar, Vedanta philosopher, logician, musician and astrologer. Again, not just generic intelligence.
- **line2805:** the text links trinal malefic patterns to mantra/tantra knowledge, with type of use allegedly modified by further aspects. Distinguish historically reported practice from unverified miraculous effects.

A concrete **calculation-policy fault line** appears in the source: **line2625 is explicitly labeled as an editor's note**, not a Parashara verse, and distinguishes a standard twelfth-house Upapada base from an alternate twelfth-for-odd and second-for-even ascendant rule. A later worker must not silently treat the latter as universally sourced ancient practice. The underlying ascendant-based and Arudha-based terms need exact school/version selection and blind-test freezing.

### How this contributes to the survey coverage problem

It establishes a **non-personality inventory** of what the texts purport to infer from birth charts: formal renunciation, meditation and metaphysical attainments; family composition and birth order; adoption and marital status; parentage/offspring; vocation-specific income; community service; teacher relationships; social rank and life-stage change. It does **not** establish that any existing Life-Patterns questionnaire currently does or does not cover all these domains—we have not audited its current questions in this branch.

The next survey audit should keep four clearly different measurement classes:
1. **Observable historical events:** dated births, adoptions where independently volunteered/verified, documented occupations, initiation dates, public service projects.
2. **Behavior and preference:** meditation practice, giving, teacher relationships, educational interests.
3. **Private experience:** meditative absorption or unusual realizations, measured phenomenologically without asserting attainment.
4. **Unverifiable or ethically unsuitable claims:** heaven after death, factual enlightenment as judged by astrology, insinuations about children's parentage/partner infidelity, defamatory moral labels, medical prognoses.

No questionnaire edits or participant-specific chart reading occurred here. The remaining meaningful research frontier is verifying calculations/source lineage, especially the divergent Upapada and Jaimini charakaraka traditions, and continued broad coverage audit of the other house/effect chapters.
