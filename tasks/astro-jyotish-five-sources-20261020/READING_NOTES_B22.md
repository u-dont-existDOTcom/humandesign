## Two nine-state Avasthā classifications are NOT equivalent

The original Sanskrit verses printed alongside J. N. Bhasin's English rendition of *Sarvārtha Cintāmaṇi* **Chapter XIII verses2–4, PDF273** give **Dīpta, Svastha, Mudita, Śānta, Hīna, Duḥkhita, Vikala, Khala, Kopī**. These are differentiated by exaltation/own/friendly/benefic signs, debilitation, enemy sign, association with malefics, defeat in planetary war, and conjunction with Sun respectively. Chapter XIII verses5–14, PDF273–275, describes their supposed effects **during the ruling planetary period**.

By contrast, the user's separately supplied *Sārāvalī* **Chapter 5 verses2–4, PDF11** lists **Dīpta, Svastha, Mudita, Śānta, Śakta, Nipīḍita, Bhīta, Vikala, Khala**. It treats solar combustion as Vikala and war defeat as Nipīḍita; its Khala derives from harmful divisional status. In Bhasin, **Vikala** instead follows conjunction with a malefic, **Khala** follows war defeat, and **Kopī** follows solar conjunction.

Thus this is **not** merely the same list translated into different spellings. A unified software `enum(9)` would silently change which physical planetary condition generates which result. Underlying versions must remain separate until original Sanskrit critical comparison and explicit computational precedence are available. See `AVASTHA_SCHOOL_MAPPING_B22.json`.

## Astrology also makes source-specific lunar calendar claims

*Sarvārtha Cintāmaṇi* **Chapter XVII verse1, PDF370** links an **extended-duration nakṣatra and tithi** with favorable outcomes. The physical PDF page image clearly reads **CHAPTER—17**, whereas its OCR says **CHAPTER-11**—a straightforward OCR error, not evidence of chapter-variant transmission.

Bhasin's immediately following **TRANSLATOR'S NOTE** explains a `kṣaya tithi` and assigns weights of roughly one/tenth/lakh across tithi, nakṣatra and lagna. This weighting is **Bhasin's explanatory layer**; the note should not be presented as an original verse unless independently confirmed. Subsequent vv3–11 qualify negative angular-malefic results through a specific **Śaṅkarācārya** school view and mitigating benefic-navāṃśa/house relationships, showing that bare “malefics in angles = disaster” is not the complete transmitted rule.

Chapter VI seventh-house **verses33–35, PDF168** allege 100/200/300 wives under divisional configurations; the bizarre historical numbers remain documented but have no demonstrated frequency or literal modern validity.

## Rath's original sutras versus proposed textual 'repairs'

The Sanjay Rath book includes a direct Sanskrit Jaimini **1.1.20–24** fixed-significator sequence on **PDF343–347**:
- Mars and younger relatives/mother; Mercury and maternal kin; Jupiter and elder lineage/husband/children; Venus and spouse/in-laws; Saturn and older siblings.
- Alongside those sutras he quotes portions of *Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra* **34.18–21** and offers possible emendations. On **PDF344**, he suggests changing a received kinship reading to “sister's son,” reasoning that the sage would not make a redundancy. On **PDF345**, he suggests the received Sanskrit `śukra` (Venus) was intended as `śakra` (Indra). Those are **Rath's conjectures**, influenced by presupposed authorial infallibility, **not independently verified manuscript readings**. Keep the received and emended words side by side; don't silently rewrite the source.
- At **PDF347**, Rath interprets “the stronger fixed significator” in a mortality-focused application as **the more afflicted** of two bodies. This is modern operational interpretation, not an explicitly equivalent original Jaimini verse.
- **PDF348–352** retrospectively interprets already publicly known Gandhi family deaths. These are **development examples** that cannot demonstrate blinded prediction accuracy.
- **PDF353–354** quotes BPHS 34.34 assigning twelve primary house significators, then develops a broader hypothesis that every house-lord carries the respective significator's traits to its destination house. The second move is **Rath commentary**, not automatically a literal BPHS 34.34 rule.
