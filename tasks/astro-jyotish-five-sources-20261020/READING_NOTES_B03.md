# JYOT B03 — spiritual vocation, meditative absorption, deliberate withdrawal and public service (2026-10-21)

**Scope:** five uploaded books; source-derived documentary claims, not real-world outcome evidence. **New: 37 locatable research candidates**, all in `SOURCE_CANDIDATES_B03.json`. Existing B01/B02 annotations remain unchanged. PDF page identifiers below are 1-indexed physical PDF pages (not the printed-page footer).

## Result 1 — named meditation/samādhi and mokṣa formulas, not generic spirituality

The original source verse *plus* J.N. Bhasin's English rendering were visually inspected on **Sarvarth Chintamani** PDF **201**, printed **198**, Chapter VII vv40–42. The astrology source explicitly discusses the pairings:

- VII.40: 10th lord in ninth, ninth lord strong by beneficial association/aspect → **japa/mantra repetition, meditation, samādhi**.
- VII.41: ninth–tenth lord linkage plus strength of the navamsa-sign lord occupied by tenth lord → japa, meditation or samādhi.
- VII.42: beneficially aspected ninth/tenth lord in designated exalted-varga-style divisions → claimed **realization of the Supreme**.

The original printed text and English translation were also directly viewed for Chapter VIII verse 5 (**PDF 204**, printed 201): in Bhasin's English it assigns **mokṣa/liberation** to a Pisces tenth-house condition involving Mars and benefics, while a separate condition assigns pilgrimage/ritual Ganges bathing.

**Source-critical limitations:** printed Devanagari and aligned English exist, but no independent Sanskrit critical edition or variant has yet been collated. English distinctions between a reported **meditative experience**, actual **religious observance**, and metaphysical **liberation** must stay separate. A participant's statement of attaining mokṣa cannot be called independent external verification.

This corrects a *coverage gap* in our existing source inventory, **not** proof that a newly found formula works. It does not imply a source rule for ordinary day-to-day awe, surprise or synchronicity; those remain unconfirmed by this examination.

## Result 2 — the same sources distinguish religious sincerity and public work

- **Sarvarth Chintamani** VII.6–11, PDF 195–196, printed192–193: different factors are said to imply established religious/moral devotion, religious institution leadership, sincerity, hypocrisy, and sinfulness. The page images, including VII.8–13 at PDF 196, were inspected. These are not interchangeable: an institutional role is potentially observable, whereas ancient moral characterizations are culturally charged and cannot be used to accuse actual people.
- **Sarvarth Chintamani** VII.43–56, PDF 201–203: many alternative ninth-house conditions forecast charitable conduct, plus **resource/affluence moderators** (VII.47–49) and devotion to a guru (VII.55–56). A positive charity formula without ability to give cannot count as a failed or successful largesse prediction without fixed resource normalization.
- **Sarvarth Chintamani** VIII.1–3 and VIII.8–12, PDF 204–205: the 10th-house domain includes public works, honors, profession, livelihood and foreign residence; the text includes **absence of service** conditions as well as claims about renovating sacred buildings/wells/tanks. These are distinct observable behaviors rather than personality adjectives.

## Result 3 — ascetic-path initiation, noninitiation and changing membership are explicit competing outcomes

From *Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra* Portuguese transmission (André Kerr; CP1252) "Yogas de Vocação Espiritual", **plaintext 5511–5531**, the author-attributed clauses include:

- **5513:** four or more **strong** planets in **one house**; religious type keyed to strongest.
- **5517:** combustion can prevent initiation despite reverence for a religious order.
- **5521:** when others lose strength due to solar combustion, the Sun's ascetic type may instead dominate. This requires precedence with the previous rule; do not silently choose.
- **5523:** Moon-sign lord/Saturn and Saturn decanate/amsa variants; first clause's *aspect direction* is ambiguous in Portuguese.
- **5525, 5527–5529:** planetary-war defeat can imply exit; several strong planets can correspond to multiple orders and the **daśā periods** indicate successive orders/changes. This is explicitly *longitudinal*, not just a natal static personality label.
- **5529–5531:** further philosophical school founder and religion-before-leadership conditions. The Portuguese book places a parallel *Brihat Jataka* formula in **editorial notes** 5515 and 5519, so this transmission does not count as independent evidence against its explicit named older source.

Independently from the other two transcriptions, *Saravali* XX.20–29, **PDF 41–42**, has faith without entrance, leaving religious orders, desire without fulfillment, strength/aspect configurations triggering entry, prosperity loss, distressed ascetic life and ruler-then-ascetic sequences. **XX.21** says another body's aspect can block initiation; **XX.22–23** require Saturn aspects to confer it and XX.23 tells us earlier yogas prevail. The scope/precedence is not specified unambiguously enough to implement from this edition without further critical comparison.

*Jataka Parijata* XV.15, **PDF 913**, contains an **angular/trinal** 4–5-strong-planet condition that is not identical to the Portuguese **one-house anywhere** formulation. XV.17, **PDF 914–915**, adds a **three-planets-tenth-house with strong tenth lord** alternative, and XV.19, **PDF 915–916**, explicitly times a yoga's outcome by a **bhukti** subperiod. XV.23 and XV.42 have editorial notes naming **Brihat Jataka** as a source; the "human forecast count" remains zero.

**Implication:** competing rule families must be versioned with exact conjunction/house/strength/aspect/dasha definitions. A model cannot post hoc count a general devotion signal as supporting a prediction of formal initiation, abandonment, renunciation, or a particular order.

## Result 4 — Jaimini ancient sutra vs Sanjay Rath's additional theories

Rath's *Course on Jaimini Maharṣi's Upadeśa Sūtra*, lesson 15 **PDF288** prints **Sutra 1.1.10**, describing seven or eight chara-karaka scheme options. At **PDF289**, the explanation that seven apply to inanimate bodies and eight including Rahu to living ones is a **Rath interpretive choice** (his commentary, not the literal content of the quoted sutra). The quoted BPHS verses supporting the scheme are also a distinct textual layer.

Rath's chapter about planet-specific atmakaraka "spiritual path" dispositions (PDF304–307) contains modern prescriptive material, including Rahu possibly correlating with spiritual renunciation given other factors. **PDF307** explicitly titles a new personal proposal **"Rath's Theory 1"** and footnote114 states such a label is used when there is no standard classical citation. **PDF317** offers "100% surety" in another author's interpretation of financial spending, followed by retrospective charts; that is **not** a scientifically measured generalization.

Rath's book and the older books can enrich candidate models only if sources and versions stay visibly separate. Rath's retrospective figures must never be mistaken for held-out forward prediction.
