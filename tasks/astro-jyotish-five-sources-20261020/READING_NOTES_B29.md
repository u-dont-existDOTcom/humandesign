The verse's reported outcome connects leadership/livelihood with forests, animals or water. The original English text's missing planet is preserved in the B15 correction audit; do not silently conceal the transmitted error.
- **18.13 and 18.20, PDF39:** the exact same Moon+Mars+Mercury+Venus+Saturn planet set has different verse outcome bundles: both relate to adversity and social recognition but with different positive/negative emphases. No human personality questionnaire can test this. Extra time/place, land/ecology and scientific plant life histories require completely different research design.
