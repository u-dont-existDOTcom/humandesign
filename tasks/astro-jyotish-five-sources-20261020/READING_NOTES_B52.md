# B52 — *Bṛhat Jātaka* occupational formula preserved in *Jātaka Pārijāta*

2026-10-22 UTC. Source lineage study, not an extra originally uploaded source, and no prediction validation. Original user-uploaded V. S. Sastri *Jātaka Pārijāta* scanned PDF924–930, XV.43–50, checked against Varāhamihira *Bṛhat Jātaka* chapter X online Sanskrit and N. Chidambaram Aiyar English commentator notes. Sanskrit printed plates/manuscript variants not independently authenticated.

**Unusually close shared wording.** Jātaka XV.43 and Bṛhat Jātaka X.1 both start `अर्थाप्तिः` (wealth acquisition), use the same Sun-through-Saturn tenth-house source-of-income sequence from lagna/Moon, and share the distinctive closing Sanskrit compound `भेन्द्वर्कास्पदपतिगांशनाथवृत्त्या` referring to a navāṃśa lord of tenth-house lord. There are real differing readings: Bṛhat Jātaka `pitṛpitṛpatni` / `śatru` versus Jātaka `pitṛjananī` / `sapatna`, and the Jātaka online servant word and missing consonant are corrupt/uncertain. This supports a common **textual formula or direct reuse**, not a proven borrowing direction or critical manuscript stemma. Do not normalize differences silently or count each rulebook as independent evidence.

**Parallel occupation family.** Bṛhat Jātaka X.2–3 maps seven classic planets to broad livelihood categories (Sun goods/medicine, Moon agriculture/water, Mars metals/weapons, Mercury letters/arts, Jupiter teaching/religious livelihood, Venus jewels/trade, Saturn labor), and Sastri's original Jātaka XV.44–50 has the corresponding seven categories plus expansions. Not all sentences are the same, and later verse additions may be genuinely distinct claims. Planet-sector categories are overlapping and too broad to imply one unique modern job title without prospective operationalization and realistic baselines.

**Commentary is not ancient Sanskrit verse.** Sastri's Notes after XV.43 PDF925–926 describe strongest-only selection but separately cite Bhattoṭpala quoting Gargi that **all relevant income indicators** should be considered. Aiyar's English BJ X.1 notes explain multiple sources when tenth from lagna and Moon are occupied by planets. The original Sanskrit does not thereby provide a globally frozen method for deciding strongest-only versus many contributors. This cannot be adjudicated after viewing a real person's job.
