## Four materially distinct wording problems

**29.12 — duplicated house in Portuguese.** The original Portuguese sentence makes the twelfth house from AL both aspected and not aspected. The online Sanskrit distinguishes the eleventh (gain house) aspected and twelfth (expenditure house) not aspected. A literal Portuguese formula would never activate; a broad paraphrase would admit arbitrary matches.

**29.15 — different exclusion predicate.** Portuguese excludes *malefic association* with twelfth from AL. Sanskrit excludes *planetary aspect* to twelfth in this context. Association and aspect are separate inputs. A benefic aspect without malefic association is an example on which the versions differ.

**29.27 — different reference origin.** Portuguese says to project seventh-from-AL yogas into second from UL (Upapada); Sanskrit refers to second from the same *pada* (AL in this chapter). In a synthetic Aries AL / Libra UL chart, Taurus and Scorpio show that the two rules are not interchangeable. Neither interpretation is promoted into a scoring model.

**29.29 — dropped family exclusion.** The Sanskrit transfer from AL to Karakamsha explicitly excludes relatives (bāndhava-varjitāḥ). The Portuguese reading omits that qualifier. Omission is not evidence that the broader family-related prediction is authorized or historically accurate.

## Positive controls and evidence boundaries

29.22 agrees at the level of transferring expenditure-source combinations from twelfth into gains from eleventh; this is inherited logic, not independent prediction evidence. 29.25 in both editions preserves the one/two/all-three Moon, Venus, Jupiter options. 29.35 extends relational categories to other padas; it does not cancel the separate kin exclusion in 29.29.
