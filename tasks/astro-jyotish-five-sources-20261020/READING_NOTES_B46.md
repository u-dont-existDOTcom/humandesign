## Important interpretive findings

**Bhasin fifth-house chapter**: vv3-13 supply many positive son-acquisition combinations; vv14-18 oppose these with loss or denied offspring. Crucially v22 on PDF140 directs evaluating both survival and loss configurations together. Vv30-31 PDF142 attach benefic aspect exceptions and sometimes a son *long after marriage* to claims that otherwise appear as denial. These may be candidate **non-event, birth and age-at-first-birth** tests separately; no selecting favorable rule after seeing a biography. His Ch4 vv130-135 PDF128-129 similarly treats benefic vs malefic aspects on mother-indicators as opposite cases. Do not personalize mortality or reproductive outcomes.

**Saravali Ch8 vs Ch9**: Ch8 pp16-18 makes assertions about pregnancy, twins, child sex and delivery timing that require **conception or copulation-time charts** and sometimes both parents' natal data. A natal date/time alone is insufficient; exclude these as NO_INPUT in an ordinary personality pilot, not as incorrect predictions. Ch8 v27 switches parent significators by day/night birth: father Sun (day) / Saturn (night), mother Venus (day) / Moon (night). Ch9 vv28-30 PDF21 distinguishes father absent, abroad and deceased; vv49-50 distinguishes unfavorable aspect, mixed aspects indicating illness, and benefic parental well-being. These are NOT the same target.

**Jataka Parijata Adhyaya IX PDF363-367**: Verse 72 refers to a child's natal star being the same as, or tenth from, **the father's birth star**, thus requires independent father's natal data and cannot be inferred from child chart. The verse makes harmful causal allegations about a child's supposed role in father loss. Vv64-67 give 9-month and 12-year intervals in some historic conditions; other source passages make different and opposite assertions. Never blame a child for a parent's death. B24's Jataka XIII adoption/late-child/timing rules are only cross-indexed, not duplicate new annotations.

**Rath Jaimini Sutra 1.1.16** English (original PDF294) labels the sixth-highest planet as putrakaraka, yet repeats **siblings and master**, the wording assigned to bhratrkaraka at 1.1.13 PDF293. PDF295-296 explicitly uses putrakaraka as offspring indicator. A probable translated-English copy-forward gloss error has been recorded as an unresolved editorial discrepancy. Rath's 7 nonliving/8 living karaka scheme is a modern interpretive selection, not independently proven by counting two citations.
