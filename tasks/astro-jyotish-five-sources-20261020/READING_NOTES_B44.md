## 2. Consequential adjacent claims in chapter 6
- 6.59–61 are **three different natal claims about marriage `bālye`** (historically childhood), not generic 'marriage in one's 20s'. Each uses distinct astrology qualifiers (proximity of seventh lord, benefic house/sign arrangement, Paravata or higher amsa, strong second lord, gentle varga). Neither exact modern adult age nor a calibrated probability is specified; child marriage is historical context, not advice.
- 6.62 links favorable fortune **after marriage** to Venus, houses and benefic support. Online translation flags `śukre'stage` as partly ambiguous ('in the seventh' versus 'combust'); do not freeze unverified computational predicates.
- 6.63 is an **opposing negative** for marriage-linked loss when multiple significators/lords have harmful conditions. 6.64 then permits **recovery after the loss** under another favorable condition. Preserve all three directions without cherry-picking retrospectively and without scoring one trajectory as three independent correct predictions.
- 6.65–66 are interpreted by VedicPupil as delayed **fortune after marriage**. Their `dūre vivāhāt` wording and implied referent must be checked against another Sanskrit/commentary witness: a careless reading as 'delayed marriage' would target a different event. Neither parse is promoted.
- 6.67 directs **reuse of the method for children**; this methodological instruction is not itself an independent birth-of-child event forecast.

## 3. Potential impossible conjunction in 6.58
The online transcription/English says Rahu **and** Ketu in the seventh, and pairs it with severe, gendered negative character/violence claims. Under the standard antipodal-node convention, Rahu and Ketu cannot occupy the same seventh sign/house. We enumerated **12/12** possible Rahu signs and found **0** co-occupation cases. The literal reading would therefore describe an empty chart configuration. We have **not** established whether the Sanskrit phrase should instead express `or`, or whether the transcription is mis-segmented. This is a research warning rather than an emendation; no accusations about a person's morals, infidelity, or potential violence from natal charts are warranted.
