## A crucial lord-versus-occupant correction

The printed *Jātaka* XV.67–68 (PDF321–322) associates source of gains with **planets occupying** the eleventh and notes a strength qualifier; XV.71 (PDF324) instead lists types of wealth source according to **which planet is lord of** the eleventh house. A planet owning the house need not occupy it. Confusing the two turns a narrow calculation into a trivially flexible occupation story. The Saturn occupational insult in this historical source is a discriminatory social classification, **not** an appropriate category for contemporary people.
