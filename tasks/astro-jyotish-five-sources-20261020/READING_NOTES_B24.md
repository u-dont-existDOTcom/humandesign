## Finding A — self-contained competing sibling models, not simply personality

`Jātaka Pārijāta` XII.19–20 **PDF762** has two quite different sibling-count schemes. Verse 19 builds around four indicators and modulates numbers via strength and divisional positions; verse 20 counts Mars-target Aṣṭakavarga benefic marks in **the third from Mars**, then subtracts those in inimical/depressed conditions. Its numerical rules are therefore dependent on exact seven-planet Aṣṭakavarga tables, source polarity and Navāṃśa calculations. The earlier B10–B12 study documented a **Saturn-Lagna sixth** Aṣṭakavarga translation omission—this new sibling method provides another reason not to treat arbitrary translated mark conventions as canonical.

The surrounding XIII? **XII.24** procedure, visibly continuing at **PDF764**, separately averages three navāṃśa positions to estimate younger sibling count. It is labeled with a **page-boundary uncertain verse number** and must be checked against the physical preceding page before any runnable model. XII.26 **PDF764** predicts **no family-property partition** when certain friendly/strong ascendant and third lords combine. Affection, number of siblings and legal division are three different observable outcomes. They cannot multiply one person's broad 'strong family ties' answer into a number of independent hits.

## Finding B — property and family dynamics are multidimensional

- `Jātaka` XII.141–145 **PDF802–803** distinguishes lodging abroad, staying with relatives, absent homeownership/tenancy, a deteriorated house, acquisition of a house and property loss. **PDF803** physically inspected: XII.143 predicts loss under its own fourth/second/twelfth-house clauses; XII.144 predicts number of sound houses from benefic-related house placements; XII.145 contrasts dilapidated versus attractive property by fourth-lord location/strength. - `Jātaka` XIII.43 and XIII.44, **PDF828**, explicitly distinguish *adopted son carrying a family line* from *biological son late in life*. These are **culturally framed, potentially sensitive family facts**, not evidence of reliable forecasting or permission to infer an absent person's private family history. Pregnancy/fertility medical guidance remains out of scope.
- XIII.49 **PDF832** offers **several rival** child-arrival timing routes (lord-of-fifth, daśā/bhukti, Jupiter trines/asterism calculations). These must remain **multiple method versions**, not a flexible pool in which a prior birth outcome selects the one correct interval.

## Finding C — ninth-house exception and literary dependency

`Jātaka` XIV.66 **PDF889/printed879** qualifies the general ninth-house malefic rule: an unfavorable planet with certain exalted/own/friendly dignity can give favorable results under that verse. Treat **the exception and negative premise together**; it cannot be a simple binary "malefic means bad" model. The **Sastri NOTES** printed immediately before v66 state that vv65–87 are also found in an earlier work, but the scanned work name is not reliably recovered by our OCR; no invented attribution or independent-witness count was made.

XIV.86–88 **PDF899–900** separately mention eighth/ninth-house astronomy, social role and learning versus Mercury/Jupiter **absence**—further examples that strength/absence conditions are part of the prediction. Historically prejudicial, medical and sexual descriptors are preserved solely as source history.
