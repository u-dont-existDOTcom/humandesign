## A specific missing Saturn/Ascendant sixth favorable mark

The uploaded English *Sārāvalī*, Chapter 53 verse 8, physical PDF page 142–143, gives Saturn’s Aṣṭakavarga contributions from Lagna at the **1st, 3rd, 4th, 10th, 11th signs only**. The Portuguese *Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra*, line5299, makes the sixth sign positive for every contributing body **except the Sun**, which includes Lagna.

Two accessible Sanskrit digitizations support the sixth in *Sārāvalī* itself: the original printed-volume transcript at [Jain Quantum Sanskrit book, frame223](https://jainqq.org/booktext/Saravali/009887), and the [Central Sanskrit University Lucknow e-book Chapter52 verse8](https://www.csu-lucknow.edu.in/e-books/saravali/p53.html). In the latter, the relevant phrase is **`तनोस्त्र्यायारिखाद्याम्बुगः`**: following `tanoḥ` (“from Lagna”), the house symbols are `tri` (third), `āya` (eleventh), `ari` (sixth), `kha` (tenth), `ādya` (first), `ambu` (fourth). This exactly reconstructs **Lagna [1,3,4,6,10,11]**, agreeing with Portuguese Parāśara. The Jain scan transcription has OCR noise in the word but still shows `ari`; the university transcription makes the parse unambiguous.

The literal uploaded English transcription remains **38 Saturn /336 total**; adding only that source-backed sixth yields **39 Saturn /337 total**. No production calculator was changed. The two Sanskrit digitizations have not been proven genealogically independent manuscripts, so this is a **strong local edition-level correction candidate, not a completed critical edition of the whole book**.

## Chapter order: correction to an earlier inference

The Sanskrit **Jain 1928 catalogued printing** and [Central Sanskrit University index](https://www.csu-lucknow.edu.in/e-books/saravali/index.html) agree on the *last three chapters* as:

| Subject | Sanskrit textual witness | Uploaded English edition |
|---|---|---|
| Aṣṭakavarga contributions | **Chapter 52** (10 slokas) | **Chapter 53** (10 slokas) |
| Manifold nonhuman births | **Chapter 53** (56 slokas) | **Chapter 55** (56 slokas) |
| Effects of Aṣṭakavarga | **Chapter 54** (12 slokas) | **Chapter 54** (12 slokas) |

The Sanskrit Chapter52 ends with an explicit colophon at Jain frame223; the next heading begins Chapter53 nonhuman births, which runs through Jain frame228; Chapter54 contains the eight-mark outcomes and closes the book (Jain frame229). The [university Chapter54 text](https://www.csu-lucknow.edu.in/e-books/saravali/p55.html) contains Aṣṭakavarga effects. **An earlier provisional idea that the Sanskrit printing omitted the “Effects” chapter was wrong: the chapter is present, but transposed in the source sequence.** Do not infer an interpolation merely from chapter order. This is a structural recensional/editorial difference, not empirical evidence.

A second source-language detail: Sanskrit *Sārāvalī* Chapter54 **verse3** describes a Sun-target count of **four** as `सदसतोः साम्यं चतुष्के` (parity of favorable and unfavorable). This supports **a specific classical four-mark-neutral outcome**, rather than deriving all such interpretations only from Sastri's later *Jātaka Pārijāta* note. It does not establish an identical rule for every target or override source-specific neutral contributions.
