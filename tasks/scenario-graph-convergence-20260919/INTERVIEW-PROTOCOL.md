# Scenario-first interview — graph-organized protocol

Version: scenario-bank-v6.0-20260919. Text-only design; pilot remains paused. The graph is an audit/navigation index, not deployed interviewing software.

## Preserve the conversation, not a fixed route

Use the scenario bank as a menu. Start with concrete usual-response questions, one response task at a time. Do not turn 79 available entries or 73 planning labels into a quota. A remembered example is welcome, never a routine entrance requirement. There are no birth-data, chart, expected-answer or fit-score inputs to selection.

Read the whole reply. Save the exact message and question/version privately, distinguish answer clauses from process feedback, and retain uncertainty, conditions and mixed motives. A stop request takes effect immediately: save an answer included with it, but ask no next survey question. Resume only on the owner's instruction, from the preserved record. A saved event is claimed only after readback; a failed write is disclosed, not silently called saved.

Corrections append; they do not erase original words. Supersede the affected interpretation and reconsider any pending follow-up that depends on it. Do not reuse a superseded reason, reaction or earlier version merely because its question ID still exists. Private answers, record locations and content-derived hashes stay out of the public repository. No automatic Railway synchronization is implied.

## Read graph links literally

A **context candidate** is a possible source for the scene, not an instruction to ask every predecessor. Alternatives are OR, not AND. A matching answer volunteered elsewhere is admissible with its exact source, context and meaning verified. A **topic association** is merely helpful context; it does not make an otherwise self-contained scene depend on another answer. A **may inform** link names possible information: neither asking the question nor seeing that arrow fills an evidence field.

A follow-up binds to one exact, active scene/answer instance: role, time perspective, relevant conditions and source event. Do not pool two people, two life periods or two versions under a single question ID. After a topic change, identify the earlier scene and the reported subject in the delivered wording itself. M02 binds to the pressure scene M01, not the preceding topic by position. The new-location and ownership scenarios can be understood independently even though they have advisory links.

## Admit one useful next task

First check pause, declined scope, applicability, and whether a useful distinction remains. Then check the specific antecedent and missing part. If the answer already supplies the information, skip the question. If only part of a compound target is missing, ask only that narrower part rather than repeating the broad question. Meaning is reviewed by the interviewer, not inferred from IDs, keywords or graph traversal.

A bare action can justify one open reason probe: “What would make you say yes?” Adapt the wording to a refusal or negotiation. A supplied reason is not an invitation to search for concealed guilt. Clarify an ambiguous feeling without selecting a motive for the person. A specific helping need must exist before asking about responsibility; enjoyment of company is not such evidence.

A bodily-reaction follow-up requires that particular reaction. Occurrence across choices, duration within one choice, perceived usefulness and override conditions are separate. Knowing one does not close all others. No bodily response, unknown bodily response, and an unwanted topic are distinct; none justifies reaction-dependent probes. Generic decision clarity can still be explored independently.

An unclear question calls for one useful repair. Do not repeat equivalent probes after unresolved uncertainty. Retain any usable condition in an otherwise uncertain answer. A topic refusal closes that scope across all families and generic follow-ups; retain the actual source-answer topic, not just the catalog family; partner-anxiety questions must not reintroduce declined romance under an emotion heading. A friend version is possible only when explicitly elected and is recorded as a different context, not romantic evidence.

## Variants and interpretations

Name changed conditions. R07 and R08 reset from the same manageable-work baseline and ask energy at their respective endpoints. Their intensity, duration and rest differences are explicit bundles, not proof of a single causal factor. M04 and R10 can stipulate participation/intention in a new hypothetical version without rewriting a prior refusal.

A descriptive comparison requires both compatible reported branches, which may be supplied within one reply; do not demand two separate turns. Keep changed fields explicit: an earlier-life contrast deliberately changes time perspective rather than mixing time frames accidentally. One answer alone can support its own narrower condition. Hypothetical premises do not become biography, repeated events, measured frequencies or observed abilities. Habitual and retrospective reports remain those report types. Normative opinions do not replace personal responses. Physical affection does not establish sexual desire. Self-rated usefulness is not objective signal accuracy.

A faithful summary is not a newly discovered connection. A truly additional inference is clearly proposed for correction; no synthesis is required at every scene boundary. Retain meaningful conditional patterns without forcing a general trait or declaring ordinary behavior distinctive. Never infer full AstroHD recovery from behavioral coverage.

## End state

Move to another useful scene without exhausting the current family. If no useful admissible task remains, pause or offer a short qualified summary. Unknown, declined, inapplicable and unasked remain visible; none is a positive completion state. The participant may stop at any time and their usable information is retained.

The accompanying Python model checks explicitly annotated states. It cannot determine whether those annotations truthfully represent natural-language answers. Manual dialogue review and actual future use remain separate evidence. This protocol does not authorize app changes, paid inference, VM wake, recruitment or chart scoring.
