Graph review

The graph organizes 79 question entries in 28 families, with 73 information-planning labels. These are not required answer counts, a trait score, or proof of AstroHD recovery.

This is a read-only document. It makes no network requests, collects no responses, and is not the Railway application.

How the conversation works

On a narrow screen, scroll each diagram sideways. Every question also has a readable text panel below its topic graph.

The graph is an index over the protocol. It does not force a path through every scene. Pausing, corrections, privacy and already-answered information control what happens next.
