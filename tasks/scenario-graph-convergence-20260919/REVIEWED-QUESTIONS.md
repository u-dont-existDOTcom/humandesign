# Reviewed scenario bank — v6.0


Text-only design. 79 available entries; no required question count. One response task per turn. The pilot remains paused.


Imagine yourself as you usually are in situations like these. Tell me what you would probably do or feel, rather than the ideal answer. If something important changes your answer, say so. You can change a scene that does not fit, skip a question, or stop. A remembered example is welcome, not required.


The full admission and interpretation rules are in `INTERVIEW-PROTOCOL.md` and `interviewer-bank-v6.json`. Context arrows name possible referents, not a mandatory order.
