## Reveal provenance

The owner supplied materially important adverse life-course information after the blind numerology and date-only astrology freezes. Because the repository is public and the reveal concerns a third party, the sensitive particulars are intentionally excluded.

## Evaluation against frozen blind readings

Both frozen readings emphasized broad personality and life-course themes such as responsibility, expressiveness, autonomy, relationship intensity, and major transition periods.

The post-freeze reveal contains severe adverse life-course features that were not specifically predicted by either reading.

Disposition:
- MAJOR OMISSION / LIFE-COURSE SEVERITY MISS.
- Generic themes that could coexist with the reveal do not count as successful prediction of the omitted severe outcomes.
- Do not reinterpret previously noted symbolic factors after reveal as if they had forecast the undisclosed adverse outcome class.
- Timing signals remain timing signals only; they do not retrospectively identify the event type.

## Research consequence

This case is negative evidence against treating a coherent symbolic personality reading as an adequate account of a person's life.

For future tests:
1. predeclare adverse-outcome dimensions separately from ordinary personality dimensions when those domains are part of the research question;
2. require explicit predictions or abstentions rather than awarding credit for generic intensity or transition language;
3. preserve severe-outcome misses even when broad personality themes fit;
4. keep clinical and forensic use prohibited;
5. if more birth data are later supplied, analysis in a context exposed to this reveal is post-reveal development, not blind validation.

## Bottom line

The honest score is that the blind numerology and date-only astrology both missed the severity and nature of the subsequently reported adverse life course.
