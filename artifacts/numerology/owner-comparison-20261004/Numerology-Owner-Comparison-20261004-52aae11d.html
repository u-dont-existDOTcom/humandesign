Date: 2026-10-04. Classification: RETROSPECTIVE OWNER DEVELOPMENT. Source methods: unchanged V2.1 effective registry, including the immutable Cheiro V1/Decoz V1 baselines. Calculation family registered in commit 6f27bf9 before first new result inspection; the life outcomes were already known, so this is not a blind preregistration.

Findings

No system or combination has demonstrated better overall prediction of your life in this pass. Among the 36 limited, explicitly defined timing candidates, the best combinations do not reduce the three counted errors of the better single indicators. Requiring agreement loses hits; accepting either system adds false importance claims and unassessed years. An always-flag control has only two counted errors because the available labels disproportionately describe major events. These figures cannot rank complete author systems.

Javane/Bunker adds finer-grained relationship descriptions, including a reproducible 2018 correspondence: annual 41/5 plus an age-33 experience 41/5. Its original temporary 41 passage explicitly includes love entering life or existing ties deepening. However, related romantic language already appears in at least 15 of the 27 annual states examined. The two 41 calculations share your birthday, and the exact-compound selector was proposed after seeing the data. This is a recorded development lead, not demonstrated incremental predictive information.

Campbell's and Jordan's separate-name-component sequences change the chronology, but Jordan's result is branch-dependent. Campbell's birth-start tape and Jordan's birth-start branch give 24/6 at the March 2013 relationship onset and January 2014 birth. Jordan's alternative displayed-age branch gives 22/4 at both events, with 24/6 starting only after the January 2014 birthday. No branch is chosen by which one fits better.

Several filters distinguish moderate 2008 from major 2018. Crucially, the simpler already-registered combination Cheiro recurrence AND Campbell's 1/5/9 change-year indicator does that before adding the new compound or Essence conditions. Every 2018-only filter still misses the other seven major years if treated as a whole-life forecaster. There is no established best combination, and no evidence here that the more elaborate filters add value over the simpler one.

3. A source-specific correspondence: 2018's double 41/5

For Javane/Bunker, the personal year beginning on your 2018 birthday is:

1 + 29 + (2+0+1+8) = 41/5.

Your given-name Triangle begins with J,O,E,L,E,D,W,A,R. The line covering ages 27–36 uses L, whose unreduced alphabetical value is 12. Its two minor-experience ages are30 and 33. At the older age 33, the activated compound is:

L12 + birthday-side29 = 41/5.

Thus the 2018 birthday year and the Triangle's age 33 experience produce the same compound 41/5 through two different calculations. They are still mathematically related birth/name-derived features, not statistically independent confirmations. The source interpretation of temporary 41/5 explicitly concerns love, abundance, fertility, changed conditions, love entering life, existing bonds deepening, or the birth of children. It also warns that impulsiveness or selfishness can damage the relationship. This is more relationship-specific than simply labelling the year a 5 of change. Source: Javane/Bunker pp.38–42,63–66,204–205; native EPUB temporary 41 passage rechecked.

Unlike a bare recurrence, this source passage names a relationship domain. That creates a retrospective correspondence with the reported 2018 relationship, not a measured superiority result. Both the compound formula and its source meaning were in the frozen audit; the new decision to use exact concurrence as a filter was not.

The source calls this a minor-process experience, but expressly says minor experiences may be just as important as major ones; the distinction concerns more current versus longer-range effects (pp.56–57). Neither label supplies a subjective major-life-event threshold. The calculation assigns an experience to age 33, not an exact meeting day. This project's completed-age convention places that state between the 2018 and 2019 birthdays. The source's unquantified proximity language does not justify moving the window to fit an event.

The exact-compound concurrence selects only 2018 in the declared 2000–2026 scan. The rival root-level version selects 2008 and 2018: 2008's annual 40/4 matches an experience 31/4 at root level. Therefore the separation depends on our post-result decision to use exact compounds rather than roots, although the book itself gives compounds distinct meanings.

The dependency can be written explicitly:

1 + 29 + digit_sum(year) = L12 + 29

reduces to

1 + digit_sum(year) = 12.

The shared 29 cancels. Only three experience compounds in this 27-year window—34, 36 and 41—even lie within the observed Personal Year range of 32–42. A sparse match is therefore not automatically surprising. A counterfactual one-year shift of all experience ages removes every exact match in this window, but that shift is an unsourced robustness probe, not a new admitted Javane/Bunker age rule or permission to alter the frozen method.

There is also a broad-vocabulary rival. Just the six temporary rows 34, 37, 38, 39, 41 and 42 explicitly discuss love, romance or marriage; their annual occurrences cover 15 of 27 birthday years. Other layers can increase coverage. This lower bound is a post-result descriptive check, not a calibrated relationship classifier. Seven of the twelve recorded events concern romantic relationships, plus one childbirth, so a relationship-heavy source will find many opportunities for a thematic match.

The 2009 annual state is also 41/5, without a concurrent Triangle 41; its outcomes remain UNKNOWN. No chance probability or matched-chart advantage was calculated.

8. What remains unresolved

The full five-author predictive ranking is unresolved, not won by the system with the best anecdote. I searched the official Decoz resources and retrieved public descriptions and sample forecasts, but did not obtain a complete 81-pair interpretation bank. The relevant horoscope integrations in Campbell/Javane–Bunker also lack a unique author-specified aggregation formula. These limits do not stop the computations or the specific comparisons above; they stop a dishonest full-author leaderboard.

Likewise, a fair future test needs independently documented ordinary periods and outcomes, not only memorable events. A separate operational reading procedure must fix what counts as a forecast, what remains conditional, and how competing layers resolve, before new outcomes. The present findings identify exact candidates and exact counterexamples for that next stage. They do not establish that numerology, individually or combined, has predicted your life out of sample.
