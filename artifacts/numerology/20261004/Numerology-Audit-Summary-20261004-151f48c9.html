What was wrong in the earlier simplifications

There is no single interchangeable “Pythagorean” calculation and interpretation system. Shared labels can refer to different constructs, and the same alphabet can be combined or reduced differently. A generic phonetic Y rule is wrong for Jordan. A universal calendar-year boundary is wrong for Javane/Bunker. Treating every44 as the same kind of master number is wrong across these systems.

The birth-name/current-name distinction also does not reduce to “legal name is active” or “Minor means negligible.” The actual source and name-use context control. No one author's rule should be imported into another because it seems to explain a known event better.

The earlier raw53/90 name sums were not Cheiro combined compounds. His component-root procedure leaves the previously reported final roots unchanged, but the raw compound labels were inappropriate. The affected historical notes are annotated separately rather than changing V1 or rescoring old predictions.

The audit also withdrew its own false criticism of a Javane/Bunker Sagittarius diagram after checking the native image. Jordan's displayed10/1 in the relevant example lies within19→10→1; it is not an arithmetic mistake. Those corrections are mandatory in SOURCE_RECONCILIATION.md. The later independent check caught one more audit error: on Jordan's printed page262, the11/2 labelled Reality had been mistaken for the final Pinnacle. The actual Pinnacles are9,3,3,9, agreeing with page263 and the formula. V2.1 withdraws that false source-conflict annotation. An audit error is not evidence against an author.

Consequence for a future personal analysis

These books materially change how a proper comparison should be constructed: separate charts, separate name-role rules, complete relevant timing layers and explicit source ambiguities. Cheiro V2 expands both period-personality and relationship analysis; the other three books provide separate competing models. The paired V1/V2 comparison withholds the entire new WWYB delta, not merely its relationship labels.

They do not establish which model fits the owner best or justify revising the importance of an event. 2008 remains moderate, not top-major; 2018 remains major; 2029 remains prospective. No new owner-history scoring was done here.

Independent check and resulting repairs

A neutral Claude Opus5.5 session, requested at max effort through the existing Claude Code subscription, completed the independent check. Its verdict was FINDS_ERROR, not blanket approval. Alongside the Jordan correction, it identified unequal effective error costs, asymmetric event ascertainment, an abstention loophole, timing-grid alignment bias and inconsistent horoscope scope. These were repaired and checked with source examples and deterministic counterexamples.

The proposed primary score now uses equal FP/FN costs; only independently observation-complete windows enter the primary analysis, and all arms share the same cells. An abstaining arm does not receive a zero-error full-coverage score. Timing superiority must hold separately under both calendar and birthday/age anchors, not whichever looks best. Horoscope scope is symmetric for Campbell and Javane/Bunker.

The reviewer inspected derived specifications and bounded primary excerpts, not the entire original corpus. It did not independently certify full-source completeness, inherited Decoz resources, or every source page. No second independent approval of the producer's repairs is claimed.
