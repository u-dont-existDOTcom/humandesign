Life Patterns research submission privacy

This endpoint receives completed Life Patterns research records only after the participant has consented to research use.

What is sent

The submission contains the frozen primary behavioral interview record and the separate CF-003 secondary record. The study instructions prohibit birth data, chart data, predictions, rankings, and scores from being sent through this endpoint.

Purpose

The records are delivered to Joel's private research store so the participant does not need to manually send JSON files after completing the interview.

Storage and access

Records are encrypted before storage on the study service's persistent database. Researcher downloads require separate researcher authentication. The submission endpoint does not run model inference.

Processors

The request is transmitted from ChatGPT's GPT Action to the study service hosted on Railway. Those providers may process network and service metadata under their own terms and privacy policies.

Participant control

Participants may decline research consent or stop before submission. ChatGPT may also show an approval prompt before the external Action runs. Questions about a submitted record should be directed to Joel through the same channel that supplied the study invitation.

Effective: 2026-09-30.
