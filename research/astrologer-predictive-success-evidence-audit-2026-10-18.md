# Predictive astrologers: contemporaneous evidence and failure audit (2026-10-18)

Status: external-claims landscape / evidence triage only. **NOT** a prospective astrology validation, **NOT** an HD chart-matching result, and **NOT** a claim that astrological predictions work. No changes to production modeling, active task lock, frozen survey, natal scoring, or existing astrology source-audit lane.

## Research question and boundary
Owner asked whether any astrologers have *amazing predictive success*, prompted by https://www.reddit.com/r/LetsFuckWithAstrology/comments/1x91num/amazing_predictive_astrologers_recommendation/ (18 October 2026). Examine extraordinary claims while separating (1) anecdotes/retrospective testimony, (2) documented predictions dated prior to outcomes, (3) independently tracked prediction *sequences*, and (4) controlled prospective evidence. Never infer that success in financial timing or mundane astrology establishes natal/personal life-event accuracy. Preserve failures and base rates.

## Result
No astrologer located in this bounded discovery pass has shown repeatedly extraordinary future-event accuracy in a sufficiently independent, representative, controlled, prospective evaluation. Three substantive leads for further investigation:

- Arch Crawford: notable **independently tracked historical market timing**, but large documented misses and financial/technical-analysis confounding; strongest third-party longitudinal track found.
- André Barbault: particularly striking, contemporary-dated possible-pandemic **2020–2021** forecast composed in 2011 and reportedly published in 2012; compelling single item with explicit conditional language and alternatives, not a demonstrated long-run batting average.
- Alexey Borealis (plus other horary astrologers): an impressive **11-event pilot** with large reported timing advantage, but documented selection, outcome-attestation, and protocol limitations; a separate automated primary-directions study in 2026 found no statistically significant effect for its specified rule.

The names from the Reddit post are **Alexey Borealis** and **Dr. Katy Jane**, with another commenter mentioning **Laura Barat**. Neither positive personal testimony in the thread demonstrates a verified prediction that was fixed before the event. The respondent who named Borealis said explicitly they had **not used his services** and had only watched content. The Dr. Katy Jane commenter appreciated descriptions of career alternatives that were already under consideration. The Laura Barat commenter could not remember what had been predicted or whether it happened.

## Candidate A: Arch Crawford — objective performance signals AND clear misses

### External, dated performance reports
- Washington Post (31 Mar 1996), citing Hulbert Financial Digest: **only 1 of 60 market timers exceeded the stock market's total return over the preceding five years, and that one was Crawford**. Over the preceding eight years **none of 40** market timers exceeded the market. This is selection among multiple forecasters, not repeat proof of causation. https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/business/1996/03/31/long-term-investors-are-the-real-experts/750fb626-5f6f-40d8-90e2-9e5fa25070f5/
- Los Angeles Times (9 Feb 1997), citing Hulbert and another monitoring operation: sixth among ~100 advisors over eight years at market direction. Actual eight-year annualized absolute returns **Crawford 14.4%, Wilshire 5000 buy-and-hold 15.9%**; the report credited Crawford's lower volatility and superior *risk-adjusted* return. He used both astrology and technical factors and said he would not use astrology alone. Avoid conflating market direction, excess returns, and risk-adjusted returns. https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1997-02-09-fi-26952-story.html

### Independently pre-outcome documented failures
- Washington Post **10 Jan 1995** records Crawford's year-end prediction that **Dow Jones Industrial Average would close 1995 at 3,200, down over 30%**. Actual 1995 close **5,117.12, up 33.5%**. Primary pre-outcome source: https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/business/1995/01/11/theres-no-future-in-foretelling-the-economy/03907c7c-5f4a-4897-9286-d147b8227eea/ ; archival DJIA table in SEC filing: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1041130/000119312515053524/d840375d497.htm
- Washington Post **9 Mar 1997** prints his expectation of **35–50% overall market decline over the ensuing two years**. The long bull market continued, with Dow year-end 1997 7,908, 1998 9,181, 1999 11,497. Those year-end observations do not *by themselves* exhaustively rule out an intraperiod dip, so checking daily/minimum drawdown over the exact March 1997–March 1999 window is a bounded future verification task. Credibly flags major directional error, not an exact computed event-by-event accuracy score. Source: https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/business/1997/03/09/quoth-the-mavens/b853ed9b-765e-4a76-b88e-5b98081bf9b9/ ; archival SEC DJIA year closes: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1041130/000095012309063859/y02598e497.htm

Judgment: Strong candidate to audit an independently monitored *sequence*, but not established astrological information increment over technical analysis or market baseline; very significant misses.

## Candidate B: André Barbault — particular pandemic forecast

- Primary text **Aperçu sur les pandémies** in archived 7-page French PDF: https://www.fabrice-pascaud.fr/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Aperc%CC%A7u-sur-les-pande%CC%81mies.pdf . It is signed at its end **Paris, 8 June 2011**. On printed page 6 / PDF page index 5 the author writes that the world **could well be seriously threatened by a new pandemic around 2020–2021**. Inspecting the original page image confirms those exact words and timing, but the hosting copy itself dates to March 2020.
- Critically, in the same paragraph he allows that the analogous celestial configuration might instead produce *geophysical catastrophes* or disruptions of *international affairs*, which lowers its specificity as a falsifiable class forecast. He uses a possibility formulation, not absolute certainty.
- A colleague's article from March 15 2020 says the piece was **written 2011**; a March 20 2021 reply reports it had **already appeared in the magazine L'Astrologue in 2012**. This original printed issue has **not** been independently checked against a library/microfilm copy this pass. https://www.fabrice-pascaud.fr/articles/apercu-sur-les-pandemies
- Important distinction: even confirming this single pre-COVID prediction does not establish unusual **overall frequency** of success, because the full universe of his many predictions and denominator of misses has not been scored.

Judgment: strong lead for contemporaneous pre-event item, fair-language specificity lower than celebratory retellings, longitudinal success not established. Next: obtain authenticated 2012 print magazine and a predeclared full-corpus event forecast audit.

## Candidate C: Alexey Borealis — published horary timing pilot and null primary-directions test

- Study article (28 Jun 2024; *Correlation* 36(2)): https://alexeyborealis.com/blog/horary-astrology-study/ . Borealis plus Anna Pavlova, Mykola Kupriianov and Marina Ambrosimova reported **11 timing forecasts**, with **9 close/exact** and two explicit misses (7 days vs actual 4, 2 hours vs actual 6). Reported **Cohen d=3.5** against ten non-astrologer controls. Follow-up analysis on the same 11 paired event questions reports **paired p ~ 6.6×10^-6**; original independent test **p ~ 6.34×10^-8**. These are valid *conditional statistical computations* under the entered case/outcome dataset, not validation of source accuracy or selection representativeness.
- The author publishes methodological documentation and nine publicly shareable cases: https://github.com/aborealis/horary-astrology-study . His own provenance disclosure states **retrospective**, **no formal preregistration**; 62 forecasts were selected from pre-existing journal cases **that had client feedback** (those with no feedback were excluded); 13/62 contained specific timing; two of 13 were excluded as potentially self-fulfilling. Event *occurrence* predictions were not scored. Outcomes were based on informal private client messages; no verified original consultation/audio/transcript or independent confirmation. Astrologers' working records and chart dates cannot prove exact earlier forecast wording or outcome integrity independently. These limitations are not allegations of dishonesty; they mean there is no independent denominator or prospective proof for 9/11.
- Separate study: **A Statistical Test of Primary Directions in Predicting Violent Death: A Null Result**, 4 Oct 2026, *Correlation* 38(2), https://alexeyborealis.com/blog/primary-directions-study/ . An automated *specified Morinus-style fatal-directions* rule on 400 quality-dated charts yields **p=0.11**, not statistically significant; supplemental larger sample ~1,102 checks effects near null. The website says its blog edition is corrected after a code audit, while published journal edition is uncorrected. This does **not** refute his horary pilot or all forms of primary directions; it is a separate negative test of one operational rule. Do not pool the results.

Judgment: clear method documentation and willingness to publish negative findings are positives for research transparency. Claim of extraordinary individual predictive skill is **unverified**. Excellent candidate for a pre-registered replication with original encrypted time-stamped forecast and outcomes.

## Relevant general empirical boundary

- Clearer Thinking 2024-25 Western *natal matching* experiment designed with six consulting astrologers: 152 astrologers, 12 questions of matching a person's description to one of five charts; average at chance (2.4/12); **none scored above five**. No positive expert-experience correlation. Source https://www.clearerthinking.org/post/does-astrology-work . It directly tests whether natal charts distinguish people, **not horary event timing or financial macro timing**. Results are negative for the tested natal-matching claim, not a global falsification of every individual astrological technique.
- Avoid counting astrological press accounts, reviews, or self-selected hits as independent predictive skill. Record **all predictions**, with event definitions/outcome windows/time anchors before outcome, whether an event occurs, how one knows it occurred, all exclusions, risks of self-fulfilling behavior, baseline forecasts available using ordinary information, and full follow-up including unverifiable/never-feedback cases.
- Follow-up prospective test candidate: a small cohort of 30–100 consenting cases drawn sequentially from eligible client questions and non-astrological comparison forecasters; preregister eligibility, event domains, failure categories, confidence probabilities, source/time-birth quality; freeze exact unambiguous predictions before outcome; blind outcome assessors where possible; score event occurrence separately from conditional timing, error distributions and calibration; retain no-show and missed cases and privacy-safe evidence. Build within astrology **research** track, never alter the active chart-modeling task or use development people as untouched tests.
