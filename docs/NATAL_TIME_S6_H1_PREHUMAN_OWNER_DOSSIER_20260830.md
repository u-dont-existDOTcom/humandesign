# S6/H1 pre-human owner decision dossier — 2026-08-30

## Status and use boundary

Every decision in this dossier is **unselected**. The dossier supplies no recommendation, ranking,
default, preselection, implied approval, or authority to identify, contact, screen, recruit,
compensate, assign, enroll, or collect data from a human. It contains no screening question,
interview prompt, disclosure wording, consent text, or recruitment text.

The current S6/H1 child ends before human screening, content creation, construct-specific search,
measurement work, reliability evaluation, or AstroHD mapping. A future owner decision would still
need a fresh Pro contract and all applicable ethics, privacy, consent, custody, scientific, and
release gates.

## Decision dimensions

### OD-01 — sufficient chart blindness and evidence

Unselected dimensions include what “sufficiently chart-blind” means; whether evidence consists of
self-disclosure, independently checked access history, environmental isolation evidence, connected-
actor review, or a combination; which uncertainty states block eligibility; and who adjudicates a
disputed or incomplete record. No exposure threshold or exception is selected.

### OD-02 — prior and connected exposure treatment

Unselected exposure classes include Human Design, AstroHD, astrology, the repository, relevant
conversations, the owner, mapping materials, individualized chart feedback, connected people or
teams, shared sessions, sources, and connected evidence. The owner must later decide how recency,
depth, indirect exposure, uncertain memory, and incomplete provenance affect each class. The current
architecture only enforces that unknown provenance fails closed and AstroHD-exposed authorship is
ineligible.

### OD-03 — author count, independence, and adjudication

Unselected options include one or multiple independent authors; parallel or sequential authorship;
whether authors may see one another's work before freeze; the number and independence of
adjudicators; tie, disagreement, and recusal handling; and whether teams, employers, or close
collaborators count as connected. No person or organization is identified.

### OD-04 — population, intended use, and accessibility

Population, geography, language, age, literacy, cultural context, disability access,
communication mode, technology access, and intended use are unselected. No convenience population
or language is treated as a default, and no claim is made that one population can support another.

### OD-05 — recruitment and exclusion governance

Recruitment channel, recruiting organization, outreach scope, inclusion and exclusion authority,
conflict-based exclusions, replacement rules, and protections for employees, students, clients,
or economically vulnerable people are unselected. No recruitment message or candidate list exists.

### OD-06 — compensation, incentives, resources, and schedule

Whether compensation is offered; its basis, amount, currency, timing, proration, expense treatment,
and withdrawal consequences; nonfinancial incentives; budget; staffing; schedule; and total
timeline are unselected. Nothing in this dossier implies that any amount is fair, affordable, or
free of undue influence.

### OD-07 — burden and session design

Session number, duration, spacing, mode, location, preparation, breaks, accessibility support,
follow-up, withdrawal burden, and maximum cumulative burden are unselected. No authoring task,
prompt, item, or procedure is specified.

### OD-08 — screening, disclosure, conflict, and recusal governance

Unselected dimensions include the screening administrator; disclosure mechanism; corroboration;
record retention; access to records; correction and appeal; conflict adjudication; recusal;
exceptions; false, incomplete, or changed disclosure handling; and periodic reassessment. The
closed schema represents only metadata states and deliberately contains no human-facing wording.

### OD-09 — content custody and access

Physical or digital storage, jurisdiction, custodian, encryption, credentials, backups, retention,
destruction, supervised access, incident handling, transfer, and audit frequency are unselected.
The repository contract requires future pre-freeze content to remain outside AstroHD-exposed
surfaces and permits only opaque IDs, status, digests, and provenance receipts here. It does not
claim that a live custody system exists or is secure.

### OD-10 — non-content use of isolated models or tools

Whether any model, transcription tool, editor, search service, or other tool may be used for
non-content administrative work is unselected, as are provider, model, configuration, retention,
retrieval, logging, and review. H1 is not optional: no model or model session may originate or
revise construct content. A new session or an instruction to ignore exposure is not isolation
evidence.

### OD-11 — ethics, consent, confidentiality, and withdrawal

Ethics-review route, legal and institutional basis, consent process, comprehension support,
confidentiality, withdrawal rights, withdrawal of contributed material, incident notification,
record access and correction, complaints, and post-study communication are unselected. No
consent language is drafted and no determination is made that the future activity is exempt or
minimal risk.

### OD-12 — failure, retry, replacement, and termination

Unselected choices include what counts as a screening, isolation, custody, authorship, quality,
conflict, privacy, or protocol failure; whether and when a retry or replacement is permitted;
whether a failed attempt remains auditable; who can suspend or terminate; appeal and remediation;
and when the whole human program ends. No result-dependent repair rule is selected.

### OD-13 — progression to construct-specific search

The required ordering is fixed but the entry decision is not: human conception must first be
independently authored and content-hash frozen, and a separate search protocol must be frozen.
Unselected choices include who verifies those receipts, the search worker, search scope, sources,
queries, stopping rule, adverse-evidence handling, and amendment authority. No construct-specific
query or source has been chosen.

### OD-14 — progression to measurement and reliability

Unselected choices include the measurement-development route, reviewers, target population,
mode, sample, estimator, reliability concepts, thresholds, missing-data handling, adverse evidence,
replication, failure rules, and whether progression is allowed. Governance completeness supplies
none of this scientific evidence.

### OD-15 — progression to the separately frozen AstroHD mapping lane

The later need to test how chart-blind constructs map onto AstroHD is preserved, but every mapping
choice remains unselected: question, ontology, features, correspondence rules, hypotheses, data,
cohort roles, model, metric, threshold, multiplicity handling, ordinary alternatives, null controls,
replication, interpretation, and release. The mapping question must be preregistered only after the
construct and reliability package is frozen and must use independent evidence. A null, weak,
unstable, or nonreplicating result remains valid and cannot authorize in-place construct repair.
