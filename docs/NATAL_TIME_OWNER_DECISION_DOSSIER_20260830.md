## Option A — Stop and archive the deterministic foundation

### Scientific question

engineering result, its limitations, and its audit trail as a reproducible neutral or negative
result.

### Evidence required

- A final archive inventory binding the qualified artifacts, source commits, and exact replay
  commands.
- A compact claim ledger separating deterministic engineering qualification from every untested
  human claim.
- A clean reproducibility check and a maintenance-or-no-maintenance declaration.

No participant evidence, questionnaire, estimator, candidate output, or baseline execution is
required.

### Primary failure modes

- Archiving without enough source/runtime identity to replay the result.
- Describing structural synthetic qualification as evidence for HD or rectification.
- Quietly allowing later unqualified components to inherit the qualified label.
- Treating repository publication or deployment as implied by the archival choice.

### Minimum governance prerequisites

- Preserve immutable evidence and corrected-command history.
- Label every scientific nonclaim prominently.
- Keep private/live data out of the archive.
- Obtain separate owner authority for any push, merge, public release, or deployment.

### Reusable existing components

artifacts, privacy/history/build gates, and supervision records are directly reusable as the
archive.

limits are frozen. No human study follows. Any later restart would be a new owner decision and a
new Pro-supervised scope, not continuation by implication.

## Option B — Measurement reliability before rectification

### Scientific question

Can candidate self-report constructs be elicited repeatedly and coded consistently, with
acceptable missingness, response-process behavior, invariance, and burden, **without** accessing
birth-time references or testing chart-based candidate recovery?

This direction tests measurement feasibility. It does not test Human Design, birth-time
rectification, chart matching, or relationship compatibility.

### Evidence required before any later rectification step

- A blinded item-development protocol that keeps chart identity and documentary time outside the
  measurement workflow.
- Construct-specific test-retest evidence with participant clustering preserved.
- A predeclared inter-rater coding unit, rater population, agreement definition, and adjudication
  rule.
- Missingness, response-style, invariance, language, accessibility, and participant-burden review.
- Role-separated development and untouched evaluation, with every adaptive choice confined to
  development.
- Evidence that the measurement process does not itself reveal or encode chart-linked labels.

### Primary failure modes

- Writing items around desired chart interpretations and calling agreement validation.
- Optimizing one internal-consistency statistic while constructs remain unstable or redundant.
- Counting repeated answers, raters, or probes as independent participants.
- Using documentary time, chart identity, or candidate-specific feedback during development.
- Selecting a favorable subgroup, coding rule, or cutoff after seeing outcomes.
- Confusing reliable self-report with evidence of birth-linked predictive validity.

### Minimum governance prerequisites

- A new Pro-approved measurement protocol before item content is written.
- Participant-level and connected-component separation across roles.
- Independent custody for any later live records, consent/withdrawal rules, and private storage.
- Frozen coding, missingness, multiplicity, and post-selection rules before untouched evaluation.
- Separate owner choices for burden/cost, participant purpose, and any eventual transition to
  rectification.

### Reusable existing methods-contract components

- Measurement-reliability constraints.
- Participant/household/relationship/shared-source connected-component splitting.
- Nested adaptation and untouched validation rules.
- Selective/post-selection claim limits.
- Documentary-source separation, privacy gates, and the release-disabled aggregate threat model.

The deterministic chart engine, candidate intervals, `T_i`, and `S_i` are not used to decide
whether a measurement construct is retained in this phase.

### What falsifies or terminates this direction

The direction terminates before rectification if no construct meets the future predeclared
test-retest, inter-rater, missingness, invariance, burden, and blinding requirements. That result
would show that the proposed measurement route is not presently usable; it would not by itself
falsify every formulation of Human Design.

## Option C — Blinded falsification study design

### Scientific question

Can a future, fully frozen HD-based method add out-of-sample information about an independently
held documentary birth-time domain beyond the strongest admissible ordinary non-HD model,
complete/no-pruning output, and participant-level permutation or mismatched-chart controls?

The immediate step would design that test. It would not implement the estimator, questionnaire,
baselines, recruitment, or participant workflow.

### Evidence required before execution

- A falsifiable target and non-scalar outcome contract that keeps coverage, width, state count,
  date coverage, and abstention separate.
- A frozen method, complete candidate universe, no-pruning comparator, strongest admissible
  ordinary non-HD comparator, and participant/component-level permutation plan.
- Separately frozen development, calibration, and locked-validation roles, with an untouched
  connected-component split.
- A measurement specification that has already met its own reliability requirements.
- Independently enforceable reference custody: separate process identity, evaluator-only
  credentials, storage permissions, and auditable release after prediction freeze.
- Predeclared handling of ineligible, partially incompatible, and incompatible documentary
  reference domains.
- A feasibility, burden, privacy, multiplicity, leakage, and selective-inference review before any
  owner commitment to live execution.

### Primary failure modes

- Circular item, feature, model, threshold, subgroup, or calibrator selection.
- Allowing `T_i` or chart identity to affect procedure execution, stopping, or `S_i`.
- Comparing against weak or incompletely tuned ordinary baselines.
- Splitting partners, households, shared records, aliases, or repeated participants across roles.
- Calling raw scores, ranks, widths, support, or duration fractions probabilities.
- Treating an underpowered or custody-invalid study as evidence for or against HD.
- Reusing exposed validation participants after methodology changes.

### Minimum governance prerequisites

- Owner and Pro approval of the later target, method family, burden/resource envelope, and claims.
- A preregistered protocol and immutable freeze before reference access.
- Independent live-reference custody stronger than the current in-process synthetic boundary.
- Participant safety, consent, withdrawal/deletion, retention, and disclosure governance.
- No participant-facing result or public ledger without separate review and authority.

### Reusable existing methods-contract components

- Candidate-complete `C_i`, role-separated `T_i`, unordered set-valued `S_i`, and exact-domain
  compatibility `D_i` definitions.
- Operative metric-v3 non-scalar semantics.
- Connected-component splitting, nested adaptation, and post-selection limits.
- Complete/no-pruning, ordinary non-HD, participant-permutation, mismatched-chart, and random
  width/state-count control requirements.
- Fail-closed discrepancy, provenance, privacy, and disclosure-threat controls.

### What falsifies or terminates this direction

A valid future locked study would terminate the tested method if, under its predeclared decision
rule, it failed to add untouched value over the complete/no-pruning and strongest ordinary
controls. Failure of blinding, custody, split integrity, eligibility, or preregistration would
invalidate the study rather than falsify the hypothesis. A favorable development result would not
substitute for locked validation.
