## Superseding rule

Prior Human Design or astrology exposure is not an automatic exclusion from H1 chart-blind
construct authorship. Having generated, read, or discussed one's chart is explicitly permitted.
Neither belief nor skepticism is a sufficient eligibility proxy.

The relevant contamination target is whether a prospective author:

- substantially organizes or describes their identity through HD or astrological categories;
- treats HD or astrology as a comprehensive or authoritative account of themselves in a way that
  would foreseeably reproduce the framework; or
- has enough semantic/technical knowledge to reproduce the HD ontology, even while rejecting it.

## Required three-status model

1. `ELIGIBLE`: prior exposure is incidental or shallow, HD/astrology is not identity-defining,
   and there is no substantial semantic knowledge that could reproduce the ontology.
2. `REQUIRES_BLIND_ADJUDICATION`: substantial semantic or technical knowledge is present,
   regardless of belief or rejection. This status is not clean eligibility and is not automatic
   exclusion.
3. `INELIGIBLE_CLEAN_H1_AUTHOR`: HD/astrology is a comprehensive or identity-defining framework
   through which the person substantially conceptualizes themselves, or the person would
   intentionally derive constructs from that framework.

People with mismatch, skepticism, partial-usefulness, or curiosity reports are not excluded for
those views. Reports such as “works completely” or “90% accurate” are not literal screening
criteria; the underlying self-concept/semantic-conditioning risk controls.

## Role boundary

People in `REQUIRES_BLIND_ADJUDICATION` or `INELIGIBLE_CLEAN_H1_AUTHOR` may later be useful as
adversarial reviewers or comparison sources only after the independent constructs are frozen and
a future contract authorizes that role. They may not shape the clean pre-freeze construct package.

## Still unresolved and unauthorized

This ruling resolves the principle and three statuses, but does not authorize or choose:

- human identification, contact, screening, recruitment, assignment, or data collection;
- human-facing questions, evidence thresholds, adjudicators, tie/appeal rules, recusal, or record
  retention;
- a population, language, geography, compensation, burden, schedule, custody system, or ethics
  route;
- construct content, construct-specific search, measurement, reliability, or AstroHD mapping;
- production changes, external accounts, Railway/GitHub mutation, push, merge, migration,
  deployment, publication, or release.

The product rationale that a person who finds HD comprehensive has no reason to use AstroHD is
explicitly discarded. The only controlling reason for clean-H1 ineligibility here is the elevated
risk of theory-conditioned construct generation.
