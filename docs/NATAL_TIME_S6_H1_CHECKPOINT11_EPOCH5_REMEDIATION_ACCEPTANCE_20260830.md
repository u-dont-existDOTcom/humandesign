## Active human prior-exposure policy

The active policy is
`state/NATAL-TIME-S6-H1-PRIOR-EXPOSURE-POLICY-V1.json`. It defines exactly three substantive
outcomes:

- `ELIGIBLE`;
- `REQUIRES_BLIND_ADJUDICATION`; and
- `INELIGIBLE_CLEAN_H1_AUTHOR`.

These are separate from process states:

- `NOT_ASSESSED`;
- `EVIDENCE_INCOMPLETE`;
- `EVIDENCE_CONFLICT`;
- `DECISION_PENDING`; and
- `DECISION_RECORDED`.

Unknown, incomplete, or conflicting evidence produces no substantive outcome and blocks clean-role

ontology-reproducing familiarity—regardless of belief or rejection—is consistent only with

comprehensive self-concept integration, or intentional HD/astrology derivation, is consistent only
with `INELIGIBLE_CLEAN_H1_AUTHOR`.

Belief, skepticism, reported accuracy, reported mismatch, curiosity, perceived usefulness,
product interest, and absence of a known exposure record are structurally barred as automatic
decision bases.
