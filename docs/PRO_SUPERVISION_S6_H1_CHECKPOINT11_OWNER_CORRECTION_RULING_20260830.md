## Controlling owner policy

- Prior HD or astrology exposure is permitted and is not, by itself, an exclusion.
- Belief, skepticism, reported mismatch, curiosity, perceived usefulness, or a claimed accuracy
  percentage is not the eligibility rule.
- The relevant concern is semantic or self-concept contamination.
- Substantial technical or semantic familiarity requires blind adjudication rather than automatic
  clean eligibility or automatic exclusion.
- Identity-defining or intentionally HD-derived self-concept is ineligible for the clean H1 author
  role.
- The product rationale that people who find HD accurate would have no use for AstroHD is excluded
  from the scientific eligibility policy.

## Three substantive outcomes

### `ELIGIBLE`

This outcome is permitted, but must not be inferred merely from absence of known exposure. It
requires a complete future evidence record supporting all of the following:

- prior exposure was incidental or shallow;
- HD or astrology is not an identity-defining framework for the person;
- no substantial semantic or technical familiarity capable of reproducing the ontology has been
  established; and
- no other contamination or incompatible-role condition applies.

Prior chart generation, reading, or discussion is compatible with this status.

### `REQUIRES_BLIND_ADJUDICATION`

This applies when substantial semantic or technical familiarity is established, regardless of
whether the person believes HD, rejects HD, reports mismatch, reports partial usefulness, or thinks
the system is highly accurate. Technical familiarity alone produces neither automatic clean
eligibility nor automatic exclusion.

This status is unresolved. It does not permit clean H1 authorship unless a later separately
authorized blind-adjudication process resolves it. This remediation may define metadata for the
state but may not define the adjudicator, evidence standard, questions, threshold, or decision
procedure.

### `INELIGIBLE_CLEAN_H1_AUTHOR`

This applies when either:

- HD or astrology substantially organizes the person's self-concept as a comprehensive or
  authoritative identity framework; or
- the person would intentionally derive the supposedly independent constructs from that framework.

This is ineligibility for the clean pre-freeze H1 construct-author role, not universal exclusion
from every later research role. A person in this state may later be considered for a separately
authorized post-freeze adversarial-review or comparison role, but cannot shape the clean construct
package before freeze.

## Separate process states

The state machine must separately support:

- `NOT_ASSESSED`;
- `EVIDENCE_INCOMPLETE`;
- `EVIDENCE_CONFLICT`;
- `DECISION_PENDING`; and
- `DECISION_RECORDED`.

Unknown, incomplete, or conflicting evidence must produce no substantive eligibility outcome. It
must not silently become either `ELIGIBLE` or `INELIGIBLE_CLEAN_H1_AUTHOR`.
