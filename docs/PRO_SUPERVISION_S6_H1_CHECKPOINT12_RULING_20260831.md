### Decision 1 — clean H1 author configuration

`A1 — SINGLE_AUTHOR_FAMILY`

One chart-blind human independently develops the initial conception.

- Benefits: lowest recruitment/custody/coordination/compensation burden; simplest isolation and
  provenance; no prefreeze interpersonal convergence.
- Costs: greatest dependence on one person's vocabulary, blind spots, culture, and conceptual
  boundaries; no independent conception comparison; one failure or contamination can invalidate
  the complete clean conception stage.

`A2 — INDEPENDENT_PAIR_FAMILY`

Two chart-blind humans develop separate conceptions without seeing or discussing each other's work
before both are frozen.

- Benefits: internal independence check; preserves genuine convergence and disagreement; reduces
  dependence on one author while remaining manageable; supports later governed synthesis after
  both independent freezes.
- Costs: more custody, recruitment, adjudication, and compensation than A1; divergent conceptions
  require explicit treatment; two people still provide limited cultural/experiential coverage.

`A3 — INDEPENDENT_PANEL_FAMILY`

Several chart-blind humans independently develop frozen conceptions before any permitted
synthesis.

- Benefits: greatest diversity; better separation of recurring phenomena from idiosyncratic
  wording; greater robustness to one contaminated, weak, or atypical contributor.
- Costs: highest recruitment, privacy, compensation, custody, and coordination burden; more role
  conflicts and deviations; substantially harder synthesis; exact panel size remains a later owner
  decision.

### Decision 2 — blind-adjudication configuration

`J1 — SHARED_BLIND_ADJUDICATION`

One common blinded adjudication unit applies the same future frozen evidence standard across all
prospective authors.

- Benefits: greater procedural consistency; lower staffing/training burden; simpler audit of
  consistent application.
- Costs: one interpretive bias or error can affect every author; correlated eligibility errors;
  concentrated access/custody point.

`J2 — SEPARATED_BLIND_ADJUDICATION`

Eligibility judgments are independently partitioned or independently rendered before any
permitted reconciliation.

- Benefits: reduces correlated error and single-point bias; better preserves author independence;
  retains disagreement as evidence.
- Costs: higher staffing, training, privacy, and coordination burden; greater risk of inconsistent
  application; requires a later separately authorized disagreement/reconciliation procedure.

### Pro recommendation

`A2 + J2`

Pro described this as the strongest balance between scientific independence and operational
feasibility: two independently frozen human conceptions provide meaningful convergence and
disagreement evidence without a full panel's complexity, while separated adjudication reduces the
risk that one adjudicator's P1 interpretation determines both author statuses.
