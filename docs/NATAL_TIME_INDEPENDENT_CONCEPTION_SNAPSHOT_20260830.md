# Natal-time inference independent conception snapshot — 2026-08-30

This snapshot predates the authorized literature/methods scan. It preserves the project's independent problem conception without selecting inferential semantics.

## Problem being solved

Given an explicitly confirmed civil date or unordered date set, a resolved birthplace timezone, and incomplete birth-time knowledge, enumerate every chart-distinct candidate interval supported by the pinned engine. A later, separately frozen layer may investigate whether independent natal evidence can reduce or describe that candidate set without manufacturing precision.

Engineering completeness, human descriptive specificity, probabilistic calibration, and participant-facing usefulness are separate claims. The deterministic candidate generator must be correct even if every later inferential hypothesis fails.

## Deterministic generation versus later inference

The deterministic layer owns evidence provenance, candidate dates, timezone resolution, actual engine state identity, exhaustive transitions, immutable manifests/freezes/results, and set-theoretic stable/variable facts. It has no rank, prior, probability, score mass, stopping rule, or recommendation.

Any later inference layer must consume a frozen candidate universe rather than regenerate or prune it opportunistically. Its inputs, mappings, calibration cohort, validation cohort, abstention behavior, and stopping rule require their own versioned freeze and supervision gate.

## Intended evidence classes

- Documentary birth-date and birth-time records, with participant-reported versus independently verified status kept distinct.
- Memory-sourced date/time facts, including an independently remembered weekday captured before calendar feedback.
- Explicit unordered candidate-date sets after a disclosed conflict.
- Natal behavioral or phenomenological responses collected without relationship evidence and without showing live candidate rankings.
- Evidence-quality and response-reliability measures that can reduce trust in an answer but cannot rescue unrelated contradictions.
- Future outcome or follow-up evidence only under a separately frozen protocol that prevents reuse of the same observation for fitting and validation.

## Leakage and validation constraints

Relationship responses, identifiers, compatibility results, and debriefs do not enter default natal candidate generation or inference. If a relationship is ever used exploratorily to refine natal time, that relationship is ineligible as validation evidence for the inferred result.

Answer keys, exact documented times, calendar-derived weekday facts, and candidate-specific interpretations remain sealed until the applicable freeze. Development humans may inform later model design but cannot be relabeled untouched validation. Evaluation splits occur at participant level, and all adaptive choices must be nested inside training/development folds.

Private exact birth data, narratives, credentials, linkage identifiers, and deterministic personal-data hashes remain out of public Git and public artifacts. Public reporting is limited to predeclared schemas, non-reidentifying aggregates, synthetic examples, and independently reviewed outcome ledgers.

## Candidate later approaches, not selections

Potential ranking families to compare later include theory-derived symbolic models, empirical response-likelihood models, and shrinkage/hybrid models. Potential uncertainty treatments include explicit priors with sensitivity analysis, prior-free or likelihood-ratio summaries, set-valued prediction, calibrated abstention, and non-probabilistic partial-order or compatibility summaries.

Potential stopping families include fixed questionnaire budgets, predeclared information-gain thresholds, stability criteria, calibrated error/coverage targets, and always-allow-abstention rules. No family, weight, prior, formula, threshold, questionnaire item, or participant label is selected by this snapshot.

## Open scientific questions

- Which natal response constructs can people report reliably enough to carry out-of-sample information?
- Does any signal exceed date/time priors, calendar/season effects, demographic baselines, and plausible mismatched charts?
- How should uncertain documentary quality and memory reliability affect inference without becoming post-hoc rescue variables?
- Which score families, if any, admit calibration on a feasible independent human cohort?
- Can useful coverage be achieved with principled abstention rather than forced precision?
- What candidate-set resolution is supported after accounting for engine precision and response uncertainty?
- How should adaptive question selection be validated without double dipping?
- What minimum sample sizes and nested validation structure are required for symbolic, empirical, and hybrid comparisons?
- How can public outcome ledgers remain auditable while protecting exact birth data and narratives?
- Which established baselines should bespoke rectification components be required to beat?
