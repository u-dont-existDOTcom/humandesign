### 2. Semantic control gap: manufactured prerequisite

The owner-facing outcome could remain intact while an assistant-created method became a mandatory dependency:

`same frozen theory-blind substrate for every target model`

was silently hardened into:

`therefore a comprehensive externally coded behavioral ontology/coder is required`.

Ordinary owner-outcome alignment can miss this because the final requested product is still promised. Outcome-progress controls can catch the strategy later when evidence becomes flat/regressive, but they are not the earliest gate at the moment of method commitment.

The prior-work scan also lacked an explicit necessity test. Once the problem was framed as ontology construction, high-quality literature about ontology engineering, psychometrics, codebooks, annotation, and inter-rater reliability could improve the chosen paradigm without establishing that the paradigm itself was required.

## Universal pilot

A separate proposal branch in `u-dont-existDOTcom/universal-dev-architecture` now carries a **non-activated** pilot:

- branch: `proposal/outcome-first-supervision-20260912`
- draft PR: #96, `Pilot: catch manufactured prerequisites before architecture hardening`
- candidate head after current-main sync: `15a7c0c9de0c98dd72764b4110cb9babfe70b40a`

The proposed minimal addition is a `Method necessity / manufactured-prerequisite check` inside the existing `templates/PRIOR-WORK-SCAN.md`, not a parallel supervision framework.

At consequential method commitments it separates:

- owner outcome;
- genuine constraint + source;
- proposed method + origin;
- what fails if the method is removed/replaced;
- strongest materially simpler alternative;
- evidence that rules the alternative out;
- ordinary-case specimen + difficult counterexample;
- necessity state: `ESTABLISHED | UNRESOLVED | NOT_NECESSARY`.

An `UNRESOLVED` assistant-inferred method may be tested reversibly but should not be hardened into architecture first.

## Four development fixtures

The pilot fixture suite deliberately includes cases where structure is correct rather than always preferring simplification:

1. Life Patterns known incident -> `UNRESOLVED`, bounded experiment only.
2. Owner explicitly requests standardized taxonomy/inter-rater coder -> `ESTABLISHED`.
3. Routine reversible internal refactor -> gate not triggered.
4. Verified hard external schema requirement -> `ESTABLISHED`.

These expected results are development labels, not an independent model benchmark.
