# B1 neutral owner dossier — future source route and authorship class

Date: 2026-08-30

## Decision boundary

This dossier does not recommend, rank, default, or select a route. It prepares two linked owner
decisions that remain blocked until ChatGPT Pro reviews checkpoint 10:

1. which chart-blind source-route class may be used; and
2. whether future chart-blind content may be authored by humans, a fresh isolated model context,
   or both.

Whichever combination is later authorized must freeze a construct-specific conception before its
first search, complete the specific existing-work scan before bespoke content, and remain isolated
from AstroHD information through the construct and reliability freeze.

## Source-route classes

| Route class | Potential contribution | Material limitation or control need |
|---|---|---|
| Established non-HD construct or instrument | Maximizes reuse and may bring prior measurement evidence, permissions, and known limitations. | Fit to the independently frozen boundary cannot be assumed; versions, licensing, populations, and adverse evidence require review. |
| Chart-blind concept elicitation | Can surface candidate phenomena without starting from an existing label. | Requires human-procedure authority, careful sampling and content-validity governance, and a later existing-work/redundancy check. |
| Chart-blind behavioral or observational taxonomy | Emphasizes observable or codable phenomena and may reduce dependence on self-labeling. | May omit internal experience; observation, coder access, privacy, and reliability procedures remain unresolved. |
| Chart-blind phenomenological taxonomy | Preserves first-person experiential distinctions without AstroHD labels. | Reporting, language, interpretive coding, and response artifacts can complicate boundaries and reliability. |
| Independent non-HD theory derivation | Can make definitions and falsifiable implications explicit before measurement work. | The chosen theory may not reflect lived or observable phenomena; theory and operationalization require separate scrutiny. |
| Staged independent hybrid | Can combine complementary sources and compare convergence before freeze. | Adds custody, sequencing, proliferation, and selective-retention risks; each input must remain independently provenance-bound. |

`UNSELECTED` remains the only active status. The generic methods scan cannot establish that an
instrument does or does not exist for a future construct.

## Authorship classes

| Authorship class | Potential contribution | Material limitation or control need |
|---|---|---|
| Humans | Avoids direct reliance on a model session and permits domain or lived-experience judgment. | Humans can still be chart-exposed or indirectly cued; recruitment, access, confidentiality, cost, and contamination controls would need owner authority. |
| Fresh isolated model context | Can produce a reproducible session receipt and operate behind an explicit retrieval/repository firewall. | Fresh context does not prove training- or memory-level blindness; no AstroHD-aware model/session can be reused, and the owner must accept the residual uncertainty. |
| Both, with separated roles | Can compare independently generated candidate sets or use one role to audit another. | Increases coordination and connected-leakage risks; actor/session/source separation and predeclared reconciliation rules become essential. |

## Invariants under every future choice

- The current AstroHD-aware Pro conversation and repository worker are ineligible to author,
  edit, select, rank, refine, or approve chart-blind content.
- Source selection because it appears likely to map is contamination.
- The construct-specific scan occurs after an independent conception freeze and before bespoke
  content.
- Construct/reliability content is sealed before a separately hashed mapping hypothesis exists.
- Mapping failure cannot rewrite the original construct; any post-exposure change creates a new
  exploratory version and preserves the original result.
- Reliability, mapping, and incremental value remain separate claim lanes.

No population, language, mode, burden, staffing, instrument, reliability design, mapping target,
feedback policy, or operational workflow is selected by this dossier.
