# 30 — AstroRRF Novelty / Habituation Extension V0.4

Status: **retrospective development extension frozen before Pair 5 birth/chart features are inspected**. Pair 5 outcomes informed the target decomposition, but not the astrological feature selection from that person's chart.

## Why V0.4 exists

Pair 5 introduces a sexual phenotype not represented by baseline libido or initial attraction:

- sex can be good or highly felt initially;
- partner familiarity can reduce arousal/desire over time;
- novelty/new partners can restore or amplify sexual response;
- the relationship can remain loving and low-conflict while the sexual system becomes unsustainable.

This must not be misclassified as simply `low libido` or `bad initial chemistry`.

## Relationship-science target

Preserve separately:

- `baseline_libido`;
- `initial_partner_desire`;
- `partner_specific_desire_stability`;
- `sexual_boredom_proneness`;
- `partner_novelty_response`;
- `habituation_rate_with_familiar_partner`;
- `attractive_other_desire`;
- `dyadic_desire_discrepancy_over_time`;
- `sexual_chemistry_initial`;
- `sexual_chemistry_longitudinal`.

External empirical rationale:

- Morton & Gorzalka (2015), review: sexual desire/arousal can decrease with partner familiarity and increase with partner novelty in both sexes, though evidence suggested a larger average effect in men. DOI `10.1080/0092623X.2014.958788`.
- de Oliveira et al. (2021), systematic review: sexual boredom is associated with sexual response, satisfaction, and varied sexual behaviors; mechanisms remain incompletely established. DOI `10.1016/j.jsxm.2020.12.019`.
- de Oliveira et al. (2023): sexual boredom and partner-/other-related desire form distinct profiles; among women, one profile combined high boredom and attractive-other desire with low partner-related desire. DOI `10.1093/jsxmed/qdac018`.

Thus novelty dependence is a legitimate behavioral target independent of generic libido.

## Frozen astrology candidate family

These are **conventional astrology hypotheses only**, not scientifically validated predictors.

### Primary novelty/freedom candidates

1. `natal_Uranus_house_5` when exact birth time is known.
   - Conventional astrology sources repeatedly associate Uranus in the 5th with unconventional romance, excitement, unpredictability, freedom, and attraction that may lose appeal under predictability/routine.
2. `natal_Uranus_major_aspect_Venus_or_Mars`.
   - Candidate for novelty/freedom/excitement in attraction and sexuality.
3. `synastry_Mars_Uranus` and `partner_Uranus_in_actor_house_5`.
   - Candidate for relationship-specific excitement/novelty, **not** for person-level habituation by itself.

### Secondary candidates to record but not weight in V0.4

- strong Uranus angularity;
- repeated Uranus activation of 5th-house ruler;
- Venus/Uranus and Mars/Uranus aspect type/orb;
- fixed versus mutable sign emphasis only as an exploratory covariate, not a scored rule;
- HD novelty/change mechanics only as a separate ablation layer if pre-existing source claims are documented.

## Explicit non-rules

- Do not infer high novelty dependence merely from Aquarius placements or generic Uranus prominence.
- Do not infer promiscuity, infidelity, or inability to commit from Uranus features.
- Do not infer low baseline libido from high novelty dependence.
- Do not infer novelty dependence from one relationship ending.
- Do not retrofit a new astrological feature after Pair 5 chart inspection and count it as a V0.4 success.
