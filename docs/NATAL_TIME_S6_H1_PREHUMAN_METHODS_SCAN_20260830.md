## Evidence and bounded dispositions

### Roles, delegation, qualification, and training — reuse directly

ICH E6(R3) provides a current official baseline for explicit responsibility, proportionate
delegation records, oversight, and training matched to assigned duties. NIST AI RMF 1.0 similarly
requires documented roles, lines of communication, training, human-AI role differentiation, and
independent assessment. S6/H1 reuses those governance constraints without assigning a real person
or claiming that any future staff member is qualified.

### Masking, separation, least privilege, and auditability — adapt and compose

ICH E6(R3), FDA electronic-record guidance, and NIST SP 800-53 support duty-based access,
separation of duties, least privilege, protected audit trails, role/permission histories, and
documentation of premature unblinding. The project adapts those controls to an information
firewall: AstroHD exposure, repository access, conversation access, connected actors, tools, and
unknown provenance are contamination-relevant states. Generic controls support the architecture;
they do not prove chart blindness.

### Immutable conception, deviations, and outcome-independent revision — reuse and adapt

OSF registration guidance provides time-stamped, read-only plans and explicit planned/unplanned
work. SPIRIT/CONSORT 2025 requires versioned amendment histories, responsibility for changes,
independent review of substantive amendments, and disclosure of the nature, timing, and reason for
deviations. ICMJE explains why prospective registration guards against selective publication and
outcome reporting. The project reuses freeze-before-search ordering and adapts amendments into
append-only supersession: later evidence cannot silently mutate a frozen conception.

### Conflict disclosure and independent adjudication — adapt and compose

OHRP conflict guidance and ORI procedures distinguish roles, require conflict consideration, and
support independent review by people without unresolved conflicts. Financial-conflict guidance is
only a baseline: S6/H1 additionally treats prior HD, astrology, AstroHD, mapping, owner,
conversation, repository, individualized-feedback, session, source, and connected-evidence
exposures as possible content-development conflicts. No threshold or exception is chosen.

### Privacy, custody, and access minimization — adapt

NIH privacy guidance calls for prospective privacy assessment, controlled access even for
de-identified data, and explicit oversight, responsibility, and restrictions. NIST Privacy
Framework 1.0 treats data collection, retention, logging, use, sharing, and disposal as a lifecycle
risk. ORI custody procedures support inventory, secure sequestration, and supervised access. The
current child stores no human data or live content; it adapts these sources only into closed,
synthetic metadata and future custody requirements.

### Recruitment, consent, compensation, and withdrawal — baseline only; owner-gated

OHRP guidance supports voluntariness, understandable consent, protection from coercion and undue
influence, fair and protocol-specific remuneration, prorated payment where appropriate, and clear
withdrawal consequences. Those principles define questions a future authorized protocol must
resolve. They do not select recruitment, payment, burden, eligibility, language, consent text, or
any human action here.

### AI/tool provenance — adapt; content authorship remains human-only

W3C PROV supplies entity/activity/agent, attribution, association, and delegation concepts. NIST
AI RMF and its generative-AI profile support documented actors, tools, test methods, human
oversight, provenance, and limitations. S6/H1 permits future non-content tool roles only if later
authorized and provenance-bound. H1 remains absolute: no model or model session may originate or
revise construct content, and a fresh session alone does not establish chart blindness.

### Null-result preservation and the later mapping firewall — compose

Prospective registration, transparent deviations, provenance, and results-reporting standards
support keeping failures visible and distinguishing preplanned from post-outcome changes. The
AstroHD-specific rule is a transparent composition: the independent conception must be frozen
before construct-specific search, later mapping must be separately frozen, and a null, weak,
unstable, or nonreplicating mapping cannot authorize in-place construct repair.
