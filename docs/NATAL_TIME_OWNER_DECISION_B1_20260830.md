# Owner decision — B1 independent-first constructs

Date: 2026-08-30

## Decision

Joel selected **B1 — independent-first constructs** after ChatGPT Pro checkpoint 9.

Joel's clarification is integral to the decision: the program must eventually test how the
chart-blind constructs map onto AstroHD. B1 changes the order and evidentiary separation; it does
not remove the mapping question.

## Intended scientific sequence

1. Establish construct-source eligibility and exclusion rules without using charts, birth-time
   candidates, HD classifications, or AstroHD labels.
2. Develop and freeze the construct definitions, measurement model, measurement protocol,
   reliability evidence, and analysis boundaries through an independent chart-blind process.
3. Only after that freeze, predeclare a separate AstroHD mapping hypothesis and evaluation
   protocol.
4. Test the mapping with blinded roles and evidence partitions that prevent HD information from
   altering the frozen construct definitions or reliability analysis.
5. Treat a null, weak, unstable, or nonreplicating mapping as an admissible result. Do not rename,
   merge, split, discard, or redefine constructs post hoc to manufacture correspondence.

The future mapping phase must distinguish at least three logically separate questions:

- whether the chart-blind constructs can be elicited and coded reliably;
- whether a frozen AstroHD mapping predicts or explains those independently defined constructs;
- whether any successful mapping adds out-of-sample value beyond ordinary non-HD explanations and
  appropriate null controls.

Reliability is a prerequisite only. It is not evidence that the later AstroHD mapping is correct.

## Current authorization boundary

This decision selects direction and authorizes returning to the same Pro supervision conversation
for one new bounded contract. It does **not** authorize:

- naming, describing, prioritizing, or writing candidate constructs;
- items, prompts, probes, response options, coding categories, exemplars, manuals, or scoring keys;
- a reflective/formative model, coefficient, estimator, cutoff, retest interval, occasion count,
  coder count, sample size, population, country, language, literacy level, mode, accessibility
  policy, or intended use;
- a mapping ontology, correspondence rule, chart feature selection, mapping model, mapping metric,
  success threshold, or mapping execution;
- charts, candidate times, documentary references, relationship evidence, live/human data,
  participant software, recruitment, data collection, or feedback;
- any claim of empirical reliability, validity, AstroHD correctness, birth-time recoverability,
  calibration, accuracy, participant benefit, or progression to rectification; or
- push, PR action, merge, migration, Railway mutation, deployment, publication, public release, or
  public disclosure.

Any later mapping contract must preserve the independently frozen construct lineage, prevent
chart-language leakage, use predeclared falsification rules, and make failure scientifically and
operationally consequential.
