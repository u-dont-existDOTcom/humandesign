# 14 — Responder Heterogeneity

## Hypothesis

The birth-chart/behavior association may be heterogeneous rather than universal.

A scientifically meaningful result could be that only a subset of people show strong prospective chart specificity, provided that subset structure generalizes out of sample.

## Prohibited analysis

Do not define responders as "people whose real date ranked highly" and then claim those same people validate the theory.

That is tautological.

## Allowed development analyses

On development humans, fit mixture/hierarchical models that estimate latent response strength and test whether it is predicted by independently measured variables.

Candidate moderators include:

- self-report consistency;
- body-signal accessibility;
- dissociation;
- hypervigilance;
- cognitive override;
- HD familiarity;
- birth-record precision.

## Validation

Freeze either:

- a subgroup classifier;
- a continuous predicted response-strength score;
- or a mixture model fitted only on development participants.

Then test whether the predicted high-signal group actually shows stronger concealed-date recovery on untouched participants.

## Outcomes

Report:

- overall performance;
- predeclared/frozen subgroup performance;
- interaction tests;
- calibration of predicted response strength;
- whether chart-description recovery and Authority usability separate.
