## Owner decision now required

Checkpoint 9 exhausted the construct-neutral work that can proceed without choosing a scientific
object. The next substantive step necessarily determines the source of the constructs whose
reliability would eventually be studied.

### B1 — Independent-first constructs

Constructs would first be defined in ordinary behavioral, experiential, or phenomenological
language through a process blinded to charts, birth-time candidates, and HD classifications. Any
later HD mapping would occur only after constructs, measurement protocol, and analysis boundaries
were independently frozen.

Benefits: strongest protection against circularity/chart-language leakage; interpretable
reliability without presupposing HD; lower familiarity, demand, and semantic-agreement risk; and a
cleaner later falsification foundation.

Costs: constructs may not map cleanly to HD; development and later mapping require more stages; a
negative mapping could terminate rectification even when the independent measures are reliable.

### B2 — HD-derived constructs

Constructs would be derived directly from HD theory or chart-mechanic descriptions while the
measurement study remained separated from actual charts and candidate times.

Benefits: direct relevance to the rectification hypothesis, less ambiguity about intended chart
distinctions, and a potentially shorter path to an HD-specific falsification design.

Costs: substantially greater circularity and cueing risk; stable responses may reflect HD
familiarity, self-identification, expectancy, or personal validation; and it is harder to separate
measurement of a human attribute from agreement with the system's own language. A positive result
would require stronger negative controls and be weaker evidence than an independent-first result.

**Pro recommendation: B1 — Independent-first constructs.** It is the scientifically stronger
continuation because it preserves separation between the measurement target and the theory later
being tested.

Pro's recommended later sequence is to define construct-source eligibility/exclusion rules without
writing constructs; preserve a fresh independent conception; conduct a construct-specific existing-
instrument/methods scan; develop candidates under chart-blind conditions; freeze definitions and
measurement model before HD mapping; and return to Pro before items, prompts, categories, or
administration content. Choosing B1 does not immediately authorize those steps; it establishes the
direction for a new bounded supervision contract.
