## Problem being investigated

Before any later rectification question can be interpretable, a future study would need to know
whether its still-undefined observations can be elicited or coded with adequately characterized
variation under explicitly bounded conditions. Apparent stability or agreement may instead arise
from shared exposure, memory, response habits, expectancy, generic self-recognition, or leakage
between people and roles. Apparent instability may instead arise from administration differences,
version drift, inaccessible presentation, genuine interim change, or missing observations.

The immediate problem is therefore architectural: define how a later study could preserve and
audit the sources of variation without deciding what will be measured or how success will be
judged.

## Limited purpose of the screen

## Candidate sources of error

The architecture must be able to represent, without yet quantifying or choosing among them:

- occasion;
- rater;
- administration mode;
- wording or form;
- language;
- accessibility adaptation;
- recall;
- interim true change;
- social desirability;
- response style;
- expectancy;
- Barnum or personal validation;
- prior feedback or chart exposure.

## Candidate architectural mechanisms

Potential safeguards to encode without activating a study are:

- repeated administrations represented as separately versioned occasions;
- blinded independent coding with frozen commitments;
- immutable protocol versions and explicit supersession;
- role separation and ordered access boundaries;
- append-only contamination records rather than silent repair;
- connected-component splitting as a future leakage constraint, with no allocation implemented;
- missingness provenance that preserves reason and denominator distinctions.

## Independently generated insights

1. Reliability cannot be a property of an unnamed thing in the abstract; every future claim must
   remain conditional on versions, conditions, replication facets, and intended use.
2. Leakage is a graph property, not merely a single-row flag. Aliases, repeat participation,
   partners, households, shared sources, recruiters, custodians, and role changes can connect
   otherwise distinct records.
3. Contamination should be monotonic history. Later reassignment or adjudication must not erase a
   prior exposure.
4. Missingness is provenance, not a measurement value. Item absence, occasion absence, dropout,
   refusal, technical failure, accessibility failure, non-applicability, and unknown cause need
   distinguishable states.
5. Agreement among coders can coexist with a consistently wrong or leaked coding process, so
   independence, access order, version freeze, and preserved originals are separate requirements.
6. Stability can be misleading when memory, expectancy, response style, prior feedback, or generic
   personal validation repeats across occasions.
7. Version lineage must be explicit whenever form, language, mode, or accommodation changes;
   comparability cannot be presumed from a shared label.
8. A validator should prove contract closure and controlled rejection only. It must not calculate a
   substantive measurement result or become reusable against live records.
