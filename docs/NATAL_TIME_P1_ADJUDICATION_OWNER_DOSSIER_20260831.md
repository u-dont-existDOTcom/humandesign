# Neutral owner dossier — P1 adjudication and H1 author configuration

Status: decision preparation only; no decision requested at checkpoint 12

## What is already fixed

P1 is ratified. Prior exposure alone is neither automatic exclusion nor automatic eligibility.
Exposure provenance, semantic/technical familiarity, self-concept integration, and intentional
derivation risk remain distinct. Belief, skepticism, mismatch, perceived accuracy, curiosity,
usefulness, and product interest are non-dispositive. The three substantive outcomes and five
process states remain unchanged.

This checkpoint adds only an architecture for representing future evidence, masked adjudication,
independent conception, access, disagreement, and provenance. It does not determine how a person
would be assessed or who could serve.

## Author-configuration option families

| Family | Potential benefit | Material limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Single-author family | Fewer cross-author contamination and coordination paths | One source can dominate; no independent author replication |
| Independent-pair family | Independently frozen conceptions permit comparison | More custody and coordination; shared pipeline risks can remain |
| Independent-panel family | More diversity and redundancy may expose outliers | Highest coordination, leakage, recruitment, burden, and synthesis risk |

Each family can be combined with a separate or shared adjudication structure. None is recommended,
ranked, defaulted, or selected. The words “single,” “pair,” and “panel” identify option families;
no operational author or adjudicator count has been chosen.

## Evidence cautions for the eventual decision

- Disclosure is useful provenance but is not a complete bias control.
- Masking may reduce some identity or prestige effects, but randomized evidence does not show a
  universal quality improvement.
- Agreement can reveal inconsistency; it does not establish the validity of the shared standard.
- Independent conception protects against prefreeze convergence, but isolation is not proof of
  conceptual independence or scientific validity.
- Excluding whole belief, skepticism, expertise, or negative-case strata can create selection bias.
- More roles and judgments may improve separation while increasing cost, burden, leakage paths,
  and feasibility risk.
- Data minimization and durable auditability pull in different directions and require an explicit
  retention decision.
