## B1 problem

The program eventually needs to test whether independently describable human phenomena map onto a
separately frozen AstroHD hypothesis. If knowledge of the proposed AstroHD correspondence shapes
the phenomena, definitions, boundaries, measures, or reliability analysis, a later match cannot
distinguish correspondence from circular construction. If a weak or null mapping is allowed to
trigger silent renaming, merging, splitting, deletion, narrowing, broadening, or redefinition, the
same circularity reappears after the fact.

The immediate problem is therefore architectural: define how later construct work can remain
independent, provenance-bound, content-embargoed, and immutable through a separate later mapping
test without deciding what any construct or mapping will be.

## Independent-first mechanism

The proposed sequence is one-way until explicitly reopened as a new version:

1. independently conceive and freeze a future construct and its boundaries;
2. review existing work for that exact frozen construct before bespoke content;
3. freeze the chosen construct version, measurement model, protocol, reliability evidence, and
   analysis boundary;
4. seal that package against mapping-driven revision;
5. separately predeclare a later mapping hypothesis and evaluation protocol;
6. test mapping on evidence not used to formulate it; and
7. test incremental value separately against ordinary alternatives and null controls.

AstroHD is delayed information for steps 1–4 and is not an input to construct authorship.

## Separate claim lanes

- **Reliability lane:** whether an independently frozen construct can later be elicited, observed,
  or coded reproducibly under specified conditions.
- **Mapping lane:** whether a separately frozen AstroHD hypothesis later predicts or explains that
  independent construct out of sample.
- **Incremental-value lane:** whether the frozen mapping later adds out-of-sample information beyond
  ordinary non-AstroHD explanations and appropriate null controls.

No lane may populate another lane's result, and no single pass status may combine them.

## Later mapping-freeze mechanism

A future mapping gate should remain closed until an independently developed construct package is
complete, content-hashed, versioned, frozen, and sealed. The later mapping hypothesis must have a
separate identity and later timestamp. Mapping evidence cannot alter the sealed construct in place.
A change after mapping exposure creates a new exploratory version, preserves the original and its
result, and makes exposed evidence ineligible for confirming the revision.

## Anticipated contamination routes

- direct AstroHD terminology or chart exposure;
- indirect semantic cues through owner examples or favored source choices;
- repository, retrieval, prompt-history, embedding, or model-memory exposure;
- reuse of an AstroHD-aware model session for content generation;
- source selection because it appears likely to map;
- mapping-role influence on authors or reviewers;
- shared people, sessions, sources, or evidence across discovery and evaluation;
- selective retention of promising constructs;
- post-mapping definition or boundary changes;
- suppression or relabeling of null, weak, unstable, or nonreplicating mappings;
- treating reliability evidence as mapping evidence; and
- treating mapping association as incremental value.

Contamination should be append-only and monotonic. Role reassignment or later isolation cannot
erase prior exposure. Unknown provenance should fail closed.

## Independently generated insights

1. Independence is a property of information access and provenance, not a verbal instruction to
   ignore known AstroHD information.
2. Later mapping is scientifically meaningful only if construct boundaries and reliability
   analysis cannot move in response to the mapping result.
3. Reliability, correspondence, and incremental value answer different questions and require
   separate evidence identities and stopping logic.
4. A null mapping is informative only when the independent construct and original result remain
   visible rather than being repaired away.
5. Existing work must be checked after a future construct is independently defined but before new
   content is written; a generic scan cannot settle construct-specific overlap.
6. Source choice itself can leak the hoped-for mapping, so route provenance and exposure state are
   part of construct provenance.
7. A fresh model session is not demonstrably chart-blind if it receives contaminated retrieval,
   prompt history, repository context, or fit-selected examples.
8. Revision after mapping exposure is legitimate only as a new exploratory version with new
   evidence; it cannot overwrite a confirmatory failure.
