## Answer: are we doing it correctly? The map is real, but the experiment is not yet complete

Research is **partly well organized**: there are exact editions, passage evidence, original user book hashes, calendar algorithms/variants, chart-engine synthetic parity, and an HD source-to-behavior evidence protocol. The recent source-heavy effort nevertheless risks a **false progress proxy**: dozens of source pages, 1,289 Chinese annotations, and beautiful diagrams do not become a correctly frozen out-of-sample human experiment.

The canonical map lives here: [`WORKFLOW.mmd`](WORKFLOW.mmd), [`SOURCE_TO_TEST_GATES.mmd`](SOURCE_TO_TEST_GATES.mmd), [`TRADITION_DEPENDENCIES.mmd`](TRADITION_DEPENDENCIES.mmd), plus [source claim schema](CLAIM_TRACE_SCHEMA_V1.json) and [concrete quarantined K'iche' Maya E/Eb' example](CLAIM_TRACE_EXAMPLE_MAYA_E_V1.json).

**Meaningful target pipeline**:
Original page/statement → explicit cultural/edition/translation lineage → classification as natal/astronomical/ritual/collective → independent time/calendar/chart policy and uncertainty → one narrow expected outcome AND opposite/refuting direction plus confounds/dependency cluster → actual participant-facing answer/question under concealment → frozen natal chart, rule, question, predicted direction/probability, matched decoys, base-rate null, survey seed and commit/timestamp → untouched consenting human responses frozen → correct proper scoring and matched random/permuted chart null → all positives, ties, misses, no-predictions and versioned next cohort. If a transition is unsupported it stays `UNMAPPED` or `ABSTAIN`, **not a retroactive human-success claim**.

The existing 14 non-HD domain candidate categories and six HD survey-domain examples are preserved separately in `tasks/astro-multitradition-source-coverage-20261022/TRADITION_CONSTRUCT_AND_SURVEY_CROSSWALK_V1.json`. These **are not 14 usable predictive claims or calibrated astrology rules**. In particular, the K'iche' E/Eb' example is missing a verified living-local-day epoch, exact regional day assignment and a stable positive/opposite travel outcome; importing ancient lowland codex calendar arithmetic into living Highland interpretation without a historical connection would be scientifically wrong. The Dresden Venus folio chart is astronomical/calendar and cannot automatically feed that E/Eb' natal example.

**The next concrete research decision** is to use the most credible already-calculated and source-supported tradition for the first small forward-blind human cohort, rather than making Maya/Bricker or book-completion a universal prerequisite. Maya can run as a distinct, source-checked exploratory cohort only when its regional calendar anchor and person-specific source claim are resolved. Keep personality, objective life-event timing, ritual/electional and source-astronomical outcomes as different statistical families, not all inputs into one “accuracy percentage”.

**No independently validated real-person forward or reverse blinded chart predictions have been reported in the audited branches.** This is a canonical descriptive map on an isolated research integration branch, not a mainline release of the HD V4.3 scorer or a proven astrology model.
