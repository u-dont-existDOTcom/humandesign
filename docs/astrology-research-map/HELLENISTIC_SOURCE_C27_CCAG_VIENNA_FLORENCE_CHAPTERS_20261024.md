## Vienna old commentary — source-defined chapter 34 / chapter 35

On **Kroll VI printed p27**, the Cod. phil. gr.115 list unequivocally prints:

- **`λδ΄. Περὶ τῆς κατὰ τρίγωνον μονομοιρίας`** (chapter **34**). The entry's incipit is **`Ἡ κατὰ τρίγωνον`** and its explicit reaches **the end of manuscript folio 205v** with a printed final/caption phrase **`κανόνιον τριγωνομοιρίας`**.
- **`λε΄. Περὶ τῆς ἀναγκαίας τοῦ ὡροσκόπου μοίρας`** (chapter **35**). Its separate incipit is **`Σκοπὸν ἔχων`** and terminus reaches **folio 209**.

The physically photographed **Vienna f205r** red heading from C26, provisionally expanded **`Κανόνιον τριγωνομοιριῶν`**, does **not** precisely equal the printed catalogue's different case/form **`κανόνιον τριγωνομοιρίας`** associated with **end f205v**. We preserve the two **distinct reading/provenance claims**, not quietly replace either. The C26 tentative literal palaeography remains nonblind and uncertified. Physical f205v also contains a red heading *beneath* the table that appears to open the next chapter; the definitive exact reading remains a separate question. The word difference may reflect scribe/editor abbreviation, misattribution or our nonblind transcription. **No correction is asserted without direct glyph collation.**

The **Vienna f205v numerical arrangement** previously judged 1–15 next to 16–30 remains provisional and no new planet labels were accepted. Neither catalogue assigns day/night meaning to the adjacent cells or proves the columns implement Paulus C05 nighttime.

## Florence Byzantine commentary — independently numbered chapters/figure

**Olivieri I printed p27** for physically distinct **Florence BML Plut.28.14** gives:

- **folio 202v**: **`Περὶ τῆς κατὰ τρίγωνον μονομοιρίας`**, the main text section.

- **folio 205r**: next section **`Περὶ ἀναγκαστικῆς μοίρας τοῦ ὡροσκόπου`**, opening with **`Τὴν δὲ κατὰ τρίγωνον μονομοιρίαν`**.

The latter *references the preceding method*, but **it is the following chapter**, not a glossary confirming which of Florence's paired glyph columns means day/night. Also, Vienna's chapter-title adjective **`ἀναγκαίας`** differs from Florence's **`ἀναγκαστικῆς`**. Neither is silently homogenized. Florence's explicit `τριγωνικῶν` caption is also not the same title string as Kroll's Vienna `τριγωνομοιρίας`. This does not, by itself, prove a different physical algorithm or a scribal mistake.

This closes a specific C26 textual-boundary question and demonstrates that the manuscript title, table caption and next chapter must occupy **different source records**.
