## First read: Paulus, *Introductory Matters*, direct source passages

1. **Chapter 26, “Concerning What One Does,” printed pp57–58, PDF pp73–74, visually read.** Paulus chooses occupational indicators from Mars, Venus and Mercury at effective houses (angles and following places, plus the sixth), Lot of Fortune and when needed lunar/solar application or planetary appearance. Paulus specifies relevance of morning appearances for Kronos/Zeus/Ares and evening rising for Aphrodite/Hermes around **seven days before/after birth**. The same pages' **scholia**, clearly differentiated in the physical edition, rank Midheaven and second place and give further occupational examples. Rhetorius ch82–83 is partly textually similar and likely dependent within Greek tradition; **do not count two statistically independent techniques** merely because different names survive.
2. **Chapter 31, “Concerning Year, Month and Day,” printed p65, PDF p81, visually read in full.** At one sign per elapsed birth-year, sign sequence cycles every 12 years; months proceed from the annual sign at **one sign per month**. For the daily sequence Paulus says **one day per successive sign**, and after day 12 resumes at the first sign, beginning from the monthly assigned sign. Worked example: Leo ascendant, 26th year assigned Virgo, and thus Mercury is lord; monthly and daily sequence then begin from Virgo. No particular birthday clock/era policy imported.
3. **Chapter 32, “Concerning the Monomoiria by Trigon,” printed pp66–67, PDF pp82–83, inspected.** Original method divides individual degrees by sect/trigon rulers; physical printed canon displays a 30-degree × four-triplicity table, followed by translator/scholion note on ambiguity and possible reversed day/night assignment. This is a **new original source mapping class**, **UNSCORED**, not the same as the 10° decan and should not be merged with an unrelated dignity table.
4. The remainder of Paulus, *Olympiodorus Commentary* beginning printed p78, is **unavailable within the acquired PDF**, although its ToC is visible; no claim about the missing text was read.

**Major correction to C02:** Schmidt fn217, printed Hephaistio p83, reports Ptolemy as **2½ days/sign**, but Robbins's critically selected English reading is **2⅓**, explicitly with a variant reading **2½**. Schmidt's note is not a sufficient basis to say *Ptolemy always taught 2½*. The difference may be a **textual variant (not simply historical disagreement)**. Model Ptolemy main and Ptolemy minority MSS separately until Greek critical apparatus is re-examined.

**Engineering direction:** Keep at least **four named daily variants**: `PAULUS_1D`, `HEPH_5_OVER_2`, `PTOLEMY_ROBBINS_7_OVER_3`, `PTOLEMY_MSS_5_OVER_2`. ## Hephaistio I — terminology trap in “face”

- **Chapter 2, printed pp25–26, PDF pp41–42**, visually read. Describes the seven wandering stars, sect/gender and planetary mixtures in a substantially Ptolemaic framework (the footnote identifies the source dependence).
- **Chapter 19, “Concerning One's Own Face and Chariots and Thrones,” printed p36, PDF p52**, visually read. “Own face” here describes a *star's geometrical relation to a luminary analogous to its domicile's aspect*—**not automatically the 10-degree face/decan dignity**. Separate chariots/thrones are delineated conditionally in the text. Different uses of “face,” “image,” and “decan” across Hephaistio, Dorotheus and later translation must be mapped by underlying Greek/Arabic terms and **cannot be flattened by a shared English gloss**.
- **Chapter 20, printed p37, PDF p53**, explicitly presents source-transmitted universal inquiries, some flagged as condensed Ptolemaic material (translator fn). Do not treat overlap as independent Hellenistic evidence.

Created the isolated [pure-Python exact-fraction comparison](hellenistic_daily_sign_variants.py) and its [five deterministic tests](test_hellenistic_daily_sign_variants.py). For one **invented shared starting sign and a toy 28-day sequence**, the relative sign indices disagree as follows:

|---|---:|
| Paulus 1 day/sign vs Hephaistio 2½ day/sign | **25/28** |
| Paulus 1 day/sign vs Ptolemy Robbins 2⅓ day/sign | **25/28** |
| Hephaistio 2½ vs Ptolemy Robbins 2⅓ | **10/28** |
| All three assign exactly the same sign | **2/28** |

Tests: **5/5 PASS** from an isolated temporary directory after retrieving the exact GitHub branch sources. No local or remote historical birth data or actual personal events were fed to the code. Fractional boundaries use Python `fractions.Fraction` (1, 5/2, 7/3), not floating-point rounding. The alternative Ptolemy 5/2 manuscript reading is an **independently named variant** even though its number is identical to Hephaistio's; its monthly calendar and source history are not presumed shared. This demonstration is **not a completed historical annual/monthly profection implementation**; clock epoch, month duration, day boundary, intercalation, sign-lord handling and sect are deliberately unspecified.
