## What we have not translated — and why it matters

Every one of the 52 group labels is now legible as a number-plus-day-name entry, but the adjacent **K’iche’ interpretive paragraphs have not been translated, collated against a complete modern critical edition, or established as natal claims**. They must remain classified as **indigenous calendrical/divinatory text of unresolved person/ritual/omen function**.

An informative exception occurs at **folio 28, group 23 (10 Aq’ab’al group)**: a set of five day labels is visible, but the corresponding right-hand gloss area appears blank in this scanned copy. This is a **visual observation about the 1877 copy at that folio**, not proof that the 1722 original or another copy lacks a gloss. We have not interpolated a meaning from the surrounding 9 or 11 groups.

The following folio 36 begins a different prayer-like section, contains a visible Spanish date line for **6 December 1722**, and folio 37 begins with the Spanish notation **De otra mano** (‘of another hand’). Combined with complete 52 groups ending on folio 35, this is good structural evidence of where this 260-day table stops. It does not make its textual semantics unambiguous.

Publisher-authorized companion study: John M. Weeks, Frauke Sachse, and Christian M. The short historical natal-day descriptions cited in that introduction come from multiple earlier authors and centuries, and cannot be silently imported into every handwritten 1722 group.

## Scientific consequences for the multi-school study

- A 1722 manuscript may group **five** number/day combinations under a *single* interpretive passage; neither 20 generalized day-sign personality categories nor 260 numerology-like individualized traits can be assumed from the algebra alone.
- The formal **5 × 52** arrangement also appears in some ancient Dresden/Madrid almanac structures, but structural analogy does not establish a common prognostic rule or independent birth-trait evidence.
- The manuscript is a **single transmitted source**. Its 52/52 labels are **52 transcription checks**, not 52 independent historical field witnesses or natal prediction validations.
- The independent 2005 Momostenango filming-date/calendar discrepancy remains **OPEN**; historical 1722 mathematical agreement cannot settle present-day birth-clock policy.
- Clinical, stigmatizing, determinist or birth-harm claims are excluded from any initial person-level test. **No participants or their private birth details were accessed.**
- The parent goal is still empirical: **predefine source-anchored chart predicates and behavioural outcomes, compare against decoys and nonastrological controls, freeze before untouched consenting human answers, report failures.** Textual research and synthetic checks alone do not meet that outcome.
