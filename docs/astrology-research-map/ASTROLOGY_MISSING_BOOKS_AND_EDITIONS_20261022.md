## Ranked missing original-source/edition acquisitions

**Tier A — materially broadens Hellenistic natal/timing coverage**

1. **Paulus Alexandrinus + Olympiodorus, *Late Classical Astrology: Paulus Alexandrinus and Olympiodorus, with the Scholia from Later Commentators*,** translated **Dorian Gieseler Greenbaum**, edited Robert Hand, ARHAT, **2001**, 178 pp. **Not found in checked Drive folder.** Prefer this combined translation, or **James H. Holden, *Paul of Alexandria: Introduction to Astrology* (AFA, 2012)** for a separately available edition; do not buy both as a hard requirement. Publisher: https://astrologers.com/product/paul-of-alexandria-introduction-to-astrology/ ; 2001 bibliography: https://books.google.com/books/about/Late_Classical_Astrology.html?id=dgszswEACAAJ . Adds lots, fixed time-lord and definitional procedures, plus later exegetical disagreement.
2. **Hephaistio (Hephaestion) of Thebes, *Apotelesmatics*, Books I and II** translated **Robert Schmidt**, edited **Robert Hand**, Project Hindsight/Golden Hind, 1994/1998 (limited/out-of-print availability); source-critical Greek edition **David Pingree, *Hephaestionis Thebani Apotelesmaticorum libri tres* (1973–1974)**. Books I–II, especially II, preserve and adapt Dorotheus, Ptolemy and time-lord procedures; this is a critical *textual-variant witness*, not independent statistical support. **Book III: *On Inceptions*** translated Eduardo J. Gramaglia, edited Benjamin Dykes (Cazimi, 2013), is primarily electional/horary and **later priority**. Researcher Robert Schmidt explanation: https://projecthindsight.net/challenges-of-translating .
3. **Julius Firmicus Maternus, *Mathesis*, Books I–VIII**. English **Jean Rhys Bram, *Ancient Astrology: Theory and Practice*, Noyes Press, 1975**, **not found** in checked Drive; original Latin public-domain critical edition Kroll/Skutsch (1897/1913) is free and indexed by book/chapter: https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Firmicus_Maternus/home.html . Start Latin source research now; borrow Bram rather than assuming a legal public-domain English full copy exists.
4. **O. Neugebauer & H. B. Van Hoesen, *Greek Horoscopes* (1959)** — complete digitized source with **free low-res searchable PDF at APS/NYU**: https://sites.dlib.nyu.edu/viewer/books/isaw_aphs000001/1 . First-order value for testing ancient horoscope reconstruction and time conventions against real historical *documents*. These do NOT supply blind human trait/event validation.
5. **Porphyry/Antiochus definitions**, including *Porphyry, Introduction to the Tetrabiblos* and *Serapio of Alexandria, Astrological Definitions*, translated **James Herschel Holden (2009)**. Alternative **Robert Schmidt, *Definitions and Foundations* (2009)** or the preliminary Levente László **Porphyry/Antiochus excerpts** available via https://theastrologypodcast.com/2020/08/13/translating-ancient-greek-astrological-texts/ (some linked files returned errors in current access probes). **Antiochus of Athens, *The Thesaurus* (fragments)**, Robert Schmidt/Robert Hand, Golden Hind 1993, is another witness, **not necessarily a fully independent authorial text** because of later Rhetorius compilations. 1993 bibliography: https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Thesaurus.html?id=b7d6oAEACAAJ . Avoid counting near-identical Rhetorius/Porphyry/Antiochus definitions three times.

8. **Guido Bonatti, *Bonatti on Nativities***, translated **Benjamin N. Dykes**, 309 pp, **not observed**. Publisher: https://bendykes.com/product/bonatti-on-nativities/ . Useful medieval Latin integration with personality, relatives, marriage, occupation, timing and overlap tracing; does not add a historically independent school.
9. **Martin Gansten, *Primary Directions: Astrology's Old Master Technique***, **full book**, published Wessex Astrologer (2009), **not observed** (chapter only). Author: https://astrology.martingansten.com/books/ . High computational/inter-implementation priority, since angular conventions, oblique ascensions and directed positions are potential reproducibility sources.
10. **Abū Maʿshar, *The Great Introduction to Astrology***, edition/translation **Keiji Yamamoto & Charles Burnett**, with Greek version by **David Pingree**, **Brill, 2019 (2 vols)**. Distinct from the Dykes *Introductions* owned. **Major free access discovery:** Warburg Institute openly hosts **four substantial uploaded manuscript/edition PDF parts** of **401, 405, 129 and 332 pages** respectively, plus a one-page explanatory notice: https://commons.warburg.sas.ac.uk/concern/published_works/9g54xh64n?locale=en . These are *public text/glossary components*, **not confirmed to equal** the complete final corrected two-volume Brill print introduction/appendices/indexes. Start from free originals; borrow final edition only if needed. The Web checker couldn't stream two 100MB+ PDFs but Warburg's own public file-set page lists page counts.

Scholarly freely listed scans at https://www.jphogendijk.nl/biruni.html and New York Public Library source record https://old-digitalcollections.nypl.org/collections/the-book-of-instruction-in-the-elements-of-the-art-of-astrology?filters%5Btopic%5D=Science%2C+Medieval&keywords= . **Not observed** in folder; more reference/calculation than direct natal discriminators.
