## The crucial source choice: which system?

- **Living K'iche' Chol Q'ij:** 20 named days combined with numerical cycle 1–13, with regional daykeeper birth-day interpretations. Komon Tohil provides specific personality-related language. The source alone does **not** fully specify every numerical-tone effect.
- **Lowland Maya Tzolk'in/Haab/Long Count:** astronomical and historical calendrical systems. They overlap with living K'iche' practice but naming, interpretation, day boundary and source periods differ. Do not silently replace a K'iche' birth reading with a lowland codex glyph rule.
- **Astronomical/ritual almanacs:** the codices may contain ritual, planting, Venus or eclipse timings. These belong to a separate **event/electional** evidence class, never automatically to natal personality claims.
- **Modern Dreamspell/13 Moon:** later invented or adapted metaphysical framework; **separate modern comparison only**, not authentic evidence from the Dresden Codex or present-day K'iche' daykeepers.
- **Neighboring Nahua/Aztec divination:** [Elizabeth Hill Boone, *Cycles of Time and Meaning in the Mexican Books of Fate*](https://utpress.utexas.edu/9780292756564/) (2007) is a **separate high-priority** source for Tonalpohualli, Codex Borgia/Borbonicus, and ancient documented newborn, marriage and auspicious date readings. Maya and Nahua share broader Mesoamerican calendrical heritage, but they are not one universal horoscope.

## Reading protocol so we do it correctly

1. **Preserve original:** source id, author/editor/practitioner, actual page and original wording within fair use, translation and locale/era, original vs modern status, confidence and conflicting witnesses. Only brief analytically necessary paraphrase/quote is admitted to public repository.
2. **Separate claim classes:** astronomy-only, natal behavioral, individual dated event, electional/ritual, relationship compatibility, general communal prognosis, cultural narrative. Only explicitly person-predictive clauses may propose person-outcome tests.
3. **Calendar conversion is a different evidence stage:** do not generate an individual's sign until a reproducible civil-date-to-260-day converter has independently checked selected Smithsonian reference dates, GMT 584283/584285 variants, time-change convention, and K'iche' live-count anchor. A 260-day rotation itself is arithmetic, not a personality prediction.
4. **Operationalize a small set of actual source-supported claims:** initial development candidates from the Komon Tohil PDF include E/Eb' travel/generosity, No'j/Kab'an caution/advice, Toj/Muluk conflict mediation, etc. All are `EXPLORATORY_ONLY`, **not yet preregistered**. Some original source claims about health, stigmatizing character or life prosperity should be excluded from first human trial.
5. **Prespecify comparison:** one unambiguous direction, behavioral question and negative, confounder/missingness handling, a 20-day-name comparison and 260-class/tone policies only if separately sourced, plus **birth-date-shuffled or phase-shifted matched decoys** and a non-astrological self-report base-rate predictor. Control multiplicity across traits, day signs and traditions; hold untouched persons separate from development people.
6. **Freeze before outcomes:** chart converter/source version/target/sign hypothesis, questionnaire version, scoring, decoy seeds and null, consent and privacy controls before any genuine respondent answers. Report failures even when a tradition calls the person symbolically "out of balance".
7. **No culture-as-proof shortcut:** a living, intellectually sophisticated tradition warrants respectful accurate attribution; its historic and cultural authenticity does not itself prove birth-day traits predict individual behavior.
