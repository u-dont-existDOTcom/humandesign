# Primary-direction historical source notes

Original Lilly 1647 pages 510 and 651-655.

The seven pages were visually inspected in Wellcome's public-domain scan of the 1647 printing. They are bundled with this branch, separate from the 2003 retypesetting.

At printed p.510, the contrast between forward/backward and direct/converse belongs to the construction of zodiacal rays in a speculum. It does not authenticate the modern engine's shortest signed angle as a complete historical direction method. On p.651 Lilly names Argolus as his favored contemporary computational authority but does not supply an executable specification.

On p.652, he warns that a two- or three-degree error in the Ascendant can displace the purported event year by a similar amount. This is the textual motivation for the synthetic birth-time sensitivity check; it is not evidence of predictive validity.

At p.653, a preference for directions following the succession of signs refers specifically to the Part of Fortune. It must not be generalized to every angle or body, or used to classify the present diagnostic filter as fully historical.

At pp.654-655, Lilly conditions the intensity of a direction on the natal strength of BOTH its significator and promissor. He names essential dignity and angularity and explicitly allows little or no event when both are weak. He also says good and adverse directions can manifest within the same year and invokes profections, revolutions, or the stronger natal promissor to resolve a shared-topic conflict.

At p.670, the previously archived first-edition printing qualifies the favorable Midheaven–Jupiter aspect with Jupiter's natal strength. This aspect candidate therefore remains CONDITIONAL, NOT SCORE-READY. A simplistic Jupiter domicile/exaltation flag would not satisfy all the conditions just described.

**Scope boundary:** Neither the direction-sense convention nor Argolus's exact geometry, natal strength, house-based contextual judgments, and profection/revolution arbitration is frozen here. This research keeps synthetic calculations separate from all human predictive evidence. The main cross-tradition, forward-blind astrology comparison remains OPEN.
