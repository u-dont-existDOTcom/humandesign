Anonymous Message-Style Rating Packets — Optional and Offline
==============================================================

This ZIP contains six different local HTML forms. Give each voluntary,
independent reviewer one different file. Please do NOT distribute any
researcher-only ZIP, scoring key, notes about intended styles, or code
with the reviewer forms.

Each file contains 24 fictional event invitations. Your task is to select
which of six short communication-style descriptions seems closest to
each message, or "None / unclear." You may also rate your confidence,
whether the three event facts are correct, naturalness and perceived
pressure. There are no right answers about you. Taking part is optional.

How to use:
  1. Open one G39_Anonymous_Message_Reviewer_Rxx.html in Brave, Chrome or
     another modern browser. You do not need an Internet connection.
  2. Review the 24 screens. You may stop at any time.
  3. Click "Export anonymous ratings (.json)" after the last screen.
  4. If you wish, send that downloaded JSON to the person who invited
     you. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

Privacy and scope:
- No name, birth date, birth time, birthplace, contact detail, medical,
  religion, relationship, or private psychological information is requested.
- The form runs locally, without analytics, cookies, external scripts,
  background requests, localStorage, or automatic uploads.
- Closing the form discards anything not already exported as a local file.
- These are fictional researcher-written invitations. No actual event
  registration is offered. The task rates messages, not people.
- Please do not try to infer how the authors intended each message to be
  categorized. Give your own immediate impression.
