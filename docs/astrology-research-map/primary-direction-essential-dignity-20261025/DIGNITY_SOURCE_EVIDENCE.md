# First-edition William Lilly: bounded natal dignity reconstruction

Source witness: William Lilly, *Christian Astrology* (London, **1647**), Wellcome Collection original book b30338724, printed pages **101–105, 115**. Six unaltered public-domain facsimile images are in `original_1647_pages/`. The earlier direction-effect witnesses at pp.654–655 and 670 remain in the preceding Western research archives. This is source research, not a claim that astrology predicts events.

## Readings verified against photographs, not merely a modern summary

- **Page 101, Chapter XVIII:** Strength is described through five *essential* dignities—domicile, exaltation, triplicity, term, face. Domicile receives 5 points. This alone does not establish an actual person or event.
- **Page 102:** Exaltation gives 4 throughout the sign, regardless of the named exaltation degree. Triplicity gives 3 only when the planet rules the element in the nativity's day/night sect. Lilly explicitly says Sun in Aries **at night** has exaltation but no fiery triplicity. Term gives 2 points.
- **Page 103:** Face (the decan) gives 1 and counts as some dignity, so a planet in its own face should not be marked peregrine. Accidental factors such as angularity, speed and aspect are separately noted.
- **Page 104:** Original tables list rulers, exaltations, day/night triplicities, five successive term rulers for each sign, and three 10-degree face rulers. This code transcribes the **literal table of this printed edition**, not an assumed idealized Ptolemy or later standardized Egyptian table. Although Lilly titles the table 'according to Ptolomy', that label by itself does not establish identical term boundaries across the ancient and 1647 witnesses.
- **Page 105:** Each term is interpreted as a half-open run of sign degrees: in Aries, Jupiter's first 6 degrees, then Venus to degree 14, etc. Mars rules the water triplicity in either sect. The text separately describes detriment/fall as oppositions to domicile/exaltation.

## Limits of the 115 and 654–655 point tables

The original p.115 scores visible essential conditions domicile +5, exaltation +4, triplicity +3, term +2 and face +1; detriment -5, fall -4, peregrine -5. It explicitly includes *mutual reception* by domicile/exaltation as alternative ways to earn the two major positive scores. These reception features are **not yet computed** and must not be silently assumed absent. It also specifies accidental points for house position, speed, retrograde motion, combustion, stellar contacts and planetary aspects; this update computes only house position **as a separate diagnostic**, never an entire accidental strength score.

The same author's later directions (original pp.654–655) say observable intensity depends on strength of **both** the significator and promissor and can be absent in weak natal circumstances. A Midheaven/Ascendant *angle* is not an ordinary planet in the page-104 zodiacal-strength table, so its full significator strength cannot be inferred by scoring a Jupiter promissor alone. One favorable MC–Jupiter aspect interpretation on original p.670 expressly depends on Jupiter being strong in the radix. The 1647 author has **not given a unique numerical cutoff** for that condition in the sampled passages, and we do not invent one.

**Hard distinction:** the numeric sum built here is only a `partial_source_visible_score` of listed essential table factors; the report uses `UNRESOLVED_RECEPTION_ACCIDENTALS_AND_CONTEXT` for full natal strength. No event outcome is categorized as a prediction success or failure.
