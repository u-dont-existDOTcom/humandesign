# Next empirical gate: do not substitute source work for human validation

**Owner root outcome remains OPEN:** a historically auditable, precommitted comparison of multiple astrology traditions' claims against genuinely untouched, forward-blind human evidence and proper controls. This is a feasibility blueprint, **not** an enrolled, frozen or validated trial.

## What this source phase now makes clear

The original 1647 Lilly table yields reproducible partial essential/house position components, but no single source-backed Boolean predicate saying Jupiter is 'strong in radix' has yet been reconstructed. In particular, the directed Ascendant/Midheaven's strength cannot be inferred merely by evaluating a Jupiter promissor. Full mutual receptions, combustion, planetary aspects, house/radix circumstances, directional sense and other historical conditions are still unresolved. The 96-chart source table contains **zero** complete strength verdicts.

The separate synthetic birth-time study showed ~1.25 symbolic-year contact-age changes from a five-minute recorded-time displacement under the **modern diagnostic** one-degree-per-year key. The original narrow ±0.5-year event window therefore does not remain stable under ordinary ±5-minute birth-time uncertainty. Wider uncertainty bands predictably enlarge chance-hit opportunities and must affect null exposure time equally.

## Explicit alternatives for the first human evaluation

**A. Historically authenticated Lilly arm:** not score-ready until the exact algorithm, conditionality and outcome mapping can be specified. It must remain `UNRESOLVED`, not be rescued by calling a partial +5/+4 score 'strong'. Continue primary-source work without consuming final-test human answers.

**B. Separately named contemporary diagnostic arm:** its fully specified geometric/temporal algorithm can be registered *as a new falsifiable hypothesis*, with clear notice that it does not test authentic complete Lilly. Allow all previously specified eight source-associated candidates rather than tuning to particular past events; account for overlaps, incomplete ascertainment, and multiplicity. This arm is an optional experiment, not a mandatory replacement for A.

**C. Multi-tradition comparison:** Hellenistic, Jyotish, Chinese and Human Design predictions should be independently source-mapped and frozen as distinct arms. Align observable outcome coding across traditions only when the original constructs truly map; never use one tradition's post hoc hits to rescue another's losses. Composite models require their own separate preregistration and baseline.


## Protocol freeze before actual prospective participants/outcomes

1. **Participants and inputs:** independently timestamped DOB, local time and birthplace; documentary birth-time quality; a clear UTC conversion with historical time-zone/DST handling; recorded uncertainty interval, e.g. certified ±2 min versus rounded ±5/±15 min. Prior knowledge of any astrology/HD claims and consent are recorded. Development participants and anyone whose life story has already shaped rules are explicitly excluded from untouched final validation.
2. **Calculation lock:** hash the ephemeris, requested AND returned mode, sign/house systems, all direct/converse arms, exact term table source, candidate rules, interpretation conditions, chart features, age windows and randomization before outcomes. If a chart's direction moves materially across the birth-time interval, emit a **set of possible contact years** or `UNRESOLVED`; never select the convenient minute.
3. **Outcome definition:** record appointment, promotion, involuntary dismissal, documented grants and formal adversarial filings as distinctly observable modern targets. Eligibility/opportunity is frozen (e.g., there must be a role to lose), as is the minimal materiality threshold for patronage. Dates and archival evidence are coded by an assessor who does not see astrology charts. Historical Lilly passages have broader alternatives; label each modern operational predicate as a constrained experimental interpretation.
4. **Prediction package:** store explicit contacts, expected event domains, windows, dependence groups, negative/none predictions where warranted, declared uncertainty, hashed predictions and controls before any outcome collection or decoding. The source-conditional MC–Jupiter arm is **not scored** while 'strong in radix' is unresolved.
5. **Baseline and multiplicity:** match control windows for each participant's eligible years, and retain the same total exposure-year count. Independently compare to shuffled chart labels and date/time/place-matched decoys where valid, with one predeclared primary statistic and multiplicity correction across event domains and method variations. Report every chart and event including no hits, unascertained records, tied ranks and conflicting predictions. Calibrated probability scoring is only allowed after independent calibration, not after reading final cohort outcomes.
6. **Cohort/power:** study size must be driven by independently estimated domain event rates and the fraction of participants whose birth-time precision provides adequate timing discrimination; the synthetic 96-chart occupancy count is **not** an estimate of those human event rates or forecast skill. Do not invent a power figure from unobserved prospective human outcomes.
