## New source reading via a modern scholarly edition

Michael W. Zellmann-Rohrer, *Three Deluxe Horoscopes*, *Archiv für Papyrusforschung* 70/2 (2024), pp283–316, DOI `10.1515/apf-2024-0019`, re-edits P. Berol. 21347 at pp290–297. **Indexed excerpts from pp291 and 297** were readable, but the Berlin university PDF itself returned an Anubis access page or HTTP 429. This is **not a claim to have inspected the complete 2024 printed Greek apparatus or photographs in that paper**.

The scholar's partly restored Greek at **lines 7–8** attributes Libra's trigon to **Mercury**: `τριγώνω Ἑρμοῦ`. He identifies the ending as supporting Mercury, inferring **a nighttime nativity**, since the separately attested triplicity rulers for air are **Saturn by day** and **Mercury by night** (cf. Paulus, *Eisagogika* §2). The papyrus is second century and **predates Paulus's 378 CE treatise**. It documents an earlier related convention; it cannot prove the early scribe consulted that later written book.

At **lines 8–9**, the editor proposes the restored Greek **`[μονομοι]ρία Σε[λ]ήνης`** (Moon's monomoiria). Square brackets record missing or uncertain ink, so this is not a fully preserved source literal or independently confirmed palaeography. The source excerpt does not supply a secure complete Ascendant longitude and algorithm; consequently **zero new monomoiria numerical calculations** are admitted from P. Berol. 21347.
