## Direct Greek source-to-test interpretation checked at C29

The publicly available original-language *Eisagogika* rendition was read directly in [Paulus, Eulogikon, chapters 32–33](https://eulogikon.org/works/paul-alexandria-astrology-sum-aa), source lines 303–310. Chapter 32 explicitly specifies **`τὰς μοίρας τοῦ αἱρετικοῦ φωτὸς`** (degrees of the sect light) and qualifies the table as daytime, with a changed order at night. Chapter 33 explains use of the triplicity monomoiria **`πρὸς τὴν τοῦ ὡροσκόπου μοιρικὴν κατάληψιν`** (for the degree determination of the Ascendant), while listing other rival procedures.

This source evidence specifies a *horoscope construction/rectification computation*. **The checked passages themselves do not assert a concrete behavioral trait or independently observable personal future outcome as a function of the resulting ruler.** This is a statement about those *specific passages*, not a claim that the entire Greek corpus lacks all predictive claims.

[Explicit source-to-human disposition](HELLENISTIC_C29_SOURCE_TO_HUMAN_TEST_DISPOSITION_V1.json) therefore records four algorithmic variants as `SOURCE_CALCULATION_ONLY` / `NO_PERSON_OUTCOME_MAPPING_FROZEN`, and marks a possible comparison against independently documented Ascendant geometry only as an **unrun technical-reconstruction hypothesis**. It does not count as predicting personality or life events. Boer 1962 apparatus has not been inspected and is not substituted by this modern text edition.
