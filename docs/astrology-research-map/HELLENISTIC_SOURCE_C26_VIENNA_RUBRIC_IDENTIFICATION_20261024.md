## 3. Critical-edition dependency checked, not falsely closed

The 1963 contemporary scholarly reviews of **Ae. Boer, *Heliodori, ut dicitur, in Paulum Alexandrinum commentarium* (Teubner, 1962)** were rechecked:

- **Charles Mugler, REG 76 (1963), p294**: Vienna A used as a principal witness for Boer, with chapters concerning Paulus 1–8 supplied from later Byzantine recension because absent from old witness: https://www.persee.fr/doc/reg_0035-2039_1963_num_76_359_3742_t1_0294_0000_2

They warn against calling the Vienna original an authentic author autograph or casually equating a later Florence witness with the earlier copy. **Boer's actual printed Greek diagram, locus and critical apparatus have still not been opened.** Google Books bibliography/snippets, Perseus catalog and the reviews do not substitute for the printed apparatus.
