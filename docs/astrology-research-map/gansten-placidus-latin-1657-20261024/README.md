## Direct original Latin findings

Thesis 58 (printed p.11): successive solar days after birth are related to successive symbolic years, at the equal natal hour. Translation by Cooper agrees.

Thesis 59: explicit distinctions between direct and converse motion; not the same claim as a modern mathematical negative or 360-minus-direct arc.

Thesis 60: original Latin states that the angles cannot be directed with converse motion ("motu converso non possunt dirigi Cardines"). Cooper's 1814 translation agrees.

Theses 61 and 62: converse directions receive rays/parallels in mundo, with "nullo modo in zodiaco": zodiacal rays are excluded. Cooper 1814 agrees. The generic mathematical converse zodiacal-ray arm from earlier research must NOT be labeled a historically authenticated Placidus 1657 arm, although it remains available to test OTHER historical systems.

Thesis 65: the Sun's right-ascensional increment on the FIRST subsequent day determines the FIRST year, second day the SECOND year, etc., as originally printed. The current accumulated variable solar-RA key is thus corroborated by original 1657 Latin at a method level; precise historical UT/TT, coordinate, lunar and fractional-day conventions are still not authenticated.

Original printed p.19 also contains a converse-motion canon about a significator moving with the universal motion toward the promissor. The full role-specific geometry must still be checked against all relevant canons.
