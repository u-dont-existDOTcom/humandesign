## Why this is a new finding rather than simply repeating C19

C19 documented a scholarly proposed *Moon's monomoiria* for the Libra Ascendant of **P. Berol. 21347**, but conservatively left the missing Ascendant degree uncalculated.

The **same author, Michael W. Zellmann-Rohrer**, explicitly supplies a **bounded four-element set** in *Three Deluxe Horoscopes* (2024), *Archiv für Papyrusforschung* **70/2, p.296**, commentary to **P. Berol. 21347 lines 3–9**. His reading of the partially restored Moon monomoiria is consistent with completed-degree positions **2, 9, 16, or 23**. The exact ancient number does **not** survive clearly; these are **editorially reconstructed mathematical possibilities**, not four independently legible source numerals. The study's corrected/reconstructed Greek has brackets and uncertain letter forms; see C19 for exact original-image provenance.

The author finds **2°35′ Libra** particularly attractive on contextual grounds **if** the Egyptian terms were used, because 2°35′ is in the third ordinal degree and in Saturn's Egyptian terms. This is an editorial preference, NOT a proven correct source restoration. The printed page text was recovered from search-indexed PDF text excerpts; direct PDF requests were challenged with HTTP 429/Anubis and the actual page **was not visually opened**, so do not treat this pass as a direct rendering of the critical edition or full-page collation. The separate original Berlin recto was already visually inspected C19.

## Computation independent of any favored restoration

**First source — by-sign planetary monomoiria:** retain the existing, previously frozen C04 sevenfold sign-start calculation (Libra domicile ruler Venus, then the seven-planet Chaldean sequence). The Moon occurs in ordinal degrees **3, 10, 17, 24**, corresponding to completed degrees **2, 9, 16, 23**. These exactly reproduce the 2024 editor's **four** proposals without changing the source model.

**Second source — Egyptian term divisions:** Ptolemy, *Tetrabiblos* I.20, Egyptian terms table (Loeb 1940 English edition, p.97; University of Chicago's verified web edition): in Libra **Saturn 6°, Mercury 8°, Jupiter 7°, Venus 7°, Mars 2°**, with endpoints at completed-degree boundaries 6, 14, 21, 28, 30. Ptolemy describes competing Egyptian and Chaldean/own term systems, so this is explicitly an **assumed historical variant**, not a universal ruler table.

## Necessary negative checks

1. **Off-by-one indexing:** confusing completed degrees **2/9/16/23** with ordinal second/ninth/sixteenth/twenty-third degrees produces **Mercury** in the frozen by-sign algorithm, not Moon. The 2024 conditional 2°35′ position is the **third ordinal** degree. The source-only regression explicitly rejects that index confusion.
2. **Unconditional uniqueness is false:** under the Moon reading alone there are four valid positions. Calling the Ascendant definitively 2°35′ would silently promote an editorial conjecture.
3. **Method transfer remains uncertain:** Ptolemy's Egyptian terms are not guaranteed to be the Berlin scribe's terms. His separate term table is also distinct; do not substitute it invisibly.
4. **Document independence:** P. Berol. 21347 is not P. Berol. 9825 and is not Oxford P.Oxy. 4277. The latter's existing three published-label calculations remain **1 agreement / 2 disagreements**, not affected by this new conditional source reconstruction. The former predates Paulus's 378 CE book; similar technique does not prove it was copied from that later book.
5. **No human validity evidence:** these positions were derived *after seeing a historical scholar's restored label*, solely to analyze source transmission. They cannot count as matched predictions of a living person's character, a blinded prospective test, or a model feature promoted into production.
