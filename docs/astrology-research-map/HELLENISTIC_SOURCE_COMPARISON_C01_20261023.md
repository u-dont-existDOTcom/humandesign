## C01.B Longevity releaser's fifth dignity — refined textual comparison

- **HEL-II ch.26**, **printed p79**, PDF **p45R**: source-translated sequence in choosing a releaser's governing planet names **domicile, exaltation, trigon/triplicity and bounds**. Schmidt's **editorial footnote 199** compares his understanding of **Pingree's Arabic Dorotheus III.2** as adding the **decanate**, and proposes that it could be a later Arabic interpolation. His dating hypothesis remains **unproven**, not an ancient statement.
- **DOR-DYK III.2**, **printed p189** / PDF **p207**, numbered sentences **4–5**, **directly rechecked**: fifth item appears as the **“lord of its image”** following bound, house, exaltation and triplicity. Dykes's fn53 summarizes the **fourfold** Hephaistio comparison, but the translated Arabic passage itself contains five references. This **corroborates the structural 4-versus-5 disagreement**, not that English `image` is linguistically **identical** to Schmidt's `decanate`/Pingree's translation. Underlying Arabic word, manuscript variants and original Greek alignment remain unresolved.
- **DOR-DYK III.2**, **printed p190** / PDF **p208**, sentences 7–15: candidate releaser selection depends also on sect/light, angular/good places and aspect of rulers. It should not be reduced to “add fifth dignity, therefore one more automatic life ruler.”
- **PTO-ROB III.13**, printed p340 / PDF **p364** contains `decans` in an **editorial Robbins footnote summarizing Bouche-Leclercq**, not as Ptolemy's own proof of decans in the HEL longevity procedure. Do **not** cite this note as a direct ancient five-dignity statement, and do not cite Schmidt's remarks as proof that Ptolemy never used decans anywhere.
- **Status:** two **source readings** `HEL_4` versus `DOR_ENGLISH_IMAGE_5`; a tentative interpretation `PINGREE_SCHMIDT_DECAN_5`. They are *not* synonymous until lexical provenance established. The eventual falsifiable computation must locate cases whose selected ruler differs; never select the variant after viewing a biography.
