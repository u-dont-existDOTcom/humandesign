# Discover Your Unique Life Patterns + InnerSignal Integration

Status: roadmap architecture, development only. No deployment authorization.

## Product direction

Treat **Discover Your Unique Life Patterns** as the durable personal-pattern substrate and make InnerSignal an optional integrated mode rather than forcing users to maintain two unrelated personal models.

The participant owns the connection. Nothing is shared across modes unless the participant enables it.

## One-way first integration

Initial integration direction:

`Life Patterns evidence -> participant-reviewed Personal Pattern Profile -> consented InnerSignal context`

InnerSignal may read selected profile sections such as:

- recurring patterns;
- context-dependent patterns;
- known triggers;
- conflict/relationship patterns;
- decision tendencies;
- effective and ineffective responses;
- strengths and underused strategies;
- developmental changes;
- important unknowns;
- current reversible experiments.

Each claim retains evidence provenance and confidence/limits.

InnerSignal must treat these as historical observations, not identity declarations or destiny.

## No silent reverse write

InnerSignal sessions must NOT silently rewrite the research evidence or locked Life Patterns profile.

If InnerSignal notices a possible new pattern, it may create a proposed update containing:

- proposed pattern or exception;
- source InnerSignal session reference;
- participant-facing explanation;
- whether it contradicts, narrows, or extends an existing pattern;
- participant approval state.

Only explicit participant approval may move that proposal into the ordinary mutable personal profile. Confirmatory research freezes remain immutable.

## Integrated app modes

Longer-term participant navigation can expose modes such as:

1. **Discover** — build/update the evidence-backed Life Patterns profile.
2. **Understand** — explain a current situation using evidence-linked historical patterns.
3. **Coach** — identify fitting paths, Pattern Transfer opportunities, and low-stakes experiments.
4. **InnerSignal** — hypnosis/inner-child/journal/synchronicity work using only participant-authorized profile context.
5. **Relationships** — compare two independently created profiles with both people's consent.
6. **Research lenses** — after behavioral lock, compare HD, astrology, raw astronomy, combined and empirical model predictions.

## InnerSignal safety/interaction boundary

The existing InnerSignal therapeutic interaction model remains separate from research scoring. Life Patterns evidence may personalize tone, known triggers and helpful framing, but therapeutic interactions cannot retroactively improve a frozen birth-model test result.

Any high-leading therapeutic interpretation should remain out of the neutral research evidence layer unless independently re-elicited and participant-confirmed there.

## Portable profile contract

The Life Patterns portable export remains the interoperability boundary for other assistants too, not only InnerSignal. A future integrated database may avoid file transfer internally, but the same explicit fields and permissions should remain exportable so the participant is not locked into this app.

Required principle:

> The personal model belongs to the participant; applications consume it with permission.
