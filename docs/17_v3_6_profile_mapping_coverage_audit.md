# 17 — V3.6 Profile Mapping Coverage Audit

## Purpose

This file records what the frozen 2026-08-22 V3.6 NetInformation audit actually scores and what it deliberately leaves unresolved. It exists to prevent a reduced model from being mistaken for complete behavioral coverage.

The rule is conservative: a behavior may be real and important without receiving symbolic score if there is no sufficiently constrained predeclared HD pathway.

## Explicit contradiction

Only one symbolic contradiction is mechanically penalized in this audit:

- complete 16-48 versus the target's explicit denial of a generalized independent drive to practice/perfect technique for mastery itself. Severity is limited to `0.50`, because repetition and skill remain available instrumentally.

No contradiction penalty is assigned merely because a candidate lacks a favored channel, nor for 32-54 ambition, 25-51 competition, emotionality, care/nourishment, or other broad interpretations that are not clean direct opposites of the target.

## Deliberately unresolved / unscored constructs

The following remain behaviorally important but do not receive mechanical V4.3 evidence in this audit:

- sweet-versus-savory food-combination preference and other PHS-style dietary details;
- fine-grained tactile, aroma, music, aesthetic, and environmental preferences;
- humor style;
- generic romance, sensuality, attachment, and intimacy where no sufficiently narrow pathway was frozen;
- sleep-boundary and dream phenomena;
- nonviolence/de-escalation as a generic trait;
- home-sanctuary aesthetics beyond the scored retreat/routine/resource constructs;
- travel motives;
- confidentiality style;
- institutional dissent where a narrow standard mapping was not established;
- calculated versus pure gambling risk;
- generic caregiving capacity. V3.5/V3.6 explicitly invalidate `can care strongly -> generalized nourishment drive` as a scoring shortcut;
- autobiographical calendar/age windows, which are excluded for leakage;
- any dynamic/relationship/advanced-variable interpretation not represented by an explicitly frozen mapping.

These omissions are neutral. They are not treated as mismatches.

## Anti-simplification note

The audit is not architecture-only: it includes Type, Authority, six diagnostic Center predictions, profile alternatives, complete Channels, hanging-Gate alternatives, and two side/body-specific carrier activations. It also records unresolved content rather than silently deleting it.

At the same time, this one-off audit is not the final reusable production `mapping-library-v2`/century-cache implementation. Its purpose is to test the V4.3 NetInformation-first ordering against the already-existing profile with a frozen, inspectable mapping set and verified SWIEPH astronomy.
