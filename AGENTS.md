# AGENTS.md

## Mission
Build a reproducible, blinded Human Design research harness from the specifications in this repository.

This repository tests four different questions and MUST keep them separate:

1. ENGINEERING VALIDATION: Can the decoder recover hidden chart/date/time states from responses generated by the same frozen model?
2. HUMAN REVERSE-MATCH VALIDATION: Do real human responses contain out-of-sample information about their actual birth-derived HD chart/date/time?
3. FORWARD-BLIND VALIDATION: Given independently supplied birth data, does the concealed birth-derived chart prospectively predict blinded questionnaire responses better than matched alternative charts and non-HD baselines?
4. RELATIONSHIP VALIDATION: Given two independently calculated natal charts, do frozen connection-chart mechanics predict or describe pair dynamics with out-of-sample specificity?

A successful synthetic test does not validate Human Design in humans. A retrospectively accurate relationship description does not validate natal reverse matching. Forward-blind results, reverse-matching results, relationship evidence, and practical Strategy/Authority outcomes must not be pooled to rescue one another.

## Mandatory broad-search order

Do not start another full 100-year behavioral ranking until:

1. the production Swiss `.se1` files are provisioned and their hashes recorded;
2. the engine explicitly requests SWIEPH and proves from returned flags that SWIEPH was actually used;
3. the complete cacheable M0-M2 feature registry and exact boundary engine exist;
4. the canonical century cache is built and verified;
5. V4.3 scorer/mapping compliance tests pass.

After that, ordinary broad searches MUST read the verified century cache. Do not regenerate 100 years of astronomy for every target/profile rerun.

Direct JPL files are optional parity inputs. Verified compressed Swiss `.se1` files are the normal production engine.

Forward-blind testing does not require a full broad search, but it still requires the same verified chart engine and a frozen compliant scoring/mapping layer for any model version claimed as V4.3. Broad decoy panels should use the verified cache once available.

## V4.3 compliance rule

A run may identify itself as `V4.3` only if all of these are true:

- the full required feature registry for every frozen mapping is present;
- Gate/Line/Channel/planetary-carrier predicates required by the model are calculable;
- flexibility penalties are applied;
- dependency and corroboration caps are applied;
- duration-weighted conditional prevalence is active where the declared scoring contract requires it;
- exact stable intervals are used where candidate time is uncertain;
- the complete declared universe is rescored after accepted target changes for reverse-match runs;
- the ranking tuple follows `reference/core/v4_3_scoring_algorithm.md` exactly;
- the astronomy/cache provenance is verified;
- requested and returned ephemeris modes agree and are SWIEPH for canonical production calculations.

If any item is missing, fail closed or label the result honestly as a reduced model such as `M0-architecture-only`; emit `v4_3_compliant: false`. A reduced model must never be presented as an approximation that is "basically V4.3".

## Forward-blind validation rules

Forward-blind prospective validation is defined in `docs/20_forward_blind_prospective_validation.md` and should become the preferred first untouched human test of chart -> behavior prediction once the model is frozen.

- Participant supplies DOB, exact local time, and birthplace/timezone before testing; birth-time source quality and uncertainty must be recorded.
- Calculate the natal chart privately. Do not reveal Type, Authority, Profile, Centers, Gates, Channels, Variable/PHS, or expected answer directions before response freeze.
- Freeze/hash the birth input, chart features, engine provenance, model, questionnaire, question selection, predicted option support/probabilities, dependency structure, decoy/control specification, software commit, and timestamp **before scored answers are collected**.
- Questionnaire wording must be behavior-first and concealed-direction; randomize option order from a frozen seed where appropriate.
- Freeze raw/coded responses before reveal or model change.
- Prefer direct proper scoring when calibrated probabilities exist. Do not call symbolic support values probabilities when they are not calibrated.
- Support a cohort `N x N` response-to-chart identification matrix: each participant's responses are scored against all independently calculated cohort charts, and the true chart rank/percentile is reported.
- For small cohorts/single participants, use preregistered matched decoy panels plus a broad panel; report both rather than choosing whichever looks better.
- Use chart-label permutation for the primary group null when possible.
- Record prior HD familiarity before testing. The strongest primary cohort is HD-naive people with documented birth times.
- Development participants may be used to refine the model; untouched validation/final-test participants may not.
- Do not discard failures by asserting that the participant is `not-self` or otherwise retrospectively redefining the target.
- Forward-blind results MUST NOT alter the current participant's frozen model after answers are seen. A redesign creates a new model version and requires new untouched participants.

## Color/Tone/Base rules

Advanced substructure remains fail-closed until `docs/19_base_level_substructure_validation.md` passes all engineering gates.

- Calculation reproducibility is not behavioral validation.
- Do not expose authoritative Color/Tone/Base output until Swiss provenance, deterministic boundary tests, independent implementation parity, golden/reference parity, and uncertainty propagation pass.
- Base-sensitive natal output must be stable across the documented birth-time uncertainty interval or be reported as unresolved/a set of possible states.
- Only after calculation parity may CTB/Variable/PHS/Rave Psychology enter the forward-blind model as a deeper nested layer.
- Freeze exact CTB -> downstream construct mappings and concealed questions before untouched responses.
- Require incremental out-of-sample improvement of the CTB layer over the same model without CTB before calling it behaviorally validated.
- Prefer concrete downstream discriminators and safe repeated-measures tests over free-form raw-Base storytelling.
- No medical, nutritional-treatment, legal, or other high-stakes recommendation may be made from CTB/PHS.

## Relationship module rules

Relationship/connection analysis is a separate research module under `src/hdmatch/relationship/`.

- Start from two independently calculated natal charts; never change natal activations to improve a relationship narrative.
- V1 must prioritize Ra's source-defined surface: combined Center configuration, connection-chart splits, and Electromagnetic/Dominance/Compromise/Companionship mechanics.
- Preserve the direction of Dominance and Compromise.
- Preserve shared Gates separately; do not double-count them as complete Channels.
- Treat Electromagnetic as attraction/spark mechanics, not as universal positive compatibility.
- Do not invent a soulmate probability or compatibility percentage and attribute it to Ra.
- Sun/Earth-to-Node geometry may be reported as context; it must not be interpreted automatically as lover/soulmate status.
- Relationship outputs MUST NOT add support to natal `NetInformation`, alter natal prevalence, or break V4.3 dependency controls.
- If one partner's time is unknown, enumerate every exact stable natal state in the declared local-day window and report invariant versus time-dependent pair mechanics. Never select the time that gives the nicest relationship story.
- A known couple whose history was already inspected is a DEVELOPMENT case. It cannot validate a relationship model fitted or refined on that same couple.
- Respect third-party privacy: pair mechanics may be computed from supplied data, but avoid presenting speculative private psychological claims about an absent partner as established facts.

## Non-negotiable research rules
- Never use the answer key during blind recovery.
- Never put secret answer keys under the decoder project root during a blind run.
- Hash/freeze predictions before answer-key reveal.
- Split human data by PERSON before question selection, mapping fitting, hyperparameter tuning, calibration, or evaluation.
- Post-hoc fitting on DEVELOPMENT humans is allowed and encouraged.
- Performance on those same development humans is not evidence of predictive validity.
- Keep a final untouched human test cohort.
- Report failures, ties, unresolved intervals, and negative results.
- Timezone is not independently identifiable from personality when local tuples resolve to the same UTC instant.
- Do not report a single minute when the scored state is stable across a wider interval.
- Missing support is not contradiction.
- Trauma/body-access variables may reduce measurement reliability; they may not turn a mismatch into support.
- Unknown/context-dependent responses may remain unscored; never force them to one side.
- Do not repeatedly re-ask established behavioral constructs in new wording and call the result independent evidence.
- Separate capacity from preferred use when the target explicitly distinguishes them, especially persuasion.
- Separate workload intensity/duration from energy sustainability; do not force `engaging work sustains` and `extreme overwork depletes` into opposites.
- Score reported phenomenology rather than requiring a user to self-label an HD construct such as `Splenic`.
- No medical, legal, financial, relationship-safety, or other high-stakes recommendations from Human Design.

## Implementation principles
- Deterministic chart computation.
- Exact boundary-event generation/root solving, not only coarse time grids.
- Reproducible random seeds.
- Versioned schemas and frozen mappings.
- Small modules with tests.
- Structured logs for every run.
- CLI first; API second.
- Use typed Python.
- Prefer pure functions for scoring and pair mechanics.
- Cache chart states and ephemeris results.
- Prefer the verified precomputed century cache for broad searches rather than rebuilding astronomy on every profile.
- Never accept successful coordinate output as proof that the desired ephemeris was used; inspect returned flags.
- Never optimize evaluation cases.
