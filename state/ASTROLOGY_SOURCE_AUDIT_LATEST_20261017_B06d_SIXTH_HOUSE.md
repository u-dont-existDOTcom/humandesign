## Source anatomy — not clinical anatomy or evidence

Five classes of historically alleged health indicia: ascendant and lord, Sun/Moon, sixth house/ruler, planets in sixth, and seventh house/ruler. Lilly asks about strength/weakness, beneficial versus malefic/fixed-star aspects, and humoral balance, giving conditional favorable and adverse results. Aphorisms propose illnesses and lifespan effects from house placements, Sun/Moon aspects, planetary sect and ascensional placement, and combinations of Saturn/Mars. They are source claims with many overlapping alternatives, not independent patient observations.

The source also distinguishes (1) an astrological significator of sickness from (2) an alleged author/cause of sickness, then grades presumed severity using weakness, unfavorable aspect, aspect exactness and angular position. The author further adds zodiac sign, nature of malefic, sign of malefic, whether indicators are above/below horizon, and a cross-reference to earlier house-disease correspondences. No testable prospective rejection/tolerance rules or patient records accompany these claims.

**The complete preserved structural table surface** consists of two independently addressable source cells for each of the **seven planets**: associated body parts and named historical conditions, for 14 planetary cells with 99 normalized terms. The separate **twelve zodiac-sign cells** contain 51 normalized terms, mostly body regions, with Aries also listing historical conditions. There are therefore **26 structured table cells and 150 semantic source-list items**. These are conservative readings of 1647 printed lists, **not a verified verbatim diplomatic transcription, 150 distinct people, 150 experimentally confirmed medical mechanisms, or 150 usable diagnostic rules**.

The matrix records Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury and Moon in original sequence, and Aries through Pisces. The original Moon row's opposite female/male eye-side distinctions are retained as period text only. Mercury's ambiguous historic disease word and Jupiter's right-margin qualification remain flagged rather than assigned modern diagnostic meanings. Period terms such as leprosy, melancholy, humours, falling-sickness, matrix and pox cannot be repurposed as validated current medical syndromes from astrology.
