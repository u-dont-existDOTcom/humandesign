## Historic source conditions and counterexamples

Lilly makes **Sun/Moon** first priority, **Saturn/Mars** second, the **eighth house/ruler** third, the **Ascendant lord** fourth, and **fixed stars** of supposed Saturn/Mars nature fifth. His initial source has six named period fixed stars with original positions: *Caput Medusae 21 Taurus*, *Oculus Tauri 4 Gemini*, *Hercules 18 Cancer*, *Right Shoulder of Orion 23 Gemini*, *Cor Scorpii 4 Sagittarius*, *Chele 9 Scorpio*. Those are original SOURCE sign-degree descriptions, not current star locations; exact historical coordinate epoch, identity and some glyph parity remain unresolved. One source treats star proximity as **five degrees**, not a universal distance to every house cusp.

The original then gives MANY alternative, often compound supposed indicators: both lights in 'violent' signs but different signs and not beholding one another; luminaries in contact with two violent fixed stars under named Ascendant/eighth-ruler conditions; one violent-sign light and the other contacting a star **plus** an infortune; combinations of Saturn/Mars afflicting luminaries under violent-sign, angularity, essential dignity and eighth-house rulership conditions. These cannot be reduced to 'Mars anywhere means violence'.

Other historically alleged death modes are assigned to planet, sign and house, often yielding numerous mutually dissimilar alternatives from a single configuration: Saturn linked with drowning, shipwreck, poison or collapse; Mars with fire, iron, lightning, weapons, falls or other injuries; fiery/airy/watery/earthy/humane/poisonous zodiac classes with their own unverified categories; tenth/eleventh/twelfth/sixth-house analogies to period officials, animals, workers or alleged violence. The author favors two or three testimonies concurrent rather than a lone signifier, but does not give a deterministic full hierarchy, calibrated outcomes or a reproducible probability.

**Crucial counterexamples and limits in the original photographs:**

- On **PDF679/printed645**, Lilly explicitly says a supposed violent-death configuration can be negated when benefic planets are placed in both the Ascendant and the eighth house.
- On **PDF681/printed647**, after Saturn-linked scenarios, Lilly explicitly says a *benevolent planet mitigates these judgments*.
- On **PDF682/printed648**, after Mars-linked scenarios, Lilly says one or both luminaries must be configured with Mars or the judgment is **invalid**.
- On **PDF684/printed650**, Lilly states the older aphorisms **do not operate independently** of principal significators and have to be blended into an astrologer's judgment. He claims examples from personal experience, but supplies neither a blinded sample nor a false-prediction denominator.
- Also on **PDF684**, Lilly says that *if none* of the aforementioned violent-death configurations appears, natural death by sickness/disease is alleged, with type assigned by the eighth-house occupants or ruler. This is a historical source *negative alternative*, not evidence of natural versus violent death accuracy.
- Finally, Lilly explicitly says the **time of death is only in God's hands** and he would not wish an astrologer to be positive about timing. He discusses speculative hostile directions/Apheta and critical years while insisting on modesty. **No real-person death timing, medical risk or safety warning is authorized.**

## Attribution, high-stakes stigma and nonvalidation

The source refers to methods by **Ptolemy, Schoner, Cardanus, Albubater, Haly and Origanus**, but this batch has not independently collated these historic authors. It also makes period moralizing claims about supposed thieves, murderers, 'heretics', social classes, legal punishment and suffocation/hanging, and sometimes conflates nonfatal harm (e.g. visual injury) with mortality. None can legitimately classify or warn about a real person. The original text often invites broad selection among incompatible outcomes, creating serious retrospective fitting risk.
