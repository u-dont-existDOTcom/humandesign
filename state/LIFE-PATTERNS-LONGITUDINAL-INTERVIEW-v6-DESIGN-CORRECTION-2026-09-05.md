# Life Patterns longitudinal interview v6 — design correction

Date: 2026-09-05

## Correction

Earlier interview iterations blurred two distinct concepts:

1. the **project purpose**: collect behavioral evidence for later empirical AstroHD / longitudinal model comparison; and
2. the **interviewer blind**: the elicitation/coding context must not receive AstroHD mappings, predictions, birth/chart information, or model-fit results.

Theory-neutral interviewing does **not** mean the data collection is scientifically unrelated to AstroHD. It means the behavioral evidence is elicited and frozen without telling the interviewer what AstroHD predicts.

## Why v5 was insufficient

The v5 targeted extension emphasized incidents from the most recent 1–3 years. That can improve current behavioral codability but is insufficient for the project's age-indexed secondary-progression hypothesis.

The repository's frozen progression convention explicitly maps elapsed age to a day-for-year progressed ephemeris state. Longitudinal testing therefore needs behavioral episodes with usable age provenance across different periods of life.

The frozen neutral codebook independently supports this requirement by preserving `life period` as context and defining derived temporal-change summaries from time-ordered comparable opportunities.

## v6 design

`state/LIFE-PATTERNS-LONGITUDINAL-INTERVIEW-PROMPT-v6-2026-09-05.txt`

V6 uses paired neutral event forms:

- earliest clear direct-memory example before age 18;
- most recent distinct qualifying example from roughly the last 3 years;
- optional middle-life bridge examples for selected forms.

Every episode preserves approximate age, age precision, memory provenance, contemporaneous knowledge, action sequence, outcome, and uncertainty.

Childhood is intentionally included. Odd, mundane, or apparently unimportant childhood memories are not excluded merely for lacking obvious significance. However, direct first-person memory is distinguished from events known only through later family stories, and childhood constraints/limited agency must remain visible rather than being interpreted as adult-equivalent choice.

## Existing v4 pilot

The six already reviewed v4 episodes are not discarded or repeated. They remain a separate `prior_broad_pilot` elicitation class. They may contribute development evidence when codable, but they must not be relabeled as if they had been selected by the v6 paired longitudinal sampling rule.

## Status

V6 is a development/stress-test instrument. It does not promote the codebook or interview to validation-candidate status, authorize target-model scoring, or remove the requirement for later blind calibration and a frozen validation route.
