## Separate historical temporal endpoints

Lilly selects planets connected by conjunction or partile aspect to natal Sun/Moon, favoring the strongest eligible. Strong Jupiter or Venus allegedly bring continued increase in dignity with little effort, moderately afflicted benefics slower rise with obstruction, and weak benefics temporary status that diminishes but does not totally disappear. **Strong Saturn or Mars similarly may indicate continued dignity**, although with repeated risks/interruption; moderately weak malefics gradual erosion with a remnant of fame; severely afflicted malefics complete loss. Thus the source is not reducible to 'benefic good, malefic bad'.

Mercury allegedly brings rapid rise and temporary continuation, but eventual difficulty because it is called unstable. Another clause says the person's **understanding** can preserve the honor when moderately fortified Mercury threatens ruin; this is a source-stated behavioral exception, not an inevitable natal result. The original also permits weak Mercury to produce substantial early promotion followed by unfortunate loss.

Without light-aspecting planets, the author explicitly falls back to the best planet occupying tenth house; if tenth is empty, tenth ruler and its zodiac sign. One claimed enduring status rule requires BOTH tenth ruler and cusp sign in fixed signs, supplemented by friendly aspects from **Sun, Moon, Jupiter or Venus to either tenth cusp or its ruler**. These are composite, conditionally eligible rules rather than several independently successful human observations.

## Internal fixed-star and political aphorisms

The `Speciall Aphorismes` discussion names Hyades, Hercules, Cor Scorpii and Lanx Australis as angular source indicators and adds hard malefic aspects for a possible spectacular rise followed by collapse. Another scenario combines a Sun source with unfavorable historical directions and revolutions to allege enduring loss of high position; **only one** adverse trigger supposedly results in temporary fall followed by recovery. Original solar-sign glyphs are not all firmly deciphered or computationally executable.

Lilly also asserts an extremely broad political source case that a king behaves justly when the King's nativity aligns with that of the kingdom/people. This would require distinct independently calculated charts and outcomes; no national-chart aggregation method or records are supplied. Two old combinations involving the Moon at Midheaven or Moon in Scorpio/Mars Taurus yield pejorative claims of lifelong infamy. These are unsupported historical judgments, **not real-person morality, public-safety, mental-health or career assessments**.
