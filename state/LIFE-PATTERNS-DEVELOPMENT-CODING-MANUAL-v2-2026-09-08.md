# Life Patterns development coding manual v2

Status: **development candidate** for partial-source v8/v8.1 transfer evidence. This is a new version. The frozen v1 manual and all v1 packages/packets remain unchanged historical artifacts.

## Purpose and blind

Apply the supplied theory-neutral Life Patterns measurement package to participant-reported behavioral evidence. Do not use or infer any external model, typology, birth-derived system, diagnosis, spiritual framework, personality score, or target hypothesis.

Target-model, birth/chart, prediction, fit, rank, and outcome information must be unavailable during coding.

## Measurement authority

Use substantive measurement inputs in this order:

1. immutable reconciled theory-blind codebook v1;
2. frozen theory-blind non-action ambiguity-resolution amendment;
3. content-addressed resolved codebook view;
4. resolved development ontology;
5. Structured Coding Procedure V2, including exact non-action and Other Specified registries;
6. `docs/research/LIFE_PATTERNS_RECURRENCE_EVIDENCE_POLICY_v2_2026-09-08.md` for recurrence-evidence semantics;
7. this manual;
8. the supplied development evidence task.

The recurrence policy supersedes only the episode-centric recurrence aggregation language of the v1 development manual/codebook for this new development version. It does not rewrite the historical v1 source artifact.

## Evidence roles are distinct

Do not collapse:

- generalized behavioral recurrence self-report;
- bounded episode;
- exception/counterexample;
- sampled opportunity observation;
- external behavioral record;
- trait/interpretive label;
- narrator-stated causal explanation.

Specificity/detail is not evidential independence.

## Episode task

A development episode task contains one bounded behavioral episode reconstructed from the participant's v8/v8.1 transfer material. It is development-only and may have partial exact source coverage. `exact_source_segments` are primary evidence. Transfer summary text is secondary orientation and cannot substitute for missing exact source support when asserting an observed episode value.

Do not split/merge the episode or import another episode/global pattern unless present in the task's exact source evidence.

A concrete episode establishes what occurred in that bounded instance. It does **not** mechanically increase the frequency strength of a generalized recurrence claim merely because it is a confirming example.

## Repeated-series / generalized recurrence task

A repeated-series task contains exact participant text relevant to behavior reported across multiple opportunities/occasions. It is not an episode and must never be counted as one.

A direct generalized statement such as `always`, `every time`, `usually`, `often`, `sometimes`, `repeatedly`, `daily`, or `common throughout my life` is recurrence self-report evidence when the exact source supports it.

### No minimum-count requirement for generalized self-report

An observed recurrence self-report does **not** require conversion into a numerical occurrence floor. `minimum_reported_occurrences` may be recorded only when exact source text itself supports a bounded lower count/rate. Do not invent `2` merely to satisfy a schema.

### Recurrence strength

Classify only what exact source supports:

- `universal_language`
- `near_universal`
- `usually`
- `often`
- `sometimes`
- `rarely`
- `repeated_unquantified`
- `bounded_rate_or_count`
- `context_conditional`
- `changed_over_time`
- `unclear`

These categories mean **reported recurrence strength**, not objectively verified opportunity-level frequency.

### Exceptions

Keep exception status separate from recurrence strength:

- `exceptions_explicitly_denied`
- `exceptions_reported`
- `exceptions_not_probed_or_unknown`

Do not infer `exceptions_explicitly_denied` from the word `always`. If exceptions are reported, preserve them and qualify the recurrence rather than rescuing the original universal language.

When exact source supports it, classify exception frequency as:

- `none_reported`
- `almost_never`
- `sometimes`
- `often`
- `context_dependent`
- `rough_rate_or_count`
- `unknown`

### Evidence basis

Identify the frequency evidence basis:

- `generalized_self_report`
- `bounded_rate_or_count_self_report`
- `sampled_opportunities`
- `external_record_or_observation`
- `mixed_basis`

For current v8/v8.1 exact-source series, use only what exact source justifies. Summary fields are orientation and cannot upgrade the basis.

## Episode-frequency firewall

Hard rules:

1. A self-selected confirming episode is not independent frequency support for an already asserted recurrence claim.
2. Multiple self-selected confirming examples do not become representative by accumulation.
3. Episode values remain valid episode-level evidence.
4. Counterexamples can qualify recurrence and reveal boundary conditions.
5. Opportunity-level frequency inference requires a sampling frame or bounded exhaustive opportunity set that makes observations representative/informative.
6. Never create a recurrence score by adding confirming episodes to a generalized series claim.

## Primary evidence state

Use only:

- `observed`
- `insufficient`
- `not_applicable`

For an episode, `observed` means the exact source supports the episode-level behavioral value.

For repeated-series evidence, `observed` means the exact source supports a report that the specified behavioral value recurred in an interpretable scope. It does **not** mean objective frequency is independently verified.

Use `not_applicable` only when the observable prerequisite is affirmatively absent. Use `insufficient` when it may apply but exact evidence cannot support a reliable decision.

## Substantive values and sequences

For `observed`:

- emit only values allowed by the exact resolved ontology;
- never invent/rename/merge/split values;
- use `single` for one supported value;
- use `ordered_sequence` for two or more supported values with established order;
- preserve order exactly;
- use `unordered_multiple` only when multiple values are independently supported but order cannot be established;
- do not emit multiple values as a hedge; use `insufficient`.

## Other Specified

Use registered `OS` only when the observable prerequisite is satisfied, behavior belongs in the observable, and no listed value adequately represents it. An observed OS response requires a concrete description grounded in exact source evidence. OS is not a substitute for insufficient evidence.

## Non-action gate

A registered non-action value may be coded only when all four elements are established:

1. awareness;
2. meaningful opportunity/window;
3. reasonable feasibility;
4. established non-occurrence during that window.

If any element is unclear, use `insufficient`. Silence/non-mention is never non-action.

Recurring non-action claims do not waive this rule. Exact source must support the behavioral non-action claim; do not transform a generic recurrence statement into refusal/delay/avoidance without the prerequisites.

## Missingness and uncertainty

Allowed flags remain:

- `UNK`
- `APPROX`
- `CONFLICTING-RECALL`
- `UNCLEAR-AGENCY`
- `UNCLEAR-AWARENESS`
- `UNCLEAR-OPPORTUNITY`
- `UNCLEAR-FEASIBILITY`
- `UNCLEAR-WINDOW`
- `UNCLEAR-SEQUENCE`
- `UNCLEAR-ENDPOINT`

Use only when relevant. Do not manufacture precision from approximate ages, periods, counts, rates, scopes, or exception frequencies.

## Source provenance

Observed episode and series annotations require exact source-segment citations from the supplied task. Transfer summaries cannot serve as primary evidence.

A participant-stated explanation is evidence that the participant reported the explanation, not proof of objective causation.

## Context and recurrence follow-up semantics

When a generalized recurrence claim is being collected/interpreted, useful uncertainty usually concerns:

1. the opportunity/scope denominator;
2. boundary conditions;
3. exception frequency;
4. temporal change;
5. meaning/sequence/prerequisites when still ambiguous.

Do not require a confirming anecdote as ritual corroboration. Ask for an incident only when it has expected information gain or belongs to a valid sampling design.

## Person-level aggregation

Keep recurrence self-report, episode existence evidence, counterexamples, and sampled frequency evidence separate.

A person-level summary may preserve a generalized recurrence statement even when there is no confirming episode. Conversely, one vivid episode does not establish recurrence.

Do not increase recurrence-frequency strength merely because confirming examples exist. Do not average incompatible evidence roles into a pseudo-precise score.

## Theory exposure

Preserve task theory-exposure provenance. Do not infer hidden exposure. Exposure metadata is not a behavioral value.

## No confidence theater

Do not invent numerical confidence, personality scores, rankings, latent traits, typology assignments, or pseudo-precise frequency estimates. Use explicit uncertainty/missingness and preserve the evidence basis.

## Development boundary

All outputs are development-only. Automated self-consistency cannot promote them to validation. Independent human calibration and a separately frozen validation route remain required before confirmatory target-model comparison.
