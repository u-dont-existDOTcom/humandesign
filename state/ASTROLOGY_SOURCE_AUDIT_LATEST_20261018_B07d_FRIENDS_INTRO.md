## Conditional source hierarchy and differences between friend count and integrity

The author first instructs astrologers to consider planets **occupying the eleventh house or aspecting it**, then planets **occupying the first house**, categorized benefic/malefic/intermediate, and finally the **lord of the eleventh and planets aspecting that ruler**. These are prioritized alternatives and fallbacks, not three equal votes.

**Strong Jupiter or Venus** in eligible placements are alleged to indicate many friends, friends who provide material/practical help, and sincere affection—three different possible outcomes. The original also adds a lunar-node glyph whose alleged result depends on six favorable versus six unfavorable zodiac signs; exact original sign/glyph parity remains to be resolved. Weakened benefics allegedly produce moderate friendships, little assistance, or mixtures of sincere and deceitful people, even when friend count itself is not precisely forecast.

**Saturn or Mars** in the relevant houses is claimed to indicate **numerous but insincere friends even when the planets are well dignified**; when those planets are weak, the verdict becomes **few and unreliable friends**. The source compares the southern node to the malefics but expressly says it is less adverse than Mars. Lilly adds an unsupported retrospective anecdote about especially bad friends from a peregrine malefic in a movable or common sign. No reference cases or denominator are supplied, and the categorization must not be treated as evidence of real dishonesty.

**Sun, Moon and Mercury** are placed in a neutral category, with one additional glyph unresolved. Their strong placements in first/eleventh allegedly give numerous but socially modest and more reliable friends, whereas weak positions imply few friends and instability. When planets of many natures occupy those houses, the author allows mixed relationships and asks for the most fortified planet; when both houses are empty he falls back explicitly to the lord of the eleventh. No quantitative resolution for ties is given.

## Special friendship rules and distinct social outcomes

The `Speciall Rules concerning Friends` subsection attributes many friends to benefics in any of **six named houses** (eleventh, fifth, tenth, seventh, first or ninth), versus many *enemies* to malefics in the twelfth or angles. It separately mentions difficulty finding friends if Cancer rises, lack of princely favor from Moon in Aquarius/Pisces, and poor friendship from separation of first- and eleventh-house rulers. Reciprocal retrograde separation between tenth- and first-house rulers allegedly changes **who dislikes whom** in a sovereign–native relationship. Those political/social attitudes are not the same as a friends count.

The closing original lines assign Saturn eleventh supposedly false or idle friends, Mars eleventh no faithful friends, Sun eleventh influential friends, Venus eleventh friendships made through women, Mercury eleventh many friends, and **Moon eleventh widespread acquaintance while still providing few close friends**. This explicit distinction between visibility/acquaintances and close reciprocal relationships is an important conceptual caution. No real person's sexuality, loyalty, character, trustworthiness or social life is established by such assertions.
