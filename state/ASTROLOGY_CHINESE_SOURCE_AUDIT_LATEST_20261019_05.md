## Methodologically important original claims

- The text explicitly defines **day stem as 天元**, **earthly branches as 地元**, and hidden-branch factors as **人元**; it lists Yang stems Jia/Bing/Wu/Geng/Ren and Yin Yi/Ding/Ji/Xin/Gui, with conditional traditional seasonal metaphors for each.
- The Earthly Branches section identifies four birth/growth branches Yin/Shen/Si/Hai, four storehouses Chen/Xu/Chou/Wei, and four defeat branches Zi/Wu/Mao/You. It distinguishes clash, punishment, and harm; outcomes depend on relative strength and chart context, not merely a single universal condition.
- Its particularly relevant caveat is **「陽順陰逆，其理固殊，陽生陰死，其論勿執。」** and the commentary **「若專執生死推斷，則誤矣」**: historical source explicitly warns against blindly or rigidly deriving judgments from life/death stage labels. It is not itself evidence about actual lifespan.
- The pattern chapter differentiates Officer and Killing interactions based on source conditions, warns against treating generic remote linkage or mixed-month categories as automatic patterns, and conditions the hurting-officer/Officer claim on chart strength, seals, wealth and competition.
- The annual luck chapter distinguishes a **ten-year 大運** analogy from **太歲** annual influence, and prints specific Yin/Yang and stem/branch harmony/amity examples; these are textual case illustrations, not an implemented forecast for each of 60 stem-branch pairs.
- `體用論` explicitly describes multiple possible analysis levels for body/function and rejects choosing a static interpretation independently of chart context.

The bounded `bazi/ditiansui_historical_conventions.py` implements **only exact enumerations and a handful of source-declared harmony examples**. Missing/unverified birth calendar, solar-term timing, source body-strength heuristics, competing school changes and whole year/day/hour pillar calculations remain unspecified; unsupported combinations fail closed or return unknown. Never infer health, lifespan, morality, relationships, gender identity or criminal behavior from old text.
