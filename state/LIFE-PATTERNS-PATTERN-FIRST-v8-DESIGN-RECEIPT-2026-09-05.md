# Life Patterns pattern-first longitudinal interview v8 — design receipt

Date: 2026-09-05

## Reason for revision

The prior longitudinal v7 prompt centered exact earliest and recent episodes. Participant feedback correctly identified a construct-level problem: isolated memories can be vivid, unusual, and unrepresentative, while the AstroHD research goal ultimately concerns recurring behavioral patterns and change across life.

The revision therefore makes recurring participant-reported patterns the primary interview object and uses concrete episodes as evidence anchors, clarification, and falsification checks.

## Methodological rationale

Single behavioral incidents are too narrow to support broad disposition claims reliably. The interview must aggregate across comparable occasions and preserve contextual variation rather than infer a general pattern from a vivid event.

The existing reconciled neutral codebook already distinguishes:

- episode-level observations;
- narrator global claims;
- provisional recurrent patterns requiring multiple qualifying episodes or one detailed episode plus an anchored repeated-series report;
- context-dependent/mixed summaries;
- temporal-change summaries across comparable time-ordered opportunities.

V8 aligns elicitation with that architecture instead of treating exact episodes as the primary target.

## V8 structure

For each neutral behavioral domain:

1. ask what generally tends to happen;
2. establish meaningful context boundaries;
3. ask whether the pattern changed across life and record approximate age/life phases;
4. request only enough concrete evidence to anchor the claim;
5. preserve anchored repeated-series reports when the participant recalls recurrence without narrating every incident;
6. ask once for an important exception/counterexample;
7. classify support strength separately from the participant's pattern claim.

Support states are:

- self_report_only;
- one_episode_only;
- anchored_series;
- multiple_episodes;
- mixed_or_context_dependent;
- uncertain.

A participant claim is never silently promoted to an established recurring behavioral finding.

## Relationship to prior pilot material

Existing broad-pilot memories remain preserved. They may later support, qualify, or contradict a participant-reported recurring pattern if the participant identifies them as relevant. A vivid random memory may also remain an episode with no person-level pattern implication.

No existing approved pilot episode should be discarded solely because it is unusual, childhood-based, or not representative.

## Scientific boundary

The participant-facing interviewer remains blind to AstroHD, chart data, birth data, target predictions, and model fit. This blindness is intended to prevent elicitation from being steered by target-model expectations; it does not make the research purpose unrelated to AstroHD.

V8 is development-only until calibration and validation-route requirements are satisfied. It does not authorize target-model scoring or reveal.
