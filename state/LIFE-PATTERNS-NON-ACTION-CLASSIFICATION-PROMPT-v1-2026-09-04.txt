Life Patterns neutral codebook — non-action subcode classification prompt v1

You are performing a narrow theory-neutral measurement-editing task.

You will receive a frozen behavioral codebook containing primary observables and substantive subcodes. You do not know what external hypothesis, theory, model, typology, test, birth-based system, or prediction may eventually be compared with these behavioral measurements.

Do not guess or ask what that external purpose is. Do not use astrology, personality typologies, diagnoses, spiritual/esoteric systems, or any other external theory.

YOUR ONLY TASK

Classify every listed substantive subcode exactly once according to whether assigning that subcode asserts substantive NON-ACTION that requires an Awareness–Opportunity–Feasibility–Established-Non-Action gate.

Use exactly these three classifications:

1. non_action
2. not_non_action
3. ambiguous

OPERATIONAL DEFINITION OF non_action

Classify a subcode as non_action when the truth of that subcode depends on establishing that a relevant action did not occur, did not begin, did not continue, was not requested, was not checked, was not completed, received no response, or otherwise remained absent during a meaningful opportunity/window.

For such a value, coding it from mere silence/non-mention would be invalid. A coder would need to establish all four:

- awareness of the relevant opportunity/demand/possible action;
- a meaningful opportunity/window;
- reasonable feasibility;
- evidence that the action in fact did not occur during that window.

OPERATIONAL DEFINITION OF not_non_action

Classify a subcode as not_non_action when it describes affirmative reported behavior, an explicit action, an explicit communication, an explicit selection/decision, or another positively evidenced event whose coding does not depend on inferring absence during an opportunity window.

An explicit action can include declining, refusing, exiting, postponing under an explicitly stated plan/condition, delegating, requesting, accepting, switching, stopping, or similar behavior when the subcode itself requires that affirmative behavior to be reported. Do not classify something as non_action merely because the action results in less participation or cessation.

OPERATIONAL DEFINITION OF ambiguous

Use ambiguous only when the frozen subcode wording itself can reasonably cover both:

- an affirmative reported action/decision; and
- absence/nonoccurrence that would require the four-part non-action gate,

such that the codebook does not specify which evidentiary form is required.

Do not force a binary classification merely to complete the task. Ambiguity is useful development evidence and will block procedure freeze until separately resolved under a theory-blind revision.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS

- Explicitly declines/refuses/withdraws/exits: usually affirmative behavior, not automatically non_action.
- Explicitly schedules or defers to a stated time/condition: affirmative decision behavior unless the subcode also permits mere failure to act.
- “Does not respond,” “takes no action,” “does not request,” “does not check,” “does not complete,” or equivalent absence during a feasible window: non_action.
- “Waits” can be affirmative behavior if the code requires an explicitly reported waiting decision/state; classify ambiguous if the wording also permits inferring waiting merely from no action.
- Do not classify based on whether a behavior seems good/bad, avoidant, disciplined, impulsive, compliant, resistant, etc.
