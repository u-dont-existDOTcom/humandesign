**Historical Hyleg selection:** Source defines Hylech/Hyleg/Apheta as the planet or point alleged to signify lifespan when directed. Lilly begins with **Sun by day, Moon by night**, if in eligible Hylegical houses **first, tenth, eleventh, seventh, ninth**, with specified house orbs; candidates under earth are generally excluded **except within 25° of Ascendant**, and the eighth/twelfth are normally ineligible. If the preferred light fails, he tests the other. If both fail he considers a prior new Moon in a day chart or full Moon in a night chart, a planet with greatest essential dignities (at least three fortitudes) in the selected longitude and an eligible Aphetical house. Otherwise take Ascendant. He also acknowledges some schools always take Ascendant. In night charts alternative points include natal Moon, preceding full-Moon degree, and natal Part of Fortune; day charts can regard the last new Moon even if a full Moon intervened. Comparison of both luminaries sometimes overrides simple sect preference using house location, dignity and favorable dispositors.

**Explicit authorial uncertainty:** On original PDF563 Lilly says he remains dissatisfied with both how to determine the Hyleg and who correctly counts as the death-associated Anareta/interficient planet. This is **not a categorical rejection of any one theory or proof they fail**; it is his own explicit statement that he has not settled their interpretation.

**Anareta candidates and competing traditions:** The historical eight-house interficient position includes **5° before the eighth cusp or 25° after**. Another tradition nominates eighth ruler, planet conjoined to it (a branch Lilly says he does not approve), and the relevant dispositors. Older printed marginal Montulmo opinion disputes some harmful sextiles. A further attributed Arabian set includes directed sixth/eighth/seventh/fourth cusps, water sign conditions, cloudy/dark sectors, eclipses, comets, azimene degrees, angular relations of Mercury, and other associations. These numerous alternatives are retained as original-source explanations, never independent mortality events.

**Alcochodon or giver of years:** One tradition selects the planet with greatest essential dignity at Hyleg and an aspect to it, assigning its supposedly great/greater/lesser years (numeric source cross-reference to first edition pp.57–83). It allows obstructive directions, injuries and general calamity as exceptions. A light in its own house/exaltation may be Hyleg and Alcochodon; otherwise sign ruler can be selected. For tied planets prefer one aspecting the Hyleg, then greater essential dignity, and historically consider oriental position by day and angular first/tenth position.

**Second explicit negative statement:** On original PDF565 Lilly again says he is *not sufficiently satisfied* with Hyleg, Anareta or Alcochodon and intends to check his cases of deceased people before publishing further confidence. No original certificates, dated events, independent corroboration or forward test are supplied.
