# Life Patterns v2 — focused semantic tests

Candidate-only specification.

- `T001 ABSENCE_ROUTE`: a factual nonoccurrence placed in a non-absence assertion type must fail; an attributed appraisal about nonoccurrence remains appraisal.
- `T002 TIMING_IMMUTABILITY`: evidence first obtained after a thread's first proposal must remain post-first-proposal through every revision and cannot become a preproposal anchor.
- `T003 TERMINAL_GROUNDING`: a revised terminal proposition unsupported by its inherited earlier anchor cannot resolve accepted; current-proposition grounding is required.
- `T004 FACT_SUPERSESSION`: a correction must append a provenance-bound successor, preserve the prior fact, forbid branching/in-place mutation, project only the current leaf, and disallow superseded operative grounding.
- `T005 FACT_QUALIFICATION`: source-bound fact-specific uncertainty must remain attached to that fact through freeze/projection; an episode-only note is insufficient.
- `T006 PERSON_LEVEL_FIREWALL`: multiple episode facts cannot be aggregated into person-level recurrence/typicality/frequency/trait/pattern; that semantic level may come only from an accepted resolved pattern.

Regression expectations: preserve open-world facts, four-gate absence semantics, positive-fact preservation, attributed appraisal, source-supported sequence, post-first-proposal scope semantics, no raw-source reread, deterministic freeze, participant person-level authority, and no completion denominator.
