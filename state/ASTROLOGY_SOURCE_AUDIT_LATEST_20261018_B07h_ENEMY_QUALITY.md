## Source distinctions and counterintuitive strength rankings

The author chooses planets in the seventh or twelfth houses, rulers of seventh/twelfth and planets opposing the luminaries. He distinguishes supposed **open adversaries** indicated by seventh-house occupants or planetary oppositions to the lights from supposed **concealed adversaries** indicated by twelfth occupants. This is a period source attribution, **not evidence that anyone is secretly hostile or deceptive**.

A key counterexample is the **lord of the eleventh in the seventh**: according to Lilly, people signified by it can allegedly change from friend to enemy or back as favorable or adverse historical `Directions` occur. The converse example places **lord of the twelfth in the Ascendant**, claiming such adversaries instead prove friends because the Ascendant ruler restrains malice. Both are conditional relationship reversals rather than permanent labels.

An internal `Prevalency of Enemies` subsection then ranks three alleged source factors: (1) planetary nature, (2) natal house placement, (3) essential dignity. In one original counterintuitive claim, planets called **benefic** are associated with **powerful enemies**, while those called **malefic** with less prominent ones. Regardless of benefic/malefic character, a sufficiently dignified/angular signifier is also said to imply influential enemies; succedent planets allegedly middle rank, cadent little rank. Essential dignities are alleged to indicate power; peregrine, detriment or fall obscure status; weaker triplicity, term or face dignities intermediate or low circumstances. These alternative hierarchies have no numerical weights, confidence score or quantified social class boundaries.
