# Life Patterns Neutral Codebook — Independent Replication Crosswalk

Date: 2026-09-03

Status: theory-exposed audit of two independently generated theory-blind development drafts. This document does **not** revise either draft and does not authorize target-model mapping or validation use.

## Inputs

- `state/LIFE-PATTERNS-NEUTRAL-CODEBOOK-INDEPENDENT-DRAFT-v1-2026-09-03.md`
- `state/LIFE-PATTERNS-NEUTRAL-CODEBOOK-INDEPENDENT-REPLICATION-DRAFT-v1-2026-09-03.md`

The first draft was generated in a fresh theory-blind account/chat from a relatively detailed prompt whose domain examples were written by a theory-exposed source. The replication draft was generated in another fresh theory-blind account/chat from a substantially less seeded prompt that explicitly instructed the author to choose substantive behavioral domains itself.

## Main result

The replication materially reduces the concern that the first draft's major behavioral domains were artifacts of the theory-exposed seed prompt.

Of the replication draft's 18 substantive codes, **17 have a meaningful direct or composite analogue in the first draft**. One replication construct, response to explicit rules/procedures, has no dedicated first-draft counterpart and therefore demonstrates that the replication did not merely reproduce the first prompt's implied domain list.

This is evidence of **domain-level convergence**, not proof of zero latent pretraining exposure and not evidence for Human Design/AstroHD validity.

No obvious Human Design or astrology-specific vocabulary, architecture, or canonical construct structure appears in the replication draft.

## What independently reappeared

Despite the less seeded replication prompt, the following broad behavioral families reappeared independently:

- initiation/start timing;
- planning/preparation;
- decision/choice process;
- continuation after difficulty;
- strategy change after feedback;
- commitment/intention follow-through;
- repeated routines;
- checking/review;
- information seeking;
- help seeking;
- social coordination;
- response to requests/limits;
- disagreement behavior;
- interpersonal repair;
- engagement with unfamiliar/uncertain options;
- allocation under competing constraints;
- behavior during strong reported internal states.

This makes it less plausible that those broad domains appeared in Draft 1 only because a theory-exposed prompt author selected them to resemble a target model.

## First-draft material that did not reappear as separate replication codes

Several Draft 1 observables were not independently regenerated as their own primary codes. Some are represented only as modifiers, subcodes, or general procedure in the replication:

- OBS002 Reported cue guiding the next action;
- OBS005 Exposure to a recognized possible loss;
- OBS006 Option generation as distinct from choice resolution;
- OBS008 Revision after an initial decision as a distinct observable;
- OBS013 Use of external structure;
- OBS017 Response to a recognized error;
- OBS018 Completion and follow-up as a distinct observable;
- OBS020 Change after prior experience as a distinct observable;
- OBS021 Response to a salient competing option;
- OBS023 Recovery/re-engagement as a distinct observable;
- OBS024 fatigue/load and OBS025 pressure as separate observables rather than context/state modifiers;
- OBS029 Communicating needs, limits, or uncertainty;
- OBS034 Context-linked variability and OBS035 temporal change as primary observable IDs rather than derived summary procedures.

These non-replicated distinctions should **not** be deleted merely because they failed to recur once. They should be treated as candidate extensions requiring blind reconciliation and/or pilot evidence rather than automatically promoted to the consensus core.

## Replication-only material

`NBM-E03 Response to Explicit Rules or Procedures` is the clearest substantive addition unique to the replication. This is useful methodologically: it shows that the less seeded author generated at least one plausible domain beyond the first draft rather than simply converging by reproducing an implied list.

## Measurement-quality comparison

The replication draft is substantially more compact: 18 substantive codes rather than 35. That reduces coder burden and several redundancies noted in the first-draft audit. It also handles context/time variation more often as modifiers and summaries rather than dedicated observables.

Potential advantages of the replication structure, independent of any target model:

- fewer primary codes;
- clearer separation of goal continuation from strategy modification;
- explicit Awareness–Opportunity–Feasibility non-action gate;
- explicit memory certainty/missingness fields;
- explicit separation between episode coding and recurrence summaries;
- a dedicated rule/procedure construct;
- less duplication around intention-to-action than Draft 1.

Potential limitations:

- some behaviorally distinct mechanisms from Draft 1 are compressed into broad codes;
- C03 coordination is broader than the first draft's social-initiation construct and may hide initiator/responder distinctions;
- F01 combines emotion and bodily states that may prove behaviorally heterogeneous;
- A03 combines several choice mechanisms that Draft 1 separates;
- B01/B02 may require careful sequential multi-coding to preserve immediate barrier response versus later strategy revision;
- the two-occurrence recurrence default remains a development convention requiring pilot justification rather than a validated threshold.

## Leakage audit

No direct indicators of Human Design/astrology leakage were identified in the replication:

- no HD types, strategies, authorities, profiles, centers, gates, channels, circuitry, signatures, not-self themes, definition, incarnation-cross structure, or close neutralized equivalents;
- no zodiac signs, planets-as-personality-significators, houses, aspects, elements/modalities, rulerships, or birth-time constructs;
- no birth input or target-model output is requested by the coding system;
- the output explicitly rejects personality typing and external-theory alignment.

The replication still cannot prove absence of latent pretrained knowledge. Its scientific value is that the relevant prompt context was theory-blind and substantially less domain-seeded, and the first output was frozen before model comparison.

## Current disposition

1. Preserve both drafts unchanged.
2. Do **not** select codes based on later HD/AstroHD fit.
3. Treat the 17-overlap result as evidence for a robust candidate behavioral domain family, not as a final ontology.
4. Do not have the current theory-exposed project AI perform substantive reconciliation.
5. Use a third fresh theory-blind author/session to reconcile the two drafts using only measurement criteria: behavioral observability, nonredundancy, coder burden, evidence requirements, and expected human reliability.
6. Freeze that reconciliation output before revealing any target theory/model.
7. Then conduct blind human pilot coding/reliability development before promoting a validation-candidate release.

## Scientific claim boundary

This replication supports only the claim that many of the broad behavioral coding domains are reproducible under two differently seeded theory-blind generation contexts. It does not establish construct validity, human inter-rater reliability, model discrimination, or validity of any birth-derived theory.
