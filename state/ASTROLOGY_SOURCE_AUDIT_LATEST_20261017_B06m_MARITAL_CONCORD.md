## Source-only classifications and useful methodological constraints

Lilly links the presumed number-of-wives/marriage planets and Moon to harmony. Fortunate planets in **friendly aspects to Moon** supposedly mean lasting affection and companionship; malefics plus hard square/opposition to Moon allegedly mean continual quarrelling. He **does not** simply equate aspect favorability with spouse affection: his text gives **intermediate cases**, such as a benign planet in an ill aspect still yielding moderate agreement, and a malefic in a friendly aspect still producing indifferent or incomplete accord. The favorable/malefic nature and the favorable/hard aspect must be recorded as separate antecedents. He explicitly says these original claims refer to **Moon and seventh-house lord**, not to every other significator.

The chapter then calls for **comparison of two independently computed nativities** when available, emphasizing one spouse's Sun in the very **same sign and degree** as the other's Moon, or the inverse wife Moon at man's Sun. This is much more specific than merely sharing a zodiac sign; it cannot be evaluated without two authenticated time/place birth records and independently computed chart positions. When the second chart is unavailable, the source does not authorize choosing one after observing the relationship narrative.

Lilly also contrasts sextile/trine with hostile aspects, multiple benefic and malevolent testimonies, Venus twelfth and alleged class/wealth fortune, mutable and common zodiac signs, supposed dissatisfaction with one partner, and compounded Moon-Sun humane-signs/benefic configurations across PDF632–633. The alleged negative branch requires adverse Sun–Moon contacts **and** a hard malefic contact, not simply an unspecified malefic presence. The original paragraph crossing the PDF632–633 page boundary must be treated continuously. Other lines claim the wife is secretly attracted to another man from a waning Moon–Mars conjunction in a movable sign, or that Moon rules seventh, wanes and occupies eighth to imply unhappy marriage. These are unsupported sexual-moral or intimate psychology stereotypes. They are not evidence of real human jealousy, infidelity, psychological motives, abuse, danger or happiness.
