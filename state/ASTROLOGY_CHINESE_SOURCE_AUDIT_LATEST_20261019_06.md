## Material conceptual findings and literal source constraints

The source explicitly differentiates two nominally 'wood' groupings, `寅卯辰` (*direction/方*) and `亥卯未` (*three-harmony/局*). Its 05 commentary warns that merely combining them may become **too strong**, and that compatibility of Heavenly Stems and subsequent luck states is material. It does not license the simple rule “more Wood → always better.”

The 07/08 pair explicitly contrasts `真從`/genuine following, `真化`/genuine transformation and their `假` counterparts. A source example defines genuine following by an extremely weak day-master with **no supportive factors** across three source levels; another defines apparent following by **some remaining support**. The text says externally visible conditions such as wealth/official force, month-command, hidden support, and day/month/year/hour placement determine which interpretation could apply. A classifier lacking complete independently verified birth pillars and hidden-stem placements cannot infer these labels responsibly.

The 11 `精神論` contrasts historic elemental *essence/spirit* metaphors and states that one should not assess them `一偏` (one-sidedly). These words are old-school symbolic cosmology, **not** modern clinical measurements of mood, spiritual experience, nervous system functioning or biological vigor.

The 12 `衰旺論` explicitly qualifies the heuristic `旺則宜洩宜傷，衰則喜幫喜助`: **strength can contain weakness** (`旺中有衰者存，不可損也`); **weakness can contain strength** (`衰中有旺者存，不可益也`); even apparent extreme cases may break the general rule. These are text-internal counterexamples against naive ordinal strength→outcome predictions.

The 08 transmitted **commentary** includes moralizing and family-status conjectures about `心術不端` and `異姓孤兒`. Those are **historical allegations** in unverified transmitted prose, not evidence of an actual person's morality, adoption background, family relationships, future wealth or achievement. They must never be used as a person-level fact or survey ground truth.
