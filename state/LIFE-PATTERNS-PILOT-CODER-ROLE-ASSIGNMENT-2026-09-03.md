## Assignment decision

### Blind Human Auditor A

The external person designated by the owner may serve as **BLIND-HUMAN-AUDITOR-A** if the blind attestation in `docs/research/LIFE_PATTERNS_BLIND_CODER_PACKET.md` is satisfied before coding begins.

The auditor codes a strategically selected calibration subset independently, without seeing LLM labels first. The auditor is not expected to code the entire corpus.

Generic prior familiarity with astrology, personality systems, psychology, or behavioral science does not automatically disqualify the auditor. Project-specific target mappings, predictions, chart outputs, model-fit results, and information about which neutral codes favor a target model must remain unavailable during the blind pass.

### Primary coding

The full development corpus is intended to be coded by a high-capability theory-blind LLM using repeated isolated passes under a frozen prompt/procedure.

At least three independent blinded LLM passes are the planning default when feasible. Raw outputs remain frozen. Disagreement routes to audit/adjudication rather than being silently forced into a label.

The exact production model/version is not pinned by this role-assignment record.

### Owner

The owner is substantially exposed to the Human Design/AstroHD research question, target-model structure, and project-specific mappings. The owner therefore does not count as a blind calibration auditor.

This is role separation, not a claim that the owner cannot apply the codebook carefully.

The owner may perform a separate **theory-exposed sensitivity pass** after the blind automated and human-auditor outputs for the relevant sample are frozen, without seeing those outputs first.

The owner sensitivity pass:

1. uses the same frozen codebook/procedure/corpus;
2. is explicitly labeled theory-exposed;
3. is excluded from blind human calibration metrics;
4. does not silently determine codebook revisions;
5. may be compared afterward to test whether target-theory exposure materially shifts neutral coding.

## Current development dependency

A second external human is **not required to start the LLM-primary development pilot**.

Current development roles:

- primary production coder: high-capability theory-blind LLM, exact model not yet pinned;
- blind human calibration auditor: `BLIND-HUMAN-AUDITOR-A`, eligibility pending attestation;
- theory-exposed sensitivity coder: owner, optional and post-freeze only.

If a later validation route requires a formal multi-human replacement/equivalence test, additional human annotators may be recruited at that later stage under a separately frozen design. That later requirement must not be back-projected into the current development pilot.
