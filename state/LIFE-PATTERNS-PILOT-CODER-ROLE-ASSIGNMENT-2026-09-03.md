# Life Patterns Pilot — Coder Role Assignment Boundary

Date: 2026-09-03

Status: planning/assignment record only. Does not authorize contact, recruitment, payment, target-model exposure, validation promotion, or model execution.

## Owner-supplied candidates

The owner designated two people for possible coding: the owner themself and one external person.

Personal identities are intentionally not stored in this public repository record.

## Assignment decision

### Blind Coder A

The external person designated by the owner may serve as **Blind Coder A** if the blind attestation in `docs/research/LIFE_PATTERNS_BLIND_CODER_PACKET.md` is satisfied before reliability coding begins.

Generic prior familiarity with astrology, personality systems, psychology, or behavioral science does not automatically disqualify the coder. Project-specific target mappings, predictions, chart outputs, model-fit results, and information about which neutral codes favor a target model must remain unavailable during first-pass coding.

### Owner

The owner is already substantially exposed to the Human Design/AstroHD research question, target-model structure, and project-specific mappings. Therefore the owner must **not** count as one of the two independent theory-blind benchmark coders required by the frozen blind-pilot protocol.

This is a role-separation decision, not a claim that the owner is unable to apply the codebook carefully.

The owner may optionally perform a separate **theory-exposed sensitivity pass** under these safeguards:

1. the two blind benchmark coders complete and freeze their independent first-pass outputs first;
2. the owner does not see either blind coder's labels, notes, disagreements, or reliability results before completing the sensitivity pass;
3. the owner uses the same frozen codebook/procedure/corpus;
4. the owner pass is explicitly labeled theory-exposed and excluded from the primary human-human reliability estimate;
5. the owner pass does not determine codebook revision decisions;
6. any comparison between owner and blind-coder outputs is reported only as a methodological sensitivity analysis of possible theory-exposure effects.

## Remaining benchmark dependency

A second external theory-blind human is still required as **Blind Coder B** for the primary human-human reliability benchmark.

The benchmark pair is therefore:

- Blind Coder A: owner-designated external person, eligibility pending attestation;
- Blind Coder B: not yet designated.

Optional non-benchmark sensitivity coder:

- theory-exposed owner, only after blind first-pass outputs are frozen and without access to them.
