## Survey: reports are not discoveries

The application now dispatches a direct formulation explicitly. An exact single-source quotation continues to use the original direct-report authority mechanism. A faithful paraphrase or summary across several answers is saved separately as a model-authored source summary, linked to the original statements and corrections. It is not sent to inference approval, does not manufacture a participant Yes, and does not enter the research core as a participant-adjudicated proposal.

Your patterns identifies these items as “Summary of your reports — not a new inference.” They remain inspectable and correctable. Original accepted records and source wording are not rewritten.
