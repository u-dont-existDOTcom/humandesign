Several source-interpretive pitfalls preserved:

- The printed Body Lord for Zi/Wu years is `火玲星`, not automatically resolved to either Fire Star or Bell Star. The original literal glyph may encode a historical combination or textual error. No runtime horoscope assumption.
- The Life Lord *selected star* is not automatically its location. A source example for Wu Life-palace picks Po Jun and separately searches Po Jun's placed palace.
- Historical Life/Body/gender/infant mortality and good/bad social rank language is documentary rhetoric, not medical/safety/death/legal, present-day gender-essentialist, moral or personality classification.
- The transmitted original elemental taxonomy uses mixed labels for Tian Tong (Water+Metal), Tan Lang (Water+Wood) and Qi Sha (Fire+Metal); do not normalize these to one modern school or collapse **18 source stars** to **14 chart primary stars**.
- The Ji-Chou annual trinity of flow positions is an example, *not* a proven rule for all sixty-year stem/branch pairs.
- The childhood limit verse explicitly supplies ages 1–6 plus age 15; the intermediate ages 7–14 should not be generated automatically from a vague `余年一派顺流行` without corroborating exact traditional palace/count rule.

The module `ziwei/quan_shu_vol2_life_body_taxonomy.py` represents a **narrow subset** of these original source placement conventions, accepts only separately validated input year branch/life palace and supplied actual selected-star palace; no Gregorian→Chinese lunar calendar, no real full 14-star chart engine, no annual solar-term or leap-month conversion, no actual human forecast. It returns explicit errors for missing/unlisted chart/calendrical inputs. The full 65 original records contain short exact source quotes + conservative English explanations and source uncertainties; this is *not* an independent sinological translation review.
