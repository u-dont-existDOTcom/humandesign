## Crucial source limitation: no stellar causation of religious belief

Lilly explicitly warns readers not to suppose the stars **enforce or cause** a person to belong to a religion or be religious/irreligious. The author says piety and godliness come from divine grace, while the stars are claimed to disclose *natural inclination* and whether a person persists or changes in it. These are distinct statements: denial of coercion/causation, the author's period theological account of piety, and his narrower symbolic/interpretive claim about disposition and persistence. None constitutes scientifically measured religious causality, religious truth, or validated personal character forecasting.
