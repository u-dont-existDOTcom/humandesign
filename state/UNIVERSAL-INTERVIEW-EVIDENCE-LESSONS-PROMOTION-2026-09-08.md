The promotion preserves these cross-project lessons:

- specificity/detail is not evidential independence;
- generalized behavioral recurrence self-report is evidence and should preserve its quantifier/scope;
- self-selected confirming anecdotes are conditionally sampled and are not independent frequency observations merely because they are concrete;
- default recurrence follow-up is denominator/scope -> boundary conditions -> exception frequency -> relevant temporal change;
- ask for concrete incidents only when they have expected information gain or arise from a valid sampling design;
- counterexamples/exceptions often have greater discriminating value than another confirming anecdote;
- actual opportunity-level frequency needs an appropriate sampling frame rather than volunteered examples;
- do not collect evidence by format/episode quota when the quota is unrelated to the inference;
- distinguish recurrence self-report, bounded episodes, exceptions, sampled opportunities, trait labels, causal explanations, and coder inference;
- retrieve relevant owner-supplied methodology before substantial bespoke method design/freeze;
- human judgment tasks require human-facing interfaces; machine JSON/JSONL is interchange, not the human task;
- when a load-bearing method defect is found before collection, preserve the prior freeze and create a versioned theory/target-blind revision rather than collect under the known defect or silently reinterpret it.
