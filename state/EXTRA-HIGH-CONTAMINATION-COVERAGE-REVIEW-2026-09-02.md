## Extra High verdict

Proposal 1: **REVISE**.

- Do not label a person contaminated. Use a chart-blind, prediction-blind, non-blocking
  `theory-language exposure signal`.
- The signal means only that transcript wording is consistent with prior terminology
  exposure. It cannot establish altered answers, intentional matching, target
  knowledge, endorsement, invalidity, or causal contamination.
- Preserve specificity, source, stance, and language assessability separately.
- Prompt echo cannot elevate a prior-exposure signal. Generic paraphrase matching must
  remain conservative, and unsupported languages must be unassessable rather than
  automatically clean.
- Keep the signal out of eligibility, stopping, prompt flow, scoring, exclusions, and
  primary analysis. Diagnostics and future prespecified secondary sensitivity analysis
  are the outer bound.
- A post-questionnaire direct exposure self-report is a possible later design choice,
  not existing owner authorization.

Proposal 2: **ACCEPT THE INVESTIGATION; DO NOT YET REQUIRE EXPANSION**.

- There is no scientific basis for “70 more” or any other numeric target.
- Audit construct/rule coverage, direct versus indirect evidence, shared statements,
  gaps, and repeated probes in the current bank first.
- Evaluate any addition for incremental/discriminative value, reliability, response
  process, burden, leakage, and demand risk.
- Repeated probes are multiple indicators of one construct, not independent
  confirmations.
- Freeze candidate wording, mappings, scoring, and analyses before participant answers;
  later findings may inform only a future version.
- The endpoint is the smallest questionnaire that adequately covers the prospectively
  defined construct space with sufficient independent evidence and acceptable response
  quality. Count is an output, not an input.
