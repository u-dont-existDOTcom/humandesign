## Important conflicting online textual witnesses

A later, longer transmitted `滴天髓闡微` (Di Tian Sui Chan Wei, in Chinese Text Project and other mirrors) has:
1. Original verse `流到何方住` rather than the short text's `流向可方住`;
2. Second case `得從引助` rather than `無從引助` — different literal semantics of whether elemental support exists;
3. End `所以為憎也` rather than `所以為僧也` — different characters and therefore potentially entirely different outcome characterization. `憎` can mean dislike, whereas `僧` refers to a Buddhist monk. **Do not combine these as if they were the same original clause.**

The longer version also attributes extensive explanatory commentary to Ren Tieqiao; this does not establish its correspondence to the short online edition or an authenticated first printing. Multiple sites can copy a single text and are not independent original witnesses. This study retains the short oldid844368 as **one immutable transmitted witness**, explicitly flags variants and refuses to select a favorable reading based on known people. Independent earliest printed folio/collation is still needed.
