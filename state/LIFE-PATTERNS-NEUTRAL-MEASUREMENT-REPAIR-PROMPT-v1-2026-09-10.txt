REQUIRED PRINCIPLES

1. **Specificity is not independence.** Do not treat several codes derived from the same behavioral act as several independent observations merely because they capture different levels of description.
2. **Missingness is not nonoccurrence.** Phrases such as `not reported`, `unknown`, or transcript silence cannot establish that an action did not occur unless an explicit exhaustive evidence frame justifies that inference.
3. **Affirmative-plus-absence hybrids must be decomposed conceptually.** If a code says the narrator affirmatively did X while Y did not occur, define the affirmative behavior separately from the exact absent proposition. The Awareness–Opportunity–Feasibility–Established-Non-Action gate applies only to the absent proposition.
4. **Name the absence target.** Never use a generic `did not act` gate where the relevant absence is, for example, no alternative search, no request, no comparison, no discussion, no engagement, or no transition.
5. **Facets must be explicit.** When an observable mixes timing, content, mechanism, ownership, source, outcome, extent, or another separable facet, identify facet membership and whether cross-facet values may co-occur.
6. **Do not force one global relation onto unlike facts.** The revised response contract must support co-present facets and preserve temporal order where order is genuinely established.
7. **Resolve nesting and composites.** Where a broad value, narrow value, and/or composite sequence can all describe the same fact, specify a deterministic specificity/precedence/cumulative-coding rule. Do not leave blind coders to decide ad hoc.
8. **Preserve chronology when it matters.** Composite trajectories may need event/stage-level representation rather than several apparently independent static labels.
9. **Preserve uncertainty.** If a boundary cannot be resolved from the current neutral concept, prefer an explicit versioned clarification, split, or retirement over fake precision.
10. **No target optimization.** No repair may be justified by how well it could fit any external model.
