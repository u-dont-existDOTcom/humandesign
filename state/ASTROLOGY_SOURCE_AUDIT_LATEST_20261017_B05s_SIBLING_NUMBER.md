## Historical source conditions

The author selects the third-house window, third ruler and any intercepted planets, with Mars as alleged general brothers indicator and Moon as sisters indicator. Conjunction of all or a majority of supposedly fruitful planets in fruitful signs is claimed to indicate many siblings; barren planets in barren signs few or none; a mixed planet/sign combination gives an intermediate inference. Lilly additionally weights the **number and potency of contributing significators**, without providing calibrated integer count thresholds.

The source identifies Jupiter/Venus as fruitful, Saturn/Mars as barren, optionally adds lunar nodes with ambiguous glyph interpretation, gives mixed luminous Sun and Moon conditions, and treats Mercury as **conditional on its configured planets**. It ranks aspects conjunction over trine, trine over sextile, sextile over square, square over opposition, and partile over platick.

All twelve signs are recorded separately with their authorial explanations. Scorpio is historically classed fruitful *despite* Mars rulership; Libra and Aquarius also receive qualified classifications. These exceptions matter if source rules are ever implemented reproducibly. The sign descriptions use antiquated analogies and reproductive stereotypes, not empirical biology.
