## Source-relevant methodological findings

- **13, moderation:** The commentary explicitly treats balanced phases as an ideal **but offers exceptions** (weak native/strong wealth-official factor; powerful useful factor; unusual directional pattern). Neither simplistic single-axis balance scores nor exceptions have an out-of-sample accuracy estimate.
- **14, hardness and softness:** Multiple precise heavenly-stem/month examples qualify what counts as an appropriate balancing influence, and include a plainly **sexist/moralizing analogy** about women. That analogy is obsolete rhetoric, not a claim this project endorses about real people.
- **15, following established direction:** A set of historical metaphors (`不可逆`) concerns conformity to a prevailing dynamic, not a reproducibly computed biographical forecast.
- **16, heat/cold and moisture/dryness:** Text assigns traditional five-phase seasonal predicates and conditional **exceptions** to phase combinations. These are symbolic cosmology, not physical measurements, nutrition, diagnosis or climate advice. An unusual original `陰逄` character sequence is preserved as printed.
- **17, month command:** Old text explicitly identifies hidden Jia/Bing/Wu stem timing by segments after `立春`: Wu Earth before/through the seventh day, Bing Fire days eight through fourteen, Jia Wood from day fifteen; precise inclusivity is part of old source wording. This is **not independently checked** against verified classical calendrical conventions, astronomical solar term ephemerides, or any reconstructed natal chart.
- **18, birth time:** Contains a sub-hour distinction `子時生人前三刻三分` versus `後四刻七分`, and acknowledges materially different life outcomes among people with identical year/month/day/hour combinations. It invokes finer timing, geography and family context as explanatory differences, and makes the statement **`十有九驗`**, apparently a roughly nine-in-ten success assertion. **No underlying cases, denominators, failures, locked model, prospective design or human observations** are supplied. Count it as an unverified *author claim*, never as observed test accuracy. Without defining calendar granularity and admissible geography/social inputs before testing, adding these explanations after failed predictions would destroy falsifiability. Another unusual original `東酉` is preserved instead of silently normalizing.
